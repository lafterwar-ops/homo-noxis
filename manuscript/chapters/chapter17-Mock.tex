\typesetchapter{Chapter 17}{Mock}

\begin{room}

(Sarah): --- The other six stations, Urs?

(Urs): --- Confirm it. Seismic sounding. I have checked the clocks and the terrain model. The second reader worked separately and returned the same surface positions.

(Sarah): --- Put them on the map.

(Urs): --- More than twenty usable fixes since this started. Eleven minutes apart, all afternoon. The stations receive each stroke at different times; with this many around the source, I can place it from several combinations and check one against another. These are the positions in time order.

(Kylian): --- It goes back on itself.

(Urs): --- Turns at the end of a line and works along the next. Those last points take it east, towards Atum's sector.

(Kylian): --- Have you heard from the three people?

(Urs): --- Nothing. We recovered the end of Atum's transmission here. The other stations give us the track after that.

(Kylian): --- Could the other stations help?

(Urs): --- They do not talk to one another or have each other's locations. Each cell reports here on its own line. We have put the others on hold: doors closed, no visitors without direct clearance.

(Sarah): --- Amastan's line?

(Urs): --- One of his people has answered. He can carry a message.

(Sarah): --- Use that line. I will give the location myself. Ask what his people have seen. Nobody is to enter Atum. And send Alice the coordinates of the survey. I want the latest satellite coverage and whatever she can pull from the archive. Wider than the track. Include the roads into it.

(Urs): --- How far back?

(Sarah): --- A month to start. Keep the dates on everything.

(Kylian): --- Could this loose the Beast?

(Sarah): --- A surface survey cannot do the work of your buried engine. It can help them find it.

(Kylian): --- By what comes back through the Mouth.

(Sarah): --- Yes. And they can move round it, take another return and narrow down where it is. I want to see what they have brought to dig with.

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

(Urs): --- Alice has sent the first images. The newest is radar, less than an hour old. This one is an optical image from this morning. Better detail, but the vehicles will have moved.

(Sarah): --- Put our track over the new one.

(Urs): --- Here. Four heavy vehicles on that cleared strip. The final fixes run along it.

(Sarah): --- There they are. Vibrator trucks. Nomad 90s, by the look of them. And beside the camp, a drilling rig.

(Paul): --- Can we see this moving?

(Urs): --- These are separate passes. I have asked for the next one. We can compare what has changed between them.

(Sarah): --- Go back to this morning. Widen it.

(Kylian): --- Those are the folds. The second ridge is above them. All that white below it?

(Urs): --- Tents. I will enlarge that part.

(Sarah): --- Keep the whole site beside it. Paul, this outer camp has the parked lorries and the storage rows. There is a cleared road from it to the rig. Across the wash, another enclosure.

(Paul): --- More tents.

(Urs): --- Alice marks that one as accommodation, probably. There are vehicles dug into the ridges above both. She has picked out positions round the other side too.

(Kylian): --- Round the Mountain?

(Urs): --- Round the part we can see. She reads it as a military perimeter. The access gaps are covered.

(Sarah): --- How old is the first image with a camp?

(Urs): --- The second of April. Yesterday. The first lorries and two rows of tents beside the southern track. The previous image is from the evening of the first. Bare ground.

(Paul): --- Montel collected the recording on Monday. The thirtieth of March.

(Sarah): --- Three days before those vehicles appear. And the rig?

(Urs): --- Still on its transporter yesterday. This morning it is on the pad, with the second enclosure beside it. More lorries at the road junction. They have been unloading through the night.

(Paul): --- Deliveries?

(Urs): --- Probably. I can show you where they stood on each date. I cannot follow them between the images.

(Sarah): --- Fuel, water, drilling pipe. Men to move it, men to guard them. Expand the southern road.

(Urs): --- Another stopping place here. Vehicles on both sides. Alice has marked a possible checkpoint.

(Kylian): --- All this since yesterday?

(Sarah): --- The camp. They had three days to bring the first loads here. I would look for trucks and a rig already in the region. Alice can work back along that road. Urs, get me the field reports for those dates.

(Paul): --- A survey could account for the trucks and rig. Could it account for all the rest?

(Sarah): --- With an armed escort, yes. But this escort has positions all round the ground they want to enter, and supplies enough to stay. Somebody has paid to bring the survey, the drilling and the soldiers together. This is a government-sized operation.

(Urs): --- The western edge is clearer in this morning's optical pass. Shall I put that up?

(Sarah): --- Put the shot positions back over it.

(Paul): --- What is it?

(Sarah): --- Those steps where the blocks join. Urs, find the salt survey from twenty-three years ago. The original field plan, in Horizon.

(Paul): --- You recognise it?

(Sarah): --- Perhaps. We had to cut a damaged section out of a receiver cable on that job. That left it short, with fewer pickup points. I moved some of the shot positions round the gap so we could finish with the equipment we had. We kept using the layout after replacing the cable. It gave good results.

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

(Urs): --- I have the plan. I have aligned the main rows. Both joins we have covered fit it, within the error of our positions.

(Sarah): --- Yes. The same unequal steps. I taught four men to work from that layout. The results were published; the field plans stayed in Horizon. Somebody has those plans, or learnt from one of my crews.

(Paul): --- France?

(Sarah): --- Montel receives Kylian's lessons. Now somebody is using my private survey method behind a military perimeter. France is where I would start.

(Paul): --- It need not be the same channel.

(Sarah): --- No. The camera cannot teach a survey I made twenty-three years ago. If the two collections meet, they have what he knows of the inside and what my instruments found from outside. Urs, have Alice keep the roads under review. I want the next pass beside these as soon as it arrives.

(Kylian): --- Was Jean-Charles supposed to ask me all that at the safe house?

(Sarah): --- Elise had Justice's packet. Those questions should have gone through her.

(Kylian): --- He told me not to trouble her.

(Paul): --- You agreed.

(Kylian): --- He had kept me alive in the car. By supper he was straightening my collar for me. When he asked about the Mountain, there was something I could do for him. I wanted him to ask again.

(Sarah): --- He did help you. We do not have to erase that to examine the questions.

(Kylian): --- I still had the book on my knees. He waited when I stopped. He did not make me give him any of it.

(Paul): --- That is worth keeping. So are the questions he chose while Elise was elsewhere. Will you give him the route?

(Sarah): --- A leg at a time. Urs opens the transport. Elise divides the route. Jean-Charles receives the ground he needs when he needs it. He gets no station register or call signs. I have no better field operator, and I have no proved traitor. I can withhold a page from either.

(Paul): --- You could ask him why he kept Elise out of it.

(Sarah): --- And receive an explanation I cannot check before we leave. I will have Elise check the old access lists against the names attached to those field sheets.

(Kylian): --- Do they know what an answer can become?

(Sarah): --- I would pay to know that. My Scottish father's Auld Alliance meant uranium for France. My English half appears to be fighting France now.

(Paul): --- You are still deciding how much each of us is allowed to know.

(Sarah): --- You have questioned the man I trust to take us through a desert. I have heard you. I am changing what he is given. Put that on the same record as my appetite for the Mountain. I shall not ask you to rub either out.

(Urs): --- Another stroke, Madame.

(Sarah): --- Have someone keep the sheets. Wake the parts of Horizon that never touched Martinez. Open the routes into that quarter the camp does not know it controls. Close the old roads behind us where we can. You ran them against smugglers, Urs. Use them against men carrying my maps.

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

(Paul): --- Kylian. Before we follow those orders. She has told us she wants the thing beneath your Mountain in her own keeping.

(Kylian): --- I heard her say she was the one hand.

(Paul): --- She has not withdrawn that claim.

(Kylian): --- Ask her to withdraw it and she will say a few words for you. Will that change whose aircraft we need? She wants the soldiers gone. I want them gone. My council meant nothing but good when it erased me. Sarah has bad reasons for taking the road I need. I will go with her and watch what she reaches for when we arrive.

(Paul): --- That may be sound judgment. It may also be how she recruits everybody.

(Sarah): --- Urs, tell Jean-Charles to have the vehicles ready at the strip. Elise gives him the tactical packet on the ground.

(Elise): --- Kylian. Are you coming with us?

(Kylian): --- I am going back.

(Elise): --- They let you leave once. That does not give you permission to return.

(Kylian): --- I asked them to send me to find the enemy within. The five decreed it; Va-Sheva is the ear I report to. At that Threshold I can name the errand before they decide what to do with the man carrying it.

(Paul): --- The distance was meant to keep you from bringing the danger back.

(Kylian): --- It is already there. Five Keepers behind five walls, keeping their part from one another, while an army draws the rooms under their feet. I have findings.

(Elise): --- Findings do not stop a blade.

(Kylian): --- No. Get me to the Threshold. I will answer for coming back.

(Sarah): --- I need to know whether you can do this, Kailan. We cannot carry you up to the seam and then turn back.

(Kylian): --- I can.

(Paul): --- A little while ago you could scarcely speak of her.

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

(Kylian): --- She is still there.

(Paul): --- Yes.

(Kylian): --- Nothing I have just learnt moved her outside the Mountain. I keep seeing her with him at the well. I cannot make that stop. But I can see where those machines stand, too. I cannot stay beside this lake and let them reach her without warning.

(Paul): --- You need not say more.

(Kylian): --- I want her alive, Paul. Let that be enough to get me to the door.

(Sarah): --- Then we leave for Geneva at dusk. Urs has less than an hour. I can put us through the outer chain. At the inner seam I need you.

(Paul): --- Take me with you. Kylian, you said you would try.

(Kylian): --- I remember.

(Paul): --- I want to see that coffin. If it is Egyptian, I want to know how it got there. I could walk into a workshop and ask what they are making. Somebody could actually answer me.

(Sarah): --- Elise, a place for Mr Morgan.

(Paul): --- Thank you. Box the reels. Not this one yet. I need the travelling machine running before this stops.

(Sarah): --- You have it?

(Paul): --- Running. Elise, will you take this interview reel when I stop it? Keep it with the sealed interviews and my notebooks, please.

(Elise): --- I will. The instruction reels go in the fire case. They keep their own boxes.

(Paul): --- I am stopping the fixed machine now. The travelling machine continues.

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

(Elise): --- These are your travel papers. Keep your own sets in this order. The first name takes the helicopter from here. The second goes on the private aircraft's manifest. The third starts at the vehicle chain. Learn them on the way; answer to the name on the page you are carrying.

(Kylian): --- And if they ask me something I do not understand?

(Elise): --- Wait for me.

(Kylian): --- As before.

(Elise): --- Yes.

(Sarah): --- The chain, Elise.

(Elise): --- Helicopter from the lawn to Geneva. Private aircraft to a prepared strip, where Jean-Charles meets us. Vehicles south, animals from the folds. Amastan's line meets us there, or what remains of it. None of the separate bookings should give a clerk much to remember.

(Kylian): --- Who has them all?

(Elise): --- I have their order and your identities. Urs opened the aircraft, fuel and vehicles without the final ground. Each air crew knows its own leg. The vehicle lead gets his road when the aircraft lands. Amastan's line has the last ground and none of the air. Jean-Charles gets his tactical packet when we leave the final vehicle. Sarah knows where the pieces meet. There is no page with the whole journey on it.

(Paul): --- And if one hand fails to meet the next?

(Elise): --- We do not improvise a replacement identity in front of a border officer. Stay beside me. At the folds it will be Amastan's people you obey, however familiar the ground looks to you, Kylian. You came out once. They have to go on living there.

(Paul): --- You have given me no papers for the recorder.

(Elise): --- Madame's medical instrument until the vehicles. The manifest says so. After that, on your body. Keep it with you if a man offers to carry it.

(Paul): --- And the fire case?

(Elise): --- It comes with us, sealed, with one copy of the route papers. If anyone enters the house after we leave, the archive room burns separately. Your interview reels keep their own seals. Nothing is being put back into the instruction archive.

(Paul): --- How much of this was ready before the alarm?

(Elise): --- The way out of a compromised house. I did not have the destination. Please learn the first name before you reach the pilot.

(Sarah): --- Time?

(Elise): --- The helicopter is nearly ready. After that we require air permission, fuel, roads, and the routes still to belong to you.

(Paul): --- I had hoped at least the roads were certain.

(Elise): --- So had I. One small bag each. Only essentials. Pack for very hot days and very cold nights, and wear shoes you can walk in.

(Sarah): --- That is settled. Urs, come and help me pack.

(Urs): --- Yes, Madame. I will bring your medicines.

(Kylian): --- Paul. I know we are in a rush, but this is important. Come back to the tree with me. Bring the machine.

(Paul): --- All right.

\centerline{}

(Kylian): --- Last time we stood here I put the needle away. I want you to have the mark before we leave. Without it, I have very little hope of getting you admitted to the Mountain. You will need it for a sealed hearing, and even then the Keepers can refuse you.

(Paul): --- The needle and stone?

(Kylian): --- Yes. The rite spends the stone. What I carry is the last that ever left the Mountain: one hand, one setting. After you there is no other. That is why a Veil's mark was holy. It cannot be multiplied like words on your paper.

(Paul): --- Then use it.

(Kylian): --- Sarah and I carry ours. There should be a third hand that belongs to neither of us. You would witness what we do, and stop either of us.

(Paul): --- Shadow.

(Kylian): --- You watched the house on a captive's word. You found the eye and did not pull the cord. I have no completed office to hand you, Paul. Only the charges, and the mark. Kneel. Hear the charges. There is no book to hold them but you.

\begin{scripture}
By the dark the first morning left unlit: \\
I call on you to guard the Mountain from the enemy within. \\
By the proud who gathered the whole into one hand, and drowned for it: \\
I call on you never to seek the whole. \\
By God, who held His breath while the first Light was struck: \\
I call on you to carry this to your grave, and give it up to no pain past it. \\
And by the Beast beneath the Mountain, keeper of the first wrath and the next: \\
for Shadow must guard also from Shadow: \\
if ever I am the hand that reaches for the whole, \\
be the hand that ends me.
\end{scripture}

(Kylian): --- And now the covenant. Answer me true. One day they will take you and open you. What will they find?

(Paul): --- \ldots{}

(Kylian): --- Stop smiling. This is no joke.

(Paul): --- Of course. Very serious indeed.

(Kylian): --- One day they will take you and open you. What will they find?

(Paul): --- My face. And nothing behind it.

(Kylian): --- Give me your free hand. Hold still. I have waited a long time for a use for this needle.

(Paul): --- You need not enjoy it quite so much.

(Kylian): --- I am trying to keep it straight.

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

(Kylian): --- Your blood on the stone. Then it is sworn.

\begin{scripture}
blood enters service; \\
the face enters the dark; \\
the name enters silence.
\end{scripture}

(Kylian): --- Rise now as Va-Paul, Veil of Shadow: a name you will hear once, keep to your last hour, and never say aloud; for in Shadow we speak neither prefix nor suffix.

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

\needspace{10\baselineskip}
(Paul): --- Is that all?

(Kylian): --- It will heal to a mark no untrained eye can read. The stone has done its work. Give me a moment to wrap your hand.

(Paul): --- I need both hands for the machine.

(Kylian): --- Then keep it. I will work round you. There. How do you feel?

(Paul): --- \ldots{} This is the moment I am meant to say a deep and meaningful thing. \ldots{} Unlit?

\end{room}
