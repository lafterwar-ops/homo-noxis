\typesetchapter{Chapter 8}{Light}

\ornament

The Hall of Ceremony filled before the First hour, and it filled past its own long memory, for a double ascension in Light was the greatest thing the Mountain would show anyone in a lifetime. The old were carried down from their beds. Children stood between their parents' knees. The lower workers came up with their hair still wet from the steam chambers, and the tiers packed shoulder to shoulder and rib to rib until the stone itself seemed to breathe. Above the whole of it the Pendulum swung, the great disk crossing on its long wire over the forbidden circle at the heart of the Hall, out and back, slow and even, closing its round in near three days as it had done through every day of every life in that room.\par

They had lit the Hall sparingly, and from angles a man in the crowd would let his eye slide past. Above the northern descent the Genesis gravure held what light it was allowed: the carved Mountain warm along its ridges, the Oasis in green shadow, Adam and Eve half-shown, the five children coming and going as bodies shifted below them. Over all of it, in the carved sky, one small hard point. The eye of Heaven.\par

\ornament

Ka-Syphiron came in carrying the last strong lamp, and the lesser flames became servants to it. He called me down out of my tier by my name. I went to him and knelt, and the floor was cold through my robe, and he laid his hands upon my head, and they were dry and heavy and sure. ``Sa-Kailan of Light,'' he said, and the name I had carried all my life came back off the walls changed. ``You have found what was hidden from you. You have opened what was closed to you. You have learned that the eye may find what the hand is forbidden to hold. Rise no longer as Candidate. Rise as Veil. Rise as Va-Kailan of Light.'' I rose, and the white cloth at my wrist was lawful now, and he turned me by the shoulder and sent me back up to my tier; and I climbed to my place among my people and stood, and the great disk swung on between us.\par

Then the two of them took their places at the Pendulum. Ka-Syphiron stood at the far reach of its swing, on the god's side, under the carved sky and the eye of Heaven, and faced the light. Va-Raedin knelt on one knee at the near reach, on the beast's side, over the dark of the forbidden circle, and faced the deep. The great disk crossed between them, out and back, and passed close by each in its turn.\par

\ornament

Then the Hall began to lose its light, and it went out of the Hall the way a tide goes out, slowly, and with an even hand. Somewhere behind the crowd the feeds of the lamps were shut, one upon another, in an order hidden behind the packed dark. A high slit above the northern arch went black. A reflector lost its line, and a whole thread of the Hall was gone. The gravure dimmed by degrees: the Mountain lost its ridges, the Oasis sank, Adam and Eve went under, and the five children held one breath longer, pale in the last slant of it, and then they too were gone. The eye of Heaven held last of all, one point above the drowned Mountain; then a slit closed somewhere far off, and it went with the rest.\par

There remained one flame. It stood in Ka-Syphiron's hand, in the throat of his lamp, small as the flame at the end of a struck taper, and around it the Hall opened out enormous and packed and black. Five thousand of us stood in that dark, shoulder to shoulder, and kept silent, and breathed as quietly as a body can breathe; and the one small fire in the old man's hand had the whole of that vastness and all those thousands to hold back, and it held them, and only just. Above it the great disk crossed on through the black, out and back, felt now in the floor and the teeth.\par

\ornament

Then the ceiling answered. A sound came down so low the bone took it before the ear, and up in the throat of the vault the great weights loosed themselves each after each and took up their command; and out of the dark above the Pendulum a dome began to come down. It came slowly, a vast cap of old black rock lowering itself over the swing and the two men and the one flame; and at the crown of it a round eye stood cut clean through the stone, and the Pendulum's wire ran up through that eye into the dark. As the dome came down along the wire, the crossing of the great disk began to shorten. Out and back, and less far; out, and less far; the disk drawing in its swing the lower the dome came, as though the stone were gathering the crossing into itself as it fell.\par

And as it fell, Ka-Syphiron spoke across the narrowing swing to the man on his knee.\par

``Va-Raedin of Light. What have you learned?''\par

And Va-Raedin, over the dark, facing the deep, answered him. ``What must remain dark.''\par

The dome came down onto the floor. The disk had drawn its crossing to a stillness as the stone met the stone, and the round eye at the crown carried the last of the flame away inside. The Hall stood pitch black. The one light was within the stone. The great disk hung still. The dark was total, and the clock of my people had stopped, and time with it.\par

Something moved inside the dome. Five thousand of us stood in the stopped dark and listened, and could tell nothing of it. A sound, once. Then a shorter sound. Then the long dark, and the disk hanging still, and the held breath of a whole people.\par

\ornament

Then the Mountain took the weight of the stone back into itself, and the dome began to rise. It climbed the wire, and as the round eye lifted clear of the disk, the great Pendulum came off its stillness and moved, very slightly, and then farther; out, and farther; the crossing lengthening the higher the stone climbed, the clock of my people taking up its count again from the place the dark had stopped it.\par

A seam of gold opened along the floor beneath the rising stone, and widened, and the one small flame came back into the world in a living hand. Ka-Raedin stood in it, on the god's side of the swing where the old man had stood, and the flame in his hand was as small as it had burned before, and around it the packed black Hall opened out as vast as before, holding as many, holding as still. He wore Ka-Syphiron's robe, the shoulders of it cut for a larger man. In his other hand he held the ceremonial knife, and along its edge, and along his knuckles, and on the stone at his feet, there was blood. Ka-Syphiron lay in the blood on the floor of the Hall, in his underrobe, his chest still, his face uncovered.\par

The dome climbed on into the vault and went home in the ceiling; and above the two men, the living and the dead, the great disk swung out once more to its full reach and found again the slow round it had kept since before any of us drew breath.\par

Ka-Raedin lifted the small flame over the body.\par

``Light is reborn,'' he said. ``The chain holds.''\par

And the Hall gave it back to him, five thousand voices out of the black: ``Light is reborn. The chain holds.''\par

\ornament

And upon the word, every lamp in the Hall took fire at once.\par

Every plate and every reflector and every hidden throat of light woke in the one instant, and every aperture of the gravure fell open together, and the light came down and across and up out of every wall and every angle of the stone at the same time, and went into five thousand pairs of eyes that had stood in the black so long that it struck them like a blow. The Hall blazed. Mirror threw to mirror and threw again, gold upon gold; the carved Mountain and the Oasis and the eye of Heaven stood up all at once out of the dark and burned; and the new Keeper of Light stood in the centre of the glory in the gold robe, the flame in one hand and the blood on the other, the great disk crossing bright above his head. The people flinched from the size of the light, and wept, and put their hands across their faces, and looked again through their fingers, and were blinded, and were glad.\par

\ornament

Then the Keepers of the Mountain gathered to the new Keeper of Light, and went out of the Hall together by a way cut in the wall, for the thing that follows an ascension; and a while after, they returned, and the rite was accounted whole. I stood in my place among my people with the light pouring over us, a Veil of Light one hour old, the white cloth lawful at my wrist, and the great disk swinging its slow round above us all.\par
