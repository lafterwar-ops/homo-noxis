\textit{He cut the chain cleanly because he loved Light more than he loved being remembered gently.}\par
\textit{--- Ka-Raedin, of Ka-Syphiron}\par

% 1-The Hall becomes Light's body

For four days I have circled this morning, the one thing I came least able to say. So let me say it plainly. In a Hall heated on purpose, I watched a man arrange his own death so that a title could reach my wrist; and I walked out with a title on my wrist and no gladness in me at all. Here is how it happened.\par

The Hall of Ceremony had been warmed again.\par
For Faith's Ascension the warmth had been mercy: heat stored for old bones, children, long standing.
This warmth had no mercy in it.
It gathered low beneath the eastern side and withdrew by degree from the other alcoves, so that every step toward Light changed the skin.
The western stone held Balance's damp coolness.
The central circle breathed with Faith's tempered air.
The north remained stern and unwelcoming under Shield.
Justice permitted no excess warmth at all.\par
The lamps had been arranged differently too.
There were fewer flames than before, each one set exactly where it was told.
Narrow shafts struck plates of polished stone set high beneath the eastern vault, then broke sideways into threads, each thread placed where no ordinary worshipper would think to look until it touched a face, a robe edge, a bowl, a blade, a white knot of cloth.
The central gravure remained visible, unevenly.
The Mountain in the carving held a low bronze warmth.
The Oasis rested in greenish shadow.
Adam and Eve were half-lit.
The five children appeared and disappeared whenever a body shifted below them.
Above all of it, in the carved sky, a small bright point remained steady.\par
The eye of Heaven.\par
Children knew that little point before they knew the theology.
It was a small light, constant, a hard speck above the Mountain.\par
Today it watched.\par
I noticed it and regretted it.\par
Word had travelled faster than water.
A double succession in Light.
Keeper and Veil.
Ordinary memory held nothing like it.
Old men had been carried from sleep.
Children stood between parents' knees.
Workers came with damp hair from the lower steam chambers.
Balance clerks sat with tablets already open.
Faith attendants lined the central circle in white, holding their song.
Justice had placed witnesses at three levels of the Hall, as if the event might try to escape by height.
Shield held every arch, visible and invisible.\par
I stood beneath the eastern descent and fought the memory of laughter.\par
% 2-The private room returns
The room behind the old gallery survived the morning.
It returned in fragments that made the Hall less bearable.\par
The hidden bottles.
Honey on my tongue.
Ka-Syphiron's hand moving with absurd solemnity over contraband cups.
Va-Raedin receiving praise and saying yes because the old Keeper had trapped him in affection before witnesses too few to become record.
The woman appearing at the bottom of the cup when liquid corrected the curve.
The paper figure undressing and dressing again because interruption had become motion.\par
Now the same man who had arranged it stood elsewhere, still hidden, and the Hall had been prepared for his ending.\par
I kept my hands still.
The white cloth at my wrist had been changed before dawn.\par
A provisional knot, not yet made lawful.
It sat too close to the pulse.
Every beat seemed to strike it from within.\par

At first, nothing happened.
Then the ceiling answered.
A sound moved above us, so deep it seemed to reach the bone before it reached the ear.
Dust fell from a seam I had never noticed.
The last lamp shook in Syphiron's hand, but he held it high.
Somewhere inside the Mountain, weights released themselves one after another, accepting command.\par
The Thread was gone.\par
The Pendulum hangs from the throat at the top of the vault, on its long wire, over a drop no honest eye can measure; it swings its slow crossing and closes one full round nearly three days after it opens it. In all my life I had never seen it stilled. Its turning is a count, set beside the water-count and the star-count as one of the three clocks the Mountain trusts; halt it and the count breaks, to start again only from the beginning of the long round.\par
Yet it had been stilled. The great disk had been drawn to the western wall and lashed there, God's face to the stone, and the Thread carried aside, so that the space above the forbidden circle stood open and waiting. I had walked in that morning beneath that emptiness, and the wrongness of it I had put down to grief.\par
A square darkness opened above Syphiron and Ka-Raedin.\par

% 20-Va-Kailan leaves as Veil
The Hall was no longer dark.\par
The eye of Heaven watched again.\par
Ka-Raedin turned away.\par
``Before the First hour,'' he said.\par
``Yes, Keeper.''\par
I remained after I should have moved.
Long enough to know that staying longer would be clinging.
Long enough to feel the old hunger for hidden things searching for somewhere to go and finding no clean path.\par
I left through the eastern passage alone.
No one stopped me.
No attendant corrected my pace.
No older Veil placed a hand at my shoulder.
The route opened because my title now carried permissions. Once I had imagined those permissions as freedom.\par
In a long gallery the looms stood side by side, and the weavers kept their talk up over the clack of them.\par
At the first turn, I looked back.
The Hall of Ceremony lay behind me in ordered light.
The eastern alcove held one strong thread, no wider than a blade.
It touched the place where Syphiron had stood and went no farther.\par
I thought of all the night before had shown me through play: Faith's reversed echo, Balance's power drawn from heat, Justice reading the future from the exposed past, Shield in every implied sealed door, the Beast held among the five so that no single order held it.
I had left understanding perhaps a quarter of it, and I had thought a quarter a great deal.\par
I walked on.
The Mountain had changed around the fact.
My name had entered record.
My wrist carried the white cloth.
The people had struck stone.
A Keeper had died.\par
The rite had given me power. In the same hour it had killed the man in front of me to do it, and it had made me stand there and watch.\par
