\typesetchapter{Chapter 15}{Knocks}

\begin{room}

--- She has stopped my account again --- twice now in a single telling. A man sets down his whole life a morning at a time, and cannot reach the end of it for the hand she keeps laying flat across the page.

--- I stopped it for cause. Sit, Mr Morgan; you have been standing with the machine under your arm since you came in, which is how a man stands when he means to reach for a thing he has not been given leave to hold.

--- Let the reaching be mine, then, and not the house's and not hers. She gave me back the word \emph{knock} across this table, and I have not been able to lie down under it since. So it will be had out before I give the rest of the road. I have carried it up here in the dark to be rid of it. It is mine.

--- Then it is the thing I would have asked you myself. An hour is not gone since the drums in your next room drew a shape, and before anyone in this house had named it, you named it whole --- a Keeper rising. You did not reason your way to it. It was already in you. How.

--- Because I have felt it come up through the floor with my own two feet. Twice. The Faith woman, when I was a boy of fifteen; my teacher's Keeper, four years on, the old man in the lowered box. Both times the five went out of the Hall by a door I was never shown --- a door on no route a Veil is given, no candidate to follow them through it, no witness, no record I ever saw. They were gone behind it a short while --- a silence, two, long enough to feel the wrongness of the waiting and no longer. Keeper work. A rite of readiness. That is all a Veil is told, and a Veil who asks for more is told he will learn it the morning he holds the office, or die without it.

--- And no Veil in eleven thousand years ever went looking for the door.

--- It is not a door you break into on a dare. It is simply left off the map. A Veil is given the whole Mountain, pressed into him over years until he can walk the dark and never touch a wall --- and this one door is cut out of that map so cleanly that a Veil does not even feel the shape of its absence. I felt it. It is my flaw and always was. I stood in a passage where the map promised only rock and knew there was a room behind it, the way you know a word has been left out of a sentence spoken in the dark. And I said nothing, because a Veil who tells the elders he can feel a room that is not on the map is a Veil the elders begin to watch.

--- And after the door shut on the five --- what came up through the floor.

--- The Mountain answered them. Late, after the Hall had half emptied and no one minded the quiet. Under the deepest floor. A knock, and then a shape to it --- small parts, one after another, evenly set, and then done. The second time, dust came down off a seam in the pillar above me, and I looked for a servant to blame and found none. I was young enough still to be lost among the elders, and I felt it come up through the soles of me before I heard it in the air. Five times the stone pressed up against my feet, evenly, without hurry, and then let go; and an old woman beside me said the blessing for the settling of the Mountain and laid her palm flat on the pillar. I knew it for no settling. I had counted it. A settling does not count. But I took her blessing for my own, and let the made thing be a holy thing.

--- Five strokes, evenly set, and a slower thing waking under them. Both times.

--- \ldots{} Why does a man paid to weigh whether I am real care what the ground does under a rite no Veil is ever shown?

--- Because the ground kept a copy.

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

--- I was given a folder when I came to this house. A thin thing, at my level --- a few traces off a drum, a few dates typed down the margin. I took it for a test of you and set it aside four days, because you never once gave me a lie to catch. Now look. This is the morning the Faith woman rose, reckoned off your fifteenth year, give or take a season. This is the morning your teacher's Keeper rose, four years on. And these two are the lines the house holds nearest to those mornings.

--- \ldots{}

--- Understand what the paper can honestly say, and no more. Your two mornings stood four years apart, and each shaking reached whatever instrument was listening in its own year within seconds or minutes of being made --- not days. Different ears, in different places, through their own hiss; so the two lines do not sit one upon the other, edge for edge, and I would not trust the man who told you they did. But once you have allowed for all of that, the same figure stands up out of both: five strokes at the same deliberate spacing, the same slower return beneath them, out of the same quarter of desert, and each falling on the very morning your people changed a Keeper. The shape, the source, and your two dates, agreeing without ever knowing of one another. Any one of those, chance might hand you. Not all of them at once.

--- \ldots{} The Bowl of the Last Prayer. You give its Ear the one true note, and it hands that note straight back off its Mouth, whole; give it a false one and it gives you silence. It cares nothing for how loud you are, only whether the note is the note. \ldots{} You are telling me the ground is a Bowl. That it gives the same shape back to the same blow, and that a patient ear, set far enough off, could learn one blow from another.

--- That is the whole of it, and said better than I said it. And there is the reason no one else ever caught it: your people fire the thing into a hole, not against the rock --- into a void cut for the purpose, so most of what it makes goes to shoving air and warming a wall, and only a thin part is coupled into the ground. It muffles the blow. It does not swallow it. What comes up out of that desert arrives as a whisper, easy for any array to discard unless a reader already knows where and when to listen, and refuses to put the question down.

--- \ldots{} They are the same.

--- They are close. I am not the one who can tell you how close. But even I can see a matched pair when it is laid before me.

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

--- These two are mine. Do you understand what you are holding. These are my mornings. This is the woman I watched wounded at the throat when I was a boy. This is the old man I watched climb into a stone box and die on purpose while I stood under the pillar of Light and wept. And you have them here, on a strip of paper, in a case, in a house in the cold north.

--- I have every one my monitoring ever gave me, from the morning I learned where to listen --- not one, every one. Your Keepers do not die often, and that is the mercy that made the thing findable in the end: something very small, returning in the same ordered figure, at long and patient intervals, out of a place the maps all swear holds nothing but distance.

--- \ldots{} Then a man with enough of these does not merely know the Mountain is there. He counts us. Every knock is a Keeper dead and a Keeper risen, and enough of them, across enough years, give him the calendar of our dying --- how often the chain takes a Keeper under, the length of a life in the office, the rhythm of the whole. He would know when the Mountain changes hands, and never once have looked on a face.

--- The drums gave me the dates. Horizon gave me the names.

--- And you would know the one hour we are weakest. When it changes hands. The morning the five are apart behind their door and the new grip has not yet closed on the office. You have kept the calendar of that morning, in the dark, in a case, longer than I have drawn breath.

--- I have.

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

--- Then hear what your calendar has just done. The drum in your next room drew the knock --- in the same ordered figure, the shape you hold on that paper: the same five, the same spacing, the same slow answer under them. A succession. And no Keeper was near the chain. I said it when it drew, and I say it colder now: not one of the five was old, or ailing, or owed to the chain. There was no death waiting. There was no morning due.

--- One knock without a death is already one too many.

--- It drew twice. Inside the single hour. \ldots{} A rite of readiness is worked once, by the five together, the morning a hand changes. It cannot be spent twice in an hour, any more than a man is born twice in a night. Two knocks are not two Keepers risen. Someone has taken the thing my people guard under their deepest floor and struck it, or drawn its shape, or made the Mountain say its one word --- apart from any lawful succession. Whether the hand knew what the outside would hear, I cannot say.

--- Who.

--- That I cannot give you from this room. Whether it was worked inside our own walls or answered from somewhere out here in your country; whether by a hand of the five, or a hand that has only learned to counterfeit them --- I have no way, sitting here, to say. \ldots{} I knew from Reggane that the wall was gone. What I had not understood until this morning was what had crossed it: not merely proof that the Mountain existed, but the calendar of its deaths. And the answer to who is striking my Mountain is not in this house. It is out on the road they put me on the night they stopped my heart in the record and sent me up through the dark.

--- Then take it up there.

--- \ldots{} I will tell it from the well they walked me out through in the dark, the night they took my name off me.

\end{room}
