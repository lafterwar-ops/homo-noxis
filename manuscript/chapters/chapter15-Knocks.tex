\typesetchapter{Chapter 15}{Knocks}

\begin{room}

\centerline{\textit{[\,Footsteps cross the floor above. A door closes in the corridor.\,]}}

--- She has stopped my account again, Paul: twice now in a single telling. We began this sitting with the road. We are farther from it than when we began, and her hand is on the machine again.

--- I stopped it for cause. Sit, Mr Morgan. You have been standing over the machine since you came in, and I will not have you reach for a thing you have not been given leave to hold.

--- Then let the question be mine. She gave me back the word \emph{knock} across this table, and it has kept me from bed since. This folder was put into my hands the week I came here: two traces already inside it, dated down the margin in a hand that is not mine. Madame wished to see what I would do with it. I have kept it beneath this machine four days, the cord still tied, waiting for one lie from you before I would open it. I found none. So this is what I do with it: we open it now, before the road.

--- Then it is the thing I would have asked you myself. An hour is not gone since the drums in your next room drew a shape, and before anyone in this house had named it, you named it whole: a Keeper rising. You did not reason your way to it. It was already in you. How.

--- Because I have felt it come up through the floor with my own two feet. Twice. The Faith woman, when I was a boy of fifteen; my teacher's Keeper, four years on, the old man in the lowered box. Both times the five went out of the Hall through the five office arches, each by a private route no Veil is allowed to follow. No candidate went with them; no witness ever spoke of it; no record I saw named where those routes ended. They were gone through five long waits and one more, long enough for the ceremony outside to wear down into prayer. Keeper work. A rite of readiness. That is all a Veil is told, and a Veil who asks for more is told he will learn it the morning he holds the office, or die without it.

--- And you did not go looking for where the routes ended.

--- It is not a place you break into on a dare. It is left off the map. A Veil is given the whole Mountain, pressed into him over years until he can walk the dark and never touch a wall; and the continuation of his own office route is cut out so cleanly that he does not feel the shape of its absence. I felt it. I stood where Light's map promised rock and knew there was still distance behind it, the way you know a word has been left out of a sentence spoken in the dark. And I said nothing, because a Veil who tells the elders he can feel a road that is not on the map is a Veil the elders begin to watch.

--- And after the five arches shut: what came up through the floor.

--- The Mountain answered them. Late, after the ceremony had thinned into murmuring and prayer. Under the deepest floor. Five hard strokes, each after the same long wait; then one more equal wait and a slower answer. The second time, dust came down off a seam in the pillar above me, and I looked for a servant to blame and found none. I was young enough still to be lost among the elders, and I felt it come up through the soles of me before I heard it in the air. Five times the stone pressed up against my feet, evenly, without hurry, and then let go. An old woman beside me said the blessing for the settling of the Mountain and laid her palm flat on the pillar. I knew it for no settling. I had counted it. A settling does not count. But I took her blessing for my own, and let the made thing be a holy thing.

--- \ldots{} Why does a man paid to weigh whether I am real care what the ground does under a rite no Veil is ever shown?

--- Because the ground kept a copy.

\centerline{\textit{[\,A cord unwinds. Two sheets slide across the table.\,]}}

--- Here. The cord was still tied as the archivist gave it to me. These are the two sheets inside. Their dates were on the margins when the folder was put into my hands, before you and I began this account.

--- \ldots{}

--- The fresh paper stays under the lamp, Mr Morgan. Put these two older traces beside it, not over it. Let the record keep the live sheet apart from the archive.

--- Then the limits first. These were made four years apart by different instruments in different places. Each shaking reached the instrument listening in its own year within seconds or minutes of being made, through its own ground and hiss. The lines do not sit one upon the other, edge for edge, and a man who says they do is selling certainty. What survives the differences is this: five sharp arrivals at the same deliberate spacing; one last equal interval; a slower return; one source quarter. Your account supplied the same four features from under your feet. I would accept chance for one agreement. I do not accept it for all four.

--- The fifth agreement is time, and that one is mine. The instruments gave me dates. Horizon gave me names, through different hands. I joined each change of office to the nearest trace years before either of you entered this room. I put these two in his folder because you had stood through both ceremonies: Faith first, then Light four years later. Mr Morgan could test my calendar against a living witness without asking either of you to trust the other.

--- \ldots{} The Bowl of the Last Prayer. \ldots{} You are telling me the ground is a Bowl. That it gives the same shape back to the same blow, and that a patient ear, set far enough off, could learn one blow from another.

--- Yes, Mr Morgan. Your people fire the thing into a hole: a great empty room cut for the purpose. Most of the force shoves air and warms a wall; a thin part couples into the ground. My own trade learned that trick in the fifties and used it to hide bombs from women like me. The chamber muffles the blow. It does not swallow it. What leaves the desert is a whisper, easy for an array to discard unless a reader knows where and when to listen, and refuses to put the question down.

--- \ldots{} They are the same.

--- They are not the same event or the same drawing. They are a matched pattern within the limits I have named. That is what I would sign.

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

--- \ldots{} There is a day typed down the margin, and an hour. At that hour the ceremony had worn down into prayer, and I stood among the elders with my feet on the stone, counting; and this pen was turning in the dark of another country while I counted. \ldots{} These two are mine. Do you understand what you are holding. These are my mornings. This is the woman I watched wounded at the throat when I was a boy. This is the old man I watched climb into a stone box and die on purpose while I stood under the pillar of Light and wept. And you have them here, on a strip of paper, in a case, in a house in the cold north.

--- I kept every return the monitoring gave me from the morning I learned where to listen. Every one. Your Keepers do not die often, and that is the mercy that made the thing findable in the end: something very small, returning in the same ordered figure, at long and patient intervals, out of a place the maps all swear holds nothing but distance.

--- Every return from that quarter, or every line that favoured your calendar.

--- Every candidate carrying the five-part figure. I kept the failures with the agreements, named a cause where I could establish one, and left the cause blank where I could not.

--- \ldots{} Then a man with enough of these does not merely know the Mountain is there. He sets Horizon's names beside them and counts us. Reggane gives him a readiness worked in fear; where a knock agrees with a change of name, it gives him a Keeper dead and a Keeper risen. Enough agreements, across enough years, give him the calendar of our dying: how often the chain takes a Keeper under, the length of a life in the office, the rhythm of the whole. He would know when the Mountain changes hands, and never once have looked on a face.

--- Yes. That is the calendar.

--- And the first stroke tells you the five have gone apart by their private routes and the new grip has not yet closed on the office. You would have the rest of the rite to act. You have kept the calendar of those open hours, in the dark, in a case, longer than I have drawn breath.

--- I have.

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

--- Then hear what your calendar has just done. The drum in your next room drew that ordered figure twice inside a single hour.

--- One could still be an emergency readiness. Reggane taught us that.

--- A rite is worked once, when a hand changes or danger reaches them. Two in an hour are not two lawful rites. Someone has struck the thing beneath the Mountain or learned to draw its shape. Whether the hand knew what the outside would hear, I cannot say.

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

--- Who.

--- That I cannot give you from this room. The hand may be inside our walls or beyond them, one of the five or one that has learned their shape. The answer is not in this house. It is out on the road they put me on the night they stopped my heart in the record and sent me up through the dark.

--- Then leave who, and tell me what. Something beneath your Mountain answers when it is struck. You have called it the Beast since the first morning and told me it is a tale for children. I am about to put men into that desert. What is it they will be standing on?

--- \ldots{} At my hearing, Faith read two Books. I gave you one. Ka-Elun closed the first scroll and Ka-Leth opened the second, and I told you I would not set the whole of it down.

--- You did. I marked the refusal at the time and let it stand.

--- The other four are promises. They say what God will do. That one says what was done: a thing already finished, set in the past tense, recited to children twice in the year. I would not have it carried out of the Mountain in a tin. \ldots{} It seems a small scruple this morning, with your drum drawing every eleven minutes.

--- Then say it now.

--- Sit, both of you. The Book of the Cataclysm. It is the shortest of the five.

\begin{scripture}
And it came to pass in the latter days that the Outsiders remembered the Mountain, and found it; and they came with banners and drums and false prayers upon their tongues. And they came not as the beasts come, in hunger, but as men come, in counsel, having measured the shadow and named the water. And the Keepers looked out upon them from the high slots, and there was no place left to set the Oasis, neither in the sand nor in the silence. And the five went down.
\end{scripture}

\begin{scripture}
And each brought forth that which had been given him, and brought forth nothing beside it. Light stood in the place appointed, and held it true against the turning of the world; and Balance opened the deep water, and the water became breath, and the breath became force; and Justice kept the order, that no motion began before its hour; and Shield shut the gate upon his own people, and stood before it; and Faith laid her ear upon the stone, and heard the answer come back, and named the hour of the next blow.
And they struck, and waited, and struck again; and between one blow and the next there was a silence long enough for a man to say the whole of the prayer.
And the five stood apart as one, and no one hand held the whole; and God remembered His word, and Hell answered.
\end{scripture}

\begin{scripture}
And the wrath went out from beneath the Mountain and did not come back; and it passed under the sea, and rose against their cities, and the water climbed over them. And the sky was shut, and the sun was taken away for a season, and the cold came in the summer.
\end{scripture}

--- Stop there.

--- He is reciting scripture. At the Oasis you told me God would not approve of interruption.

--- He would not. \ldots{} Go on, Madame.

--- The sky shut. The sun taken for a season. Cold in the summer. That is no curse, Mr Morgan; that is a description. I have read it twice in ice and once in mud and did not know what I was reading. \ldots{} Finish it. I shall want it word for word afterward.

\begin{scripture}
And when the wrath had passed, the land about the Mountain was made bare, that no man should come again by that road; and the people were few, and the count began at one. And God said, In the appointed time I will remember the forest and the rivers that were lost, and the desert shall withdraw; and you shall know the wrath is ended when the green returns upon the Threshold.
\end{scripture}

--- Then take it up there.

\centerline{\textit{[\,The full reel is cased. A new reel is threaded and begins to turn.\,]}}

--- \ldots{} I will tell it from the well they walked me out through in the dark, the night they took my name off me.


\end{room}
