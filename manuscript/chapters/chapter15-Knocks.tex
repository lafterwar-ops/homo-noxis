\typesetchapter{Chapter 15}{Knocks}

\begin{room}

(Urs): --- Well, Herr Nemo? Which caviar is best?

(Kylian): --- None of them. May I have some more cream?

(Urs): --- You have tried all five?

(Kylian): --- Yes. The cream is wonderful. I want more on this potato. And that butter, please.

(Paul): --- Here. You have hardly touched the salmon.

(Kylian): --- I was saving it. We smoke fish at home, but ours takes some chewing. This comes apart on my tongue.

(Urs): --- There is another whole plate of it. Elise, would you pass the blinis?

(Elise): --- Take them. I need that space.

(Sarah): --- Paul, give her some room. And pour me a little of that before Urs puts the bottle out of reach.

(Urs): --- A little for everyone?

(Sarah): --- One glass each. We still have work to do.

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

(Elise): --- Sarah, I need the two ceremony sheets.

(Sarah): --- Paul has them. The folded pair, Paul, at the back.

(Paul): --- Here. Mind the edge; it has split.

(Kylian): --- May I see?

(Sarah): --- The earlier one would be Faith. Four years later, Light. I found them after our first interview, when I had your years to work from.

(Kylian): --- These were here all that time?

(Sarah): --- In the archive. I knew where the signal came from. I could not put a ceremony to either date.

(Kylian): --- And that is the hour?

(Sarah): --- The instrument's time. Urs will check it against the others.

(Kylian): --- Where does he die?

(Paul): --- Who?

(Kylian): --- Ka-Syphiron. On this.

(Sarah): --- Earlier. These would be the tests afterward.

(Kylian): --- Then that is Ka-Raedin down there.

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

(Kylian): --- I was still in the Hall. Everybody was waiting for them to come back. You could have been watching this while we waited.

(Sarah): --- Somebody was. I would have read it later.

(Kylian): --- What did you write beside it?

(Sarah): --- Cause unknown. Paul, let him keep it a moment.

(Paul): --- Elise needs the scale.

(Elise): --- I have it. He can keep the paper.

(Kylian): --- And now you can put his name there.

(Sarah): --- If you want me to.

(Kylian): --- You do not need my permission. You have the account.

(Paul): --- Kylian, would you rather we put those away?

(Kylian): --- No. I want to know what else they tell you. When the first stroke comes, could somebody outside know a Keeper has just taken the chain?

(Sarah): --- With what you have told us, yes. Until then it could also be an emergency test, like the one after Reggane.

(Kylian): --- By the first stroke all five are below. The new Keeper has only just begun the work. Whoever was watching would have the rest of the rite.

(Sarah): --- Yes.

(Kylian): --- Who gets these readings?

(Sarah): --- Each station keeps its own. Urs and Elise bring them together for me. Your account stays here.

(Kylian): --- I have given you a good deal more than dates.

(Sarah): --- You have. Urs, the field gets unannotated instrument records. The names and the ceremony sequence stay in this room.

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

(Urs): --- Elise, send me Geb again. The whole file, please.

(Elise): --- It is already on your laptop. Open the second one.

(Urs): --- Sarah, come round this side. I have put them on the same scale. The live one is the lower line.

(Sarah): --- Enlarge the tail. Keep the scale beside it.

(Elise): --- There is very little below it. Most of the energy is at higher frequencies. I have checked against the old records at three stations; the new peaks are a tenth of the old ones at one, a hundredth at another. The instruments are calibrated. The broad, slow return is missing from all three.

(Kylian): --- So nobody is being raised?

(Sarah): --- It would be a very different test. Let us get the other stations before we say that.

(Paul): --- How much energy would make a mark that small?

(Urs): --- I still have to sum the waveforms. On these first readings, perhaps a hundred to ten thousand times less than the old events. Less again would reach anything deep. I would not put a number on that yet.

(Kylian): --- I would like to know whether they can feel it.

(Urs): --- Inside? I cannot tell you from these stations.

(Sarah): --- Where are you putting the source?

(Urs): --- Up here, almost level with the ground. Within the error, it could be right on the surface. But look at the second stroke. I will put the first back so you can see both.

(Elise): --- I get the same movement using Nut.

(Sarah): --- How far can you move those clocks before the points meet?

(Urs): --- Farther than the corrections in their logs. I will check the remaining six, but I used to pay crews to do this: make a noise, move, make another, read the echoes. It looks like seismic sounding. Water, rock worth drilling --- I cannot tell you what they want from a trace.

(Kylian): --- They are looking inside. Like the Bowl. Sound goes in through the Mouth and something comes back through the Ear.

(Urs): --- Except you can carry the thing making the sound round to another place and ask again.

(Sarah): --- Urs, finish the checks. Elise, stay on the live feeds.

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

(Paul): --- Kylian, your plate is under that sheet.

(Kylian): --- I have finished.

(Urs): --- With the caviar too?

(Kylian): --- Yes. You can have it.

(Urs): --- Thank you.

(Paul): --- We can go back to the road while they work.

(Kylian): --- I skipped the fourth book to save time. What happens when the Outsiders find us. I suppose we have time for it now.

(Paul): --- Yes. The machine is running.

(Kylian): --- We recite it twice a year. The children learn it.

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

(Kylian): --- The Book of the Cataclysm.

\begin{scripture}
And the Outsiders multiplied in the open land, and their kings became many, and their cruelty became without number; and they remembered the Mountain in dreams, and hated the shade they could not find. And they gathered armies, and forged blades, and raised engines of war, and came with banners and drums and false prayers upon their tongues. And they said, Let us find the Mountain, and take the Oasis, and drink the waters thereof, and break the Temple, and make ourselves gods upon the Earth.
\end{scripture}

\begin{scripture}
And the Outsiders came unto the desert, and the desert rose against them with thirst and wandering; yet they turned not back. And they drove the weak before them, and cast down the weary, and slew the beasts that bore them, and drank what was forbidden, and ate what was unclean; and the sun burned them by day, and the cold struck them by night, and still they came. And the desert saw their hearts, and the Mountain heard their footsteps, and the Oasis trembled in the shadow.
\end{scripture}

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

(Elise): --- Urs. Another one.

(Urs): --- I have it.

(Elise): --- Geb, Nut, then Atum.

(Urs): --- Further towards Atum. Still at the surface.

(Sarah): --- Send me that when you have checked it.

(Paul): --- Sorry, Kylian. After the Oasis trembled.

(Kylian): --- The Keepers.

\begin{scripture}
And the Keepers stood before the Temple, and each brought forth the secret that had been given unto him. And the Keeper of Shield sealed the threshold, and closed the Mountain against the wrath to come. And the Keeper of Justice set the Key, and counted the Number, and took witness of the five, that no hand should move before the others. And the Keeper of Balance moved the deep waters beneath the Earth, and loosed them by measure into the chambers below. And the Keeper of Faith spoke the Prayer in its appointed rhythm, and listened for the answer of stone. And the Keeper of Light danced the Dance of Light, and the hidden signs were revealed, and the way was made visible. And the five stood apart as one, and no one hand held the whole.
\end{scripture}

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

(Elise): --- Have you lost Atum too?

(Urs): --- Mine has stopped.

(Elise): --- Geb and Nut are still coming through. Atum has sent nothing since that last packet.

(Sarah): --- Leave it. Do not send anything back yet.

(Kylian): --- Are there people there?

(Elise): --- Three.

(Sarah): --- Ask the field office when they last spoke to them. Use the separate line, and have them hold any visit or delivery to Atum. I want to know whether the crew has been heard from somewhere else. Any call to the station comes through me first.

(Kylian): --- They might need help.

(Sarah): --- They might. We would be sending somebody towards whatever made them stop transmitting. I want to know who is near enough to help and which road they would have to take.

(Elise): --- I will get the duty list as well.

(Sarah): --- Ask for Amastan's line too. Urs, see what the receiving end kept. Whatever is still in Atum can wait.

(Urs): --- There should be a buffer here. I will look.

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

(Kylian): --- Is Elise coming back?

(Sarah): --- As soon as she has an answer.

(Kylian): --- I can wait.

(Sarah): --- Give us the rest. She can hear the recording.

(Kylian): --- You are still looking at the screen.

(Sarah): --- I am listening.

\begin{scripture}
And God remembered His word, and Hell answered. And the deep waters fell into the fire beneath, and the breath of Hell rose in the hidden chambers, and the works of the Mountain began to move. And the Beast beneath the Mountain struck the roots of the Earth with his fists, and the ground broke beneath the feet of the Outsiders. And the prayer travelled through stone, and the weak places of the Earth answered; and the poisoned waters rose, and the volcanoes erupted, and fire came upon the left hand of the army, and the sea rose upon the right hand thereof; and the mountains shook, and the sky was made black with ash, and the Outsiders were burned, and drowned, and crushed, and scattered; and they tasted the cruelty which they had given unto the world.
\end{scripture}

(Sarah): --- The ash. Say that part again.

(Kylian): --- And the sky was made black with ash.

(Sarah): --- Does it say how long the sky stayed black, or how far the ash went?

(Kylian): --- Those are all the words.

(Paul): --- Let him finish, Sarah. I can play it again.

(Sarah): --- All right. Forgive me.

\begin{scripture}
And when the wrath had passed, the armies of the Outsiders were no more; but the forest was wounded, and the forest was broken, and the open waters were spoiled, and the land around the Mountain became desert. And the people wept for the world that had been saved and lost in the same hour. And God said unto them, Dwell no more in the open, but enter the Mountain, and keep the Temple, and remember the price of being seen. And the Temple was sealed, and the people went beneath the stone.
\end{scripture}

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

(Sarah): --- We should look for that ash in the core surveys. Ice, lake beds. Sulphur carried high enough by an eruption can cool the climate.

(Kylian): --- About eleven and a half thousand years since the count began.

(Sarah): --- Urs, bring up the Greenland temperature plots in the survey catalogue.

(Urs): --- Here. The end of the Younger Dryas. The warming is close to his date. A long cold spell before it. If I widen this --- there is another cold interval further back. The recovery is a different shape.

(Paul): --- Much of the Sahara used to be green. Lakes, grassland, trees along the water. We should look at the pollen records nearest the Mountain.

(Sarah): --- Send me the reports for both cold intervals, and the Saharan lake cores. Any dated ash layers too. When you have finished with Atum. We need dates for the ground around the Mountain.

(Urs): --- Sarah, I have the end of Atum's transmission. The source was coming towards its sector when it stopped. I need the other clocks and the terrain model before I can put it any closer. Give me an hour. If a feed is incomplete it will take longer.

(Sarah): --- Work with Elise beside the drum, and keep it running separately. Those three come first. Bring me anything that changes; the finished report can follow.

(Urs): --- I will leave the chocolate. You have barely touched it.

(Sarah): --- Take some for her.

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

(Kylian): --- Any word from Amastan?

(Sarah): --- I am still waiting.

(Kylian): --- I should go back.

(Sarah): --- We may all have to. First I need what happened after they sent you out. Every person you met along that road.

(Paul): --- We stopped with Va-Sheva.

(Kylian): --- I remember where we stopped.

(Paul): --- I have to change the reel.

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

(Paul): --- Whenever you are ready.

(Kylian): --- They took me up to a well. Before that, there was something Justice had to do.

\end{room}
