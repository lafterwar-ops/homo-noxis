## Water, work and firing

- Life Reserve and war-reserve do not share a pipe. Destroying their common quarter can endanger both; that is not hydraulic identity.
- A full load chamber is an eighty-metre cube: 512,000 cubic metres of water, represented as 512,000 tonnes. Five risers/pressure wells fill it. Do not silently revive a million-tonne cube or a five-load total firing.
- Counterweights accumulate the work for the first full lift over years. A governed share of the later deep steam/pressure raises the following load. The heat reservoir supplies work; this is not perpetual motion.
- The initial five calibration drops are smaller and strike a side cavity without opening the deep conversion place. They provide the test on which Justice checks the ruling's conditions.
- Only after calibration, the legality check, the four other assents, Kailan's final Faith assent and the operators' releases does the first full load fall.
- Operators retain individual refusal points. Passage through the Lower Earth Gate removes the clean abort; Shield subsequently contains the pressure. Justice's last physical release does not displace Kailan's final spoken assent.
- Full loads continue for five days. Faith hears the interval afresh, a little beyond twenty minutes; the closing narrative gives the rounded twenty-minute duration. Two parts in five of the war-reserve are consumed and three remain.
- The conversion place is miles beneath the Hall. Water/heat interaction fractures the deep source. The attempted catastrophe draws on strain already stored in the far fault system. The low whole-Earth oscillation is a diagnostic timing component, not itself the energy that breaks the planet.
- Light's target is fixed by the Mountain's site and the Earth opposite it. Survey drift matters. It is not a freely aimable weapon.
- The local diagnostic return and evidence of a newly rupturing far source are distinct observations. Sarah explicitly separates measurement from her subsequent catastrophic forecast.
- At the end, the conversion place is flooded, broken and cold. Remaining water does not imply an immediate second firing. Do not certify a reheat date or a decommissioning solution not demonstrated in the text.
- Whole's eleven-day examination reports what the offices tested. It does not scientifically prove every institutional prediction they make about the distant future.
