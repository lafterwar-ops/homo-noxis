## Offices, seals and judgment

Sa- marks a Candidate, Va- a Veil, Ka- a Keeper. Use the rank and name belonging to the narrated moment; Va-Maera becomes Ka-Maera only after her later rising. Five offices are Light, Faith, Balance, Shield and Justice. Their knowledge is divided; cross-office knowledge requires the actual standing shown in the scene.

The Lesser Seal is a containment command, not a judicial application invented by Kailan. Va-Sheva issues it after receiving the evidence. The soundless chamber bears the joined marks of Faith and Shield. Its existence does not establish who was interrogator, subject or trainee there.

Kailan's Trial move is his own procedural construction. Do not restore a previously announced condemned person's old right, a guaranteed appeal, or coaching that predicts the whole move. He obtains the offices' hypothetical answers, offers proof under seal, and petitions to have the charge heard before execution. None of the five challenges that petition; Justice suspends the hearing for that accusation and restores limited standing. Their acceptance creates the precedent. Silence here is failure to contest jurisdiction; it is not a general affirmative vote.

The Threshold Exception still requires the five explicit assents and its warnings. The Cataclysm hearing still admits the surviving one-in-five alternative. Legal authorization, a person's assent, a physical interlock and irreversible release are separate acts.

At firing, Sarah reports the measured return; Paul supplies Faith's recovered instructions; Kailan brings them into agreement. They reconstruct Faith together. Sarah and Paul are heard but have no vote. Kailan gives Faith's fifth and final assent. Sa-Elian attends the station and shares its physical interlock with Sarah; he adds no vote. Justice retains the last mechanical refusal after Kailan's word. Paul's lack of a vote or lever does not erase the practical work he contributes.

## Population and lives

The population changes. Approximate references to thousands describe scale, not a fixed quota. An exact total requires a dated or presently returned count for a narrated purpose. The Ascension ceremonial count and Balance's fresh ward count of 5,011 in Seal are such observations. The latter must not be reused as a post-death census. Do not infer a universal birth-equals-death rule, an exact perpetual population, or a new reproductive policy from retired population notes.

## Ending decisions and open outcomes

Paul has a shorter inside record and a route out available. He chooses the complete eleven-day record and stays. Neither the narration nor the canon certifies his death as the only possible preservation strategy. Keep the fear, disagreement, offered alternatives and his material responsibility in the firing.

Horizon must preserve the outside account, teach its danger and bring it back before another trigger. The engraved account is accessible only after a trigger; its late availability cannot itself prevent the next act. Kailan inherits Horizon and the surviving Blackwood authority; he leaves with Sarah and Sela after his last kneeling before the Pendulum. Raedin retains Light's internal chain.

Va-Sheva remains, alive and pregnant, following the public permission to bear her child in the Mountain. Jean-Charles died defending its inner gate. Faith and Shield privately conspire behind the Outsiders' backs; support from other Keepers is not settled. Kailan records the prayer, blade and reply but does not understand the private arrangement and does not witness its outcome.
