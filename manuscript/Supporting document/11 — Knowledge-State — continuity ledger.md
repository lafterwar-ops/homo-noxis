## Narrator knowledge at narration time & disclosure (§26.5)
- **Kailan (2026):** narrates his early life retrospectively and **knows the ending** (he fired the Beast; the aftermath) — but he is still **innocent of the outside frame** (Reggane, Sarah's seismics, the planetary meaning), so his account hands the reader dread he does not feel. He discloses his account fully and does not perform; his *withholding* is only of things he still does not understand.
- **Sarah (2026):** knows all of her history; discloses **brutally** ("you will not have the excuse of having thought me better than I am"); withholds exactly one thing — **the route** ("the last locked room in a house I have otherwise thrown open," Ch11).
- **Paul:** the honest instrument; conceals his *reactions* (not facts) from K to keep the account flowing; the record is "the one thing left in this house that is still honest."
