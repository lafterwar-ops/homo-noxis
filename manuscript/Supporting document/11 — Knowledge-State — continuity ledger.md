## Narrator knowledge at narration time & disclosure (§26.5)
- **Kailan's Swiss testimony (Ch1–16):** narrates his Mountain life retrospectively but speaks before the return and firing; it may not use knowledge acquired in Chapters 17 onward. He remains innocent of the outside frame until Sarah's recorded testimony teaches it to him. **Chapter 17 has no prose narrator:** it is the live record, so no later interior knowledge may enter it.
- **Sarah (2026):** knows all of her history; discloses the Reggane theft, distributed search, use of Étienne and Martinez, compact and growth of Horizon in her own testimony. She withholds exactly one thing — **the route**, stated explicitly in Ch13: "I will not give you the route. The machine may keep the confession. It will not keep the way in."
- **Paul:** through Ch17 he is the honest instrument and investigator, concealing reactions rather than material facts while facts are still unread. Chapter 17 puts his post-camera evidence onto the live record and gives him continuous custody of the travelling machine. **Chapter 18 explicitly makes him narrator** for the journey from the lawn to the Mountain.
- **Chapter 19 source boundary:** the opening returns to the same Greater Seal room, sets Paul's Ch18 return reel aside, threads and labels Kailan's reel, and explicitly hands the hearing account back to Kailan. Kailan narrates the public hearing from witness; the five private depositions are quoted from Paul's sealed reel, whose custody he states. Paul, not Kailan, is the sole human whole-holder during the hearing.
- **Chapter 20 source and knowledge boundary:** Kailan explicitly continues that same Greater Seal reel and narrates the Hall he witnessed; Sarah's title correction occurs in one same-frame room interruption. The Hall distributes complete operation across five stations. Sarah reads the returning ground, Paul supplies Ka-Elun's exact deposited prayer, and Kailan compares both against the visible station states; this composite reports into Faith but owns no office control. Paul plays the deposited reel during calibration, makes a breath-exact working transcription checked by Sa-Elian, seals Ka-Elun's reel with Sa-Tavan, then records the assent/firing on a fresh Hall reel. Jean-Charles dies defending the inner Shield gate before the first full load; Kailan witnesses it directly.
- **Chapter 21 source and knowledge boundary:** Kailan continues in the same small room behind the Greater Seal after Paul sets the Hall reel aside. He names Paul's day-numbered Vigil reels and Va-Sheva's admitted Shield audit as sources. Jean-Charles's four-day audit is replayed from the Shield reel because he is already dead. Va-Sheva proves his operational disclosure while excluding claims whose ownership cannot be shown and refusing to coerce a private answer about motive or love. Sela's progression is direct Kailan witness: shared bodily warmth under fear, disclosure around the seventeen Marseille names, then declared romantic love. The fifth-stroke mechanism opens only an unknown stair on day 79.
- **Chapter 22 source and knowledge boundary:** Kailan's same Greater Seal account continues down the day-79 stair. Kailan directly witnesses every discovery; Paul records the named readers. The 145,000-year figure is a computed sum of the live sixth-cycle face and five Justice-sealed captures, with Sarah limiting the stellar check to phase corroboration and refusing to assign specific outside scars. Reset knowledge comes from five mutually dependent office walls. Ravar's motive and case remain unknown: the signature establishes only human authorship and a pointer below. At the shrine Paul explicitly marks the source change from the machine/named witnesses to the Founder wall, which Chapter 23 renders in the distinct `founder` form.
- **Chapter 23 source and knowledge boundary:** there is no prose narrator and no opened room. The uninterrupted `founder` poem is the shrine wall itself, read by all named witnesses after Chapter 22's explicit source handoff. Its 103 quatrains disclose Ravar's wound, decisive acts, human cost, proof-firing and culpability in his own partially self-aware voice. They deliberately do not settle every later ritual, object or meaning: Ravar records early accretions already escaping his design, and `I am the Beast` remains his accusation of himself rather than the book's exclusive definition. Chapter 24 resumes Kailan's account only after the wall is finished and performs the judgment the wall withholds.
- **Chapter 24 closing-state boundary:** Kailan narrates witnessed decisions and clearly labels later forest knowledge as reports reaching Horizon. Sarah explicitly makes him heir to Horizon/Blackwood and defines the outside manuscript as the only pre-trigger warning; everyone knows the engraving opens only after a trigger. Kailan infers the Light chamber's liquid-metal sphere and underwater route, and Ka-Raedin's refusal to answer confirms it. Va-Maera's whispered Chapter 6 prayer and Ka-Dhavar's presence imply, but do not narrate, the later killing of pregnant Va-Sheva.
