*Purpose: fix, once, the dates and ages the book depends on, resolve contradictions, and mark what is deliberately left open. Built by auditing the manuscript + canon (evidence cited). This ledger GOVERNS; where a chapter drifts from it, the chapter is wrong.*

> **⚑ AUTHOR RULING (governing, overrides all prior chronology):** the present frame is **2026**. Sarah Blackwood is **27 at Reggane (1960)** and therefore **93 today (2026)**. The manuscript's on-page frame prints "the seventeenth of March," "the nineteenth," etc. — **no year on the page** — so the year lives only in canon. Sarah's full 1960→present pursuit spans **66 years** (rendered "sixty years" in prose), while Horizon itself begins with the 1974 compact.

**Two clocks run in this book.** (1) **Outside/frame time** — real dates, anchored hard to **2026**. (2) **Mountain deep time** — the ~145,000-yr founding and six-cycle depth (Archive-truth), read on the page as the lived *"eleven thousand years"* since the last reset. Keep them distinct; never let Mountain "eleven thousand years" imply the frame date.
