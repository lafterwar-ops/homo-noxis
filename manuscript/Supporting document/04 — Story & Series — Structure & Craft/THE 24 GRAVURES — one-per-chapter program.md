# The 24 Gravures — a Ninth-Gate program for *Homo Noxius*

One engraving per chapter. Not illustration — *emblem.* The rule set:

1. **One decisive subject per chapter.** No split screens, no composite of several images, no min-maxed cram. A single thing, on plain ground, in the austere line-engraving manner of the *Ninth Gate* woodcuts (black, high-contrast, etched; a small chapter numeral I–XXIV the only caption; no title, no explanation).
2. **Reveal 5–10% of the chapter, maximum.** The emblem shows a sliver — an object, a relic, a gesture — and withholds the scene, the plot, the secret. A first reader meets a mystery; only the finished (or re-reading) reader hears it click.
3. **Every gravure perpendicular to every other.** No motif is used twice. The big icons are deliberately *refused* — no pendulum, no token, no beam, no Mountain silhouette. Each chapter is an *excuse* to surprise the reader with one more, entirely different, face of the book.
4. **Embargo holds.** Nothing here spoils Ravar, the palindrome, the six cycles, or the deep machine. The emblems gesture; they never confess.

Read in order, the twenty-four are a gallery of unrelated objects. Read *again*, they are the whole book laid out on a table before a word of it was spoken.

## The set

**I · Ascension → THE EMPTY COWL.** A ceremonial cowl collapsed on the ground, still holding the shape of a head just drawn out of it. *Reveals:* an office is a shape people fill, not a person. *Withholds:* the death, the succession, the labyrinth beneath the rite. — *garment / void.*

**II · Mirror → THE FACE-DOWN GLASS.** A hand-mirror lying face down, its back tarnished silver. *Reveals:* something here reflects — and is being kept turned away. *Withholds:* what it would show, and the whole doubled structure of the interview. — *mirror / glass.*

**III · Oasis → THE BEE.** A single honeybee, wings spread, seen from above. *Reveals:* the dead place is secretly farmed, pollinated, alive. *Withholds:* the descent, the flash, Va-Sheva, the Book of Blood. — *insect (life).*

**IV · Mountain → THE BLIND FISH.** A pale cave-fish with skin grown over where the eyes were. *Reveals:* things are bred, here, to live and thrive without ever seeing. *Withholds:* the offices, the mirror-lit galleries, the five thousand. — *creature (eyeless).*

**V · Shadow → THE OVERTURNED CUP.** A drinking cup on its side, a little wine pooled and stopped. *Reveals:* the truth spills only when the discipline slips. *Withholds:* Syphiron, the covenant, the caverns, the walk. — *vessel / drink.*

**VI · Festival → THE BITTEN APPLE.** A single apple, one bite taken, a hair-thin worm-track in the skin. *Reveals:* a discord, a whim, a *not-yours* seeded at the heart of the celebration. *Withholds:* the light, the rite, the warning. (This is the Eris nod — the apple without the goddess.) — *fruit / discord.*

**VII · Chain → THE WORN LINK.** One link of heavy chain, rubbed bright and thin with age at the bearing point. *Reveals:* something vast has been held, a very long time, by something small. *Withholds:* the Beast, the leash, the training-rooms, the gamble. — *metal / link.*

**VIII · Light → THE HOODED LAMP.** A lamp with its hood drawn fully down, unlit. *Reveals:* in the house of light, light is the thing most carefully withheld. *Withholds:* the pitch-black room, the memory of light, Ka-Raedin. — *light-source (denied).*

**IX · Heaven → THE FLAKING STAR.** A single painted star peeling off dressed stone, a curl of pigment lifting. *Reveals:* the heaven overhead is paint on rock. *Withholds:* the northern halls, the astronomy, the sky-lie. — *architectural paint.*

**X · Threshold → THE HOLLOWED SILL.** A stone doorsill worn into a shallow bowl by feet, and no door in the frame — only a fine unbroken line of dust across it. *Reveals:* a threshold can be a law you don't cross, with nothing at all to stop you. *Withholds:* the Exception, the split of the Hours, the drawer. — *architecture / sill.*

**XI · Reggane → THE FUSED GLASS.** A lump of green desert glass, sand melted to a bead. *Reveals:* the outside has had its own apocalypse, and keeps its own relic of it. *Withholds:* Sarah, the test, the father's margin, the tea. — *mineral (the outside's Beast).*

**XII · Confession → THE MUTE BELL.** A small bell with its clapper cut out and gone. *Reveals:* a place built to take the sound away and give a man back only himself. *Withholds:* Paul's turn, the anechoic cell, the photograph. — *silenced instrument.*

**XIII · Infiltration → THE PINNED PHOTOGRAPH.** One photograph, pinned at a single corner: a woman against pale rock, over-bright. *Reveals:* the enemy was let in, and the letting-in was filed. *Withholds:* Horizon, Amastan, the bargain, the drawer. — *photograph.*

**XIV · Trial → THE CHILD'S SANDAL.** A single small sandal, alone, laces slack. *Reveals:* a death here is *spent*, like a coin, and a child's will do. *Withholds:* the antechamber, "Faith supports death," the Book of the Beast. — *footwear / the spent innocent.*

**XV · Knocks → THE FRESH CRACK.** A hairline fracture in stone, brand new, a few grains of dust caught falling. *Reveals:* something is striking the mountain from the far side. *Withholds:* the enemy, the drilling, Sarah's methods, the count. — *stone / fracture.*

**XVI · Horizon → THE SINGLE HAIR.** One long dark hair, coiled, on pale linen. *Reveals:* the lever into a fortress was intimacy, and it left a trace. *Withholds:* Va-Sheva, Jean-Charles, the counterfeit rite. — *intimacy / the human lever.* *(Alt: swap in ERIS here — a lean figure, back turned, one apple in hand — if you want a single mythic body among the objects.)*

**XVII · Mock → THE TAPE REEL.** One reel of magnetic tape, a loop spilling loose off the flange. *Reveals:* the whole account is a physical thing, and it has just been picked up to be carried out. *Withholds:* the fake-knock reveal, Martinez, the handoff of the narration. — *recording medium.*

**XVIII · Return → THE BURNING EDGE.** The corner of a sheet of paper, curling, a single line of ember advancing. *Reveals:* out in the world a network is being burned before it can be taken. *Withholds:* the gate, the captured people, the drum of poison. — *fire / paper.*

**XIX · Seal → THE BLEEDING EAR.** A single ear, a thread of blood at the lobe. *Reveals:* the one who could *hear* has been struck, and the hearing is gone. *Withholds:* the five, the roof-fall, the depositions, the firing. — *body / the ruined sense.*

**XX · Cataclysm → THE PENDANT DROP.** A single drop of water hanging, about to fall, from the tip of a still finger. *Reveals:* the end of a hemisphere is begun by a thing this small, done on purpose. *Withholds:* the Beast, the arithmetic, the scale. — *water / the smallest gesture.*

**XXI · Quarantine → THE WIZENED SEED.** One seed, shrunken and a little malformed, set alone. *Reveals:* whatever survives will come up *wronger* each time, and someone is counting that. *Withholds:* the ninety days, Ka-Leth's ledger, the door at day sixty, the stair down. — *seed / entropy.*

**XXII · Clock → THE WORN TOOTH.** One gear-tooth, alone, its face polished to a nub by uncountable turns. *Reveals:* the god is a machine, and this is the least, most patient part of it. *Withholds:* the odometer, the tally, the reveal, the name. — *gear / the machine's atom.*

**XXIII · Beast → THE BROKEN COMB.** A woman's bone hair-comb, one tooth snapped. *Reveals:* at the dead centre of a world-ending engine is a private, human loss. *Withholds:* Ravar, Ithe, the first fire, "I am the Beast." — *personal relic.*

**XXIV · Whole → THE ROPED BOX.** A plain wooden box, closed, tied off with cord. *Reveals:* everything you have just read is what was inside it — and it got out. *Withholds:* nothing it hasn't earned. The gallery closes on the container of itself. — *the book, as an object.*

## Style & production notes

- **Manner:** single subject, centred, on empty ground; austere intaglio/woodcut linework; deep blacks, fine hatching; no colour. Think the *Nine Gates* plates: an object rendered with uncanny seriousness, captioned only with its numeral.
- **Caption:** the chapter numeral (**I–XXIV**) and nothing else. No titles, no legends. The withholding is the point.
- **Signature device (optional, very Ninth-Gate):** hide one recurring mark in every plate — a tiny mirror-symmetric glyph tucked in the hatching — so that a reader who notices it, and lays the plates side by side, is being told *there is a key here* long before the text admits one. (It can quietly rhyme with Ravar's palindrome without ever naming it.)
- **The through-line:** I·Ascension (empty cowl) and XXIV·Whole (closed box) bracket the set — an emptiness and a fullness, a shape drawn *out* and a truth carried *in*. XX (the drop) is the hinge the whole book turns on, disguised as the smallest plate in the gallery.
