## 0. THE MANDATE IN ONE BREATH

One **standalone** novel. Literary speculative fiction about **systems people cannot see, information they do not possess, institutions whose purposes are forgotten or concealed** — and the persecution of those who cross the compartments that keep the whole from being seen. The Mountain (ceremonial, compartmentalised) and the Outside (algorithmic, commercial, intelligence-driven) are **two forms of the same blindness**, not opposites. The philosophy is *generated by events*, never laid on top. Nothing may depend on a sequel; a sequel may remain imaginable but the book must land completely: the Cataclysm fires, the whole is assembled, it escapes the Mountain for the first time, the Pendulum starts again.

**Kailan is not the martyr of perfect truth. He is the martyr of crossing compartments.** He sees real structure and can still misread its meaning catastrophically.

## 1. DEEP-TIME & SCIENCE LOCKS (derived, not inherited)

Researched against: Neanderthal/Denisovan admixture timing, Toba, Younger Dryas, antipodal seismic focusing, EPICA 800k ice-core record.

- **FOUNDING AGE — "on the order of 120,000–140,000 years" (working figure ~145k), and deliberately NOT stated as a false-precise fact.** Neanderthal-grade cognition (~1,500 cc) demonstrably existed across this whole window, so a hidden archaic-lineage civilisation of this antiquity is defensible; material traces vanishing over ~145k millennia is *more* credible than over 10k. **Mechanism of the number in-story:** the Year-Clock gives an *exact mechanical tick-count* (one tooth per solar year), but converting ticks → absolute calendar age, and confirming the prior burns, is done by **Sarah + Paul cross-dating against outside geology/ice**, and it comes with **stated uncertainty**. The characters reach *an estimate and a range*, not a decree. This honours §XIII ("remain unresolved… research… oldest/largest plausible") and the theme (even the recovered whole resists total certainty).
- **CYCLES — SIX burns counting the present one; FIVE prior seams in the Year-Clock.** Irregular spacing (the outside rebuilds at variable rates), *not* a clean metronome. "Sixth Cycle Archive" (front matter) is preserved and now *earned*.
- **THE MOST RECENT PRIOR BURN (the fifth) ~11,000–13,000 years ago**, at the last glacial termination — the drowning the outside half-remembers as **Atlantis / the Richat structure**. Outside science reads that era through **meltwater pulse / Younger-Dryas cooling** (mainstream) — the Mountain's causation is the hidden truth *behind* the accepted natural story, **and the fit is imperfect on purpose** (not every seam maps cleanly to a known event; the YD impact idea is refuted, so the Mountain's volcanism/tsunami mechanism only partially overlaps it). This is the novel's engine in miniature: a real event, a natural explanation everyone accepts, a concealed cause.
- **DO NOT pin a burn to Toba (~74k)** — the bottleneck/volcanic-winter claim is now largely debunked; attaching the Mountain to it would be the "cause of everything" error and would fail informed scrutiny.
- **THE BEAST MECHANISM (operational precision, not total theory):** water metered onto deep geothermal heat → flash steam → pressure pulse driven down a cut throat (Balance); optical/geometric deep surfaces bend and **converge** the seismic wave through the core to the planet's antipode (Light); Faith **times** each pulse to arrive *in phase* with the returning echo so the swing *builds* by resonance; Justice governs permissions/sequence/records each irreversible step; Shield contains recoil and holds the abort before the point of no return. **Antipode = Tonga–Kermadec (Ring of Fire).** Antipodal seismic focusing is real *for impact-scale energy*; the **speculative core** is that a slow **resonant drill** reaches that energy without an impactor. Keep it operational (what each office controls, what is measured, what error is fatal). **Do not over-quantify the energy budget** — that is where informed scrutiny would break the premise. The result: Ring-of-Fire cascade + Pacific-rim mega-tsunami + eruption + volcanic winter = world reset. The Sahara siting is *because* it is antipodal to the Ring of Fire. Burns hide in ice cores among ordinary large-eruption sulphate spikes.
- **GEOGRAPHY LOCKS (retain):** latitude **21.3°N**, longitude **~0°** (Reggane meridian, due north ~560 km); **Richat = Atlantis due west** same parallel. Fix lingering 21.6° everywhere.
- **PENDULUM:** Long Return **≈66 h ≈ 2.75 days**; beat ≈22 s; L=125 m. Retire "65 h" / "two and a bit days."
- **POPULATION: 5,000** (fix Ch4's internal "~3,000").
- **Succession drill = the five-part signature.** Instituted by Ravar at every Keeper succession (re-certify the chain; an operator changed; the machine must answer). A few drops → five even strokes + long soft roll. This is the heartbeat detected by Sarah (Reggane, 1960), matched by Paul, counterfeited by the enemy, amplified into the sixth burn.

**Open science flags for later expert check (record only):** energy budget of resonant vs. impact antipodal focusing; ice-core detectability of engineered burns; whether ~145k founding overstretches archaic material culture; whether a 5,000-person closed population is genetically viable over ~145k with the stated reproductive controls (population-genetics check).

## 4. INSTITUTIONAL ARCHITECTURE (the Beast as a chain, not a button)

The five offices are an apparatus that: prevents individual operational control; keeps the Beast operational; ensures almost nobody understands what their labour sustains; **turns maintenance into religion**; makes each *individual* decision lawful; divides responsibility so no one feels like the author of catastrophe; funnels the civilisation toward activation **without a single obvious tyrant**; and survives because each office believes it prevents the others becoming dangerous.

**The Beast is the chain of lawful, compartmentalised decisions that makes the weapon fire — and then uses the firing as proof the chain was sacred.**

**"Let no one hand hold the whole"** primarily prohibits complete **operational control** — not understanding. Danger = when complete understanding + authority + custody + unilateral action occupy one hand (Ravar; then Kailan-as-the-eye; then a single seizing state). The **PAL/token** is the physical proof: five must collude to arm.

**Legal path to Threshold Exception (Paul):** an anomalous/impossible succession signal appears → Kailan's testimony, Sarah's account, Mountain records, and outside seismic evidence **conflict** → evidence shows the Blackwood/Horizon system used **without Sarah's authority** → each Kahir can verify its own fragment but **none may lawfully obtain the others'** → Justice concludes neither activation nor refusal can be lawfully decided from five isolated accounts → the Mountain requires a **witness outside the five**. Paul qualifies: no Kahir, no key/lever/blood/command, trained to preserve and compare, already found productive contradictions, can hold knowledge without operational control. Constitutional line: **"A hand may act. A witness may only record."**

**Legal path to activation:** each office makes a locally rational decision under real institutional pressure; no villain presses one button; Justice's likelihood-rule + the impossibility of lawful refusal from isolated accounts + Shield's threat-read + Balance's arithmetic (sealing is *also* a slow death) + Faith's interpretation **slide the system lawfully toward the Beast**. Every step permitted separately; the point of no return is named and crossed with no last-second moral rescue.

## 5. THE DOCUMENTARY FORM (a real textual constitution)

The book we read = **the Outsider Copy**, assembled by Paul. **No event may be narrated from a source that could not have produced it. No omniscient drift.** Two modes only, plus admitted documents:
- **`{room}`** — live interview transcript (dashes, no tags, *In Noxius* / *In English*).
- **First-person account** — from whoever is telling (Kailan; later Sarah, given during the urgent return; later **Ravar's recovered autobiography**, read by Kailan & Paul side by side; later Keeper depositions to Paul; Justice proceedings; Shield records; operational transcripts during activation).
- **Admitted documents** — scripture blocks; recovered inscriptions; rare brackets/gaps/disputed-word notes (used sparingly; the main account stays immersive).

**Contradictions between accounts are preserved where they drive investigation** — each ideally yields a question → a check against another record → a new physical fact → an "oh, no" discovery → the next plot move. Never vagueness for its own sake. Translation may surface via a few disputed terms — no academic overload.
