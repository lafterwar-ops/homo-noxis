## 3. WHAT THE MAP SURFACED — DEFECTS (with disposition)

1. **The live-crisis thread died for three chapters — FIXED (S38).** The alarm is raised at full pressure in Ch11 (drum draws a second time; *"Cancel the car for the morning, and every car after it"*), then Ch13 and Ch14 never acknowledge it, and **Ch15 opened in flat contradiction** — Paul *"I should be gone by morning"*, Kailan *"whatever car you have ordered"* — pre-alarm material sitting post-alarm. Ch15's opening room rewritten: nobody is leaving, the cars were stopped the night the drum drew twice, and it has gone on drawing. Converts three chapters of slack into pressure and removes a hard contradiction. *Residual: Ch13/Ch14 rooms still don't reference the clock; Ch14 l84 ("four nights") is at least consistent with Sarah's "tonight, and the nights after it."*
2. **Ch9→Ch10 hand-off was the one arbitrary seam — FIXED (S38).** Every other narrator switch is bridged (Ch10→Ch11 has the model block). Ch10 opened cold in Kailan's voice. Added a short room bridge (Paul turns the floor back: "Did your people keep any record at all of hands like hers?") that motivates the switch, leads into the chapter's existing first line, and does **not** pre-empt the Tomb reveal.
3. **Ch23 opened cold in Ravar's voice — FIXED (S38).** Ch22 legalises the source ("What follows is Ravar's"); Ch23 crossed a chapter boundary with no re-anchor — the one place a reader could lose whose voice it is. One-line re-anchor added.
4. **Three chapters have no irreversible event: Ch2, Ch3, Ch7.** All three earn their place on setup/revelation density, and Ch7's warmth is the currency Ch8 spends. **Not defects — but they are where any pacing complaint will land.** Recorded, not actioned.
5. **The present-day plot escalates every chapter; the past plot escalates only in Ch1 and Ch6.** Ch2/3/4 past-strands are instruction and tour. Structural observation for any future compression.
6. **Sarah's testimony is only two chapters (Ch9, Ch11).** If "Sarah's testimony" is meant to be a co-equal pillar of the architecture, it is currently carried by two chapters plus room presence. Open question for the author.
7. **Ch17–18 are narrated from an unspecified later vantage.** The departure from the Swiss house is well staged (Ch16 l105: Paul stops the reel, boxes it, pockets a travelling machine), but **arrival at a new narrating position is never staged.** The record stays legal (Paul's machine is diegetically present) but the "where is this being told from" question goes unmarked from Ch17 to the end. **NOT FIXED — flagged for author ruling**, since staging it touches the whole back half's frame.
8. **Lecture-shaped passages (revelation with no present stake):** Ch20 l21–29 (water-listeners/ashkeepers/salt-fish catalogue); Ch23 l75–79 (decoded-relics glossary — the one passage that reads as an appendix inside a chapter); Ch23 l31–37 (longest uninterrupted Ravar stretch without a frame break). Trim candidates, not errors.
