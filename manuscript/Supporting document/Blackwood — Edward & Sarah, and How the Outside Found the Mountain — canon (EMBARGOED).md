## 2. Edward Blackwood — the founder

- Born ~**1900** (London). Built **Blackwood Commodities** into a global house on **oil, metals, and uranium**; deep **French-military** ties; business that put him repeatedly into **Algeria and the deep Sahara.**
- Through the uranium/desert dealings — and the orbit of the **1960 Reggane** test (see §5) — he **began to sense something buried out there.** He chased the whisper for the rest of his working life and **never found it.** *His daughter did, after him.*
- **Faded from the story by the 1970s** (died or withdrew). By 1998 he is a **portrait** — large enough to preside over a room, the founder hung above the fire.
- **Use (Ch3):** the portrait beat. *"I was told you would tell me about Edward Blackwood."* Paul says a careful, benevolent little and withholds the rest; the **scale of the dynasty seeps into K** without a figure named; and, quietly, the crumb is planted — a uranium-and-desert founder who sensed the Mountain and died short of it. K reads the portrait with Light's eye (who was this man, what does the room say he wanted).

## 3. Sarah Blackwood — who found the Mountain

- Born **1933** (London), Edward's daughter and heir — **65 in 1998.** A **seismic researcher.**
- **~1960 (age 27):** borrowing **nuclear chain-reaction theory** from a French physicist, she applies it to **seismic data** and finds a signature that should not exist — and it leads her to **Homo Noxis.** *(This is the salvage jewel: it is exactly how a modern would detect our seismic-**resonance** Beast — an engineered signature in the earth. It makes the Blackwood contact **seismic in origin**, and makes Paul her lineal successor in method.)*
- Across the following decades she becomes the house's **secret-keeper and gatekeeper**: monopolizing and steering seismic research worldwide so no one else stumbles onto the signature. **Motive is NOT protection of the world** — it is control, monopoly, and ultimately the **whole** (the doctrine and the Beast's operation), i.e., the double game.
- Canon anchors she must satisfy: she **has been to the Mountain** and the top of the house **holds the location** (Reveal Sequence); she is the **addressee of the Declaration** (*"To Sarah Blackwood." — signed "The Mountain."*); her name sits in the Mountain's **sealed Shield archive** K finds in Ch10 (with Amastan, Ka-Xhian, Reggane 1960). By 1998 the relationship has escalated from *hide it* to *contact and extraction* — K is out, and the Declaration is coming to her.
- What she still **lacks**, and why the interviews exist: not the *where* (she has that) but the **whole** — the divided knowledge, the operation of the Beast — which only K carries.

## 4. The seismic through-line (the real keeper)

The Blackwoods come at the Mountain **through the ground**, across generations:

> **Edward** (uranium, the desert, an instinct he never proved) → **Sarah** (the seismic signature detected, 1960 — the resonance found from outside) → **Paul** (the seismic-resonance scientist, taking K's account, his hunger spiking on every pendulum / stone-listening / tremor beat).

Three people, one instrument: the earth itself. This is the spine to keep; it welds the Blackwoods to the resonance Beast and gives Paul his ancestry of method.
