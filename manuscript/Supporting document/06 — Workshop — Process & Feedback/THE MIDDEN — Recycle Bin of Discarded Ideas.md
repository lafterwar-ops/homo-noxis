# THE MIDDEN
### The Recycle Bin of Discarded Ideas

*A refuse heap. Everything here was once thought good, and was let go. Nothing is deleted from the world merely because it was cut from the book; it is moved here, where it can be dug up again. As the manuscript grows in coherence, connection, and complexity, this document grows in chaos, incoherence, and disarray — that is its nature and its use. A discarded idea is not a dead idea. It is an idea waiting for the chapter that can hold it.*

*Rules of the heap: never prune, only add. Keep the reason it was discarded, because the reason is usually more useful than the idea. When an idea is resurrected, do not delete it here — annotate it "RESURRECTED → [where]," so the strata stay honest.*

## I. KAHIRS & DOCTRINE — discarded

**The "curse" reading of *let no hand hold the whole*.**
Once the divided-knowledge law was a superstition with teeth: *if you learn the who and the where and the how and the what, you hold the whole, and the Beast (Big Foot) comes and eats you.* Discarded. **The Beast as pure myth (no mechanism).**
Early instinct: the Beast is a story Shield tells, nothing under the Mountain at all. Discarded. The Beast is **real** — engineered seismic resonance driven down to magma. *Contradiction:* guts Book 8's payoff and the whole deterrent premise; the Mountain is a bunker built around a weapon, not a shrine built around a rumour.

**The Measure as the "Fourth hour."**
A permission strip once read *"the Fourth hour, the Measure."* Discarded — there is no Fourth-called-Measure. The five Hours are **First Light, Second Silence, Third Measure, Fourth Record, Fifth Blade** (Light / Faith / Balance / Justice / Shield). The Measure is the **third**. *Contradiction:* invents an hour that does not exist and mis-assigns Balance's hour.

## IV. WORLD, COSMOLOGY & SCRIPTURE — discarded

**The "Sixth Cycle" cosmology.**
A model in which *now* is the sixth cycle, the African Humid Period was the last Cataclysm, and cycles average ~25,000 years. Superseded. (Series Direction and the Reveal Sequence still run the sixth-cycle version.)

**Reggane → latitude, Richat → longitude.**
The triangulation roles, reversed. Correct: **Reggane** lies due **north** → shares the meridian → gives **longitude**; the **Richat** lies due **west** → shares the parallel → gives **latitude**. *Contradiction:* swaps the two anchors; a reader doing the geometry lands in the wrong desert. **The pendulum's "two and a bit days."**
The Foucault Long Return was once "two and a bit days" / "two days past a third," implying ~27° N. Retired for **≈ 65 hours ("nearly three days," "two days and two-thirds")**, which is what 21.6° N actually gives and what keeps Reggane-due-north-four-weeks honest. *Contradiction:* the old figure quietly relocates the Mountain ~5° of latitude and breaks the march-distance.

**Scripture orientation: "fire on the right, sea on the left."**
An attempted amendment (in *The Last Cataclysm* doc and the stray `book of the covenant.docx`) flips the gravure so the reader faces **north**, water/Atlantis on the **left**, volcanoes on the **right** — and even claims "scripture has been amended to match." **The Mountain *is* Atlantis.**
Discarded. The Mountain has nothing to do with Atlantis. **Atlantis is the Richat Structure** — the last people to defy the Mountain, drowned when the forest vanished; its ring is their grave and a fixed point to triangulate from. *Contradiction:* would make the hidden bunker the famous lost city — the opposite of hidden.

Retro-fix owed: strip K's references to the Eiffel Tower and the metric system from the Trial chapter. *Contradiction:* K cannot know 1998's Paris landmarks or SI units inside his account of the Mountain.

# THIRD STRATUM — the frozen parallel book
### (the "Third-Person Repo," a June draft that became a different novel)

*Added 2026-07-12, excavated from the frozen `Third Person Repo` — an entire earlier draft, thirteen chapters of it, that a worker log marks "a stale June draft — not touched." It is not the same story in another voice; it is a substantively different novel that happens to share a world and a cast. Where it agrees with the current book, nothing is logged. The names here are ghosts — watch for them creeping back.*

## Kahirs & Doctrine — discarded

**Horizon as an internal Mountain office, held by Ka-Henry.** In the frozen draft "Horizon" is one of the Mountain's *own* offices, and "Ka-Henry, Keeper of Horizon" recovers Paul's document. Current canon abolishes both office and man: Horizon is Paul's (captured) *outside* organization; no Ka-Henry exists. *Why:* the double game requires Horizon to be an outside power wearing the Mountain's vestments, not a seat at its table.

**The Covenant's fifth book as "Promise," and nothing beyond it.** The frozen fifth scripture is *Promise* (the Beast chained, the people reborn) — end of story. Current canon keeps *Promise* as scripture but adds a meta-layer: "Book 5" of the *series* becomes Ravar's autobiography. *Why:* the founder-is-the-hero reveal did not yet exist. (cf. below)

## Characters — discarded

**Paul Morgan, "the Outsider," serving "The Outsiders."** The frozen threshold page is "Written by Paul Morgan the Outsider," and the prologue has him working for an organization "unapologetically called 'The Outsiders.'" Current canon fuses interviewer and organization into Horizon-captured-by-Blackwood. *Why:* a plain enemy is less cruel than a friend whose own side was taken. (cf. Stratum II, "Paul the knowing snare.")

**Ka-Sheva, Keeper of Shield.** The frozen intro hands the manuscript "to Ka-Sheva, Keeper of Shield" — a fossil from when Sheva held the Keeper rank; the frozen chapters already contradict it (calling her Va-Sheva, giving the keepership to Ka-Dhavar). Current canon settles it: **Va-Sheva, the Shield Veil and K's love; Ka-Dhavar, Keeper of Shield.** *Why:* Sheva is the beloved equal, not the office-holder.

**"Kylian / Sa-Kylian," the operational name.** In the frozen Ch13 the outside handler renames the hero for field use: "For operational use, you will be Kylian." Current canon keeps him **Kailan / K**. *Why:* the rename belonged to the exiled-operative ending that no longer stands. (cf. the exit-pipeline ending, below.) **RESURRECTED → Ch4, 2026-07-13:** revived as **Sa-Kilian of Horizon** — not a field alias but K's intake *designation* as a Horizon specimen (Va-Kailan filed back down to a Sa-), which he throws in Paul's face to show he sees the apparatus. Distinct from the exile-operative use; see `Chapters 4–6 — The Turn`.

**Va-Elun / Ka-Elun (Faith), an undecided prefix.** The new Faith figure is "Va-Elun of Faith" in one chapter and "Ka-Elun" in another; standardized to Ka-Elun, so "Va-Elun" is a discarded spelling. *Why:* a name that could not decide its own rank. *(And note: Va-Elise from Stratum II actually lives here — "Elise, the outsider processor in grey, breaking seals with ink from a tube.")*

## Plot & Structure — discarded

**The frozen order — Ch3 "Mountain," Ch4 "Oasis" — and a 12-chapter plan with no "Chain."** The frozen draft runs Living-Mountain before Oasis; its `book1-structure.md` preserves a still-earlier **twelve-chapter** book in which Ch8 is *Martyrdom* directly (no *Chain*) and Ch10 is titled **"The Wrong Confession."** Current canon inserts *Chain* (→ thirteen), drops the "Wrong," and swaps to Oasis-before-Mountain. *Why:* the Beast's mechanism earned its own chapter, and the reader needs the open sky early. (cf. Strata I & II, chapter order.)

**Reggane as only a 1960 blast photograph.** In the frozen Ch10 Tomb, "Reggane, 1960" is merely the caption under an archived photo of "a terrible flower of light rising from the horizon, too large to be flame" — an outsider atrocity relic, carrying **no geodesy at all.** Current canon promotes Reggane to the due-north longitude anchor of the triangulation. *Why:* the whole coordinate machinery was a later installation. *(Reggane's full arc, for the record: blast-photo-in-the-Tomb → geodetic anchor spread across chapters, briefly even the Oasis flash → and now, after the Ch3 rebuild, back to Ch10 only, but as an anchor rather than a snapshot.)*

**The Horizon exit-pipeline ending.** The frozen Ch13 ends with K's name "held," his standing "ceased," his body carried out through a tunnel beneath a dead well fitted with "outsider materials: metal ribs, smooth white panels, glass bulbs that gave steady light without flame," where Jean-Charles and Elise take delivery of him as an exile-agent. Horizon here is the **Mountain's covert door to the outside** that turns K into an operative — not (current) Paul's captured identity. *Why:* superseded by innocent-Paul and the Noxius-Ravar destiny.

## World / Cosmology / Scripture — discarded

**One Cataclysm, no cycles.** The frozen Covenant's *Cataclysm* is a single apocalypse — the Outsider armies burned and drowned when the Keepers loosed the Beast. There is **no five-cycle cosmology, no "fifth," no Younger-Dryas / Atlantis drowning.** Current canon makes the last Cataclysm the fifth and ties it to Atlantis. *Why:* the deep-time clock (and the dread of a sixth cycle ahead) is a later structure. (cf. Stratum I, the Sixth-Cycle model — itself a *later* overshoot the other way.)

**No geodetic layer at all.** The frozen draft has **no pendulum, no 21.6°N, no Richat, no Atlantis, no equinox beam, no magma** — zero hits across the whole repo. The entire latitude/longitude/period triangulation that now drives Paul's hunger was installed chapter-by-chapter in later passes. *Why:* the frozen book's threat is mythic, not checkable; the coordinate machinery is what makes the outsider tape *dangerous.*

**No Ravar.** The founder-is-the-hero reveal is nowhere in the frozen draft; it leaves K an exiled operative called "Kylian," not the once-and-future founder. *Why:* the terminal reveal had not been conceived. This is the single largest thing the current book *has* that the frozen one lacks.

## Prose & Method — discarded

**Single-layer third person, and the epistolary "Clémentine" confession.** The frozen book is close third person throughout ("Kailan had been standing for longer than he wished to admit"), and its prologue is a guilt-letter — *"Clémentine, / I cannot ask for forgiveness without first setting down the chain of events…"* — signed Paul Morgan. Current canon is two-layer first-person testimony with Paul as interviewer/preserver: no penitent letter, no named beloved. *Why:* found-testimony needs the hero's own voice, and the confession-to-Clémentine belonged to a Paul who was the story's guilty conscience. (cf. Stratum II, the third-person pivot & Clémentine.)
