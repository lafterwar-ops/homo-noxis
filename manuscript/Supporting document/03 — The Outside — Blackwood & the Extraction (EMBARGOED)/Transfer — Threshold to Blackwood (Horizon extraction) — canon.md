# Transfer — The Threshold to the Blackwood Estate (Horizon extraction) — Canon

*Purpose: lock the authority, custody, condition, route, and timing of Kailan's removal from the Mountain to the Blackwood Estate. Chapter 16 is the governing on-page account. This document holds the off-page logistics only so later references do not drift.*

> **AUTHOR-RULING ALIGNMENT:** the present frame is March–April **2026**, not November. Kailan leaves the Mountain only weeks before the interviews. He is conscious from the south threshold to the dead well and during the outward journey. Any older note that sedates him through the desert, dates the first interview to November, or assigns the route reveal to Chapter 13 is superseded.

---

## 1. Governing dramatic principle

The transfer is a custody story before it is a travel story. Its essential movements are:

1. the Mountain removes Kailan's public name while keeping his body alive;
2. Faith gives him a last blessing and Shield walks him out through the south threshold;
3. he crosses the desert consciously for two nights under Shield and Justice custody;
4. at the dead well, Horizon receives him, removes the lower dark cord, and assigns a new operational name;
5. Va-Sheva chooses the containment that preserves his breathing, then leaves him;
6. Horizon moves him through deliberately unreadable outside transitions to Switzerland;
7. at the estate, Ka-Sarah defines the new cage and outside optics gives him a real tomorrow.

Chapter 16 may render these movements fully because their emotional meaning is the chapter. Later references use breadcrumbs and physical callbacks, not a repeated itinerary.

---

## 2. Parties and authority

- **Justice** removes Va-Kailan from ordinary sequence, holds the name from common use, and sends a witness with the body as far as the dead-well handoff.
- **Shield** owns movement from the sealed route through the Mountain's south threshold and across the two-night desert path. Va-Sheva commands the transfer. Three wardens accompany her.
- **Horizon** receives the body at the dead well under the Exception authorised by the five offices. It is the Blackwood-funded outside structure created by the 1974 compact: a line that preserves certain Mountain facts outside while controlling what reaches the Mountain from outside.
- **Va-Elise** executes the Horizon intake record. She receives the Justice packet, records the Shield-to-Horizon custody change, removes the lower dark cord into a timed envelope, assigns the operational forms Kylian / Sa-Kylian, and supplies the disguise spectacles.
- **Jean-Charles** is the field receiver, escort, and practical instructor. His easy questions during the journey also gather route intelligence. His hand on Kailan's shoulder at the well is the first instance of the gesture Chapter 17 repeats.
- **Ka-Sarah / Sarah Blackwood** commands Horizon at the estate and imposes the Exception restrictions.
- **Paul Morgan** belongs to the Horizon house in the present frame but is not a mover on Kailan's extraction and is not a first-hand source for the route. His knowledge remains compartmented; in Chapter 16 he learns the road from Kailan's account.

## 3. Route lock

### On-page, governing legs

- **Origin:** the Mountain's south threshold in the central Sahara. Geography canon governs the coordinates and orientation.
- **Shield leg:** two nights on foot, hiding under stone by day. Kailan is awake, walks under his own power, and can remember the desert, the passing vehicle sound, Va-Sheva's answers, and the dead well.
- **Dead-well exchange:** a concealed Horizon passage opens below an abandoned well. This is the formal custody boundary. Justice delivers sequence; Shield delivers movement; Horizon signs for the body.
- **Outside leg:** vehicles, controlled rooms, and aircraft. Kailan is awake but cannot reconstruct the route: he does not know outside geography, travels through darkness and sealed transitions, and is given few place names. The manuscript truthfully compresses this to `vehicle to room to aircraft` before Switzerland.
- **Destination:** the Blackwood Estate in the Saanenland above Gstaad, Switzerland.

### Off-page logistical spine

The following remains available as behind-page infrastructure but must not contradict Kailan's conscious account:

1. concealed well passage to a remote desert vehicle and staging field;
2. light aircraft out of the deep interior;
3. protected long-haul movement into Geneva, using the old Jamaican diplomatic favour if that breadcrumb is ever retained;
4. Blackwood family helicopter from Geneva to the estate helipad.

The aircraft and helicopter may be separate machines within Kailan's compressed sequence. He is never said to identify their models, fields, borders, or exact number. Do not reintroduce sedation to hide a route that darkness, compartmentation, and his lack of outside vocabulary already make unreadable.

## 4. Condition, silence, and first reception

- Kailan is exhausted, dehydrated, frightened, and culture-shocked, but conscious. He is not a bundle under cloth and does not sleep through the desert.
- At the Swiss house he barely sleeps and refuses speech for **eleven days**. He eats. When questioned, he may give minimal written answers on a slate and erase them in the questioner's presence; this does not break the spoken-silence fact reported in Chapter 18.
- On the **twelfth day**, Ka-Sarah receives him. She is ninety-three. She does not rise; her narrowed shoulders, veined hands, controlled thumb tremor, and continued command carry the age.
- The first formal Paul/Kailan interview begins **2026-03-17 at 09:00**. The extraction therefore begins in late February or early March 2026, leaving room for the two-night Shield walk, the outward travel, eleven silent days, and Ka-Sarah's first reception before the interview cadence begins.

## 6. The Jamaica favour (optional breadcrumb, off-page)

Blackwood kept Kingston's lights on in the 1980s through an alumina-for-oil rescue when ordinary credit failed. The old debt can plausibly buy one quiet Jamaican diplomatic movement into Geneva in 2026.

If used on page, keep it to a searchable crumb: **alumina / the eighties / Kingston / when no one else would**. Do not explain the ledger, and do not make Paul the person who arranged or witnessed Kailan's transfer.

Real-world colour source retained in the research layer: the Marc Rich / Jamaica alumina–oil episode described in *The World for Sale* by Javier Blas and Jack Farchy. Blackwood occupies the archetypal space; it is not a literal biography.

## 7. Knowledge and disclosure rules

- **Kailan knows:** the legal erasure, south-threshold exit, two-night walk, dead well, Jean-Charles and Elise, the custody wording, the new names, the spectacles, Va-Sheva's departure, sensory fragments of vehicles/rooms/aircraft, Switzerland, the estate, eleven days of silence, and Ka-Sarah's restrictions.
- **Kailan does not know at transfer time:** Jean-Charles's service history; the later leak; the flight's diplomatic cover; the airfields; the full Horizon network; or the exact route on a map.
- **Paul knows before Chapter 16:** that Horizon brought Kailan to the estate and that Elise and Jean-Charles were involved. He does not know the road in Kailan's lived order until the Chapter 16 account.
- **Sarah and Elise know:** the logistical route and institutional authorisations.
- **Va-Sheva knows:** the Shield route and dead-well handoff; she need not know every outside leg.

No narration in Chapter 16 may use later knowledge to label Jean-Charles a traitor, rehearse his campaigns, or reinterpret his questions. The setup is his observable attention and the request to keep small route details from Elise. The payoff belongs to Chapters 18 and 21.

## 10. The shadow on Horizon

Horizon is not a clean rescue. It preserves Kailan's life through a lawful chain and immediately turns that life into controlled custody, renaming, surveillance, instruction, and use. The same apparatus that saves him also asks Jean-Charles to measure him and Elise to inventory him. Outside optics is genuinely freer than Light's sealed knowledge, but access to it is rationed by another institution.

That doubleness must remain observable rather than announced: sweets and assessment; protection and imprisonment; a public diagram read under supervision; a tomorrow offered inside a property Kailan may not leave.
