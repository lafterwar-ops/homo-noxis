## 1.1 The date, and the proof (the ice ledger)

**The exact date: c. 9,700 BCE** — the **Younger Dryas termination**, dated in the Greenland ice (GISP2 / GRIP, Summit) to **11,703 b2k** (years before AD 2000). Two independent witnesses converge on it: the **ice** (11,703 b2k ≈ 9,700 BCE) and **Plato** ("9,000 years before Solon" ≈ 9,600 BCE) — agreement inside a century, from a Greenland drill-core and a second-hand Egyptian temple-tale that never met.

**Why the ice is the proof.** An ice core is an **annual ledger** — one layer per year, countable like tree-rings, or like the Mountain's own equinox-clock — and each layer records that year's cold (oxygen isotopes) and that year's snowfall. At the drowning-horizon the ledger shows a **near-vertical step**: temperature leaps **~10 °C** and snow accumulation roughly **doubles**, the main step accomplished in **~25–35 years**, its sharpest sub-steps in **~5 years**. A whole planet's climate reorganises inside a single human life. This is the physical warrant for scripture's *"the forest was destroyed in a single day… the waters spoiled, the land made desert"*: at the scale of the record the change **is** effectively instantaneous — **the myth compressed the truth; it did not invent it** — and the witness is the outside northern ice, not the Mountain's own word.

**The clock agrees.** The Mountain's year-clock has beaten one equinox at a time ever since: **~23,400 festivals of equal light** (11,703 × 2). And the first human temples — **Göbekli Tepe, ~9,600 BCE** — rise in the very centuries after: *when the wrath had passed, they built again.*

*(Real-world anchor: Younger Dryas–Preboreal transition dated 11,703 b2k on the GISP2 timescale; abrupt +~10 °C and doubled accumulation at Summit, Greenland, main step ~25–35 yr. The graph François supplied — NRC, *Abrupt Climate Change*, 2002 — is this record.)*

## 2. The Cataclysm Gravure — how it reads, and how to show it

The great relief on the Hall's northern face, struck by the equinox-noon beam (see *Geography & Orientation — canon lock*). It is **doctrine rendered as image**: the two fates of humankind set on either side of the Mountain.

### 2.1 Composition (three zones — the POV faces NORTH, so west = the viewer's LEFT)

*(Orientation lock: the Mountain is due south, so the relief looks out to the north over the oasis. West is therefore the viewer's **left**, east the **right** — and scripture, as amended, reads **water on the left, volcanoes on the right.**)*

- **Centre — THE MOUNTAIN, and NOXIUS.** The Mountain rises singular and closed. Around and within it, **the Five** — the offices, the children of the first morning — each holding **one part** of the whole and no more. This is *Homo Noxius*: the human who survives **because the knowledge is divided, because no one hand holds the whole, because the way is hidden and humble.** The saved are the faithful under the Mountain's law — not the mighty, but the *split*. Show them small, ordered, sheltered, ascending into the stone.
- **LEFT / west — the drowned side — ATLANTIS.** The defiers who **held the whole** — and drowned for it, taken by the **water / the great wave** that comes from the west. This side carries all the cost, and all the clues (§2.2).
- **RIGHT / east — the fire side — the volcanoes.** The Cataclysm's other face: fire, ash, and the breaking earth. The two flanks are the two deaths — **water left, fire right** — and only the divided Mountain between them endures.

### 2.2 The LEFT side (west) — make it *almost too clear* that these drowned are Atlantis

Load the left-hand third (west) with cues a knowing eye cannot miss, while the faithful in-world see only "the proud who were taken":

- **The concentric rings.** A great **ringed city — alternating bands of land and water around a central island.** This is the single loudest tell: it is Plato's Atlantis *and* the Richat's geology in one shape. Carve the rings unmistakably.
- **The Eye.** Render the ring-city as an **eye** — the central island its pupil — so it reads as *the Eye of the Sahara* closing under the wave.
- **Open magnificence.** **Columns, a raised temple, wealth displayed in the open** — everything the Mountain hides, Atlantis flaunts. Their glory is their indictment.
- **The one hand that holds the whole.** At the city's heart, a **single figure — king or priest — grasping the whole light / all the parts in one hand.** This is the sin the Declaration names: *the hand that holds the whole makes of itself a god upon the Earth.* It is *why* they drown. Set it dead-centre of the ring so the eye finds it.
- **The wave, and the fire before it.** Above, **the darkened, wrong-looking sun.** Below, **the great wave** rolling in on one bearing (the south-west flow), the rings going under, figures swept from the terraces, the pupil-island vanishing.

### 2.3 The reading (the doctrine the picture *is*)

The two sides are one argument: **the divided are saved; the whole-holders drown.** Atlantis is not punished for being wicked — it is punished for **gathering the whole into one hand and standing up as a god.** The Mountain survives by refusing exactly that. This is the visual root of the Declaration's law (*"no single hand may ever hold the whole"*) and of the whole Kahir division of knowledge — the gravure is where the doctrine and the history are the same object.

### 2.4 The lighting (tie to the equinox beam)

At noon on the equinox the southern shaft crosses the Hall and **falls on the Mountain and the Five — Noxius lit "as if the first morning returned."** The **drowned left side (west) sits in the unlit lower stone**, where the beam "vanishes." Light blesses the divided; the dark keeps the whole-holders. The one day a year the picture is fully lit is the one day it can be fully *read* — and even then, the faithful read only half of it.
