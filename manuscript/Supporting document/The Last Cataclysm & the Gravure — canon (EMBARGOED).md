## 1. The timeline of the last Cataclysm

*(Real-world anchors in brackets — the truth the Mountain holds and the outside world only half-guesses. The Mountain's account and mainstream science disagree on cause and speed; that disagreement is deliberate and protected.)*

- **~12,900 years ago — the sky-fire.** A fire crosses the heavens; the world cools and staggers. *[Younger Dryas onset; the (contested) Younger Dryas Impact / comet-airburst hypothesis.]* This is the warning the defiers ignore.
- **~9,600 BCE (~11,600 years ago) — the drowning.** The cold breaks all at once; the waters rise and a great wave runs across the world. **The ring-city of the defiers goes under.** *[Younger Dryas termination; abrupt meltwater sea-level surge; Plato's own date for the fall of Atlantis — "9,000 years before Solon."]* The traces it leaves are **oriented on a single bearing (south-west)** — the signature of one directional flow, not the many-directioned scratch of ordinary weather. This is the last Cataclysm.
- **In its wake — the survivors raise the first stones.** The few who kept to the Mountain's way rebuild; the oldest megaliths date to exactly this dawn. *[Göbekli Tepe, ~9,600 BCE — "advanced ancients" at the boundary.]*
- **~11,000–5,000 years ago — the green morning.** The world recovers and turns green, the Sahara with it; the drowned ring lies under water, then under sand. *[The African Humid Period — an episode WITHIN this cycle, not a cataclysm. It carries none of the doctrinal weight; it is only weather.]*
- **~5,000–3,000 BCE — the sand returns and the outsiders multiply.** The desert closes over the Eye and buries the ruin; the first cities rise as the green dies. *[End of the AHP; rise of the first civilizations.]*
- **Now — the outsiders have found the Mountain again.** Their fire in the deep desert, their eyes in orbit, their empire of trade. *[Reggane 1960; satellites; Blackwood.]* By the Mountain's law a Cataclysm comes not on a clock but **when the outsiders find the Mountain** — so the next one is gathering. **[EMBARGOED: this is the sixth; we stand near the end of the fifth.]**

## 2. The Cataclysm Gravure — how it reads, and how to show it

The great relief on the Hall's northern face, struck by the equinox-noon beam (see *Geography & Orientation — canon lock*). It is **doctrine rendered as image**: the two fates of humankind set on either side of the Mountain.

### 2.1 Composition (three zones — the POV faces NORTH, so west = the viewer's LEFT)

*(Orientation lock: the Mountain is due south, so the relief looks out to the north over the oasis. West is therefore the viewer's **left**, east the **right** — and scripture, as amended, reads **water on the left, volcanoes on the right.**)*

- **Centre — THE MOUNTAIN, and NOXIS.** The Mountain rises singular and closed. Around and within it, **the Five** — the offices, the children of the first morning — each holding **one part** of the whole and no more. This is *Homo Noxis*: the human who survives **because the knowledge is divided, because no one hand holds the whole, because the way is hidden and humble.** The saved are the faithful under the Mountain's law — not the mighty, but the *split*. Show them small, ordered, sheltered, ascending into the stone.
- **LEFT / west — the drowned side — ATLANTIS.** The defiers who **held the whole** — and drowned for it, taken by the **water / the great wave** that comes from the west. This side carries all the cost, and all the clues (§2.2).
- **RIGHT / east — the fire side — the volcanoes.** The Cataclysm's other face: fire, ash, and the breaking earth. The two flanks are the two deaths — **water left, fire right** — and only the divided Mountain between them endures.

### 2.2 The LEFT side (west) — make it *almost too clear* that these drowned are Atlantis

Load the left-hand third (west) with cues a knowing eye cannot miss, while the faithful in-world see only "the proud who were taken":

- **The concentric rings.** A great **ringed city — alternating bands of land and water around a central island.** This is the single loudest tell: it is Plato's Atlantis *and* the Richat's geology in one shape. Carve the rings unmistakably.
- **The Eye.** Render the ring-city as an **eye** — the central island its pupil — so it reads as *the Eye of the Sahara* closing under the wave.
- **Open magnificence.** **Columns, a raised temple, wealth displayed in the open** — everything the Mountain hides, Atlantis flaunts. Their glory is their indictment.
- **The one hand that holds the whole.** At the city's heart, a **single figure — king or priest — grasping the whole light / all the parts in one hand.** This is the sin the Declaration names: *the hand that holds the whole makes of itself a god upon the Earth.* It is *why* they drown. Set it dead-centre of the ring so the eye finds it.
- **The wave, and the fire before it.** Above, **the sky-fire / a comet.** Below, **the great wave** rolling in on one bearing (the south-west flow), the rings going under, figures swept from the terraces, the pupil-island vanishing.

### 2.3 The reading (the doctrine the picture *is*)

The two sides are one argument: **the divided are saved; the whole-holders drown.** Atlantis is not punished for being wicked — it is punished for **gathering the whole into one hand and standing up as a god.** The Mountain survives by refusing exactly that. This is the visual root of the Declaration's law (*"no single hand may ever hold the whole"*) and of the whole Kahir division of knowledge — the gravure is where the doctrine and the history are the same object.

### 2.4 The lighting (tie to the equinox beam)

At noon on the equinox the southern shaft crosses the Hall and **falls on the Mountain and the Five — Noxis lit "as if the first morning returned."** The **drowned left side (west) sits in the unlit lower stone**, where the beam "vanishes." Light blesses the divided; the dark keeps the whole-holders. The one day a year the picture is fully lit is the one day it can be fully *read* — and even then, the faithful read only half of it.
