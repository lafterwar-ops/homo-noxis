# Veil's Chamber — the Diffraction Reveal (optics) — canon (Book One)

*Purpose: make the Veil's-Chamber reveal survive a physicist reading it. The emotional logic — Kailan is shown that **"light does what water does,"** that a beam is a **wave**, and that the last lie of Light is the one Light itself cannot suppress — is already excellent. This doc fixes the apparatus so the rings are real: the **red glass**, the **pierced token**, and the numbers that make clean pale-and-dark rings appear on the wall.*

---

## 0. What the reader must physically see

A single focused beam of Light passes through a **finely pierced token** and, instead of landing as one sharp point, opens on the far wall into a **set of concentric pale-and-dark rings** — a bright centre, a dark ring, a fainter ring, and so on. That ring pattern is **diffraction** (the circular-aperture *Airy* pattern): the fingerprint of a wave. Water in a still bowl, tapped, does the same in ripples; Kailan is meant to feel the two as one fact.

---

## 1. Why the red glass is not decoration — it is the whole trick

**White light will NOT make clean rings.** Every colour diffracts to a *different* ring size (ring radius scales with wavelength), so under white light the rings of red, green and violet overlap into a **smeared rainbow blur** — no countable rings, no clean reveal.

**The red glass fixes this.** It passes only a narrow band of deep red and blocks the rest, so the beam is effectively **one wavelength** (*monochromatic*). Now every ring is drawn at a single size: **sharp, dark-bordered, countable.** The red glass is the **monochromator**, and without it the demonstration fails. (This directly answers the beta/fresh-eye worry that the pattern "would not appear as clean rings under sunlight" — under *sunlight* it would not; under *red-filtered* light it does.)

- **Wavelength:** deep red, **λ ≈ 650 nm** (a good, saturated red; the glass passes ~630–670 nm).
- **Bonus:** red is the **longest** visible wavelength → it diffracts the **most** → the biggest, most dramatic rings for a given hole. Red is the correct choice on physics *and* on theatre. (It also rhymes with the red warning-pigment of the deep scripture — keep that resonance.)

---

## 2. The token hole — the correction to "about a millimetre"

**Instinct half-right, number wrong.** The hole must be **much larger than the wavelength** (so it is a clean circular aperture, not a point) — but in absolute terms it must be **small: a fine pinhole, ~0.1–0.2 mm**, *not* ~1 mm. Here is why, with the governing relation.

**Airy geometry.** First dark ring radius on a screen a distance *L* from the hole of diameter *D*:

> **r₁ = 1.22 · λ · L / D**

Take λ = 650 nm and a chamber throw of **L ≈ 6 m** (aperture to far wall):

| Hole D | First-ring radius r₁ at 6 m | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **0.15 mm** | **≈ 3.2 cm** | **hand-sized rings across the wall — the reveal.** |
| 0.10 mm | ≈ 4.8 cm | larger, fewer, dramatic |
| 0.30 mm | ≈ 1.6 cm | modest but clear |
| **1.0 mm** (the guess) | **≈ 4.8 mm** | rings only millimetres wide — you must press your eye to the wall |
| ~0.65 µm (≈ λ) | light sprays into a near-hemisphere | **no rings at all**, and almost no light passes |

**So the operative scale is a pinhole of ~0.15 mm** (≈ 230 wavelengths across — the sweet spot: many times λ, yet small enough to throw the pattern to human size). A 1 mm hole gives real rings, but only ~5 mm wide — too small for the scene. Wavelength-sized holes give a formless glow.

**"The correct size vs. the wavelength," made exact:** the hole is not sized *to* the wavelength; it is sized so that **1.22 λL/D lands the rings at a readable size for the chamber's throw L.** Choose the wall distance first, then the hole follows. For a chamber of a few metres, that is a fraction of a millimetre.

---

## 3. The other requirement: coherence (and how the chamber supplies it)

Clean rings need the light to arrive **coherent** — orderly in phase — in two senses, both already provided:

- **Temporal (colour) coherence** → the **red glass** (§1). One wavelength = waves that stay in step.
- **Spatial coherence** → the **focused beam.** Because the light reaching the token is a **focused/collimated ray** from Light's optics (effectively from a small, distant, point-like source), it is spatially coherent enough to draw the rings. A broad, uncollimated glow would wash them out; a focused beam will not.

This is why the reveal lives in the **Veil's Chamber specifically**: it is the one place with both a **precisely focused beam** *and* the crafted **red glass** and **pierced token** — the last, most advanced instruments of Light. A shepherd with a lamp could never see it; a Veil with the chamber's optics can.

---

## 4. Why the hole must be *crafted*, not punched

The reviewer's phrase "precisely formed circular structures" is exactly right and now has teeth: a **ragged or non-circular hole blurs the rings** (each burr diffracts its own way). Clean Airy rings demand a **truly circular, sharp-edged aperture** — which is why it is a **made Light token**, worked to tolerance, not a hole poked in a card. The precision of the token *is* the sharpness of the reveal. Good: it makes the object precious and the discipline's craft load-bearing.

---

## 5. Optional deepening (only if wanted)

- **Counting rings ⇒ measuring the wave.** Because r₁ = 1.22λL/D, a Veil who knows L and D and *measures* the ring can back out **λ** — Light can literally weigh the colour of light. A quiet way to show the discipline is real physics, not liturgy.
- **The unsuppressible lie.** Mirrors, shadows, refraction can all be *placed* by a hand (Light's other four lessons). Diffraction is the wave asserting itself **around** the hand — the one behaviour Light cannot stage or forbid. That is the thematic reason it sits **last** and in the **Veil's** chamber: the office's final secret is that its own medium obeys a law above the office.
