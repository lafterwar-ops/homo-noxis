# Population, Genetics & Reproduction — canon (§22.4 / §22.5 / §23.2)

*Purpose: make the sealed people **genetically and demographically survivable** across deep time, and tie reproduction to the theme (the Intimate Loss). Sits under the Mass/Energy canon (population **LOCKED 5,000**, fixed carrying capacity) and beside the embargoed **Species canon** (the loss, the ghost lineage, the mtDNA/Y mismatch). Believable to an intelligent non-specialist; bounded speculation flagged. **On the page this is husbandry-of-people that calls itself doctrine — seeded, never stated.***

---

## 1. THE NUMBERS
- **Founding population:** Ravar gathered "the sharpest engineers and scientists of the age, **~10,000–50,000**" (Species/Ravar canon). So the gene pool **started large and diverse** — a whole city's worth of minds, not a handful of survivors.
- **Steady state:** **5,000 sealed residents** (locked), at the fixed carrying capacity of the farmed apron + galleries (Mass/Energy canon), plus a floating margin of field-hands/goatherds at the apron's edge.
- **MVP context (real conservation genetics):** the **50 / 500 / 5,000 heuristic** — ~50 to avoid short-term inbreeding depression, ~500 to retain adaptive variation, **~5,000 for long-term evolutionary viability**. A **randomly-mating** 5,000 would still bleed diversity to drift over 10⁵ yr; a **deliberately-managed** 5,000, founded from 10k–50k, sits at the defensible edge. The Mountain therefore does not leave breeding to chance — and that management is the hidden engine of its whole marriage/office law.

---

## 2. THE DEEP-TIME PROBLEM & THE SOLUTION (managed breeding)
**Problem:** a closed 5,000 over ~11,000 yr since the last reset (and, across cycles, ~145,000 yr) will lose heterozygosity to drift and accumulate inbreeding unless kinship is actively minimised.

**Solution — the studbook hidden inside scripture:**
- **Justice keeps the lineage record** ("the record of sequence… the Key, the Number, the Binding," Covenant) — in truth a **kinship ledger / studbook**: who descends from whom, so pairings can be chosen to **minimise mean kinship** (maximise retained diversity), exactly as conservation programmes and long-isolated human isolates (e.g. managed founder populations) do.
- **Faith supplies the doctrine** that makes people accept assigned or constrained pairing as holy (marriage as rite, not romance).
- **Balance supplies the cap** (below).
- **Result:** a 5,000 population managed for minimum kinship, from a large founding, can hold viable diversity across the ~11k continuous span and, across the cycles, far longer than random mating would. **This is defensible; do not claim it is easy or indefinite.**

> The cycles are **bottlenecks** (only the sealed survive each reset) and *reduce* diversity — so each reset spends some of the founding endowment. Six resets in 145k yr is survivable *because* of the large founding + active management, and *only just*. Part of why the Mountain is a machine winding down (thematic dividend).

## 3. REPRODUCTION CONTROL (population held at the cap)
A fixed carrying capacity means **births must balance deaths** — the population cannot grow. Under Balance's ethos ("what is wasted in peace shall be missing in hunger"), a birth is a **counted cost**:
- **A slot system:** the right to bear is **granted, spaced, and timed** by office — effectively a birth per vacancy, plus managed replacement. Late/【scheduled】childbearing, spacing between births, and permission are the levers.
- **Mechanism = the same three offices:** **Balance** counts the slots against stores/water; **Justice** records lineage & authorises the pairing; **Faith** sanctifies it. No single office controls reproduction whole — the same "no one hand holds the whole" partition.
- **On-page seed (already present):** the **goatherds "who discuss births endlessly"** (World-Life canon) — the animal husbandry is the *visible* rehearsal of the husbandry practised, quietly, on people. K should come to see the parallel **late** (of a piece with runbook-as-faith, map-as-upkeep).

## 4. OFFICE & HEREDITY (earned, not inherited — with a managed aptitude layer)
- **Ascension is EARNED** (Ch1: Va-Elun *proved herself* fit; office is won by aptitude and trial, not birth).
- **But aptitude clusters in lines**, and the breeding-management **selects for office aptitude as well as diversity** — "**Veils bred to office**" (Species canon) means *raised, selected, and — partly — bred* for the disciplines the Mountain needs (Light's eye, Faith's ear, Shield's frame, Balance's patience, Justice's weighing). This is a soft eugenic layer folded into the kinship-management: keep the pool diverse **and** keep the offices supplied.
- Reconciliation: **office is not heritable rank** (a Keeper's child inherits nothing automatically), but the *pool* is shaped so aptitudes persist. Ch1's earned Ascension stands; the shaping is invisible and long-term.

## 5. THE DAUGHTERS — why the control is gendered
The Intimate Loss was specifically the **loss of women** to Sapiens (Species §3). So the reproduction/movement control **falls hardest on women**:
- **The partition, the scheduled movement, the Veil-binding** — "the body that is never quite one's own" (Species §3) — is, at root, the Mountain **guarding its daughters** against the outward bleed that nearly dissolved the species.
- This is the **self-indictment (K's late seeing):** the very rigidity that guards the women is the same rigidity that historically **drove them out**. The Mountain's cure for losing its daughters is the disease that lost them. *Their virtue is its bleeding wound.*

## 6. SPECIES-LEVEL GENETICS (the ghost; the mismatch)
- **Homo Noxius = a Denisovan-grade African "ghost" lineage** — big-brained (Harbin ~1,420 cc), never in the fossil record, detectable in the reader's era only as faint archaic autosomal DNA in the living (Species §1).
- **The mismatch (keep it honest):** living humans carry archaic **autosomal** DNA but **no archaic mtDNA and no archaic Y** — both the maternal and paternal archaic lines **failed to carry through** (Species §6). So even "our blood lives on in them through our daughters" is a **proud story over a sadder biology**: the lines that should have carried it failed.
- **The Mountain = the last un-absorbed remnant** — it carries the **full Noxius genome** intact (the pure line), which is exactly what makes K's DNA place him as the living cousin no one has bones for (Ch5, S-Ch5 fix). The absorption/mismatch is what happened to the *departed*; the Mountain kept the whole.

## 7. BOUNDED SPECULATION / HONEST LIMITS
- **Defensible:** large founding (10k–50k) → managed 5,000; kinship-minimisation extends viability; birth-capping in a fixed-capacity habitat; a Denisovan-grade ghost lineage; the mtDNA/Y mismatch.
- **The leap (bounded):** that a pre-modern order can run an effective millennia-long kinship-management by record and doctrine alone. Grant it as **the Mountain's core competence** (record-keeping *is* Justice's discipline; they have kept a studbook for longer than any culture kept anything), while conceding it is **imperfect** — the pool *is* thinning; the machine *is* winding down.
- **Never claim:** that 5,000 is comfortable forever; that the Mountain gains outside genes routinely (Threshold admissions are rare and **not** for breeding — Sarah, Amastan's line, are guards/liaisons, never studs); that office is inherited rank.
