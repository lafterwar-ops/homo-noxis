# The Mountain — Mass, Energy, Water, Air & the Balance Quarter — canon (Book One)

*Purpose: build the physical machine **before** we write a word of it into the prose (the recurring mistake was the reverse — decorate first, engineer never). This doc is the ledger that must close: where the calories come from, where the water comes from, where the air moves, where the waste goes, where the smell goes, and how the Balance quarter is actually laid out, room by room. It sits under the **Geography lock** (latitude 21.3° N, gates south, Light north, hidden by three ridges) and beside **THE MOUNTAIN AS ORGANISM** (the loops). Where they touch, Geography wins on place, this doc owns the numbers.*

---

## 0. The governing law (and the reviewer's challenge, answered)

**Recycling conserves matter; it cannot make calories.** A closed loop of goats, mushrooms, crickets, fish and compost recovers energy that would otherwise be lost — it never adds new energy. Every calorie a body burns entered the system once, from **outside**, as sunlight fixed by a plant. So the ledger has exactly **two primary inputs**, both ancient and both finite:

1. **The Sun** — captured wherever it can be, and *not* only twice a year at the Festival. Primary calories are grown in **daylight**: partly **outdoors** (the ~600–1000 m Oasis and the terraces), and partly **downstairs**, in mirror-lit galleries fed by sunlight **piped into the rock by Light** — the Oasis *demultiplied* through the Mountain (§1b, §2). What is fixed is the **total sunlight captured**, not where the plants happen to stand.
2. **A fossil aquifer** — a dead sea of ancient groundwater under the desert (the Continental-Intercalaire type), tapped by the spring. It is being **spent, not replenished** (rain here is almost nil). The Mountain drinks a buried ocean that will not be refilled. It is held as **two reserves** — a measured, visible **Life Reserve** and an immense, hidden **Strategic ("Beast") Reserve** known only at Veil level (see §3f).

**One-line resolution of the "the Oasis is too small to feed them" objection:** *the visible Oasis is only part of the farm — by design.* The Oasis you see (now ~600–1000 m across) feeds one to one-and-a-half thousand; the Mountain feeds its five thousand from that **plus** a dispersed apron of terraces, palm-groves and concealed wadi-fields totalling **~2.6 km²**, hidden by the same triple-ridge geometry that hides the green (Ch3: only a narrow south-west sightline sees any of it at all). The interior **conserves and upgrades** that harvest; it does not create it.

**Thematic dividend:** the two things that keep the Mountain alive — the sun's yearly gift and a finite buried sea — are both *borrowed and running down*. Balance is not a metaphor. It is the accountancy of a people living on a fixed inheritance, which is exactly why waste is heresy and why *"what is wasted in peace shall be missing in hunger."*

### 1b. Primary production (the farmed apron)

- **Net food yield, intensive shaded oasis polyculture:** ~1.5–2.0 × 10⁷ kcal / hectare / yr (blended dates + grain + pulses + vegetables; dates alone run higher, ~2–4 × 10⁷, but are not the sole diet).
- **Area required** (at 1.75 × 10⁷ kcal/ha/yr):
  - 3,000 people → **~157 ha ≈ 1.6 km²** (≈ 520 m²/person)
  - 5,000 people → **~260 ha ≈ 2.6 km²** (≈ 520 m²/person)
- **Sanity check:** 520 m²/person sits inside the known band for intensive irrigated oasis subsistence (300–800 m²/person). Good.
- **Visible Oasis:** now ~**600–1000 m** across (K's own rough pacing — "five, six, maybe ten" pitches; "six hundred, or a thousand" steps) ≈ **~30–80 ha** → feeds **~700–1,500 people**, i.e. **~15–30%** of the need. The balance comes from the **demultiplied downstairs galleries** and the terraces, so the open-field **hidden apron shrinks** to whatever is left (a few hundred hectares of *light-capture* equivalent, much of it collectors + galleries rather than open field). *The Oasis's size is author-tunable; the ledger scales with it — a bigger Oasis simply means less to hide downstairs.*
- **The demultiplied Oasis (downstairs):** most primary growing need not sit in open fields. **Light** captures sun at surface collectors and light-wells and **pipes it down by mirror** into tiers of subterranean **growing galleries** — the Oasis replicated through the rock. This hides the farm, uses vertical volume, and makes **Light a food discipline, not only a doctrine.** *Physics honesty:* this **relocates** growing, it does **not** beat the ceiling — total calories stay capped by **total sunlight captured** (collectors + Oasis ≈ the ~2.6 km² equivalent), and mirror losses make the capture footprint, if anything, a touch larger. What shrinks is the tell-tale **open-field** footprint; what grows is disguisable ridge-collectors and invisible downstairs galleries.

**The three tiers (classic oasis stacking — yield *and* water strategy):**
1. **Date palms** overhead — the canopy; highest calorie density, deep-rooted, sun-hardy; also **shades** everything below.
2. **Fruit / nut / oil trees** mid-storey (fig, pomegranate, olive-type oil-seed).
3. **Grain, pulses, vegetables** on the floor, in palm-shade — barley, millet, sorghum, lentils, beans, chickpeas, onion, herbs.
The shade of tier 1 cuts the floor-crops' water loss by ~30–50% (see §1d).

### 1c. The conservation loops (upgrade & rescue — NOT primary)

The Organism loops recover otherwise-lost energy and, crucially, convert cheap plant bulk into **protein, fat and micronutrients**:
- **Goats** — graze the desert margins and crop residue → milk, cheese, whey, meat, hide, sinew, bone, dung (fuel + fertiliser).
- **Insects (crickets/mealworms)** — on grain husk & press-cake → protein at very high feed-conversion.
- **Fish** — on scraps + algae skim → protein; the tanks' green water is itself a crop.
- **Mushrooms** — on spent straw → food from residue that no animal wants.
- **Fermentation** — preserves the glut of harvest against the lean season (the real killer in a fixed system is not the average, it is the **trough**).
- **Compost / bone / ash / cloth** — return nutrients to the tiers (§3b).
**Contribution:** the loops recover perhaps **10–25%** of the food-energy that plain agriculture would lose, and supply most of the **animal protein & micronutrients** — but they sit **on top of** the 1.6 km² of primary production. Remove the apron and the loops starve within weeks.

### 1d. Water

- **Crop water (dominant term):** shaded oasis ET ≈ 1.0–1.5 m/yr over 1.6 km² → **~1.6–2.4 × 10⁶ m³/yr ≈ 4,400–6,600 m³/day.**
- **Domestic water:** 3,000 × ~20 L/day ≈ **60 m³/day** — trivial beside irrigation.
- **Source:** artesian spring off the **fossil aquifer** (structured as the two reserves of §3f); flow on the order of **thousands of m³/day**, gravity-fed to cisterns then distributed. **Finite** — a slow drawdown is the Mountain's deepest quiet vulnerability.
- **Recovery (trims loss, never replaces the aquifer):** greywater → reed-beds → algae → fish → back to the tiers; **condensation on the Mountain's own plants** — dew that gathers on the leaves in the night-chill and **drips inward through seams in the rock**, deliberately caught and never wasted (Ch3's dew-into-the-seam detail — canon); mulch & shade to suppress evaporation.

### 1e. Energy that is not food (light, heat, metal)

| Use | Source |
|---|---|
| **Ambient light, upper/outer halls** | **Mirror-piped daylight** — the Light discipline's real, non-doctrinal job: sun caught at controlled apertures and walked deep by polished mirrors. Cuts lamp-oil demand, ties Light to survival, and — crucially — **grows food downstairs** (§1b, the demultiplied Oasis). |
| **Deep light** | Oil lamps (pressed oil-seed), tallow. |
| **Cooking / kilns / ovens / ferment heat** | Date-palm biomass (fronds, spent trunks), crop straw *(competes with loops — rationed)*, dung-cake, charcoal. |
| **Forge / metallurgy** (mirrors, bronze/iron tools, blades — **low tonnage**) | **A coal seam** in the Mountain — mined and **measured strictly by Balance** ("the coal measurers"). Finite, like the water. |
| **Winter heat** | Coal + biomass + the interior's thermal mass (deep rock is a flywheel). |

### 1f. Air

- **The engine:** the **great pendulum shaft** (crown oculus → deep Hall) is a **chimney**. Warm interior air (bodies, lamps, kitchens, forge) rises and leaves by the oculus; cool dense night-air is drawn in through **low intakes on the shaded north flank**. Forge + kitchens are the strongest draught-drivers.
- **Turnover:** 3,000 people + fires need large, continuous exchange for O₂/CO₂ and smoke; the shaft + vent network + periodic Festival flushing carry it.
- **Direction is doctrine:** everything that stinks or fouls sits **downstream / high on the exhaust side**, so odour and smoke travel **up-and-out**, never back across sleeping quarters (§3c).

## 2. Macro geography — where the machine sits

*(Under the Geography lock: crescent Mountain, gates **south**, Light **north**, hidden by three ridges; only a narrow SW sightline sees green.)*

- **The Sacred Oasis:** now ~**600–1000 m** across (~30–80 ha), the visible green heart — spring, prayer-stones, the holiest tiers, and real cultivation. What outsiders and children mean by "the Oasis." Feeds ~700–1,500; **still not the whole farm.**
- **The Hidden Apron (~1.6–2.6 km²):** the actual farm — **dispersed**, never one open field:
  - **Terraced flanks** climbing the shaded (north/inner) faces of the crescent and the two flanking ridges;
  - **Concealed wadi-fields** in the folds *behind* the ridges (each pocket small, cumulatively large, none visible from any single sightline);
  - **Palm groves** filling the deepest shade nearest the water.
- **The Demultiplied Oasis (downstairs):** tiers of **mirror-lit growing galleries** in the mid and lower levels, fed sunlight piped from the surface collectors by Light — the farm folded into the rock, invisible from without.
- **The Goat range:** the desert margins and the outer ridge-slopes, worked by goatherds who live at the edge.
- **The Sealed Interior:** the mined Mountain proper — the five **quarters** (one per Kahir), living cells, the Hall of Ceremony, the service spines, and, deepest, the **Tomb / the Beast**. Sealed against *sight*, not against *air or traffic*: food and fuel enter through controlled thresholds exactly like prayer-access (counted out, counted back).

## 3. The systems (the flows, named)

### 3a. Water cycle
Aquifer → artesian **spring** → **settling cisterns** (grit drops out) → gravity **distribution channels** → (a) domestic draw, (b) tier irrigation → **greywater** gathered → **reed-bed filtration** → **algae ponds** → **fish tanks** → nutrient water back to tiers → ET loss to sky, partly recaptured by **night condensation** into the rock. *Balance's water-listeners read the channels by ear (canon) to catch a blockage or a theft before it shows.*

### 3b. Nutrient / waste cycle ("nothing has one life")
- **Night-soil + kitchen scrap + spoiled straw →** compost caverns (worms, heat) **→** fertiliser for the tiers.
- **Ash →** lye → **soap + latrine deodorant** + soil sweetener.
- **Bone →** stock, glue, needle-stock, bone-meal.
- **Urine →** collected for **tanning** and **nitre** (leather, and — quietly — the chemistry Shield would rather Balance did not think too hard about).
- **Worn cloth →** bandage → **pulp → paper** (the record-keepers' sheets, Ch10).
- **Press-cake / husk →** insects & fish (§1c).

### 3c. Air & smell
- Intakes **low & cool**, on the shaded flank; exhaust **high**, up the great shaft.
- **Latrines, compost, tanning, fish and the forge** placed **downstream** (exhaust side) so their air is *leaving* the Mountain, never crossing living quarters.
- **Deodorising:** ash/lime over latrines; sealed compost; the constant draught itself is the main defence — a still Mountain would stink, so **ventilation is hygiene**, which is why draught-keepers are a real office.

### 3d. Fuel cycle
Coal seam (Balance-measured) → forge + winter heat; oil-seed → press → lamp oil (+ press-cake to loops); palm biomass + dung + charcoal → ovens/kilns; mirror-piped daylight offsets lamp demand by day.

### 3e. Hygiene & disease
- Washing at every threshold (canon); bathing & laundry in **warmed greywater** before it goes to the reed-beds.
- **Compartmentalisation = a disease firebreak.** The same **Lesser Seal** that contained Kailan (Ch10) can wall a plague into one quarter: bodies named, counted, sealed by Kahir. The Mountain's surveillance has an **epidemiological** logic, not only a political one — one of its genuine virtues sitting inside its cruelty.

### 3f. The two water reserves & the manufactured miracle *(EMBARGOED — Veil-level for the Beast reserve)*

The fossil body under the Mountain is held and known as **two reserves**, not one:

- **The Life Reserve** — the visible, accessible water: the spring, the cisterns, the measured channels. **Balance measures it to the cup**, and the whole population knows it exists and roughly how it stands. This is the water of §1d and §3a.
- **The Strategic Reserve ("the Beast's water")** — an **immense** hidden body of **460,851,200 m³**, its very *existence* **Veil-level knowledge**. Five reduced calibration drops followed, after judgment and activation, by 360 full drops across five days consume **184,340,480 m³ (40%)**, leaving **276,510,720 m³ (60%)**: enough water for one further complete activation while retaining a **92,170,240 m³** severe-shortage floor. Its primary purpose is bound to the Beast (embargoed — see the Beast / Cataclysm canon); but it also serves as the secret backstop of the Life Reserve. The surviving water does not make the fired machine immediately reusable: the deep place is then flooded, broken and cold.

**The manufactured miracle (how the two connect).** In drought or crisis the Life Reserve falls and fear rises. **Faith** calls the people to a **sacrifice to God** — an offering, a penance, a withheld ration, a season of labour. When the sacrifice is made, **God answers**: over the following days the Life Reserve is seen to **refill**, and Faith's sermon names it grace rewarded. In truth **Balance** has quietly opened a hydraulic path — **communicating vessels** — and let the immense Strategic Reserve top up the Life Reserve by nothing more miraculous than hydrostatic pressure seeking its own level. *Faith supplies the meaning; Balance supplies the water; the people supply the belief;* and the Mountain buys obedience with its own hidden sea.

This is the Kahirs' epistemology-as-power in a single machine: a **real, engineered "miracle"** — true in its water, false in its cause — that welds sacrifice, faith and survival into one self-confirming loop. Only Veil level and above know the tank behind the god. It is also the sharpest argument for the founding law: **Balance and Faith must never be one hand,** because a hand that held both the water and the sermon could manufacture God at will.

*Ledger note:* the Strategic Reserve does **not** change the primary-input accounting (it is still *spent, ancient* water, not new) — it changes *who knows it is there* and *how it is spent*. It is the deepest term in the Mountain's finite inheritance.

*Balance governs water, food, stores, resources and the count. Its quarter is the Mountain's **gut and pantry**, sited in the **mid-levels wrapped around the water-court**: downstream of the cool north intakes, upstream of the compost/exhaust, so clean cool air enters over the food rooms and leaves over the foul ones.
