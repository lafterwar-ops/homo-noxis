## 0. Core idea

The five offices — **Light, Faith, Balance, Justice, Shield** — are not merely priesthoods. Each is a **scientific discipline preserved as religion across deep time.** The Mountain is, among other things, an **archive of pre-Cataclysm knowledge**, deliberately **partitioned** so that no one office holds the whole — the same law that guards the Beast guards the knowing. Each office's embroidered **sigil is a discipline-glyph**: a compressed diagram of that discipline's central idea, worn as a holy mark, understood as an ornament, and legible — to a knowing eye, assembled — as a proof.

The doctrine the offices recite over their own separateness — *"Keep the secrets apart; let no one hand hold the whole"* — is therefore true on three floors at once (§4). Above, it is scripture. Below, it is command-and-control. Below that, it is the partition of the sciences themselves.

## 1. The offices as disciplines

### LIGHT ≡ optics *(Locked.)*
Beams, mirrors, shafts, refraction; and, to come, **spectroscopy** — *"angle means salt"* (Ch5's rhyme with Balance). The office keeps **the Mirror** (the wall of 256 beams), the art of **tracing a beam source-to-fall**, and the reckoning of what the sun does across the year. Ch4's account is built on it: the beam-walk, the wide throats, the crew that re-aims the source-mirrors with the seasons, and the crime of **contraband light** — light off the ledger (see the Ch4 canon). Sigil: the discipline-glyph of optics (beam / refraction geometry). *[Discipline locked; the spectroscopy step is Ch5's.]*

### BALANCE ≡ chemistry & metrology *(Locked.)*
Measurement, ledgers, and the water. Balance keeps the accounts of every measured thing and runs the **salt-house**: **sealed distillation** — evaporate the brine in sealed vessels, condense the pure water back (no water wasted in the desert), and **weigh the residual salt/trace against the ledger** — a **gravimetric assay** dressed as a rite. Green robe; its working creed, *"noticing is an office."* This is the office that will rhyme with Light in Ch5: **gravimetry (weigh the salt) against spectroscopy (read the salt by angle)** — two disciplines converging on the same fact from opposite sides. Sigil: the discipline-glyph of measure (balance / retort). *[Discipline locked.]*

### JUSTICE ≡ probability & inference (Bayesian) *(Locked; surface usable now.)*
Justice is the science of judging under uncertainty — **Bayesian inference** — and its **sigil is a visual proof of Bayes' theorem**:

- a **rectangle** — the sample space, *the record*, all cases that have ever been;
- a **square drawn as a diamond** — event **A**, *the case at hand*;
- a **circle** — event **B**, *the conditions* surrounding it;
- the **lens where diamond and circle overlap** — **A ∩ B**, the case seen under its conditions.

The glyph *is* the theorem:

> **P(A | B) = P(B | A) · P(A) / P(B).**

**Sa-Tavan's method (Ch4) is this theorem spoken in prose.** He assembles **the reference class** of all like cases (the **prior**); he reads **what followed each** of them (the **likelihoods**); he **matches the conditions of the present case to theirs and keeps the nearest true one** (conditioning on the evidence — the choice of the right reference class); the result is **"the likeliest"** (the **posterior**). He **judges upon the likeliest, "never upon my liking."** Then he grants **the gentlest ruling the posterior will bear** — *mercy where the record has room; the Mountain first only where both cannot be kept.* Black cord; the tablets; sequence and order are his instruments. The Bayes glyph rides on his robe. *[Discipline locked. This is the one office whose science may show on the surface now — the sigil is on the page and the method states it — provided it is never named as "Bayes," only enacted.]*

## 2. The sigils as discipline-glyphs

Each embroidered mark is a **compressed diagram of its discipline's central idea** — beautiful as ornament, exact as proof. Justice's is the fully worked case (§1): a Bayes diagram anyone can wear and no one in-world reads as mathematics. The others follow the same logic (optical geometry for Light, the balance/measure for Balance, the knotted interval-line for Faith, the perimeter/seal for Shield). The embargoed point: **assembled, the sigils say more than any one alone** — they are the partitioned knowledge rendered as heraldry, and a knowing eye that could set all five side by side would begin to read the whole the doctrine forbids.

## 3. Justice's method, worked (for continuity)

Because the Justice ≡ Bayes surface is usable now, the mapping is set down here so every future Justice scene stays honest to it:

| Bayesian element | Sa-Tavan's words / act |
|---|---|
| Prior — P(A) | assemble the reference class: *all like cases that have ever been* |
| Likelihoods — P(B\|A) | read *what followed each* such case |
| Conditioning on evidence | *match the conditions of this case to theirs; keep the nearest true one* |
| Posterior — P(A\|B) | *"the likeliest"* |
| The verdict rule | *judge upon the likeliest, "never upon my liking"* |
| The mercy rule | *the gentlest ruling the posterior will bear — mercy where the record has room* |
| The hard limit | *the Mountain first, only where both cannot be kept* |

The method reads as devout jurisprudence and *is* inference; the reader who knows the theorem watches Bayes update in a temple.

## 4. The doctrine, read three ways

*"Keep the secrets apart; let no one hand hold the whole"* is true on three floors, and the book descends them in order:

1. **Moral / scriptural (surface).** The oldest law: pride gathered the whole and drowned for it (see *The Last Cataclysm & the Gravure*). Humility keeps the secrets apart. This is all the faithful — and K, in his account — ever hear.
2. **Weapons-custody (the middle floor).** A **distributed permissive-action system.** No single office can assemble or arm the buried device (**the Beast**); custody is split so that only collusion across offices could fire it. The **Light token is a permissive-action link (PAL)** — a *physical key*, not a symbol. This is the same engineering safeguard the midden already fixes as the true reading of *let no hand hold the whole* (nuclear command-and-control; five must collude).
3. **Partition of knowledge (the deepest floor).** Beneath even the weapon: the **sciences themselves are split among the offices**, so that no office can reconstruct the whole of what the pre-Cataclysm world knew. The sigils, being discipline-glyphs, are the tell — **assembled, they say more than any one alone.** The division that guards the Beast also guards the knowing that built it.

## 5. The token (the clue)

An ancient **platinum-group disc** — of the metals K has no name for:

- **paler and colder than silver**, yet **heavier than gold of the same size**;
- **untarnished after uncountable years**, and **unmarkable by any file**;
- **machined with impossible precision** — beyond any hand-tool the Mountain owns;
- pierced by a **central pinhole** and ringed by **many fine, even, concentric circles** — *"like the rings a dropped stone leaves on a still pool."*

**Reader-facing clues (embargoed):**
1. **The rings echo the Richat Structure** — the **Eye of the Sahara = Atlantis, due WEST** (per *Geography & Orientation* and *The Last Cataclysm*). Paul's printed, redacted plate is a **photograph of the real thing** — *"yours is red, mine was pale upon pale"* — the paper image against the platinum master.
2. **It reads toward a PAL / key** — the physical link of the weapons-custody floor (§4.2). The concentric geometry is doing double duty: the Richat rhyme *and* the key.

**In-account:** K **cannot name the metal** (platinum-group is outside his world) and **cannot read the token**; he drops it in water and stumbles on refraction — which has nothing to do with it (the irony: the token is a key and a map, and the boy learns only that water bends light). The reveal of what it *is* comes later. *(For the Ch4 beats and the 2026-room recognition, see the Ch4 canon.)*
