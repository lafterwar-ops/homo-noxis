## 2. ONE-PARAGRAPH OVERVIEW
The Beast is **one vertical water-hammer engine on a five-office constitution.** A measured payload of water falls down a central shaft, through a lower Earth Gate, onto an engineered deep hot-rock interface, and flash-converts to a supercritical/steam pressure pulse driven into the Earth. A **full firing is five such payloads, released one Earth-mode period apart**, tuned to drive the planet's **radial "breathing" free oscillation (₀S₀, T ≈ 20 min 28 s)** in phase; the coherently-built oscillation, focused toward the **antipode**, trips an already-critical crustal ring. The **Mountain supplies only the trigger; the Earth supplies the catastrophe** (Ch9's leverage line). Steam from each stroke reloads the next (regenerative). A **readiness rite** after every Keeper succession runs the *same machine* at a tiny test volume: five low-energy test-strokes, one period apart, and Faith certifies the Earth's **phase-coherent return** — "the Earth answers." Five offices each hold one indispensable piece; **no one hand holds the whole.**

## 3. VERTICAL ARCHITECTURE (one axis)
Top → bottom: **(a) Load Chamber** (normally empty) → **(b) Upper Load Gate** → **(c) central visible Drop Shaft** (the throat) → the **Hall of Cataclysm** wraps the shaft → **(d) Lower Earth Gate** (Shield) → **(e) deep conversion chamber** at **(f) the engineered hot-rock interface.** Water goes **down the centre**; recovered steam pressure returns **outward through five radial channels.** One throat, one fall, one Earth, five hands around it.

## 4. PLAN GEOMETRY (a visible constitution)
Five operator **stations** at the vertices of a **regular pentagon** around the central shaft; their mechanical links form a **five-pointed star.** The five operators **see one another, see each other's hands and engaged controls, and see the central fall.** They are **not** in five sealed rooms at the moment of operation — division is *epistemic/functional/constitutional/procedural*, never an inability to communicate. They **enter by five separate office routes** to their stations (so "the five go apart" stays true in **route, knowledge, and station**) and arrive in **one shared Hall.** The decisive fall is **seen** through **five recessed, shielded observation embrasures** glazed with polished quartz/mineral windows onto the chasm (never unprotected beside an open industrial fall).

## 5. DIMENSIONS & THE GREAT RESERVE
- **Load Chamber (full payload):** **80 m × 80 m × 80 m = 512,000 m³ ≈ 512,000 t** water. Normally **empty**; filled only to prepare a full firing. (Scale is part of the awe — do not casually resize.)
- **Great Water Reserve:** **lateral** to the axis, supported by the Mountain's mass, at/near-below the load-chamber working elevation (NOT suspended above the Hall), protected from accidental direct drainage, **much larger than one load.** The machine lifts **only the current payload**, never the whole reserve.

## 6. COMMUNICATING VESSELS + IMPOSED PRESSURE (raise the payload)
Passive connected vessels cannot raise water above their common level, so the lift is **actively imposed:** the Reserve feeds **five pressure wells / feeder systems**; **steam-driven pistons / hydraulic accumulators / pressure chambers** displace water up **five large communicating risers** into the overhead Load Chamber; once full, the chamber is **isolated** (feeder gates close). Five matching **level indicators** let the five operators watch the columns rise together.

## 7. STEAM-RETURN (regenerative) CYCLE
Each deep stroke: (1) water hits the hot-rock interface → (2) flash/supercritical pressure pulse → (3) **principal seismic energy into the Earth** → (4) a **controlled fraction** of pressure diverted into **five radial return passages** → (5) driving five pistons/lungs → (6) which force the **next** measured payload up from the Reserve → (7) Load Chamber refills within **one Earth period.** *Steam supplies energy, not permission.*

## 8. FIRST-LOAD STARTER
The first payload cannot use steam from a prior stroke. **Solution (locked):** **five great hydraulic accumulators / counterweight-charged pressure reservoirs**, slowly charged before activation by ordinary Mountain labour + the deep heat over a long arming period. First load is raised on **stored energy**; thereafter the cycle is regenerative.

## 9. GATES & INTERLOCKS (never one vague "valve")
- **Upper Load Gate** (at the chamber floor): holds the payload; opens **only after final authorisation**; begins the visible fall; closes after the chamber empties.
- **Lower Earth Gate** (Shield): isolates Hall/loading system from the deep interface; **opens before** the payload arrives, **closes behind the tail**, prevents blowback up the shaft, and **diverts pressure into the five steam-return channels.**
- **Five visible interlocks** at the stations, retracted toward the operators at release. Characters may *say* "valve"; canon always knows **which gate.**

## 10. SEISMIC MODEL (LOCKED)
- **Mode:** the Earth's **radial mode ₀S₀** ("breathing" — whole-sphere expansion/contraction), **period T ≈ 20 min 28 s (≈ 1228 s; PREM ₀S₀ ≈ 1227.5 s).** Excitable by a deep radial pressure source; observed to "ring" for days after great events. Chosen for: deep pressure coupling, radial symmetry (the Mountain's breathing theology), the ~20-min dramatic interval, and the hydraulic refill period.
- **Rejected:** ₀S₂ ("football" mode, ≈ 53.9 min) — poorer coupling to a single deep radial source; retained here only as the rejected candidate. **Never mix the two on the page.**
- **Antipodal propagation (distinct!):** compressional body waves (PKIKP) to the **antipode** take **~20 min** one way. This is the *catastrophe-delivery* path, **not** the "answer."

## 11. "THE EARTH ANSWERS" — ONE MEANING (LOCKED)
The **answer = the phase-coherent return of the ₀S₀ mode at the Mountain after one full period.** Faith's receiving instrument detects the mode coming back **to the expected phase with the expected waveform** — the Earth "breathing back." It is **NOT** a body-wave echo off the far side, a surface wave round the globe, nor a second earthquake. Three distinct things the canon must keep apart:
- **A. Propagation to the antipode** (~20 min, PKIKP) → the *target/catastrophe*.
- **B. Phase return at the Mountain** (one ₀S₀ period ≈ 20.5 min) → *what Faith hears; times the next stroke; proves the pathway is coupled; the Covenant.*
- **C. Catastrophic threshold** → strokes 1–4 build the oscillation **below** the destructive threshold; the **fifth** completes the coherent sequence; after it the operators wait one period for the phase-return **confirmation** and the onset of the antipodal effect.

## 12. FIVE-PULSE TIMELINE (LOCKED)
Pulse 1 @ t=0 · Pulse 2 @ T · Pulse 3 @ 2T · Pulse 4 @ 3T · Pulse 5 @ 4T · **Answer/confirmation @ ~5T.**
With **T ≈ 20:28**, first pulse → fifth pulse ≈ **82 min**; first pulse → answer ≈ **1 h 42 min.** Even spacing (one period) throughout — **including** the fifth-to-answer gap. **Faith times it by reading the Earth's phase, not a clock.** (The evenness is the machine-tell: "a death does not keep an appointment; a machine keeps an appointment," Ch16.)

## 13. VOLUMES (LOCKED)
- **Readiness test payload: 4,096 m³** (a **16 m cube**; 1/125 of a full load; **16 m = 1/5 of the 80 m chamber height** — miniature of the machine). Small enough for **no antipodal destruction**, large enough for a **regional trace** (Reggane, ~40 km, 1960 instruments) and a minute detectable ₀S₀ response in Faith's instrument. Sustainable across countless successions.
- **Full payload: 512,000 m³.** **Full firing: 5 × 512,000 = 2,560,000 m³ ≈ 2.56 × 10⁹ kg.** (Reconcile with the war-reserve, §16.)
- The people still call these "**drops**" — drops *relative to the Beast's full load.*

## 14. ENGINEERING NUMBERS (order-of-magnitude, for prose consistency)
- **Fill (one full load in ~one period, ~1228 s):** 512,000 m³ / 1228 s ≈ **~420 m³/s total**, ≈ **~85 m³/s per riser** (five risers). At ~5 m/s that needs **~4–5 m-diameter risers.** Large but not absurd.
- **Fall/discharge:** choose **~90–120 s** (short vs the 20-min period; leaves time for the lower gate to close behind the tail). 512,000 m³ / ~100 s ≈ **~5,000 m³/s** down the throat → a **~15–25 m throat** at high velocity. Coherent, seeable, evacuable.
- **Lift height:** keep the Reserve free-surface **close in elevation** to the chamber intake (tens of metres, not kilometres) so the imposed-lift energy stays credible. **Never require 5×10⁵ t lifted kilometres every 20 min.**
- **Energy bookkeeping:** the Beast is **grossly inefficient** overall; a **very small fraction** of the deep thermal/pressure event suffices to re-lift the next load (the load's potential energy ≈ ρgVh ~ a few ×10¹² J for tens of m of head — trivial beside the deep event's ~10¹⁵–10¹⁷ J). **Acknowledged, not hidden.**
