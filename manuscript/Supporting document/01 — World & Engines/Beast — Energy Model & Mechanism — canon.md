## 2. ONE-PARAGRAPH OVERVIEW
The Beast is **one vertical water-hammer engine on a five-office constitution.** Before activation, a **readiness calibration** sends five reduced test-drops into a side cavity, one Earth-mode period apart; Faith certifies the phase-coherent answer and Justice then decides whether proceeding is lawful. Only after that ruling, five office confirmations and Kailan's final words — **"Let them fall"** — does a full payload descend through the Lower Earth Gate onto the deep hot-rock interface. Full payloads then continue at Faith's true interval for **five days**, driving a sustained trigger toward an already-critical crustal ring near the **antipode**. The **Mountain supplies only the trigger; the Earth supplies the catastrophe** (Ch9's leverage line). The radial "breathing" mode (₀S₀, T ≈ 20 min 28 s) is diagnostic, not the destructive carrier. Steam from each full drop reloads the next (regenerative). Five offices each hold one indispensable piece; **no one hand holds the whole.**

## 3. VERTICAL ARCHITECTURE (one axis)
Top → bottom: **(a) Load Chamber** (normally empty) → **(b) Upper Load Gate** → **(c) central visible Drop Shaft** (the throat) → the **Hall of Cataclysm** wraps the shaft → **(d) Lower Earth Gate** (Shield) → **(e) deep conversion chamber** at **(f) the engineered hot-rock interface.** Water goes **down the centre**; recovered steam pressure returns **outward through five radial channels.** One throat, one fall, one Earth, five hands around it.

## 4. PLAN GEOMETRY (a visible constitution)
Five operator **stations** at the vertices of a **regular pentagon** around the central shaft; their mechanical links form a **five-pointed star.** The five operators **see one another, see each other's hands and engaged controls, and see the central fall.** They are **not** in five sealed rooms at the moment of operation — division is *epistemic/functional/constitutional/procedural*, never an inability to communicate. They **enter by five separate office routes** to their stations (so "the five go apart" stays true in **route, knowledge, and station**) and arrive in **one shared Hall.** The decisive fall is **seen** through **five recessed, shielded observation embrasures** glazed with polished quartz/mineral windows onto the chasm (never unprotected beside an open industrial fall).

## 5. DIMENSIONS & THE GREAT RESERVE
- **Load Chamber (full payload):** **80 m × 80 m × 80 m = 512,000 m³ ≈ 512,000 t** water. Normally **empty**; filled only to prepare a full firing. (Scale is part of the awe — do not casually resize.)
- **Great Water Reserve:** **lateral** to the axis, supported by the Mountain's mass, at/near-below the load-chamber working elevation (NOT suspended above the Hall), protected from accidental direct drainage, **much larger than one load.** The machine lifts **only the current payload**, never the whole reserve.

## 6. COMMUNICATING VESSELS + IMPOSED PRESSURE (raise the payload)
Passive connected vessels cannot raise water above their common level, so the lift is **actively imposed:** the Reserve feeds **five pressure wells / feeder systems**; **steam-driven pistons / hydraulic accumulators / pressure chambers** displace water up **five large communicating risers** into the overhead Load Chamber; once full, the chamber is **isolated** (feeder gates close). Five matching **level indicators** let the five operators watch the columns rise together.

## 7. STEAM-RETURN (regenerative) CYCLE
Each deep stroke: (1) water hits the hot-rock interface → (2) flash/supercritical pressure pulse → (3) **principal seismic energy into the Earth** → (4) a **controlled fraction** of pressure diverted into **five radial return passages** → (5) driving five pistons/lungs → (6) which force the **next** measured payload up from the Reserve → (7) Load Chamber refills within **one Earth period.** *Steam supplies energy, not permission.*

## 8. FIRST-LOAD STARTER

First load is raised on **stored energy**; thereafter the cycle is regenerative.

## 29.1 What was WRONG (do not resurrect)
1. **₀S₀ cannot be aimed.** ₀S₀ is degree ℓ=0 — spherically symmetric *by definition*, identical radial motion everywhere simultaneously. Aiming it at a target is incoherent.
2. **The stone mirrors were incoherent at every wavelength.** Mantle P-wavelength ≈ 8–10 km; a 5 m slab is λ/2000 (scattering cross-section ~6×10⁻¹⁴); surface figure matters only to ~±800 m, so *"trued to a hair"* was **ten orders of magnitude** finer than physics notices. No crustal reflector steers a ray crossing a **fluid outer core**.
3. **Resonance is closed by Q, not by ambition.** ₀S₀ Q≈5,300 → saturation at N≈Q/π≈**1,750** coherent pulses. Driven perfectly for 25 days (~9×10⁸ m³) the radial amplitude reaches ~**20 nm**. Five strokes ≈ 5×10⁻¹¹ m. Moment ratio to Tohoku ≈ 2×10⁻⁶. **No stroke count rescues it.**
4. **The heat budget was never checked:** ~2.1×10⁶ m³ of rock must be quenched per stroke, but thermal diffusion moves heat only **3.5 cm** in one 1,228 s period. Strokes 2–5 would land on a cold wet face.
