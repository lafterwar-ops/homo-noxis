\begin{center}\small\itshape Blackwood Estate --- the Swiss Alps --- 17 November 1998\end{center}

\ornament

``It is running now,'' I said. ``Begin whenever you like.''\par

He did not begin. He was watching the machine on the table between us, the two reels turning slowly against each other.\par

``It only keeps what we say,'' I said. ``Your kind keep theirs on tablets; this is the same, only faster --- a tablet that writes itself. You need not mind it.''\par

``I do not mind it.'' He looked from the machine to me. ``How many days have I been in this house?''\par

``Four.'' The reels turned between us. ``The account comes first --- all of it --- and the questions after. That is how it is done here.''\par

``Whose way is that?''\par

``The house's.''\par

He let that answer be what it was. ``Four days,'' he said. ``Measured, walked in your gardens, given doctors, fed better than a Keeper eats.'' 
He looked past me --- at the height of the room, the fire, the good wool on the chairs. 
``The work I was sent to is meant to be poor, and hidden, and easily forgotten.''\par

I did not answer that.\par

``And you,'' he said. ``This word your people use --- Mister. Is it a rank? Am I to call you Mister Paul?''\par

``No. Just Paul.'' I said it lightly. ``Only a servant here --- for the purpose of record and sequence.''\par

``Just Paul.'' He said it back slowly, as if setting it somewhere he could find it again.\par

``Begin wherever it begins,'' I said.\par

He was quiet for a long moment.\par

``It begins with a boy who was told he had been chosen,'' he said, ``and believed it, the way you believe the floor under your feet.''\par

And he began.\par
