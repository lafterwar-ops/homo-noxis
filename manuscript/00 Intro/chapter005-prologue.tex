\typeprologue

\begin{center}\small\itshape Blackwood Estate --- the Swiss Alps --- 17 November 1998\end{center}

\ornament

The reels were turning, and I had been instructed to open by saying as little as possible. I have never in my life been much good at that.\par

``The machine is running,'' I told him. ``It keeps what we say and nothing more; you need not perform for it. The date is the seventeenth of November. My name is Paul Morgan --- Mister Paul Morgan, if we are being correct, and we shall come to that. I am told I am not to influence you. Every question you have may be answered afterward. For now I am permitted to give you only a short list of true things, and I mean to keep to it.''\par

He watched the reels and said nothing, which I had been warned to expect.\par

``I read the ancient world at Oxford --- Oxford is a city; you will not have heard of it --- on the purse of the House whose roof is presently over the pair of us. I have worked for that House four years. That is why I have your tongue as well as I do: four years is a long apprenticeship in a language no one else on Earth will admit exists. When they wanted a man to sit across from you and take your account, someone decided he had better be one who did not believe a word of it. A fresh pair of eyes. An opportunity, they told me, to learn something.''\par

I let that stand a moment, then gave him the rest plainly, because I dislike being managed and saw no reason to manage him.\par

``So that we are clear: I am here to prove you do not exist.''\par

He turned from the machine to me --- the first time he had. ``What do you mean?''\par

``When you lived inside your mountain, did you believe in outsiders? In us?''\par

``No.''\par

``Then we begin even. To me you are a rumour with a generous budget. I have disbelieved you for four years without ever meeting you; I do not propose to stop this afternoon merely because you have arrived.''\par

Something in him settled, rather than bristled. ``Mister,'' he said, weighing the word. ``Is it a rank? Am I to call you Mister Paul?''\par

``A courtesy, not a rank --- though where I was raised, the two were easily confused. Paul will serve.''\par

``Paul,'' he said, and set it down somewhere he could find it again.\par

``The account first --- all of it --- and the questions after. The house's order, not mine; left to me I should have done it the other way about.'' I laid my hand flat on the table beside the reels. ``Begin wherever it begins. And I will tell you, without ceremony, each time I do not believe you.''\par

He was quiet a long moment.\par

``It begins with a boy who was told he had been chosen,'' he said, ``and believed it the way you believe the floor beneath your feet.''\par

And --- may my scepticism forgive me --- he began.\par
