\typeprologue

\begin{center}\small\itshape Blackwood Estate --- the Swiss Alps --- 17 November 1998\end{center}

\begin{room}

--- The 17th of November, 1998, nine o'clock, first interview. I have been told by the doctors that you had been given the rest you needed, and that we should begin. We will meet here every morning for the record of your account, then lunch and instruction in the afternoon, with somebody else. If you have any questions during these sessions, you ought to write them down, and answers will be given.\par

--- You speak my tongue very well.\par

--- Thank you. I have been learning it for the past five years.\par

--- Who are you?\par

--- I'm Mister Paul Morgan. I was a student at Oxford University in geology, archaeology and anthropology for three years, and received a scholarship from the Blackwood Foundation to pursue my studies an additional three. I was then hired to join the team here; this was two years ago.\par

--- Mister. Is that a rank? Should I address you as Mister Paul?\par

--- Just Paul.\par

--- Paul. Paul, what does this do?\par

--- It keeps a record of what we say and nothing else; your kind keep theirs on tablets; this is the same, only faster: a tablet that writes itself. You need not mind it. It is there so that my memory doesn't betray the record. And I should say, before we start, that I did not believe your kind existed a week ago. Not really. And to be perfectly frank, I still don't. This is partly the reason why I am the one conducting these interviews. A fresh and sceptical set of eyes and ears, they said.\par

--- My kind. Why would you believe that my kind exists or not?\par

--- When you were in the mountain, did you believe in the Outsiders?\par

--- Partly.\par

--- Then we are even. Begin wherever it begins.\par

\end{room}
