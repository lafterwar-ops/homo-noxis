{\Large\scshape Note on the Assembly}\par
\vspace{0.18in}

\vspace{0.62in}

The account Paul Morgan assembled was closed, boxed, and carried up out of the folds by Sarah Blackwood.\par

The founder's record and the whole of the account she had come for left in her hand, by her bargain with the Mountain; that was the price of letting the enemy walk out alive. But the last pages of this volume --- Sa-Kailan's completion of his own account: the sealing of the Archive, the death of the man who finished the record, the ninetieth day, and the greening of the years after --- were set down after that departure, inside the resealed Mountain, and could not have travelled with her.\par

They were brought out afterward by the road that has carried every other thing across that threshold: the guides of Amastan's line, who have kept the Mountain's crossings for longer than the Mountain has kept a record of them. They were delivered to the house that received her, and appended here in the order Sa-Kailan set them.\par

Nothing was added. Nothing was corrected.\par
