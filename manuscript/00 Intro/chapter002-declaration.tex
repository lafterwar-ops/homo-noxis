\typedeclaration

\begin{declaration}

You were born outside, and you have stood within the Mountain. We have never spoken to your kind as one people speaks to another. We do so now, through you.
For longer than your histories run, we have held what no hand beyond ours may hold, and let the world break against us for the wanting of it. Age upon age, we have answered with fire, with the sea, and with the silence you have learned to expect.

There is a law beneath all our other laws, beneath our offices and the war between us: no single hand may hold the whole. Whoever does may make of himself a god upon the Earth, and there is no peace to be had with a god. That law has kept us, and it has never let us out.

We have gathered these testimonies and placed them in your hands. We still fear what you may do with them. But the one thing left untried, in all the turnings of this, is to give our enemy the whole account of what we have done.

You may keep these pages, destroy them, or set them before those who would have us harmed. We hang no condition upon their reading or passage; a gift so weighted is only a longer leash, and we have done with leashes. To whoever receives them, by your hand or another’s, we say:

Weigh what it has cost us, and what it has cost you, and how many times over. Do not repeat it, or wrath will come upon you once more. We do not require you to kneel. We have never required of you anything but distance. The Desert is ours.
Keep out.
The Mountain.

\end{declaration}
