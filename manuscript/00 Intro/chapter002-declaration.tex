\typedeclaration

To Sarah Blackwood.\par

You are the last of the outside who still remembers what we are, and the first we have ever chosen to answer. Let that be the measure of what has changed.\par

For longer than your histories run we have held what no hand beyond ours may hold, and let the world break against us for the wanting of it, age upon age, 
and never once replied. We do not answer you now with the sea, or the fire, or the silence you have learned to expect from us. 
We answer with the account itself — the whole of how it went, set down in full and carried out to you across every law that forbids the carrying 
— so that you may hold, once, the true shape of the thing your kind has spent itself against.\par

Read it to the end. Weigh what it has cost us, and what it has cost you, and how many times over. Then answer the one question we have left worth asking.\par

We do not require you to kneel. We have never required of you anything but distance. We ask only whether the two of us might, at last, simply stop.\par

The Mountain.\par
