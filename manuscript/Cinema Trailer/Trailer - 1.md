## HOW TO READ THIS PACKET

**Evidence classification (applied to every fact):**
- **[A] Explicit canon** — directly established in the manuscript or the wider canon docs. The packet is overwhelmingly [A].
- **[B] Canon-compatible inference** — follows strongly from established facts; labelled.
- **[C] Production invention / not established** — flagged; never silently promoted to canon. Where canon is silent: **NOT SPECIFIED IN CANON**.

**Spoiler discipline.** Facts a downstream model must *know* but must *not reveal on screen* are tagged **⚑ DO NOT VISUALISE / SPOILER**. The trailer runs on dramatic irony; the deep reveals (145,000-year deep time; six prior world-endings; the founder's identity; the firing of the Beast; the 5,000 sealed alive; Faith's death; the betrayal; Paul's fate; Sarah's true role) stay off-screen.

**Per-shot format** (as requested): 1 Explicit canon · 2 Exact textual evidence · 3 Character continuity · 4 Environment continuity · 5 Safe trailer info · 6 DO NOT VISUALISE/SPOILER · 7 Ambiguities/contradictions · 8 Optional production inference. To avoid bloat, recurring character/location/prop detail lives once in **Appendices A–D**; shots point to the appendix card and add only what is shot-specific.

**Citations.** `chN` = `chapters/chapterN-*.tex`; doc names given in full. Line numbers are approximate (source line, pre-compile).
