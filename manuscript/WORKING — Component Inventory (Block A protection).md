# COMPONENT INVENTORY — protection checklist for the Block A compression pass

*Covers Chapters 1–7 (plus 15/16/18 for Block B). Built by cross-referencing every teaching moment, object, ritual and game seeded early against its later cash-in across Chapters 8–24.*

---

## HEADLINE FINDING

**There are no orphan components in Chapters 1–7.** Every candidate that a word-frequency scan flagged as "early-only" turned out to bridge under a different name. The first act is unusually well integrated: it is not padded with decoration, it is dense with load-bearing plants.

The consequence for the compression pass is precise:

> **Cut texture and repetition *within* components. Never remove a component.**

The chapters score in the mid-80s not because they contain surplus material but because they *explain* their material more than once. That is what comes out.

### The near-miss that proves the rule

`red glass` appears in ch6 and ch7 and nowhere after — it looked like the one safe deletion. It is in fact one of the most load-bearing objects in the book:

> ch6 (monochromatic source for diffraction) → ch14 *"he had taken it from the path of the **red beam**"* → ch20 *"**red** passed through its aperture and laid a thin line across the survey mark"* → ch20 *"The **red line** at Light went out."*

Cutting the red glass would have silently broken the firing. **Verify by concept, never by keyword.**

These exist as complete groups. Removing one member breaks a pattern the book counts on.

**Rule:** prose inside a set may be compressed freely; the count may never change. The 5/6 system is the book's deepest number pattern.

## TIER 3 — GENUINE TEXTURE (safe to trim)

Not components. These are the target.

1. **Second and third demonstrations** of a principle already demonstrated once.
2. **Tour description** — queues, pipes, galleries, corridors named after the infrastructure has been established.
3. **Explanatory dialogue that restates an image** the scene has just delivered.
4. **A second character re-deriving** an inference another character or an action has already established.
5. **Institutional principle restated** by a new speaker in new words.
6. Named for cutting by the evaluators: the **Eris/solids riff** (ch6, "runs long"), the **cup gag** (ch7, "risks cuteness"), the **training-room exercise** that "runs one exercise long" (ch2), the **ecological tour** load (ch3), **queue/pipe texture** (ch4).

## THE TEST (apply per paragraph)

> Does this change **what someone knows**, **what someone can do**, **who trusts whom**, or **what it costs**?

If none of the four — cut, or convert it into an action that does.
If it survives — check the speaker is using their **reserved move**: Paul demands a date/name/source; Sarah prices it; Kailan asserts from the body; Va-Sheva gives minimum lawful content; Ka-Cheryn counts; Ka-Leth opens or closes procedure.

Exposition delivered in the right voice stops reading as exposition. This is why the compression pass and the voice pass are the same operation.

### Lesson from Blocks 2–4: the budget was optimistic

Three chapters in, every one has landed at roughly **a third of its word target** (−85 / −171 / −230 against 350 / 625 / 575). The reason is consistent and worth recording:

> **These chapters are not padded. They are compressed already.** The mid-80s scores come from *structure* — material arriving as arrangement rather than consequence — and structure is fixed by re-attributing who does and says what, which is close to word-neutral.

Do not chase the remaining tonnage by cutting deeper. The deficit is real and it is fine. Ch18 (7,628) is where the absolute word gain actually lives.

### Ch4 — what changed (18 Aug 2026)

**Structural (the score move):** Ka-Cheryn no longer designs Kailan's experiment. She concedes one sentence and one look; every subsequent move — the cracked door, the lamp check, the outer stair, the one-glass workaround with Sela at the door and Oren on the turn — is his. The chapter's own doctrine (*"what survives the test"*, planted p.1, fired on the lawn) is now dramatised in between.

**Cost:** Sela called the outgoing number herself and carried it up the stair. She holds half the proof when he shuts the door on her. The discovery is what costs him the person.

**Consequential fix:** *"Why did Ka-Cheryn help you prove it?"* → *"Why did Ka-Cheryn let you take the measure at all?"* — she permits and withholds now, which is also how she behaves in Trial, Seal, Beast, Vigil, Clock and Whole. Her one character scene finally matches her six later ones.

### Ch7 — what changed (18 Aug 2026)

**The diagnosis:** one evening delivered *seven* curricula — the chain doctrine, the obscene cup, Faith's acoustics, the prism, the memory of light, the Beast, and blackjack — plus four pieces of parting advice. ### Ch18 — what changed (18 Aug 2026)

**The diagnosis:** nine consecutive transits (estate → helicopter → aircraft → strip → vehicles → station → camels → overlook → crawl), each with a full competence display. Va-Elise counts loads; Amastan rejects tins whose caps ring; Jean-Charles counts tripod turns. Nine demonstrations, one message. The fourth through seventh are where the words were.

**Structural (the score move):** Paul opens the chapter promising *"I will set down what I saw in order and **mark what I could not read**"* — and then narrates with clean omniscient competence for twenty pages, which is the one register a man writing under his own stated limits should lack. ## STOP CONDITIONS

Stop cutting a chapter when any of these is true:

- a Tier 1 chain loses a link;
- a Tier 2 set changes count;
- a scene stops being dramatised and becomes summary;
- the world loses material specificity (the bread, the salt, the vents — these are what make the Mountain live).
