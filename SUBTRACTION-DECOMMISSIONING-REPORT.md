## Why the target was not met

Six independent subtraction passes plus my own reading converged on the same conclusion: after this manuscript's prior compression history, the genuinely removable material — second/third explanations, image-to-abstraction translations, rhetorical tics, chapter-end seals, duplicated astonishment, systems-tour-past-purpose — amounts to roughly the ~4,400 words removed here. Beyond that, the remaining bulk is the material the brief explicitly protects: ceremony, ecology, ordinary labour, ritual, humour, the five distinct trial judgements, the discovery sequences, the emotional set-pieces, Sarah's and Zero's deliberate rhetoric, and the {scripture} verse. Reaching 15,000+ by pure deletion would require cutting into those protected zones, which the brief forbids over the quota ("Subtraction, NOT flattening"; the <15,000 allowance applies "if cutting further would visibly damage necessary causality, character or atmosphere").

**Release-candidates** (each touches a protected category; needs an explicit decision to open):
- Ch4 closing lawn scene / Blackwood confession (~1,600 words of eloquent dialogue).
- Ch3–4 interview-frame philosophising (cosmos/Kepler/freedom; cinema/geology) — several hundred words of astonishment and doubt-articulation.
- Ch7 feast animation demos (flip-book/Upfall/zoetrope) collapsible to one.
- Ch9 remaining intermediate astronomy paragraphs.
- Ch14 post-verdict five closing warnings and the Ka-Sarah interview set-piece.
- Ch19–20 the "evacuate"/"negotiate" road exchanges and the firing-aftermath reflection.
- Ch23–24 further verse compression (each remaining cut lands on the offices-naming, the death-count, or the Ithe/Genesis carving).

A follow-up pass releasing 2–4 of these could plausibly reach 8,000–12,000 total; reaching 18,000–25,000 would mean thinning ceremony/discovery the reader's experience depends on.

## Part Two — choice and decommissioning

### VIII. Surviving pre-firing alternative (present)
Added in the prior pass and preserved here (ch19): Paul proposes destroying the mechanism and dying with its working ("Then buy time with it…"); Ka-Raedin gives it honest odds ("It might hold… one chance in five that the working dies with us and never rises"); the council **rejects** it. Narration now reads: "there was one other road — Ka-Raedin's wager — and that they would not take it… whether the refusing of it was the courage the hour demanded or the last and largest cowardice…" The Beast is still fired deliberately; "Let them fall" stands. The reader can name the refused road.

### IX. Kailan not absolved (audited)
The continuation/crime beat (ch25) is intact and now the load-bearing claim: "I broke the continuation. I did not break the crime… I was the eye at the centre of the firing, the hand that read the phase and said the word." He interrupts continuation; he does not retrospectively interrupt the crime. Ch25 opening already states "We had not broken the wheel that night; we had turned it." No absolution added.

### X. Staged decommissioning (integrated into ch25)
**Principle:** separate the Mountain's *life* (water, heat, air, ritual) from the Beast's *fire*. Immediate destruction is impossible because the Beast **is** the Mountain — the same deep water, heat and throat are the population's life-support — so the reset still happens and knowledge divides again.

**First irreversible act — the Severed Phase:** Faith (Ka-Elun) is dead, and she alone carried the *timing of the firing* — the phase / "note beneath the note," the one part of the whole that cannot be reconstructed off the sky (it had to be poured into an Outsider's ear to fire even this time). Justice rules that the tune will **not** be restored to any office of the Mountain. Faith keeps the prayer, calendar and rhythm ("the hymn, none of it that is a fuse"); the firing-craft dies with Ka-Elun inside the Mountain; its one reconstructable fragment rides out **only in Sarah's Outsider Copy**. A future Mountain that means to fire the seventh Beast finds the whole history and the machine "down to the last valve" but is missing the tune — to complete the weapon it must reach out of the desert and ask the world in the open, where the asking is a signature read by whoever keeps Sarah's watch. "Neither hand holds the whole of the fire. The law made to keep a weapon from one man, we turned… to keep a weapon from a Mountain."

Constraints satisfied: no immediate demolition; no single harmless lever; no public enlightenment or full disclosure; offices not abandoned; Sarah not purified; no clean revolution. Not Kailan's control (the fragment is in Sarah's copy). Not a new five-person firing priesthood (a deliberate *absence*). Sarah's copy has the required "necessary but dangerous" relationship (it holds the fragment and exposes reconstruction). It is a **wager**, not a lock (the copy can be lost/seized/sold; a future Faith of terrible gift could re-derive the tune; a desperate Justice could reach out). The generation accepts "a debt that falls due in no lifetime we will answer for." Kailan's claim adjusted accordingly: "we made the first cut."

### XI. Paul's death less predetermined (present)
Added prior pass and preserved (ch25): a real rescue is offered — cut only the core and be brought out "a day clear of the reset, alive" — and Paul **refuses** it, choosing the whole record because a half-cut stone copy "hands the next Zero the gaps." "That is not the machine choosing. It is me." His ordinary life (flat, books, nephew he'd teach to swim, Thursday chess with Urs) is seeded earlier (ch2, ch16) rather than only at the graveside.

## Canon note flagged (not a contradiction, for author's eye)
The Severed Phase means the deep phase-coupling used in the Ascension readiness-rite is what dies with Ka-Elun. The text handles this as "Faith keeps the calendar and rhythm the people live by" — i.e. the Ascension continues as a *rite of succession* but its live coupling to the Beast is the part withheld. A very close reader tracking the ch15/ch20 mechanics of the knock may want one more concrete line reconciling "the succession rite continues" with "the tune is gone"; it is currently carried by the hymn-not-fuse distinction. No hard contradiction; noted for a possible one-sentence clarification.
