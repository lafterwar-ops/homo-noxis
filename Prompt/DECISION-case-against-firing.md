# DECISION NEEDED — §6.7 / §XVII: the case against firing (Ch19 poll)

**Status:** high-value finding + prototype. **NOT executed** — this is the §3.4 high-risk moral climax and involves a genuine artistic choice only the author should make. Ready to execute in one clean insert if the author chooses option B.

## The finding (audited, Ch18–20)
The brief requires: *"Paul gives the only full case against firing,"* and the outside world must be **differentiated**, not a category. The current manuscript does **neither**:
- **No case against firing exists.** All hearing "resistance" is internal survival-logic (Light wants proof; Balance weighs the Mountain's own tomb). The narrator openly declares the absence: Ch19 L115–117 *"whether some voice cried out to stop… There was no debate."*
- **Paul never makes the case.** He is confined to witness/record + the "no single hand" warning (Ch18 L79). At the poll (Ch19 L117) he answers by **lifting his recorder**, not by speaking for the dead.
- **The outside is an abstraction.** "the far skin of the world," "a made winter" — the word *billions* never appears; the far-side dead get no face. Only the *attackers* are differentiated. (The lone exception, Sarah's "That was Geneva… Geneva is gone," Ch20 L37, lands after the decision and names a lost *signal*, not people.)

## The tension (why this is a decision, not a bug-fix)
The manuscript's "There was no debate" is a **deliberate, powerful choice**: the horror is the smooth, lawful, unprotested apocalypse — "five decent people doing the one unforgivable thing in good order, by law" (Ch18 L89). Simply inserting Paul's dissent could *undercut* that. Per §3.1, a developed scene can prove an old brief-intent artistically wrong. So the author must choose:

- **Option A — keep as-is.** The absence of dissent IS the statement. Accept that the brief's "Paul gives the case against firing" was superseded by a stronger effect. (Then update the brief/§XVII note so it's a recorded decision, not an unmet requirement.)
- **Option B — realize the brief, reconciled.** Paul, whose lawful office is *only to record*, puts one objection **on the record** — which changes nothing and is answered by no one. This **preserves** "no debate among the deciders" (it is a witness statement, not a debate) while giving the book its missing moral counterweight and one differentiated far-side face. Arguably makes the scene *stronger*: the one person present who belongs to the dying world says the cost aloud, and the lawful machine lets it fall anyway.

## Prototype for Option B (author to refine wording — this is the moral fulcrum)
At Ch19 L117, (a) tighten the opening clause to locate "no debate" among the deciders, and (b) give Paul a spoken objection before/where he now lifts the machine. Draft, kept in Paul's restrained fact-checker register (he "keeps a rule against reaching for grandeur"):

> **L117 opening, reframed:** "There was no debate among the five of us who were four. We had done all our debating in a warm room and a sealed hall and a lifetime each of coming to this…"
>
> **Paul's beat (replacing/expanding "held his little machine up a fraction…"):** *"And Paul spoke once, to the machine and to no one, because recording was the only act his office allowed him and the one his conscience would not let him skip. He did not raise his voice. `Let it be on the reel,' he said, `that an outsider was in the room, and that he said the far side is full of people who never heard the word Mountain — harbours, wards, children going to sleep under a sky that is about to be aimed at them, and not one of them came looking for you. I cannot stop this and was not brought here to stop it. I only will not have the record say the cost was never named.' No office was required to answer him, and none did."*

Then the existing K line — *"``Let it fall,'' I said."* — lands after an objection that has been heard and refused, not merely omitted. The "no single hand" theme and the firing's inevitability (L113: it is already committed the moment the drop falls) are untouched: Paul decides nothing.
