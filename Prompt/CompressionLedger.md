## Main categories of cuts
- **After-meaning tails** ("I understood…", "The truth was…", "It was not X, it was Y", "In that moment…") — kept the strongest single articulation, deleted restatements.
- **Repeated interpretation / duplicated emotional conclusions / second-and-third explanations** of the same idea.
- **Antithesis saturation** ("not X but Y") reduced toward essential distinctions only.
- **Aphoristic paragraph-landing verdicts** on ordinary paragraphs — ended earlier on image/action/consequence/silence.
- **Dialogue padding / completed-essay speeches.**
- Stacked abstractions, redundant adjectives, explanatory em-dashes, "thing"→precise noun, nonessential "whole".
