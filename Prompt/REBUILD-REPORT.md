## 2. The causal chain (spine)

1. The Mountain's founding law forbids any single hand from holding operational **control** of the Beast — not its understanding. Five offices (Balance, Light, Faith, Justice, Shield), each locally rational, together produce one globally monstrous capacity. *(§II)*
2. Sarah Blackwood proves the wall is down (Ch11–13). She and the Mountain enter Horizon by **mutual coercion** — each the other's only problem and only answer. *(§VII)*
3. Jean-Charles, a French-service operator, has courted Va-Sheva **as an operation**; his betrayal is the internal leak. "The enemy reached the Mountain through a hand it had won by love." *(§VIII)* — the exact mirror of Zero↔Ithe.
4. The enemy reaches the gate. Justice cannot rule alone or by five (founding law), so it opens the **Threshold Exception** and admits Paul as witness: "A hand may act. A witness may only record." Each Keeper deposes a fragment; Paul becomes the tongue of the record. *(§VI)*
5. The Beast fires — five offices in concert, **no single hand**: Shield keeps the token, Justice the word, Balance the valves. Resonance amplification (not a single stroke) does the work. *(§II/§V, §XIII science)*
6. Va-Sheva discovers Jean-Charles's breach and **refuses to open him / measure it** — chooses the man over the verdict. The anti-Zero move. *(§XX mirror)*
7. In the Archive, K reads Zero: the founder resisted his father's war, the father was **murdered**, Ithe was the enemy's instrument, "You were right" — and Zero's true crime was **over-extrapolation and the refusal to test his certainty**. *(§IX)* Not "a woman rejected a man, therefore apocalypse" (§XV anti-goal, explicitly avoided).
8. Paul dies sealed in the Archive completing the record — **afraid, not grateful; voluntary**; K seals it at Paul's request (anti-Zero mirror). *(§VI)*
9. The Mountain **resets and re-conceals**. Faith proclaims providence (theology left open). Two copies exist; Sarah receives the Outsider Copy and leaves **because the Mountain lets her go** (mirror of her entry). The Pendulum swings again. Truth escaped once. The reader is never told whether it was enough. *(§XVI)*

## 4. Canon decisions locked this rebuild

- **Age / cycles (§XIII):** founding **~145,000 years** — anchored to the Harbin/"Dragon Man" Denisovan horizon (≥146k, DNA-confirmed 2025); the oldest *evidence-anchored* figure, and inside the 800k-yr EPICA ice-core record so the year-clock is cross-checkable. **Six cycles** retained after examination (~26k mean spacing). Most recent prior firing ~13k, **left unnamed** (Younger Dryas connectable, not claimed). **Restraint beat** added on-page (Ch21): the cycles explain a handful of otherwise-unexplained cold centuries only — **not** the secret cause of all prehistory. Antipodal focusing confirmed real-but-modest → the book's **resonance** mechanism is the scientifically correct choice (Ch19 already frames it so).
- **No single hand (§II/§V):** the law forbids operational control, not understanding; five locally-rational offices → one globally monstrous outcome.
- **The recovered-manuscript form (Outsider Copy):** two copies, one engraved (Mountain), one given to Sarah; the book the reader holds is Sarah's.
