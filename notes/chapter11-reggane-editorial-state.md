## Governing dramatic question

“Reggane” is not fundamentally about whether Sarah can prove that she discovered the Mountain. It asks whether discovery creates responsibility or merely offers a more flattering name for possession.

- **Sarah's claim:** I heard you, understood the danger and eventually protected you.
- **Kailan's charge:** You hunted us, entered us and converted restraint into a demand for gratitude.
- **The chapter's answer:** both accounts are true, and neither makes Sarah innocent.

This is the local expression of the book's larger question identified independently in Passes 37 and 38: can any custodian hold power without turning responsibility into ownership?

## Locked architecture

### 1. The witness takes the room

Sarah introduces the original trace, establishes the terms of testimony and makes Kailan the only internal contradictor. Paul admits his own evidentiary manipulation. The translated five-stroke sequence gives Kailan bodily recognition before explanation.

**Function:** transfer narrative authority to Sarah without allowing her an uncontested scripture.

### 2. Reggane: attention becomes theft

The bomb supplies the overwhelming expected signal. Moussa makes the supposedly empty southern sector human and worth listening to. Sarah looks south, saves the recorder, bleeds on the trace, recognises the five timed disturbances and conceals the paper. Moussa refuses to certify her lie and pays administratively for it.

**Function:** Sarah's discovery begins simultaneously as scientific attention, colonial correction, possession and betrayal.

### 3. First cross-examination

Paul tests the ordinary mechanical alternative. Kailan supplies the meaning Sarah lacked: Shield called the readiness sequence because the unprecedented bomb frightened the Mountain into sealing and proving its offices.

**Function:** preserve the difference between observing a pattern and knowing what it means. Hidden knowledge makes fourteen years of disciplined uncertainty appear slow only in retrospect.

### 4. Replication, falsification and duration

Sarah attempts to reproduce faults, consults other stations, preserves Moussa's maintenance slip, ritualises the paper, partly confesses to Étienne and receives the second event after Rubis. Replication changes anomaly into deliberate machinery. Later sequences become an unknowingly compiled calendar of Mountain deaths.

**Function:** make discovery an earned proof rather than intuition; show that intimacy with evidence can become appetite.

### 5. The listening network and false mountain

Scientific and commercial stations form honest individual searches hiding one dishonest total search. Hamid prevents Sarah's obsession from becoming physical heroism. The volcanic ridge proves a lens rather than the source; failure supplies the correction that contracts the search.

**Function:** embody fourteen years, show Sarah can be wrong, create the embryonic architecture of Horizon and convert Blackwood's ordinary business into epistemic machinery.

### 6. Edward's cellar and the human perimeter

Sarah breaks the black-tree seal, assembles individually reasonable failures into a ring and gives Andrew Kerr's collapse a voice. Edward's repeated marginal name converts geography into a person: Amastan.

**Function:** change the problem from “where is the machine?” to “who maintains the human boundary around it?”

### 7. Second cross-examination

Kailan names Sarah's method as Light's construction: separated truths assembled by one hidden holder. Sarah accepts that the separation improved both evidence and exploitation. Martinez is deferred until she has inherited the channel.

**Function:** connect outside intelligence practice to the Mountain's divided-knowledge architecture without falsely equating their moral positions.

### 8. Étienne, inheritance and Martinez

The marriage is hollowed out room by room. Étienne identifies Sarah's practice of giving the true sentence adjacent to the withheld one. His deathbed ball-bearing scene turns the apparently innocent initiating hand into an accusation. At the funeral, elder Martinez offers three channels that have not yet been told to close. Seven years of refusals finally produce the first road in 1974.

**Function:** make access personal, compromised and inherited; show that the search's preliminaries are already the crime.

### 9. Present-day operation and reciprocal confession

Paul reveals that he followed Roger from the disconnected camera behind the glass and watched an unknown older soldier collect its removable memory. Sarah confirms that the house's wired cameras are hers but the second camera and dead-drop route are not. All prior interviews must therefore be treated as compromised. Closing the eye cannot recover them; touching it or confronting Roger would warn the unknown recipient. Sarah authorises exactly one final collection cycle, marked with a unique false fact embedded naturally in her second-road evidence. Kailan prevents her from repeating the fiction of empty land. Paul relocates the trap from human geography to a stored core box. The camera will then be destroyed whether the bait produces movement or not.

**Function:** form the trio for the first time: Sarah sees the intelligence opportunity, Kailan sees the human danger, and Paul makes the objection operational.

The revised sequence is now causal on first reading: camera memory, dead drop, unknown collector, all prior testimony presumed lost, decision not to alert the unknown penetration, one final exposure, Kailan's human objection, Paul's stored-core solution and mandatory closure after the next memory change.

## Open problems, in priority order

### P0 — Chapter 11 / Chapter 13 / timeline chronology conflict

The current Chapter 11 says:

- the search begins at Reggane in 1960;
- elder Martinez spends seven years bringing refusals;
- in the fourteenth year, 1974, he provides a card that makes Sarah's first drivable route;
- the dismantled car belongs to a second crossing, whose story is deferred.

Chapter 13 currently says:

- Martinez takes two years to put a face and tent to Amastan;
- Sarah then waits another year for an invitation;
- the named Amastan is a descendant of an older Amastan who resisted Edward;
- this first approach leads to Ka-Xhian and the birth of Horizon.

The governing chronology ledger says Ka-Xhian recruits Sarah and Horizon is born in 1960, directly at Reggane.

All three cannot be true simultaneously. The chapter-level dramatic logic strongly favours the current Chapter 11 date of 1974: Sarah explicitly spends fourteen years earning access, and the photograph is dated 1974. Therefore the likely resolution is:

1. keep 1960 as detection and Ka-Xhian's blinding/readiness event;
2. keep 1974 as Sarah's first admitted contact and the origin of Horizon;
3. revise Chapter 13's “two years + one year” language so it describes stages within the already established seven-year Martinez search, or remove it;
4. decide whether Edward's Amastan and Sarah's Amastan are the same man or successive men, then make Chapters 11 and 13 agree;
5. update the chronology ledger, which is currently stale on Horizon's 1960 birth.

This must be resolved before polishing Chapter 11 because a sentence-level edit cannot repair incompatible histories.

### P2 — Paul voice differentiation

Paul's structural function is excellent: he confesses evidentiary unfairness, asks the ordinary alternative, produces the core-box solution and returns the record to reciprocal testimony. His language is less exclusive. Some lines could be reassigned to Sarah without detection.

His register should be:

- dry rather than ceremonial;
- empirical rather than juridical;
- mechanism-first rather than morally aphoristic;
- occasionally understated enough to puncture Sarah and Kailan's elevated formulations.

One or two revised lines are enough. Do not add a comic routine or reopen the rejected theological exchange.

### P3 — Shared aphoristic house style

The chapter contains many excellent reversals. Their accumulation makes Sarah sound closer to Kailan and to the novel's general editorial hand.

Likely audit targets include:

- “Exact spacing is a clock.”
- “Certainty did not grow. Only intimacy did.”
- “I had spent a season approaching a mirror.”
- “The false mountain gave me the correction for the true one.”
- “These were not preliminaries to the crime. They were the crime in its patient form.”

The ruling is not to flatten all five. Keep Sarah's legal, evidentiary, commercial and accounting formulations. Question the spatial/religious formulations that sound most like Kailan, especially “approaching a mirror.” Usually the image or the interpretation is sufficient; not every image needs both an aphorism and an explanation.

### P4 — Embodied age

Sarah's “At ninety-three” statement successfully prevents temporal ambiguity, but her body then largely vanishes inside a long, perfectly controlled performance. A hearing-loss subplot is unnecessary. A single present-room physical cost—handling the trace, recovering after the translated vibration, requiring Paul to perform a small action—could retain age without weakening authority.

Only add this if it replaces an existing beat. Pass 37's larger concern about Sarah's later desert/siege endurance cannot be solved entirely inside Chapter 11.
