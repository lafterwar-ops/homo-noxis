## Governing ruling

Chapter 3 is structurally sound. Its governing movement is the conversion of sight
into evidence and evidence into custody: the modern film invites Kailan to test what
he is shown; the controlled route grants him an open sky without granting freedom;
the Oasis contradicts the small sacred image through observed work; the flash creates
an unresolved trace; and Shield closes what the boy's sight opened without deciding
what caused it. The ending properly leaves the older teller able to recognise the
boy's vanity without invalidating his felt revelation.

The compatible minimum-sufficient production package is five local interventions:
one oral-unit break, one chronology clarification, one count-syntax repair, one
tally-source anchor, and one redundant interpretive-gloss deletion. It adds no scene,
event, person, cause, diagnosis, or canon. The geography rewrite and the unidentified
speaker at the first `Good` are deferred because the traces do not supply one
materially safe choice.

   expendable only if compression is needed. The authoritative baseline is already
   within band, and the beat contributes to the collective freeze. **Resolution:
   reject deletion from the minimum package.**
