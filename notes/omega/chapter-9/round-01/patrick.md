### Cast and playable vocal identity

- **Va-Kailan, teller / remembered Veil of Light:** addresses the interviewer with exact procedural memory. His speech breath is cumulative when trapped (`candidate sequences ... rosters ... requests ... accounts`) and radically clipped when he reaches a testable limit (`I wrote the reversals and left the cause blank`). His tactics are counting, qualifying, refusing, demonstrating, and finally allowing an object or silence to say what he cannot. His vocabulary makes office, light, measurement, grief, and bodily cost collide without decorative mannerism.
- **The interviewer:** maintains interview protocol through date and count, answers the historical question without defensiveness, then pursues the visible smile. The final `Let us continue` accepts Va-Kailan's refusal to explain and restores his testimony rather than narrating over it.
- **Ka-Raedin:** changes people through assignment, precise permission, physical demonstration, direct questions, institutional oral memory, and dry humour. He can speak at length because each long speech changes tactic: diagnose, count, absolve, command; or warn, name, place an object, and require a drink. His body repeatedly completes the sentence: sitting by the basin, wringing the cloth, pouring cups, nesting them, putting a hand on stone, turning at a threshold.
- **Sela:** has no spoken line here. Her objective is nonetheless playable: preserve a private human relation under rank. She uses two fingers on the dish, one breath of eye contact, formal withdrawal, then the lamp striking stone. The replacement attendant and Va-Kailan's failure to call Sela back provide the resistance and defeat.
- **Va-Sheva and the former peers:** use formal silence, distance, salute, and onward movement. They are witnessed by Va-Kailan and never granted interior states he could not know.
- **Syphiron:** is physically absent and dead in this chapter. He is present only through Va-Kailan's witnessed memory, remembered words, and named grief. `I heard Syphiron's voice in the words` is Va-Kailan identifying a remembered cadence, not access to Syphiron's mind.
- **Sa-Tavan:** performs bounded competence. He stands still, makes Va-Kailan's fingers repeat the proof, refuses a reason he was not given, and ends at the exact edge of his commission. His syntax is declarative and segmented; `That is the whole of it` and `no more` are tactical borders.
- **Ka-Leth:** does not speak. Waiting, closing the door, applying no seal, and asking nothing are sufficient action.
- **Shield, Balance, Faith, Light, and the correcting voices:** in Va-Kailan's opening compression these are office/institution labels for routines he directly witnessed or administered. They are not free-floating omniscient agents.
