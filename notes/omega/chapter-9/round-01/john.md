## Executive diagnosis

Chapter 9 works. Its soul is not the astronomy or the geometry by themselves, but Kailan's attempt to recover his love of light without resolving the moral horror of the office that has appropriated that love. The chapter begins after Syphiron's martyrdom with a world that refuses to register the loss. It converts Kailan's promotion into social and emotional deprivation, lets his refusal of the curriculum become paralysis rather than heroic escape, and then gives him a year in which grief, beauty, method, and companionship can coexist. The final movement does not pretend the knife has become acceptable. It returns Kailan to inquiry while preserving his fear that Ka-Raedin's warmth and laughter may be another link in the same lethal chain.

The fundamental dramatic question is therefore: **Can Kailan choose knowledge and love again without that choice becoming consent to the sacrificial succession?** The chapter answers only the first half. He recovers the desire to know; he does not solve the knife. That partial answer is the correct dramatic engine. It makes the observatory restorative without making it an absolution of Light.

The strongest reading experience is the progression from institutional numbness, through the basin collapse, into the cups and the uninterrupted sky. The ordinary details do much of the emotional work: the fourth correction in a teaching hall, Sela's fingers on the oil dish, the flat obedience given to an office, the wet cloth, the uneven stool, the black ring around Kailan's eye. These keep the chapter inhabited while it carries unusually large scientific content.

The principal pressure is the middle discovery sequence. Within one year Kailan records the celestial pole, the fixed-star lattice, five wanderers, lunar phases, telescopic multiplicity, Jupiter and its four lights, the Milky Way's stars, the near equality of solar and lunar angular widths, a spherical Earth from eclipse geometry, a failed parallax attempt, the difference between solar and sidereal returns, and a revised conception of darkness. The sequence is causally and epistemically careful, but its density briefly risks reading as an achievement ledger rather than a lived recovery. The text already supplies the right counterweights—failed aimings, instrument work, missed meals, sleep beside the mounting, Ka-Raedin's monthly inspections, shared laughter, and Kailan's renewed fear—so the evidence does not justify structural invention. The correct arbitration is preservation with, at most, bounded compression of explanatory overlap if production later requires it.

## Reading-experience trace

1. **Interview-room provocation.** The question about Pythagoras establishes the human chronology and the chapter's irony before the proof appears. “Your kind is late” turns a familiar theorem into evidence that this enclosed culture reached part of the same knowledge by another route.
2. **The world continues after martyrdom.** Kailan waits for the Mountain to feel different, but water, instruction, bars, records, faith, injury, age, and death continue on schedule. The absence of institutional disturbance makes his private disturbance sharper.
3. **Promotion becomes estrangement.** The succession record, the northern halls, the rosters, accounts, quarrels, and candidate judgments show the Veil's administrative reality. Sela, the other candidates, and Va-Sheva each enact a different social loss caused by rank.
4. **The curriculum becomes the knife.** The wave-wall lesson is not a neutral science lesson to Kailan. He sees every “next thing” as one causal step toward killing Ka-Raedin. His refusal is specific, morally lucid, and ineffective at freeing him from the office.
5. **Competence hollows out.** Kailan continues every task correctly while desire disappears. The basin scene converts an interior condition into a visible failed action and lets Ka-Raedin intervene at a credible threshold.
6. **The institution contains a private language of injury.** “Ascension amnesia” is transmitted mouth to ear rather than entered in the record. Ka-Raedin's cure is not a proposition but material permission: release from duties, sleep, wasted time, and no demand to recover on command.
7. **The hidden observatory joins grief to beauty.** The climb makes the gift physical. The cups make Syphiron speakable. Kailan and Ka-Raedin grieve face to face before any scientific restoration begins.
8. **Wonder becomes method again.** Kailan first looks, then measures. The exact order matters: beauty precedes utility, but his trained habits make inquiry inevitable once desire returns.
9. **The cure remains morally dangerous.** Ka-Raedin's laughter revives the fear of succession. The chapter refuses the easy equation “wonder heals, therefore the office is good.”
10. **The year produces a true but incomplete answer.** Kailan loves light more because it crosses greater darkness. Ka-Raedin answers with inherited grief, fragile cups, and the future knife rather than reassurance.
11. **Knowledge is compartmentalised across offices.** Justice can give Kailan the geometric shape without knowing its astronomical use. Sa-Tavan's lesson is deliberately elementary in his institution and transformative in Kailan's.
12. **The chapter ends on a problem, not an explanation.** Star, unit stick, angle, two shadows, and Pendulum have been assembled. Kailan must state the question before receiving the next teaching. The reader is carried forward by active synthesis.

11. Kailan's renewed desire makes him capable of receiving further teaching, while his fear of the knife remains causally live.

## Room and narration-provenance map

The chapter passes the no-omniscience lock.

- The opening is an explicit `room` exchange. Its speakers inherit their identities and conversational roles from the established interview frame. Nothing inside it requires an external observer.
- After `\end{room}`, the established speaker continues in first-person sustained testimony. The absence of further room markers does not create an authorial camera.
- Institutional routines are things Kailan personally lived among and administered. He reports observable behaviour—voices sharpening, people moving to walls, formal greetings, rosters and accounts—not other people's unspoken interiors.
- Sela's smile, pause, lowered eyes, and struck lamp are described as visible actions. Kailan does not state what she secretly felt.
- Ka-Raedin's history of “ascension amnesia” is quoted speech and therefore has an explicit oral source.
- The observatory's restricted status is attributed to Ka-Raedin. The description of its route and instruments is Kailan's perception and memory.
- Ka-Raedin's love for Syphiron is not inferred; Kailan asks and Ka-Raedin answers “Yes.”
- The astronomical claims are Kailan's observations and explicitly bounded inferences. He distinguishes seeing, measuring, taking something for a model, failing to measure, and leaving causes blank.
- Ka-Noroth's life and the cups' origin are not omniscient history. Ka-Raedin says that Ka-Sarem told him, making the source chain recoverable within the testimony.
- Sa-Tavan's knowledge boundary is shown through what he says he was told and what he was not told. No hidden intention is assigned to him.
- The ending remains Kailan's direct memory: “I sat,” “I said,” and “watching.” It is continuous testimony, not an exterior camera looking at the Pendulum.

Minor source pressure exists only at the ordinary level of remembered shorthand: “The Mountain is a cone” and spatial labels such as “near its top” rely on Kailan's learned structural knowledge. They are not claims about another mind or unseen action and do not warrant intervention.

## Voice map

- Notices light, darkness, spatial relation, bodily strain, ritual position, and moral cost.

- Teaches by withholding the next answer until Kailan can make the question.
- Combines accounting precision, institutional authority, dry physical humour, and unsentimental tenderness.
- His care is practical: remove halls, enforce sleep, check shutters, correct loose marks, take hot glass away.
- He does not deny the knife. His year-end speech intensifies rather than resolves it.

- Speaks in formal, economical proof steps.
- Does only what was assigned, states his information boundary, returns the objects, and resumes order.

### Interview interlocutor

- Uses chronology and named human history to probe Kailan's account.
- Kailan's smile and deflection carry more character than an explanatory response would.

Voice differentiation is strong. The only point withheld from full marks is that Kailan's polished scientific exposition and Ka-Raedin's polished teaching sometimes share the manuscript's aphoristic compression. Their noticed categories and decisions remain distinct, so this is a pressure, not a voice failure.
