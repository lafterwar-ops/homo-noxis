## Phase 1 — Artifact-only first read

I did not begin with a causal synopsis. The artifact presented the following matter and forms.

### Materials, tools, and substances

Water arrives by measure, green dye marks loss and use, oil sits in a small dish, and cloth appears on a wrist, in an attendant's hand, suspended above a black basin, wet in the basin, wrung over it, laid across its rim, and later wrapped around two cups. Stone appears as wall, bars, falling seal, handholds, stair, chimney, floor, bowl, cut wedges, fitted squares, and the cold surface Ka-Raedin touches. Glass appears in Mirror, dome, long instrument, lenses, smoked filter, and eyepiece. Light is carried by lamps, narrowed to beams, split into interference bars, gathered by lenses, coloured red or blue-white, and absent from measured fields. Ink, slate, charcoal, waxed cord, bolts, brass circles, clay, and a water clock make knowledge a matter of hands.

Food and bodily maintenance are sparse but present: measured water, meals carried to or taken on the stair, sleep displaced into daylight or beside the mounting, cold entering fingers and shoulders, breath arrested in the chimney, a black ring pressed around an eye, tears drying on uncovered faces, and age making a painter's hands unfit for the brush.

### Sounds and gestures

Children recite badly. Correction remains level through the third mistake and sharpens on the fourth. A lamp strikes stone. Fist meets breast. A voice breaks on the second syllable of a dead man's name. Ka-Raedin laughs until he must sit. He and Kailan weep without covering their faces. Cups are raised, set down, nested, separated, wrapped, and returned to a robe. Wedges are rearranged until the fingers know the equivalence. The Pendulum crosses its mark back and forth.

### Thresholds and spatial forms

The chapter begins inside the dated interview room, crosses halls, a last northern passage, a hand-climbed shaft, a narrowing chimney, the Mountain's open crown, a Justice schoolroom, a closing unsealed door, and the threshold of the Hall of Ceremony. Its repeated shapes are rims, bowls, circles, spheres, discs, frames, trays, squares, shadows, rings, and orbits. These shapes alternately contain, measure, exclude, and fail to contain.

### Documents, rites, and page forms

Authority resides in a white wrist cloth, a succession record, changed names, rosters, requests, lamp accounts, candidate grades, permissions to stand, an unwritten mouth-to-ear diagnosis, slates of angles and failed aimings, and shelves marked for first-year servants. The chapter places terse dialogue around longer testimonially sourced explanations. Short paragraphs puncture the long runs of measurement. Italics hold one scientific conclusion. The final repeated phrase performs the Pendulum's motion without an interpretive sentence after it.

### Absences

No change in the Mountain answers the martyrdom. Sela disappears from attendance before she returns only as a formal subordinate. Va-Sheva passes without conversation. Kailan wants no answer from the Mirror. The centre of the stellar wheel contains no star. The sighting baseline yields no measurable stellar shift. Reversals are recorded with their cause left blank. Most telescope fields return no light. The observatory's origin remains “cut or found.” Justice supplies a shape without receiving its purpose. The chapter ends before Kailan states the problem he has been told to find.

### Plate B: lived system

The strongest ordinary-life consequence is not the observatory's grandeur but the labour and relation it displaces.

- Rank changes touch. Sela's two fingers on the oil dish become a required stop, lowered eyes, movement to the wall, and a lamp knocked against stone. Kailan's refusal to request her return makes the cost belong to his choice as well as to the office. No explanation should be added.
- Former peers become “flat obedience,” and Va-Sheva can ask only whether Light requires Shield. The office reorganises speech before it reorganises belief.
- Northern-hall authority is made of candidate sequences, apparatus rosters, requests, accounts, quarrels over ceremonial position, grading, trimmed lamps, cleaned circles, and insufficient sleep. This is inhabited administration rather than ornamental bureaucracy.
- The black-basin pause is a failure of maintenance with bodily and institutional consequence. Ka-Raedin's cure is also administrative: duties are removed, complaints forbidden, sleep reassigned, candidates made to wait or accept another grader.
- The observatory depends on worn holds, cleaned shafts, shutters, lamp accounts, lens workers, mounting screws, cooled glass, transported water, food, storage boxes, and repeated climbs. Discovery costs meals, sleep, warmth, and sight. That cost is already concrete.
- Ka-Raedin's monthly visits preserve ordinary supervision: he inspects shutters and accounts before slates, repeats loose measures, and protects cooling glass. His laughter enters through work rather than a separate intimacy scene.
- Ka-Noroth's old hands, failed damp paintings, pottery wheel, and cups make inherited grief depend on craft. This is productive ordinary life, not garnish.
- Sa-Tavan teaches with ink to his second knuckle, a school tray, shelf labels, and drills meant for servant boys. The knowledge used by an elite office is carried by elementary labour and a lower institutional rank. Kailan must take the shape and return no purpose.

The principal lived-system risk is concentration. After the opening pages, almost all maintenance is performed by Kailan and Ka-Raedin or invoked through unnamed workers. This does not demand a new witness or subplot. The existing lens workers, attendant substitution, servant training, meal supply, and apparatus accounts are enough carriers; if revised, they should be made slightly more causally resistant only where an existing action already touches them. No new domestic texture is warranted.

## Phase 3 — Carrier and explanation audit

### Abstract claims insufficiently carried

Very little is wholly uncarried. The chapter has already built material proofs for most abstractions. The vulnerable claims are:

1. “all of it correct and empty” and the statement that questions “go out” name Kailan's condition before the basin embodies it. The surrounding lamp labour supports the language, but the basin is the stronger proof.
2. “the one thing under all of it that stayed only beautiful” supplies a definitive interpretation of the observatory ritual. The year of looking, grieving, measuring, laughing, fearing, and returning can carry beauty without isolating it from cost.
3. “It is the longest part of the office, and the last taught, and the one honest thing in it” closes the moral reading of inherited grief before the cups disclose their maker and fragility.

These remain legal because they are recoverably spoken by Kailan or Ka-Raedin. The problem is artistic overdetermination, not source illegality.

### Successful carriers followed by explanation

1. The suspended cloth, sinking basin cloth, uneven stool, and wrung water already show incapacity and care; Ka-Raedin's long account then explains the sickness, lineage, mechanism, and cure. The name and actionable cure matter. The general thesis that the office is built from what breaks a man is the portion most available for compression.
2. Two men drink, name Syphiron, confess love, weep uncovered, and do not look away. The later statement of what every Light comes to do in the room explains a ritual whose physical action has already succeeded. Retain necessary provenance; consider shortening interpretation.
3. Failed aimings fill margins faster than centres. The later dark/light dialogue is not automatically redundant because it states a bounded empirical inference and gives Kailan's revised love its answer. It should be protected from any further thematic gloss.
4. Ka-Noroth's cups are thin, old, ringed, nested, separated, and carried in cloth. The sentence saying their thinness forces gentleness with grief translates the object one step too completely.
5. Sa-Tavan's rearranged wedges physically prove conserved area before his verbal theorem. Here the explanation is necessary instructional content, not a defect: Kailan must receive the proposition. It should remain concise and should not acquire a metaphorical explanation.

### One-to-one keys

- The explicit “be gentle with his grief or lose the vessel of it” equation narrows cup, hand, breakage, drink, lineage, and craft into one stable symbolic translation. This is the primary anti-key finding.
- “Light taught me that darkness is absence” is safely reopened by observed black fields and by Kailan's refusal to call them empty. Do not resolve the reopened term elsewhere.
- The lamp/question imagery approaches a key in the exhaustion passage, but literal lamp maintenance, optical light, beauty, surveillance, and stellar colour keep it plural. Do not add further extinguishing metaphors.
- The repeated circles are resonant but not keyed because they perform different work. No sentence should gather them into a single declared meaning.

### Ordinary life used as garnish

- The opening references to green dye, jars, wounds, and dead bees do not affect a later choice. Their legitimate immediate consequence is the continued routine of the Mountain after martyrdom. If retained, they should remain brief and should not be expanded.
- The lens workers, lamp accounts, meals, sleep, servant drills, wet cloth, and attendant substitution all affect cost, choice, or survival and are not garnish.
- No new worker, family, illness, or peripheral witness should be invented merely to make the observatory socially denser.
