## Cold read: unaided reconstruction

This reconstruction was written after one continuous cold read and before reopening the chapter. It is not corrected below.

### What happened, in the order I remember it

The sixth interview opens with Paul turning comparison itself into breakfast: paired teas, eggs, and toast, with Helga and Urs helping. Kailan dislikes Marmite, cannot distinguish the teas, and chooses one egg. Before the choices, he lays Greek words and stories beside Light scripture - fire, a chained monster, flood, and hope sealed in a jar - and says the Outsiders retain the pieces while forgetting the whole. Paul refuses the assumed direction of inheritance: resemblance alone cannot prove which tradition came first, and comparison chosen in advance is only a wall.

The record resumes just after Kailan has found the Veil's Chamber. Ka-Syphiron and Va-Raedin receive him in a cramped, smoke-blackened workroom crowded with lenses, jars, glass, and damaged apparatus. Ka-Syphiron explains the chain: finding the lower chamber makes Kailan a Veil, but Va-Raedin's earlier discovery of the Keeper's Chamber could not raise him until someone he trained opened the lower chamber in turn. Raedin found his chamber six years earlier and has waited in readiness. Kailan has completed the chain, so both men are congratulated.

Ka-Syphiron opens a hidden cupboard and produces an excessive feast. Hydromel poured into old white cups optically gathers a blur beneath the glaze into a naked woman. Kailan understands the image before the optics, turns scarlet, then names the inverse turning; both older men laugh. The three eat, drink, toast Raedin's six years, and relax out of institutional posture. Ka-Syphiron tells Kailan that his new office begins as a partnership without equality and warns him to distrust counsel whose cost is paid in another body.

Kailan asks how Faith can know how to press on people through voices, silences, and thresholds. Ka-Syphiron says Faith humiliates the ear as Light humiliates the eye, demonstrating echo and delay, while warning against treating the Mountain as a puzzle divided for Kailan's convenience. Raedin gives Kailan a chipped prism for after the ceremony. A whole-looking beam is not necessarily whole; the gift opens the question of what light remembers. Ka-Syphiron warns Kailan not to become drunk on other people's locked doors when Light has locked doors of its own.

With Ka-Syphiron briefly out of the room, Kailan asks Raedin why he concealed the six-year wait. Raedin says that knowing would have turned the chain into Kailan's hunger and made him try to become the boy who completed it; naming worthiness too early can damage it. He finally tells Kailan that he did well. Their intimacy turns comic when Kailan admits that he has already said `acceptable` to a servant and Raedin says the chain has closed onto itself: Kailan is becoming him.

Ka-Syphiron returns with another entertainment. A joke involving the woman in the cup and Va-Sheva embarrasses Kailan; then a question about archangels leads him to ask about the Beast. The room changes. Ka-Syphiron says scripture supplies a shape and children receive a story, but he is one of five people who keep the actual thing imprisoned beneath the Mountain. Division among offices, walls, and portions prevents one hand from holding the whole and keeps the Beast contained. A mystery invites the mind; a secret burdens the hand. Kailan will one day carry a portion and will no longer speak of it lightly.

Ka-Syphiron breaks the dread with a Justice card game resembling twenty-one. The Mountain wins ties. The discard pile is the revealed past and the shoe the future; a perfect record can change the action or wager the future deserves, though finite resources and chance still permit ruin. He warns Kailan not to steal one lesson from another Kahir and mistake it for the whole, and not to commit the ludic error of assuming that the world keeps the rules written on the table. A wall may keep a person out, a thing in, or two things apart.

Tomorrow's double Ascension will speak what is already true. Kailan must receive cloth, lamp, word, silence, or wound as condition rather than ornament. He leaves the two older men, hearing only Ka-Syphiron's broken fragment through the closing door: `Here. For tomorrow, and for-`. The next morning, Raedin leads Kailan in pale robes toward the Hall through corridors that retain the material labour of the Mountain: oil residue, bone steam, Shield barriers, Balance tins, and a Justice record.

The interview stops before the double Ascension. The machine will go to the lawn. Martinez then brings Paul a map and says nothing has happened, but he has learnt something: the Mountain lies somewhere along a line in northern or central Africa inferred from the pendulum. He traces Roger Michel Martinez from an Algerian childhood to Niger's uranium world and then to Chad during a French-Libyan conflict, but will not call him French intelligence without evidence. Sarah and Urs know nothing; the occasion Martinez awaits has not arrived, so they wait.

### Who wanted what

- Kailan wanted to understand what his discovery had made true, to receive his teachers' recognition without losing their confidence, and then to push across the Mountain's partitions toward Faith, the Beast, and the larger truth.
- Va-Raedin wanted Kailan's discovery to remain Kailan's rather than a performance designed to finish Raedin's ascent; after six years of restraint, he wanted to acknowledge him without making praise another intoxicant.
- Ka-Syphiron wanted to celebrate both links, admit Kailan into adult knowledge by controlled degrees, and leave him with questions strong enough to govern later attention.
- Paul wanted comparison without predetermined inheritance and later wanted Martinez to keep the same evidentiary discipline while investigating the Mountain.
- Martinez wanted to locate both the Mountain and Roger Michel's hidden affiliation, but accepted that the usable occasion had not yet arrived.

### What remains in residue

The first thing remembered is the breakfast comparison: Marmite, indistinguishable teas, eggs, and the argument over whether resemblance proves inheritance. The last thing remembered is Martinez's Africa map and the decision to wait rather than cheat the evidence.

The persistent visual carriers are the mutually supported shelves in the blackened workroom; the three men linked by hands on shoulders; the cupboard feast; the naked woman assembling beneath hydromel; the chipped prism opening a wound of colour; Ka-Syphiron's fingers moving from discard pile to shoe; the closed Justice box; the closing door that truncates his sentence; the work-smells and marked goods on the way to the Hall; and the line across the map.

The persistent sounds are the latch, the hiss and cooling ticks heard while Ka-Syphiron is behind the curtain, the older men's laughter when Kailan blushes, the clap that ends the Beast's pressure, and the sentence cut off by the closing door.

The emotional high is the untidy warmth of the feast, especially the toast to Raedin's six years and Raedin finally saying that Kailan did well. The emotional low is Ka-Syphiron's quiet distinction between an inviting mystery and a hand-burdening secret when the Beast becomes material rather than scriptural.

What seems most likely next is the double Ascension: public ritual will formalise Kailan as Veil and Raedin as Keeper while distributing burdens that the feast only approached. In the interview frame, Martinez will keep investigating until the awaited occasion permits a move.

Unresolved: what the Beast literally is and what each of the five holds; what Ka-Syphiron passed or meant to pass to Raedin after Kailan left; what light remembers; what Faith's apparent access to Kailan means; what the prism will reveal; whether Roger Michel worked for France; where the Mountain lies; why the machine goes to the lawn; what occasion Martinez awaits; and what Kailan's feeling for Va-Sheva will cost.

What felt certain without proof: Raedin's ascent will now occur; Kailan's new standing will expose him to dangerous apparatus and requests; Ka-Syphiron expects office knowledge to exact a bodily cost; Martinez's trail is relevant even though its institutional explanation remains unearned; and the unfinished exchange behind the door carries more than routine ceremony preparation.

## Reopened trace: want / expect / feel / hold

This is a reader-time trace, not a scene-function or architectural map.

### The paired breakfast

- **Want:** Understand why Paul has arranged pairs of ordinary foods and how they continue the Homer exercise.
- **Expect:** A discrimination test whose result will be used to argue about source, resemblance, or expertise.
- **Feel:** Sociable amusement, sharpened by Kailan's headache and his instant disgust at Marmite.
- **Hold:** Two of each item, deliberately checked dates, `aletheia` as unhidden truth, the chained fire-giver, the monster under a mountain, the sealed Hope, the flood, and the fact that one pair of teas defeats confident distinction.

When Kailan declares that the Outsiders possess the pieces of his scripture but have forgotten the whole, the want changes from `what is the experiment?` to `will Paul accept the claimed direction of inheritance?` The available expectation is a historical rebuttal. Paul's `resemblance is not inheritance` supplies it without pretending to settle which tradition came first. The reader leaves the frame holding both Kailan's exhilarating pattern-recognition and Paul's refusal to let sequence be chosen by belief.

### The shut, blackened room

- **Want:** Learn what Kailan's discovery has earned and why both older men are present.
- **Expect:** A severe private examination before a later public ceremony, possibly with punishment mixed into promotion because disobedience is named.
- **Feel:** Constriction and alertness from the latch, soot, damaged instruments, uneven stool, and the mark on Ka-Syphiron's hand.
- **Hold:** Kailan found the Veil's Chamber; Va-Raedin placed him on the path; a Veil learns who placed him.

The chain explanation changes the want to `what has Raedin already done, and what does Kailan's success release?` Six years is the clean surprise. The repeated short answers delay the emotional interpretation long enough for `I served` to resist Kailan's word `waited`. By the time Ka-Syphiron places a hand on each shoulder, the reader holds the chain through people rather than office rules: old Keeper, ready Veil, newly successful Candidate.

### Congratulations becoming a feast

- **Want:** See whether Raedin can receive thanks and whether Kailan can receive praise.
- **Expect:** Formal restraint, followed by departure and ceremony preparation.
- **Feel:** Relief that keeps breaking into laughter.
- **Hold:** `You did not fail`; the linked shoulders; Raedin's six years; Ka-Syphiron refusing to let Light stand still through an important moment.

The secret panel reverses the expectation. `Ray` briefly exposes familiarity before the hidden supplies do. The feast makes institutional rank permeable without erasing it. The private name did not survive unaided recall, but the relationship it disclosed did survive through the first bread, the toasts, Raedin's objections, and the older men's shared laughter. That is a detail that may vanish without loss; making it louder would compete with the stronger human carrier.

### The hydromel cup

- **Want:** Discover what is wrong with the cups and whether Kailan will see it.
- **Expect:** An optical trick because the room has already supplied lenses, curved glass, and a downward-burning lamp.
- **Feel:** Curiosity, then comic shock and second-hand embarrassment.
- **Hold:** A blind-looking disc, the hydromel settling, the naked woman gathering through liquid and curvature, and Kailan understanding the picture one breath before the optics.

The mechanism is available just early enough to let the content remain the surprise. `Inverse turning` makes the joke a Light lesson without explaining away the embarrassment. This whole movement remained alive and correctly retained.

### Six years and the new burden

- **Want:** Let Raedin's patience receive acknowledgment and learn what changes for Kailan now.
- **Expect:** Responsibilities, correction, and a hierarchy that survives the intimacy.
- **Feel:** Warmth with a first undertow of cost.
- **Hold:** The toast to readiness humiliated by time; partnership without equality; dangerous apparatus and other offices beginning to touch Kailan; counsel whose cost is paid in another body.

The exact list of northern halls, sequences, apparatus, and requests vanished, but the burden did not. The clean-hands warning survived nearly verbatim. The list therefore does not require increased emphasis.

### Faith, echo, and the prism

- **Want:** Know how Va-Maera could understand Light's pressure points without entering its rooms.
- **Expect:** Either a refusal or a physical account of Faith's knowledge.
- **Feel:** Appetite checked by caution; fascination edged with the fear of trespass.
- **Hold:** The Mountain as wound, machine, refuge, prison, and experiment room; Faith humiliating the ear as Light humiliates the eye; echo, delay, stone, and resonance.

The taps let the ear-example arrive before its name. The prism then changes the want from cross-Kahir access to a Light question Kailan can legitimately carry. `For after`, the wound of colour, the apparently whole beam, and the memory of light all survived. The reader knows what to ask without being told what the answer must be.

### Raedin without the Keeper in the room

- **Want:** Hear the personal answer to the six-year silence.
- **Expect:** Raedin withheld the fact to prevent pressure or indebtedness.
- **Feel:** Quiet intimacy and relief after the public joking.
- **Hold:** Kailan would have made Raedin's ascent part of his hunger; the boy trying to complete the chain would not have found the chamber; worthiness can be damaged by being named too early; `You did well`.

The servant anecdote returns the feeling to comedy while preserving the change in relationship. The line about Kailan becoming Raedin is explanatory in literal terms, but its primary action is the teacher's joke, so removing it would diminish rather than trust the reader.

### Va-Sheva, the Beast, and the unopened box

- **Want:** First, escape Kailan's embarrassment; then, when neither older man moves, learn whether the Beast is doctrine, story, or material danger.
- **Expect:** The archangel joke promises more comedy; the sudden stillness instead predicts either refusal or a narrow disclosure.
- **Feel:** Exposure gives way to dread. Ka-Syphiron's dropped smile and gaze toward the floor make the threat local.
- **Hold:** One of five; the thing itself beneath the Mountain; divided offices, walls, and portions keeping it held; mystery inviting the mind versus secret burdening the hand; Kailan's future portion.

The wooden box has already entered but remains unopened throughout the disclosure, so a comic promise stays faintly in working memory and makes Ka-Syphiron's clap a credible release. In unaided reconstruction I relocated the box's effective arrival to after the Beast. That is a false but plausible memory compression, not a reason to alter placement. The Beast's nature, the identity of the other keepers, and the mechanism of containment remain productively unresolved because the reader has the necessary boundaries: it is material enough to imprison, collective enough to require five, and dangerous enough that forgetting has a time cost.

### Justice in the cards

- **Want:** Understand why anyone plays a game in which the Mountain wins ties, and what Justice can know that the ordinary players cannot.
- **Expect:** The house advantage will become a lesson in record, risk, or institutional rule-making.
- **Feel:** Comic relief after the Beast, then intellectual wariness.
- **Hold:** Twenty-one; the shifting ace; the Mountain taking ties; exact records altering wagers without abolishing ruin; discard pile as past, shoe as future; the warning that the world need not keep the table's rules.

The principle becomes available once Ka-Syphiron says that an exact record and a condition-sensitive wager can win over many shoes. The past/future clue gives that principle a memorable physical carrier. The following sentence explaining again that a perfect past record changes the action and wager is therefore understood before it is said. This is the one clear instance of explanatory pressure beyond what the cold residue required.

### Door, morning, and interruption

- **Want:** Know what the double Ascension will require and what Ka-Syphiron must tell Raedin alone.
- **Expect:** Public ritual next, with the private feast's lessons becoming bodily conditions.
- **Feel:** Anticipation held under a small, precise exclusion when the latch cuts off the sentence.
- **Hold:** Wall as exclusion, containment, or separation; the Hall larger than Kailan's mind; cloth, lamp, word, silence, or wound; `Here. For tomorrow, and for-`.

The material corridor on the next morning keeps the Mountain physical: spent oil-cake, broth-and-glue steam, lowered bars, marked oil tins, open record. The items did not survive individually with perfect fidelity, but their labour and smell did. Stopping the interview before the Hall protects the promised ceremony rather than withholding a missing answer.

### The map and the wait

- **Want:** Learn what Martinez found and whether it justifies action.
- **Expect:** The pendulum has narrowed geography but not supplied a safe route or institution to target.
- **Feel:** Investigative excitement checked by the same discipline Paul demanded at breakfast.
- **Hold:** Algeria, Niger, Chad, French uranium interests, unexplained presence during war, no honest attachment of Roger Michel Martinez to French intelligence, and the decision to wait.

The frame closes its epistemic pressure cleanly: Paul's earlier refusal to infer inheritance from resemblance becomes the standard Martinez applies to a circumstantial trail. My cold reconstruction called the map-line `triangulation`; the text only licenses a line derived from the pendulum, not that more precise method. I also imagined that the unfinished sentence involved passing an object. The record licenses only `Here` and the cut-off phrase. Both are plausible inferences that should remain labelled as inferences.
