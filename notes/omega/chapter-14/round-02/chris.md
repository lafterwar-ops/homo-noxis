## Phase 1 — Artifact read, without causal synopsis

The chapter is made from closures, counted separations, empty receptacles, surrendered marks, and sounds that cross stone. Its first material action is a reversal: bars, chains, and doors that dropped and tightened through the night sound in reverse as jurisdictions release. The body remains the thing being passed between systems even when no cord is put on it. Custody is expressed by Ka-Dhavar's position within reach, the single touch at the shoulder, the five seated witnesses, Va-Sheva's search, the dark binding over the white knot, and the rising passage entered without a hand on Kailan.

Recurring materials and actions:

- Stone changes function without changing substance: wall becomes depth; a cold joint becomes a route; a bridge spans pits; seats and the centre table are cut from the chamber; a seam becomes a passage. Stone can listen, conceal, divide, and open.
- Light is repeatedly restricted rather than extinguished: the hooded hand-lamp is put away; striped illumination defeats trained sight; a narrow opening changes light; the token is removed and placed under the hooded lamp; an old northern light turns sand to glass; the last lamp illuminates tape and paper.
- Hands register jurisdiction and cost. Ka-Raedin sets down the lamp, Ka-Leth keeps his hands empty and repeatedly lays them on the empty stone, Va-Sheva's thumb presses a crescent into her opposite palm, Ka-Raedin closes his hand over the token, and Kailan opens his hands for Shield's search.
- Records split into kinds: Books carried by bodily memory, scrolls left untouched, the formal tablet and halted stylus, photographs and map that remain unlaid, testimony that is spoken after the tablet leaves, tape that listens, and paper on which marks dry.
- Containers recur under pressure: Mountain, sealed chamber, Hall, Labyrinth, archive, office, door, sphere, grave, open world. None proves perfectly closed.
- Ordinary life appears in short exact intrusions: a bowl strikes a table, someone laughs, a child runs six steps, packs hold water/food/cloth, a meal-place returns with the mother's thumb smoothing a cracked bowl. These are the bodies and habits for which the rooms exist and from which the condemned man is erased.
- Repeated phrases and formal hinges include “one hand,” “five,” “the centre stone,” “the record,” “useful,” “nothing,” “Exception,” “one voice,” “fourth day,” and “no one.” Ornaments divide changes in the kind of admissible knowledge. The inset room interrupts at the precise point between sentence and contest. Final tape and ink return the chapter from remembered proceeding to the material present of telling.
- Silence has several physical forms: the Labyrinth's larger hollow behind running water, the pause after scripture, the stopped stylus, the Beast's carved refusal to answer, the removed Candidate's ear, a still chamber before votes, and the listening machine.

The notable absences are as active as the objects: no restraint, no proof on the centre table, no single hand with the whole, no written record after Sa-Tavan withdraws, no Light token against Kailan's ribs, no household message or private mark, no body for the household, and no stable “outside” once the sphere is admitted.
