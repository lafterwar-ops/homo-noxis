## Phase 1 - Cold read

## Phase 2 - Unaided reconstruction, fixed before reopening

This is the reconstruction as fixed from memory before the chapter was reopened. Its gaps are retained rather than repaired from the reread.

### Remembered sequence

1. The Lesser Seal ends in a night of locks reversing. Ka-Dhavar personally collects Kailan without restraints and takes him below the known hearing rooms.
2. All five Keepers wait with Va-Sheva and a junior Justice recorder. Ka-Elun makes Ka-Raedin leave his hooded lamp behind. The party enters the Labyrinth.
3. The Labyrinth defeats the repetitions sought by a Light-trained eye. Kailan stops trusting sight, follows water and a larger silence by ear, and receives Ka-Elun's private recognition that hearing may be better in him than the eye he was trained to use.
4. They enter the immense Cataclysm antechamber: bridge, pits, chained gate, Beast gravure, five separated seats, empty central table.
5. Faith reads the sacred account of the Outsiders, the division of the five powers, the Beast, the destruction of the open world, and the people's retreat beneath the Mountain.
6. Justice states the charges and the trap: Kailan cannot answer by repeating forbidden knowledge. Va-Sheva confirms that he surrendered the proof but that surrender did not undo its movement through the Mountain.
7. The only outcomes are death or Exception. Every office votes death. Faith supplies a believable cover in which Kailan is lost on an illicit pilgrimage and honestly mourned. Ka-Raedin calls him extraordinarily useful and nevertheless votes to kill him. The word `useful` remains with Kailan.
8. Justice strips his office, Ka-Raedin takes back the Light token, and the cold where it rested registers the loss. The framing room interrupts: the drum is still drawing and the house is moving.
9. Kailan refuses to contest the judgment and instead contests the court. Through a chain of hypothetical assent, he forces the five to recognize high treason as grounds to interrupt his death hearing. He obtains just enough standing to accuse a Keeper, then gets Sa-Tavan and the written record removed before naming Ka-Dhavar and summoning Va-Sheva.
10. Kailan believes he has Ka-Dhavar. Ka-Dhavar answers before prosecution with a larger disclosure: the desert is not empty; Outsiders seek a buried black substance; Reggane was struck by a fire that made glass and poisoned the wind; old seals disguised real alarms; Shield goes outside; machines now watch the desert from above.
11. The phrase `around the Earth` breaks Justice's inherited cosmology. Light, Balance, and Faith each possess a separate observation that fits a spherical Earth, but no one office had assembled them. Justice cannot test the confluence and closes the accusation without declaring it false. Kailan's claim that the overwhelming disclosure itself is the crime does not reopen it. He is condemned again.
12. Kailan builds a second plea from `useful`: send him outside under Shield as a finder, with Va-Sheva as the ear that returns his truth to the lawful offices. Each Keeper grants Exception in the language of the Keeper's own discipline, and each also names the danger that cannot be made to disappear. Justice understands that a seamless world has no outside and grants the fifth vote.
13. The death cover remains. Kailan is alive, not forgiven, without office or a name in the Books, leashed to Shield and the desert. Ka-Raedin tells him to be the eye. Ka-Elun warns him not to turn his survival into gospel or martyrdom. Va-Sheva tells him he made Exception into a door, covers the white knot, searches away every private route, and allows nothing for Light or his household. His mother's hand at a cracked bowl comes to him; he takes nothing.
14. The recorded-teller frame returns. A little wheel of tape listens. The cold persists in Kailan's body, and a second set of five marks is dry on the drum's paper.

### Wants remembered

- Kailan first wants to understand where he is being taken and what can still be said.
- Once death becomes unanimous, he wants an opening in procedure rather than absolution.
- He wants his accusation of Shield heard and, for one charged instant, believes exposure will heal the Mountain and save him.
- After Justice closes that route, he wants his dangerous knowledge and talent spent rather than buried.
- The five want the Mountain preserved without allowing one office to become whole. Ka-Dhavar wants the external watch treated as necessity, not treason. Va-Sheva wants the crossing and later custody kept exact, without comforting Kailan.

### Strongest residue

- First thing remembered: the descent and Ka-Raedin being required to put the lamp away.
- Last thing remembered: the listening tape wheel, the cold, and the second five marks drying.
- Images: the cold seam under Kailan's palm; the huge gate made for the scale of one person; the empty centre stone; the Light token leaving a cold place against his ribs; Sa-Tavan carrying the tablet beyond hearing; Ka-Leth sweating only after the world becomes round; the dark cloth covering the white wrist knot; the mother's thumb smoothing a cracked bowl.
- Words or verbal carriers: `useful`; `I contest the court`; `The Earth has no around`; `There is no away`; `a seamless world keeps no outside`; `You made the Exception into a door`.
- Emotional high: the audacity and clean escalation of the jurisdictional challenge through the accusation of Ka-Dhavar.
- Emotional low: the unanimous first death judgment, sharpened by the truthful grief planned for Kailan's household. A second low arrives when Justice buries an accusation he allows may be true.
- Expected next movement: an outward life under Shield/Horizon, Va-Sheva as the only authorized human thread back, and a collision between the approaching Outsider search and whatever the drum's marks imply.

### Gaps and uncertainty in the unaided account

- The exact seven procedural facts and the full formal names of the three charges did not survive, although the prohibited-proof route and its danger did.
- The packs, their contents, and the inhabited sounds above the descending passage vanished.
- The precise five-by-five arrangement on the gate and its empty iron slot vanished as specifications; the gate and Beast remained as one dominant mechanism-image.
- The old ridge enquiry and the fact that Ka-Dhavar corrects Kailan's earlier `mirage` record did not survive as a separate step. They blended into the later proof that Shield knew the outside was real.
- The interval between the two historical seals, Xhian's former office, and several exact names in Ka-Dhavar's disclosure vanished. Reggane, black glass, poisoned wind, and the false-drill cover remained.
- The separate metaphors of all five Exception votes blurred into a collective warning, except Balance's `no away` and Justice's seamless-world image.
- I supplied the word `oil` for the buried black substance in memory. The chapter does not name it. That was a plausible reader inference, not a textual fact.
- I inferred that the drum is registering seismic movement and that its marks answer the Cataclysm mechanism. The chapter gives movement, a drawing drum, cold, and two sets of five marks, but does not state that interpretation here.

## Discrepancy classification

### Alive and correctly retained

The chapter's intended dominant people and forces survived: Kailan as a finder who turns every boundary into an aperture; Ka-Raedin as the teacher who votes for the Mountain before the pupil and later refuses to sentimentalize survival; Ka-Elun as the voice that recognizes his other faculty and then warns against gospel; Ka-Leth as law made physically vulnerable by unassimilable fact; Ka-Dhavar as both necessary watcher and still-unresolved accused; Va-Sheva as witness withheld, custodial thread, and keeper of hard consequence.

The main actions survived in order without reconstruction from outside knowledge. The first death sentence, the accusation, its burial, the second plea, the five Exceptions, and the secret outward transfer are distinct rather than blended. Custody is especially clear: Shield recovered the proof; Justice held the formal record through Sa-Tavan; Light recovered the office token; Sa-Tavan left with the written death sentence; Va-Sheva became Kailan's authorized return route; nothing private crossed the threshold with him.

### Vanished despite local importance

Several specifications vanished, but none currently warrants amplification. The packs, the exact seven facts, the old enquiry's administrative closure, and most numerical details of the external history serve local credibility and sequence. Their disappearance leaves the right larger residue: a prepared disappearance, an already reconstructed breach, an old lie now acknowledged, and a long-running external danger. Making each more memorable would compete with the trial's essential carriers.

The five-by-five signs and empty gate slot are the one potentially consequential image that failed to remain in their exact form. The ending's `second set of five marks` did remain. The chapter therefore creates a question without forcing the reader to equate gate signs and drum marks. Unless later text requires an exact recognition here, that distance should remain.

### Falsely but plausibly inferred

- The buried black substance reads easily as oil, but Kailan does not name it. Preserve the unnamed substance here; the inference supplies historical texture while keeping the teller within his available vocabulary.
- The drawing drum reads as a seismograph or related measuring device, but neither the narrator nor the room speaker supplies that name. Preserve the object through action and sensation.
- Ka-Dhavar's continuing relationship with an Outsider feels likely to belong to Horizon's external watch, but the accusation is never tried to an evidentiary conclusion. Necessity explains a motive; it does not establish the exact contact, permission, or innocence. Preserve that distinction.

### Understood early or explained after inference

Naming `Death, or Exception` before the unanimous death vote tells the reader that another legal category exists. This makes a later survival route imaginable, but not cheap: the first judgment is completed, recorded, and bodily enacted through loss of office before Exception becomes possible. The early knowledge functions as suspense, not a spoiled reveal.

Va-Sheva's final `There were only ever two. You made the Exception into a door` crystallizes what the reader has already inferred. It earns its place because it defines the nature of Kailan's transformation rather than explaining the procedure again. Likewise `alive` versus `forgiven` and the longest `leash` do not erase relief; they keep relief from becoming pardon.

### Productively unresolved

- Whether Ka-Dhavar committed treason, committed a necessary breach, or made those categories impossible to separate.
- What Va-Sheva would have said had the accusation reached testimony, and what her small bodily reactions mean beyond what Kailan can observe.
- Whether Kailan's claim to act only for truth is devotion, self-knowledge, survival rhetoric, or all three.
- Whether the spherical-world revelation revises doctrine, merely relocates the believer within it, or begins a deeper fracture.
- What the Beast, gate signs, moving house, drum, and two sets of five marks amount to in the teller's present.
- When and under whose custody Kailan is making the recording. The machine is visible to him and the room voice is audible, so the frame is sourced without being overidentified.
- Whether the outward road can ever remain outward. Justice's warning makes return structurally imaginable without declaring it inevitable.

### Confusing without artistic return

None rose to the threshold of a required repair. Justice's language of a throat and a loop is not immediately graspable, but Kailan explicitly carries it away without its sense; the reader is permitted to understand more, less, or differently than he does. The opacity belongs to a newly assembled thought rather than to missing narrative information.

## Phase 4 - Smallest residue correction

3. Making the connection among Beast, gate, drum, movement, and five marks explicit would convert bounded dread into an omniscient or prematurely technical answer.
