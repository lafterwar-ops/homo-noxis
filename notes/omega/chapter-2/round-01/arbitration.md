speech playable because Va-Raedin watches the trembling point while speaking,
and Theo finds the mirror/eye correction and Paul's custodian interruption
memorable. The resolution is selective, not global:

- keep the water's object-tethered distinction between disturbance, trace, and

  and explicit self-policing consequence; remove only the redundant phrase
  `It is the map that precedes the territory.` after the stronger concrete
  reversal `The wall followed the Mountain. Now the Mountain follows the wall.`

### Estate payment versus continuity and source ambiguity

John identifies first-read diffusion in the opening and geopolitical frame;
Theo says some opening joke detail does not survive cold recall; Patrick cannot
recover the haircut recipient; and Chapter 3 immediately uses the boat, strait,
Isfahan, placed-light, sea, swimming, and direct-proof handoff. No exact safe
Estate cut is established. The entire opening, Sarah/token room, morning close,
and midnight room are therefore frozen in this production round. The haircut
identity and any entrance/exit repair are deferred, not guessed.

### Mirror doctrine versus the material counterargument

Va-Raedin calls a mirror perfectly faithful, empty, and unable to keep what it
returns. Chris shows that the chapter's mirrors require salt, coal, wet glass,
angle, bodies, records, and labour, and therefore exert a counterpressure. This
is not an error to reconcile in authorial prose. Va-Raedin is a recoverable
speaker advancing a doctrine; the objects complicate him. Both sides are
protected.

### Faith sequence versus Faith ambiguity

Theo's reordering is accepted because it changes when Kailan and the reader see
the holes and lamp, not what exists or what anyone believes. Chris and John
require the material mechanism and the valves/God ambiguity to remain plural.
The resolution delays the physical answer until after Va-Raedin's prompt, then
retains the full knowledge/belief exchange and refusal.
