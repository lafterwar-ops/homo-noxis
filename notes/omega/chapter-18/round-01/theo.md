## Unaided reconstruction after the single cold read

The first thing retained is the Greater Seal framing room: Paul presses Kailan for an account of the hearing, Kailan refuses to narrate the journey, and Paul explicitly accepts the burden of carrying the route to the Mountain in first person. From there I remember an emergency evacuation from Blackwood—reels and notebooks privileged over the house, Sarah directing calls and decoys, Va-Elise compartmenting transport, and Jean-Charles remaining both practically indispensable and impossible to clear. Helicopter gives way to an almost indecently comfortable private aircraft, then to three black vehicles and a desert road designed to be forgotten.

The violated station is the first scene that returns as a complete enclosure: the open door, three cups, blood without bodies, an unfinished message, an unsuccessfully burned record box, five identical strokes arriving through the ground, and a route drawer opened with a key. Sarah names the missing keepers, understands that her living network is being pulled out of the ground, orders connected posts evacuated and their instruments destroyed, and declares Blackwood finished.

At the end of the vehicle route, Amastan inherits a name once carried by his grandfather and takes custody of the crossing. He rejects mapped safety in favour of knowledge held by people who walk the land. Felt silences the metal; tracks, a changed cairn, concealed wells, the moon and Sarah's failing strength determine the route. His claim that the sand has no masters survives almost word for word, as does Paul's act of saving it on a reel band. On the fourth night the five strokes are no longer marks on paper but pressure felt through an animal and the rock.

The camp first reads as a total enclosure of the Mountain—listening posts above, guards around, drilling below, hospital vehicles prepared for living captives, and offices separated so that no one possesses the whole. Jean-Charles goes down alone and returns with Farid, a radio and a hard case. Paul insists on recording custody before the case is opened. The papers make the industrial concession real and the secret centre a French state operation; they also reveal an internally sourced route previously held by one pair of hands. This makes Jean-Charles seriously suspect but does not prove betrayal. Farid knows almost nothing beyond his post and live-capture orders. The enemy has identified five functions and means to seize a complete living set.

The group crosses the camp's listening ground and post nine. Amastan stops before the final seam and makes Kailan carry a warning that roads outside the Mountain belong to those who walk them. Jean-Charles answers a challenge in French. Kailan works a five-cup bronze return sign; a shot reaches the plate as the seam closes behind them.

Va-Sheva receives them inside the Mountain under a lamp, pregnant and operating wholly as Shield. She controls weapons, papers, radio and recorder custody; threatens death under Mountain law; trusts Jean-Charles's hands but not Sarah; and seals the used seam. Yet her brief exchange with Kailan retains a second pressure: she contained him to keep him alive, cannot forgive herself for having been right, and cannot lose both him and the Mountain. She spends her questions on the immediate bore threat.

The last quiet residue is the common ward below the stair: food stalls, children, patched tools and especially fresh bread, all continuing because the people do not yet know. Then the first charge drives the stair sideways. Sarah identifies a shallow-bore blast; Va-Sheva says the struck route is a Faith passage to the Hall. Paul raises the still-turning recorder toward the grinding stone and follows the others at a run.

### Residue telemetry

- **Who wanted what:** Paul wanted a recoverable record whose sources and custody could survive suspicion; Kailan wanted to warn the Mountain without turning his trauma into testimony on demand; Sarah wanted to get the warning and her collapsing network home; Va-Elise wanted to preserve the route through compartmentation; Amastan wanted the crossing to remain answerable to its local keepers; Jean-Charles wanted the party through, while the question of what else he wanted remained open; Va-Sheva wanted the Mountain defended and Kailan alive without pretending those wants could be cleanly separated.
- **Emotional high:** the sequence from the ordinary ward and bread smell into the first blast. The chapter makes the threatened population present immediately before force reaches them.
- **Emotional low:** Sarah reading the three missing keepers into the recorder and acknowledging that her stations are being taken alive. Her physical decline in the desert deepens rather than replaces that loss.
- **First remembered:** Kailan's refusal and Paul's explicit narrative takeover inside the Greater Seal.
- **Last remembered:** hands on the trembling wall, the recorder lifted toward the sound, and the run toward the damaged Faith road.
- **Durable carriers:** paper blown over Blackwood's roof; three marked cups; five equal strokes; the station key transferred into Va-Elise's palm; felt-wrapped metal; the erased route and hidden well; Paul's sentence on the reel band; the camp built like the Mountain; the folded internal-source page; five bronze cups; Va-Sheva and the lamp; six warm loaves; grit moving beneath Paul's hand.
- **Expected next:** the Justice hearing must happen under active physical pressure rather than after a safe arrival, with the papers, tape and Jean-Charles's access all contested while the road to the Hall is compromised.
- **Unresolved:** whether Jean-Charles supplied the route, whether the live-capture/no-loss order conceals a divided command or purpose, who the two untranslated functions are, how much of Sarah's outer network survives, and whether the bore can be diverted without sacrificing people or water.
- **Certain without proof:** someone with privileged internal access enabled the approach; Jean-Charles is a deliberately available suspect, not an answered one.
- **Honest omissions:** I did not retain the exact sequence of all transport handoffs, the individual route reports on the airstrip, every numbered operational fact, or the names/codes beyond Farid. Those absences felt like functional compression, not failures to understand the danger.

## Comparison of text with residue

### Alive and correctly retained

- The source handoff is unusually clear. The opening does not merely label Paul as narrator: Kailan refuses the travel account, names Paul as able to do it, and Paul states the limits of his competence and knowledge. That makes the whole journey recoverable testimony rather than an invisible camera. This framing should remain in the Greater Seal exactly where it is.
- The violated station rewards inspection in the right order. The three cups first imply recent ordinary life; blood and the unfinished message permit a plausible assumption of death; tracks, missing bodies and Sarah's inference correct that assumption to abduction. Naming the three keepers prevents the deduction from becoming abstract.
- Amastan survives as a person and a source of authority, not just a guide. His inherited name, control of the loads and wells, disagreement with the Mountain's claim to the desert, and final refusal to cross the seam all reinforce one another without needing explanatory commentary.
- Paul insisting on custody before the hard case opens gives the documentary section a human action. “The paper does not” is the section's cleanest carrier: the reader understands why the evidence can be consequential without being automatically true.
- Jean-Charles remains bounded uncertainty. The text places the wire, reels, car, route questions, internal-source notation, French competence and Sarah's attention to his hands against his repeated practical protection of the group and delivery of the evidence. “Serious. Not proof.” accurately regulates the reader's certainty.
- Va-Sheva's entrance, pregnancy, legal threat and operational questioning arrive in that order and keep her tenderness from weakening her authority. Her private exchange with Kailan lands because she refuses consolation and immediately resumes work.
- The common ward is not a pause in urgency but the human measure of it. Bread reaches Paul through his own archaeological training, the recorder nearly exposes the warning, and the blast comes before the warmth has dissipated. The physical proof ends on action, without a paragraph explaining what the reader should feel.

### Productive misreadings and uncertainties to preserve

- At the station, the initial expectation that the keepers were killed is corrected into the more disturbing fact that they were taken alive.
- At the camp, a commercial mineral survey remains a plausible surface reading until custody and the service mark make the state operation recoverable. The industrial work being real is an important boundary; the cover is not a theatrical façade.
- Jean-Charles should remain neither cleared nor condemned. Do not add access information, interior motive, corroboration or retrospective explanation.
- Farid's “without loss of life” instruction should remain morally and operationally ambiguous. It may indicate restraint, acquisition, rival command purposes, or something the prisoner himself cannot interpret.
- The two untranslated functions should remain untranslated here. The reader has the intended question—the identity and purpose of the complete five—without needing an answer during flight.
- The exact reach of the compromised outer network, the fate of Blackwood's evacuees and the eventual usefulness of Amastan's closed roads remain live consequences rather than missing exposition.

### Material that vanished without requiring repair

The cold reconstruction compressed the exact airstrip reports, driver changes, load placement, number of maps and most contractor figures. This is not confusing loss. Those details make the journey feel implemented and the documents feel physically specific while reading; they do not all need to survive as later recall. Do not make them louder merely because they vanished. If compression is wanted for pace, preserve every handoff and route outcome and remove only repeated implementation detail—not the current route itself.

### Falsely available or over-early inference

The camp's most important realization is named slightly before its evidence has finished accumulating. The ridges, inward-facing posts, borehole and hospital are visible; Kailan then says it is “built like the Mountain”; only afterward does Paul observe the separation of drilling crews, guards, command couriers and medical orderlies, followed by a second explanation that each part knows only its task. The reader is first told the conclusion, then shown the strongest confirming behaviour, then told the conclusion again.

This is the only substantial revelation-order drag. It does not create confusion, but it spends the recognition twice and weakens Sarah's succeeding admission that much of the system was written in her hand.

### Source-boundary pressure

Most facts are visibly perceived, spoken into the scene, or read from records. Two passages deserve a source-legality mark before any rewrite:

1. In the first paragraph after Paul's takeover, “The enemy knew the Mountain existed. Sarah's calls bought speed” is categorical. The surrounding activity supports Paul's conclusion, but the grammar does not identify it as what he inferred or what an earlier reel established.
2. In the hard-case passage, the narration says the concession number, permits and customs entries “were genuine.” Paul can report their visible agreement and Sarah can recognise the apparatus; absent such an attribution, “genuine” claims verification beyond what this scene shows him doing.

These are source-exposure issues, not invitations to add omniscient explanation. The repair should be a short attribution or inference marker attached to evidence already present.

## Smallest coherent residue correction

If the manuscript is revised after arbitration, the smallest Theo-native change group is:

1. **Earn the camp recognition before naming it.** Keep the physical order—sensing above, guards around, drilling below, hospital at the centre, work groups kept apart—then let Kailan's “Above / Around / Below” recognition name what the reader has just assembled. Retain Sarah's admission that she bought such arrangements. Remove or merge only the duplicate “each part knows its task / cells” gloss if it still follows the recognition. No camp fact, infiltration condition, route, outcome or ambiguity changes.
2. **Expose Paul's source at the two categorical edges.** Mark the enemy's knowledge/speed as Paul's inference from the evacuation and calls (or attribute it to an existing record), and attribute documentary genuineness to what Sarah identifies or to the visible consistencies Paul can actually inspect. Do not broaden his knowledge.
3. **Leave the rest of the reading path alone.** In particular, do not answer Jean-Charles, amplify the two missing functions, explain the no-loss order, compress away the station names, move Va-Sheva's private exchange, or add commentary after the final blast.

No full-block sample prose is proposed because the needed changes are placement and source tags, not a new voice. A rewrite should preserve Paul's dry evidentiary idiom, Sarah's clipped operational speech, Amastan's territorial precision, Kailan's reluctance, Jean-Charles's defensive wit and Va-Sheva's controlled severity.
