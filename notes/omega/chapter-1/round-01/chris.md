### Phase 2 — four artifact plates

#### Plate A: material constellation

1. **The hand constellation (`K`)** begins in the first sentence when the
   Mountain opens Kailan's hand and gives him a name. It returns as parental
   preparation, ritual restraint, an offered wound, a blood print, a too-tight
   binding, the Veils' gestures, a child's covered mouth, and Elun's refusal of
   touch. It carries care, correction, office, shame, injury, control, help,
   refusal, and evidence; no single symbolic translation contains it.
2. **The weight constellation (`K`)** joins Mereth's failing body, the cowl
   carried by two attendants, dust falling from Elun as she rises, the crowd on
   its knees, the old man folding, Sheva lowering him like a full vessel, and the
   swell lifting knees and disk. Office is not explained as burden first; Kailan
   reports weight changing bodies and stone.
3. **The answer constellation (`K`, `P`, `B`)** moves from rod to Ear, liquid to
   Mouth, call to response, question to trained silence, five strokes to a broad
   return, testimony to machine, and machine record to drawer trace. An answer
   can certify, discipline, expose, or remain silent. The constellation is
   strongest before any speaker fixes it to one meaning.
4. **The opening/closing constellation (`K`)** joins the naming-as-door image to
   real arches and gates: the title is given while Mereth is removed alive;
   Keepers disappear singly behind office stone; Shield bars the mass of people;
   the arches reopen only after the return; the Genesis gravure loses its edges
   as the lamps are put out. Thresholds distribute access without requiring a
   general statement about power.
5. **The circle/five constellation (`K`, `P`, `B`)** is made from pillars,
   blood-plate channels, Kahirs, Veils, Keepers, crowd response, Pendulum travel,
   five strokes, equal intervals, and a fifth interval before the different
   return. Repetition changes kind at the expected sixth rather than merely
   repeating a number.

#### Plate B: lived system

  pillars, service lamps through the detention, and extinguish them as the crowd

- **Delay becomes bodily cost (`K`).** Five thousand people remain kneeling
