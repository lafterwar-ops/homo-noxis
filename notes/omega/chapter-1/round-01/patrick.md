## Room, source, cast, and address

### Noxius interview room

The recoverable record is the machine Paul explains to Kailan. Adult Kailan is
the witness and principal speaker; Paul is the listener who must make the
testimony exact without frightening or over-directing him. Paul begins by
trying to establish procedure and authority. Kailan counters by asking about
Paul's title, the machine, and belief. Their live objects are the recorder and,
at the close, water. The first pressure change occurs when Paul converts
Kailan's disbelief in Outsiders into permission for Paul's disbelief in the
Mountain; Kailan then accepts the invitation to begin.

### The remembered Hall, spoken to Paul

The teller remains adult Kailan. Fifteen-year-old Kailan supplies the bounded
eye and body: first row beneath Light, Shield across the forbidden circle,
Faith to his right, and Balance and Justice partly occluded. When he cannot see
a crossing, identity, gesture, or order, the testimony says so. Knowledge that
does not come from sight is attached to lessons, rehearsal, scripture, or
institutional announcement. That boundedness is one of the chapter's strongest
performance controls.

The active bodies are Kailan; Va-Raedin behind or beside him; Ka-Syphiron before
him at the northern stone; Va-Sheva across the circle beneath Shield;
Mereth/Ka-Mereth, Va-Elun/Ka-Elun, Va-Maera, the other Keepers and Veils;
officiant, clerks, wardens, attendants, and selected members of the crowd.
Institutional speech addresses the assembled people. Ka-Syphiron tests Kailan;
Va-Raedin privately corrects and recruits him; Kailan repeatedly looks toward
Va-Sheva without gaining an exchange. During the shocks, the Veils address one
another through looks and words Kailan cannot fully recover, while the crowd
answers prayer and waits for the closed arches.

### English monitoring room

The source is again recoverable: Mrs Blackwood explicitly says the machine is
already on the house record. Paul reports what Kailan's count has allowed him
to infer; Blackwood tests it against the trace she has kept since Reggane. She
then takes control of the knowledge and of Paul's future conduct. Kailan is not
present and is not granted access to this exchange.

## Spoken performance

The principal voices are distinguishable by tactic rather than decoration.
Adult Kailan uses exact sensuous recall, controlled withholding, and occasional
dry correction (`Patience`). Young Kailan's curiosity appears in dangerous
answers and involuntary looking. Paul is formal, courteous, and compulsively
exact; his hypotheses accelerate as the pattern closes. Va-Raedin speaks by
delayed reward and bodily correction. Ka-Syphiron reduces speech and lets
silence make the candidate expose himself. Mrs Blackwood's natural register is
imperative certainty: she knows before Paul has finished explaining and ends by
assigning conduct.

The long testimony remains speakable because pressure regularly returns to a
body: scratching cloth, hot knees, a caught cuticle, a cut thumb, a hand at a
hilt, a palm on stone, water tasting of smoke. The naming exchange is the cleanest
oral sequence. Question, hazardous answer, prolonged silence, recalled script,
failed recalled script, knife, and naming each give the listener something new
to answer even when the answer is stillness. The five-stroke sequence also earns
its repetition: each recurrence changes who is counting, anticipating, touching,
or withholding movement.

Three bounded failures remain performance-first revision opportunities:

1. **Paul's first recorder answer carries too many tactics in one turn.** It
   explains the machine, reassures Kailan, justifies the record, confesses prior
   disbelief, and establishes scholarly credentials before Kailan can act on
   any one disclosure. In a future manuscript pass, keep every fact but divide
   the action after the literal machine answer and again before the confession.
   A silence or Kailan's attention to the machine can make the change of tactic
   audible.
2. **The deictic `what does this do?` has no recoverable gesture in a
   dialogue-only record.** The referent is inferable, but an actor has no textual
   carrier for Kailan's first encounter with the recorder. A bounded rewrite can
   make the object verbal (`this machine`) or give Paul an answering action that
   the established record can legally preserve. This requires no room or event
   change.
3. **The English exchange briefly becomes a confirmation ladder.** `A readiness
   test` is repeated, followed by successive propositions answered with `Yes`.
   Paul and Blackwood momentarily share the same polished explanatory breath.
   Preserve Reggane, the even marks, the return, the gate inference, the
   whole-Earth mode, coupling, secrecy, and Paul's assignment, but let Paul seek
   confirmation once. Blackwood can then use the old trace as resistance and
   move directly into command. The discovery will feel transferred, not jointly
   recited.

The technical Pendulum measurement and Blackwood's long Reggane recollection
approach the edge of one breath, but both retain a playable objective: Kailan is
making scale exact, and Blackwood is breaking sixty-six years of private doubt.
They should be reperformed before any cut. Their length is not itself a defect.

## Blocking and object custody

The Hall is continuously playable. Light/north, Shield/opposite, Faith/right,
the forbidden center, the five office arches, galleries, gates, and the
Pendulum's crossing remain legible. Va-Raedin's recurring two-finger contact is
especially effective: it begins as hygienic correction at Kailan's wrist,
returns as gaze control, becomes restraint at the shoulder, anticipates the
fifth stroke, and leaves four clean streaks after the swell. Va-Sheva's seemingly
instant crossing to the old man remains legal because Kailan explicitly says he
did not see the crossing.

The sacred Bowl has one unresolved stage-custody question. Va-Elun sets it down,
Mereth strikes it, and the cowl transfers, but the testimony never sees who
takes the Bowl or whether it remains before Faith when the later rites begin.
Because it is the proof-object of succession, a future performance pass should
either let Kailan see its removal or explicitly preserve its continued place.
This is a prop handoff only; it must not alter the succession or later outcome.
The knife's post-cut custody is less prominent because attention correctly
stays on blood, plate, and naming, but an actor-table rehearsal should decide
whether Va-Raedin receives it back.

The first interview and monitoring room are more weakly blocked than the Hall,
which is compatible with transcript form, but their available objects should do
more pressure work if revised: recorder in the first room; chair, running
machine, and old trace in the second. No new prop is required.
