## Arbitration method

This ruling compares process traces, not votes, prose polish, or John's numerical
score. John tested architecture, causality, source provenance, and epistemic
calibration. Patrick tested playable objectives, speech, bodies, blocking, and
object custody. Theo fixed an unaided first-read reconstruction before reopening
the chapter, then tested reader-time residue and performed an echo read. Chris
tested material carriers, ordinary labour, formal recurrence, and the point at
which explanation begins to overtake physical proof. A proposal is authorized
only when its defect is demonstrated by its trace and the remedy remains
compatible with the other traces and all explicit locks.

The reports used different tokenizers (`4,854`, `4,885`, and `5,146`) for their
private diagnostics. Those counts are neither averaged nor used to displace the
locked baseline of `5,069` and its band of `4,563..5,575`.

No fifth diagnosis is introduced here. The minimum package below is composed
only from traced proposals. Where a trace proposed two materially different
ways to repair the same surface, the intervention is deferred unless this
ruling can narrow it without inventing story matter.

   explanation is concentrated there but demonstrates why the independent
   Kailan-Paul-Blackwood source ladder must survive. The compatible remedy is a
   very small rewrite inside the final inference exchange, not deletion of any
   evidentiary rung.

   beneath the Temple, the five sources and return, the Chains, Genesis and the
   Oasis, Elun's road, Mereth's fate, the wardens' warning, and the route from

## Conflicts

### Revise nothing versus make bounded repairs

Theo's completed process recommends no manuscript intervention because the
dominant residue survives and the source ladder works. That finding defeats a
broad repair, but it does not erase the two trace-specific legality and
performance issues identified independently by John and Patrick. The ruling is
therefore **transformed** into an intervention ceiling: authorize only the two
low-cost local clarifications and one bounded final-room calibration listed in
the production brief.

### Compress the explanation versus preserve the source ladder

John, Patrick, and Chris locate pressure in the final English exchange. Theo's
echo read shows that Kailan's embodied count, Paul's elicitation, and
Blackwood's independent trace must remain separate. The compatible result is
not a general cut. Preserve Paul's post-Hall questions and Blackwood's Reggane
recollection; revise only the short confirmation sequence after the trace so
that each unique inference appears once and remains owned by Paul or Blackwood.

### Reinforce Sheva versus trust the existing carrier

Theo initially tests a recurrence of Sheva's full-vessel rescue image, then
rejects it after the echo read because alertness, rescue, and contact with the
Shield pillar already carry her competence. John separately rejects giving her
a new investigative action. Chris shows that the rescue already reverses
Kailan's adolescent gaze. No Sheva addition is authorized.

### Resolve prop custody versus avoid unsupported staging

Patrick identifies the Bowl's untracked custody after the succession and the
knife's post-cut custody. The Bowl could either remain or be removed; choosing
between those outcomes would be a new decision unsupported by any trace. The
knife question belongs to later performance blocking unless the manuscript
develops a continuity failure. Neither enters this production package.
