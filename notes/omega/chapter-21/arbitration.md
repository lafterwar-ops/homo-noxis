agent, averaging their conclusions, or rewriting the chapter. It must separate
true convergence from merely similar wording, preserve productive disagreement,
and judge every proposed intervention against the current text.

compatible with the chapter's narrator and documentary frame, and does not alter
frozen plot, architecture, chronology, custody, character destination, or later
canon. Explanatory expansion is disfavoured. Existing strengths and productive
ambiguities identified by any trace must be protected.

1. a convergence map;

4. a minimal ordered production brief containing only accepted interventions;

   repeated self-interpretation, not new dialogue, a new transfer of the strip, or

2. **Audit setup rewrite versus audit-boundary recurrence.** Patrick would convert

   blur back into the three exclusions. The current setup already assigns acts to
   Va-Sheva, Paul, Sarah, and Kailan. The smaller post-admission object beat is the
   accepted remedy; the setup rewrite is not combined with it.
3. **Burial hard stop versus evidentiary afterbeat.** Theo would end nearer Sarah's
   statement; Patrick treats the absence of a subsequent charge as the closing
   fact of the beat. Retain `No charge came after that.` and remove only the second
   sentence that restates the burial already spoken and shown.
4. **Material proof versus retained abstraction.** Chris proposes removing the
   five-thousand-machine generalization and Paul's `consequence, not cause`
   classification. Theo's cold residue retains both as organizing moral facts,
   and Patrick finds Paul's distinction playable and character-specific. These
   are not safe consensus cuts and are rejected.

   Va-Sheva action would force the text to decide whether Paul is formally cleared,
   deliberately allowed, or procedurally bypassing her. Her safety work and lamp
   handoff already place her. The added choice would overdetermine the transfer of

6. **Evidence-room callback alternatives.** Naming the removed stones, naming the
   operational finding, and deleting the sentence are mutually exclusive. The
   current text cannot identify the intended antecedent without importing authorial
   or later-canon knowledge, so none enters the production brief.
