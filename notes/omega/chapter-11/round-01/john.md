## Governing diagnosis

Chapter 11 is already a powerful, nearly finished witness chapter. It turns a seismic anomaly into an ethical autobiography: Sarah discovers the Mountain not by privileged revelation but by stealing evidence, testing ordinary explanations, spending institutions and relationships, and following the human perimeter of a hidden system. Reggane is therefore both plot origin and moral pattern. The bomb gives a small first permission; Sarah becomes the next first ball; every later door opens because she accepts a cost that someone else partly pays. Moussa and Étienne prevent the investigative triumph from becoming self-congratulation, while the last room makes the old method recur in the present.

The chapter's governing defect is local but load-bearing. After Paul says that the official machine is stopped and the hidden camera is not, Sarah, Paul, and Kailan audibly specify the exact false-western-well/lithium/core-box tracer and the points they will watch. The hostile memory will therefore carry both the bait and the explanation that it is bait. A rational recipient can simply decline the predicted request, retest, or onward copy. The operation cannot do the work the characters say it will do. Because this is the chapter's final reversal and outgoing engine, it materially lowers causal and transition scores despite the excellence of the preceding account.

The repair should not change the breach, the decision to leave the eye open once, the one-exposure limit, the warehouse assay operation, the ethical restriction against using desert communities, or the handoff to Kailan's chosen door. It should separate **what is decided in front of the hostile eye** from **what the hostile eye is allowed to learn**. The text already provides the means: Paul and Sarah discovered the exchange four days earlier and “kept the observation between” them. They had time to design and name secure variants off-camera. In the compromised room they need only select the archive-bound variant in terms opaque to the eventual viewer. The exact lithium/core-box mechanics can be disclosed by Sarah or Paul from a recoverable source after that memory has left or after the eye is blinded, and in any event before a later reaction is interpreted.

## Reading experience

The opening produces immediate pressure without throat-clearing. Sarah changes the interview's geometry, Paul confesses a prior concealment, the trace becomes a handled object rather than a lore token, and the accelerated sound lets Kailan recognise a rite before Sarah supplies an explanation. “No one” is the right first reversal: the five strokes are not a resurrection but an emergency readiness call.

The Reggane sequence then earns wonder through ordinary life. Melons, enamel mugs, petrol, the useless icebox, the recorder frame, Moussa's attention to supposedly empty country, and Sarah's blood keep the nuclear event human-scale. The signal is legible because the chapter first establishes what ordinary explosion traces do, then lets interval, count, direction, and the slow return violate that model. The theft of seventy-two centimetres is especially effective: evidence has an exact physical size, and its moral cost is Moussa's transfer.

The long middle does not merely explain triangulation. It repeatedly changes Sarah's available action: eliminate local faults; obtain independent partial traces; receive a second sequence; build distributed ears; discover that geology has falsified the apparent source; correct the bearings; turn Edward's failures into a perimeter; identify Amastan; then acquire Martinez as passage. The false ridge expedition is indispensable because it makes a mistaken interpretation productive and prevents the investigation from looking preordained.

Emotionally, the chapter descends from boredom to shame, exhilaration, possession, guilt, marital estrangement, grief, and finally institutional self-recognition. Étienne's last ball-bearing gesture does not resolve whom he accuses. That ambiguity has force because Sarah accepts all three meanings rather than demanding absolution. The final room then converts historical guilt into live danger: the witnesses discover that their supposedly controlled confession has itself been made into contraband.

The one notable cooling stretch is the evidentiary montage between the second trace and Edward's cellar. It is good material, but it carries several consecutive retrospective summaries. This is not a request for a new set-piece. A payment pass can sharpen the thresholds between “not instrument error,” “repeatable external sequence,” “geological mislocation,” and “humanly maintained perimeter” while preserving the expedition and all proof.

The live-frame question is sharper: can these three people use a compromised room to identify one hand in the leak without repeating Sarah's original sin of treating inhabited country and uninformed people as instruments?

## Adversarial audit

### Attack the strongest material

- **Moussa risks becoming a device for Sarah's guilt.** The text resists that charge because he has technical authority, jokes, decisions, refusal, and a question about whose name the paper will carry. Do not add retrospective sanctification; it would reduce him.
- **The ball-bearing motif risks overdetermination.** It appears in courtship and death, but the second use revises rather than merely echoes the first. Étienne's failed release and accusation earn the recurrence. Freeze it.
- **The false ridge could look like plot delay.** It is the experiment that discovers the refraction error and therefore causes the corrected bearing. It is causal action, not excursion furniture.
- **Kailan's “empty” rebuke risks sounding authored for thematic closure.** It earns itself through Moussa's first intervention and directly changes the current operation's geography. Keep it.
- **Sarah's many memorable formulations risk a uniformly quotable house voice.** Her formulations arise from custody and evidence; still, in the last room allow Paul to remain plainer where operational precision is enough.

### Defend the apparently weakest material

- **Fourteen years are compressed into montage.** That is intentional and works because each segment ends with a changed search method or access condition. It does not need another expedition.
- **Edward's cellar arrives after the scientific work.** This is not a disconnected inheritance digression; corrected bearings are what make his failures legible as a perimeter.
- **Martinez appears at Étienne's funeral with theatrical efficiency.** The payroll/state-file trail establishes that he has long stood at the junction of Blackwood and French passage. His refusal of condolences suits the role. No origin explanation is needed here.
- **The hidden-camera debate is long.** Its length is justified by the decision's risk and by the need to assign culpability among all three. What fails is not length but disclosure of the operational secret to the recording device.
