## Phase 2 — Unaided reconstruction (immutable)

### What happened, in remembered order

The chapter opens in Paul's interview room. Sarah has joined Paul and Kailan and brought an old seismic trace, including the original paper with her blood on it and a darker contact copy. The apparatus translates the trace's timing into sound: five calls twenty minutes apart, then a deeper answer after another twenty minutes. Kailan recognizes the succession pattern and asks who was raised. Sarah says no one; the trace came from the 1960 Reggane nuclear test, and the mystery cost her fourteen years.

Sarah was twenty-seven at the French test, present through her physicist husband Étienne's clearance but capable in her own right. Moussa, the local instrument mechanic, corrects her dismissal of the southern sector as empty and turns on its recorder. At detonation Sarah looks south, catches a recorder jolted by the blast, cuts her thumb on the stylus, and stains the paper. Beneath the great test signal she watches five precisely spaced smaller strokes and then a broad low return. She steals the paper and calls the marks a frame fault. Moussa refuses to falsify the maintenance record, is moved from instruments to vehicles, and disappears from Sarah's life while she keeps both his truthful slip and his handkerchief with the trace.

Back in the room, Kailan identifies the sequence as Shield's readiness test: five offices call and the deep Earth answers before the Mountain trusts its sealed ways. Reggane's unprecedented local nuclear signature frightened the Mountain into testing itself. Sarah says she had not found a door, only heard someone beyond it breathe.

She spends four years exhausting ordinary explanations. Other stations hold partial corroboration. A later underground shot, Rubis, produces the same deliberate count and answer, although other blasts do not. Sarah builds a dispersed listening net inside genuine scientific and commercial projects. The crews' work is honest, but she withholds the further question determining their placement.

Initial bearings lead to the wrong volcanic ridge. On that expedition a dust wall hits; foreman Hamid saves Sarah from chasing a loose water drum and later records her dangerous choice in the expense ledger. Controlled charges reveal that buried hard rock bends the signals. Correcting for it shifts the search towards territory her father Edward's ventures repeatedly failed to enter.

Sarah breaks the black-tree wax seal on Edward's archive cellar. The separate respectable explanations for failed ventures become a perimeter when overlaid. Scottish geologist Andrew Kerr reports that local guidance does not physically stop outsiders but allows them to decide against going. Edward's notes end on the repeatedly marked name Amastan. Sarah begins tracing the man.

The room identifies her method as a Light-like construction: many honest searches conceal one further search, and the black tree on her equipment becomes Shield's mark for Sarah. Kailan distinguishes the stone perimeter from the necessary human aperture through trade, kinship, supplies, and silence. Sarah says Martinez comes later; she first has to inherit him.

Her concealment from Étienne has expanded room by room. He finds the overlays and Amastan. Their argument exposes that she has used his access while habitually offering the technically true sentence beside the withheld one. Even during the confrontation she asks him for another archive; the papers arrive. He sickens after years around nuclear tests, his injury claim is denied, and he dies. In a last ball-bearing demonstration he revises their old chain-reaction lesson: the first ball looks innocent but is not. Sarah cannot know whether he means the bomb, himself, or her and accepts all three meanings.

At the funeral, the elder Martinez presents Étienne's remaining open channels and names his own allegiance as passage. Sarah eventually spends the channel on the name Amastan. She requires access without buying, frightening, or exposing him. Martinez returns seven years of refusals before providing instructions for one vehicle, one driver, no radio, no instrument case. The card promises only that someone will look at Sarah and perhaps give her the next road. This is fourteen years after Reggane and aligns with a dated photograph Kailan has seen. The dismantled Blackwood car belongs to a later, second crossing, not yet told.

The closing room interrupts this retrospective line with an immediate breach. Paul has watched Roger Martinez exchange memory from a hidden second camera and an unknown older, disciplined man collect it from beneath a folded newspaper. Kailan had noticed an earlier memory change but misread it as private surveillance and, with Sarah, chose to preserve the dishonest instrument in order to learn from it. They now accept that the recorded disclosures about the Mountain may already have travelled beyond the house.

Sarah refuses the easy comfort of merely destroying the camera. She proposes one final exposure: a false western well and unusual lithium assay inserted into genuine Blackwood survey records, mentioned only before the hidden eye, with watchers on the real archived core box, laboratories, and offices. Kailan prevents a return to treating desert country as empty; Sarah moves the test into an existing store and its paper traffic. They agree that the next memory change is the last exposure, whether or not anyone reacts. Before Sarah tells her second road, Kailan must tell the door he chose while carrying her Shield record. The chapter ends with Sarah asking him to hold open the old trace's crease with her and begin.

### Who wanted what

- Sarah wanted to make the impossible trace ordinary if it was ordinary, then to locate its source once repetition denied that relief. In the present room she wants the compromised interview turned into one bounded chance to identify a hand in the leak, and she wants Kailan's corresponding confession before giving her second journey.
- Moussa wanted the actual southern country and the actual instrument to remain present in the record. He would not let Sarah's theft become a fault assigned to his frame.
- Étienne wanted the adjacent sentence: participation by consent rather than usefulness by concealment. He continued opening doors even after recognizing how she had used them.
- Kailan wanted to test Sarah's account against insider knowledge and then protect the exposed Mountain, but his earlier wish to observe the observer helped the record escape.
- Paul wanted an unaltered account and accepts responsibility for privileging it over fairness. In the final movement he supplies the concrete surveillance observation that changes the room.
- The elder Martinez wanted passage to remain valuable and deniable. Roger's own knowledge and allegiance remained unknown to me.
- The unknown collector's purpose was not established; I inferred only that the movement of the recording was trained and deliberate.

### What remained without prompting

The first thing remembered was the old trace translated into five calls and a deep answer, followed by Kailan's wrong-but-natural question: who was raised? The last thing remembered was two people holding the crease of that same paper while Kailan prepares to tell the fearful door choice; the canary operation and hidden eye remain immediately behind that image.

Persistent images and objects: Sarah's blood just beyond the seismic line; her stolen trace against her ribs beneath Étienne's arm; Moussa's washed handkerchief; the overripe melon bursting when the bomb's sound finally arrives; the black tree whose roots and branches stay upright when overturned; the crushed water drum and Hamid's ledger entry; a chisel through the wax tree; overlays turning failures into a perimeter; the ball-bearing tray partly releasing under Étienne's bad hand; Martinez's narrow case; the four-instruction filing card; camera memory under a folded newspaper; photograph beside trace; real core boxes carrying a false paper fact; the crease held between Sarah and Kailan.

Persistent sounds and sentences, paraphrased from memory: the bomb making the greatest weapon smell of fruit; Moussa saying that a frame which held would not mind being checked; Sarah hearing someone beyond the door breathe; Étienne asking for the sentence next to the true one; the first ball looking innocent; Martinez working for passage; Kailan warning not to make Moussa pay for the word empty twice.

The emotional high point was the hidden-camera disclosure because it abruptly converted the long historical pursuit into present exposure and made every earlier revelation dangerous. The emotional low point was divided between Moussa carrying out his toolbox while Sarah remains silent and Étienne's failing hand releasing only part of the ball-bearing tray. Moussa's consequence felt colder; Étienne's death felt more intimate.

### Expected next movement

In the immediate present I expect the false assay to leave through Roger's next camera exchange and create a traceable request or retest, while remaining uncertain whether the collector is the final recipient.

### Unresolved, inferred, and forgotten

Unresolved: whether Roger knows the memory's contents or recipient; who the older collector serves; how many prior recordings escaped; which door Kailan chose and why it looked wise; what occurred on Sarah's first and second roads; why the Blackwood car was dismantled and stored like a body; how Amastan evaluated her; and whether the canary will identify anything beyond an intermediate hand.

Felt certain without proof: Sarah's search and the bomb program share the guilt of making other people carry risks they did not choose; Étienne understood more of the withheld whole than Sarah admitted; the unknown collector has military or command experience; and the exact seismic sequence belongs to the Mountain rather than coincidence. These are reader inferences, not facts the report recommends converting into narration.

Falsely or tentatively inferred during the read: at the opening I briefly treated the trace as a death/succession record because Kailan's recognition made that reading immediately available; Sarah's correction rewarded it. On first mention of Martinez near the human aperture, I expected direct contact with the Mountain rather than inheritance through Étienne's surviving channels. I also initially expected the false lithium fact to be planted into desert operations, before Kailan's objection redirected it to stored cores and institutional paper traffic.

What I did not retain unaided: the names or order of all intermediate test sites/stations and most precise technical distinctions in the four-year elimination of ordinary causes; the exact count of crews or ventures; the detailed chronology between Rubis, the ridge, Edward's cellar, Étienne's death, and Martinez's seven years beyond the governing fourteen-year span; and most of the opening declarations about who owns the house, apparatus, or epistemic roles. Those omissions may be appropriate decay, but later comparison must decide whether any is required to form the intended question.

## Residue comparison

### Alive and correctly retained

- The opening false inference—five calls mean a succession and therefore a death—survived exactly strongly enough for “No one” to reset the chapter. This is productive misreading with an immediate reward and should not be weakened by advance clarification.
- The physical chain from southern instrument to blood-marked paper to theft to present-room trace remained the governing carrier. The chapter does not ask the reader to retain an abstract discovery when one object can carry it.
- Moussa survived as a person, a source of local knowledge, the guarantor of the true frame, and the one made to pay. His handkerchief and toolbox remained after most technical particulars decayed.
- The broad historical middle retained its necessary sequence despite losing minor detail: ordinary causes tested, repeat observed, ears built, false ridge corrected, inherited archive opened, human name found.
- Sarah's method retained both its evidentiary virtue and its human cost. “Honest findings under a concealed further question” survived without needing a general moral gloss outside the speaking characters.
- Étienne's adjacent sentence, belated papers, failing hand, and unassignable “it” remained one emotional unit. Sarah explicitly accepts three possible referents without converting that acceptance into proof of what he meant.
- Martinez remained passage rather than allegiance. The seven years of refusals made the 1974 card feel earned.
- The hidden-camera breach displaced the retrospective question at exactly the right moment. It also preserved the unknown collector as an intermediate hand rather than falsely promoting him to mastermind.
- The final operational decision remained comprehensible on one read, including its ethical correction away from the desert, and the held crease still outlasted the operational explanation as the chapter's last image.

### Vanished despite importance

- Sarah being physically sick when Rubis repeats vanished from unaided recall even though it is the only bodily registration of the moment when a private anomaly becomes a repeatable deliberate system. The surrounding paragraph carries several conclusions—two events, preserved sequence, hidden mechanism, selective operator—so the body is absorbed into explanation.
- Paul's opening admission that he chose the unaltered account over fairness did not remain as wording. Its later echo in his admission about the earlier camera change therefore registered as responsibility in general, not as the same temptation recurring at a larger cost. The residue nevertheless retained the joint misjudgment and the observed/observer frame, so this does not justify adding a reminder.
- Sarah's initial boredom did not survive. That loss is harmless: the later contrast between routine camp texture and nuclear scale carries the same pressure without requiring the word itself in long-term residue.

### Falsely but plausibly inferred

- The trace initially appeared to record a Keeper's death. Kailan makes that inference from genuine inside knowledge, so the reader is meant to stand with him until Sarah's correction.
- The black ridge appeared briefly available as the Mountain's physical location. The correction rewards attention to the difficulty of reading waves through unmapped country.
- “Martinez” initially suggested a direct broker to Amastan. “First I had to inherit him” correctly delays and complicates that expectation through Étienne.
- Sarah's first version of the camera test seemed likely to act again in desert country. Kailan's Moussa objection exposes the repeated assumption and causes an on-page revision before any action occurs.

### Understood early or explained after inference

- The title and opening trace make it clear very early that Reggane awakens or exposes the Mountain. This is not harmful foreknowledge. Suspense lies in how a twenty-seven-year-old Sarah earns the connection and what she spends to reach a road, not in whether the signal matters.
- By the time the second room names Sarah's concealed project as a pattern of true searches hiding one further search, the reader has already inferred the withheld purpose. The exchange still earns its place because it is Kailan's accusation and Sarah's unqualified assent; removing it would erase interpersonal consequence in the name of efficiency.
- Sarah's self-indicting inventory at the start of the last room repeats acts already witnessed. It is not needed for factual clarity, but it changes those acts from the reader's charge to the witness's owned account immediately before the present breach repeats the method. Preserve it.
- The camera operation is restated through proposal, objection, revision, commitment, and Kailan's “inverse veil.” Some factual inference precedes the final labels, but each restatement changes a decision or identifies who accepts the remaining risk. Compression here would save little and could blur the one-exposure limit or the distinction between finding one hand and finding the last hand.

### Productively unresolved

- Étienne's “it” remains bounded to the bomb, his own opened doors, and Sarah; neither Sarah nor the reader is licensed to choose for him.
- Kerr's lights, crossing tracks, and repeated man cannot be separated from fatigue. The evidence establishes a patterned boundary without proving the mechanism of each discouragement.
- Roger may be an informed servant, an inherited courier, or a carrier ignorant of content. The collector may be one hand among many. The plan correctly proceeds without resolving either question first.
- The number and destinations of earlier stolen memories remain unknown. “Proceed on the worse assumption” supplies a decision boundary, not false knowledge.
- The dismantled car, Sarah's two roads, Amastan's evaluation, and Kailan's wise-seeming fearful door remain promised rather than prematurely explained.
- “The two hands belong in one record” is briefly open in reference, but the final gesture—Kailan taking only the edge of Sarah's paper—gives the uncertainty a physical answer. It does not require an explanatory noun.

No material instance survived comparison. Speaker identity in the long final room occasionally requires looking one speech backward, but the knowledge and commitments remain recoverable: Paul owns the surveillance observation, Sarah owns the account and written false entry, Kailan supplies the ethical objection and coming door.
