### Rewrite A - Keep the beam alive through water, food and salt

```tex
The beam climbed a service throat, crossed above a washing gallery and came down where Balance kept the water. I followed it beside a shallow channel. Households waited with marked vessels while two green clerks walked a copper rod from notch to notch: household, work, illness, the unborn, yesterday's draw. One woman's mark stopped below the line she expected. A clerk put yesterday's tablet beside today's, showed her the two notches and moved on while she was still speaking. An ashkeeper lifted her jar into a high lamp. The curve of the clay bent the light and raised the water on one side. From where I stood, my eye would have called the jar fuller.\par

The beam went on through steam, yeast, boiled grain and ferment. Workers sealed jars and opened stored grain while they sang.\par

\begin{scripture}
Let every fruit be gathered. \\
Let every vessel be sealed. \\
Let no waste enter the Oasis.
\end{scripture}

One woman carried the words; another kept her kneading to them; a boy joined only when an elder looked round. I walked the beam into the green gallery. Growers lifted small reflectors between the rows and turned the leaf-beds toward the same light. Warm air came from the kitchens; cooler air returned past wet stone, soil and green stems. At the salt-house, a measured cup of spring water boiled in a sealed vessel. Pure water returned by the lid and pipe. The residue went onto Balance pans, and a boy copied its weight. A woman compared the mark with seven earlier ones and covered the column as I passed. I stopped. The beam did not.\par
```
