## Unaided reconstruction after the single cold read

This reconstruction was written before reopening the chapter and is not to be revised after the fact.

The first remembered thing is Kailan's sentence, "First they erased me." Beneath the hearing chamber, Justice formally removes him from ordinary Mountain life: office, household, material, movement, sequence, name. He asks what his family will be told and receives only lawful wording they can repeat without breach. Shield takes him through routes without childhood memory. A Faith attendant gives a spare threshold blessing; the last door opens outward onto cold air and no wall.

The first live-room interruption asks him to give the whole road in order — desert, aircraft, water — because the listener will not stop one door short and still hopes to return to a summer and a promised swimming lesson. Kailan warns that the road ends badly.

The desert arrives as cold, height, smell, stars and bodily incompetence. Va-Sheva leads part of the route. In shelter, Kailan asks whether she knew of Sarah Blackwood; Sheva forbids the name and says Shield lies by teaching its own that training will let them see danger before it harms what they love. An unseen vehicle passes. Near the third dawn, a dead well answers a coded contact and opens into Horizon's lit, outsider-made passage.

Jean-Charles greets Va-Sheva with a pink-ribboned box of pistachio-and-rose sweets. Her face opens in a way Kailan has never seen, then closes when she notices the wardens. Va-Elise receives the sealed body/sequence, declares the old name unusable, and gives him Kylian and then Sa-Kylian: a provisional Candidate, a demotion that hurts. Clear spectacles become disguise. Horizon is described as a structure that keeps some Mountain facts from dying within it and outside facts from entering uncontained. Kailan learns that Ka-Sarah commands it.

After outsider clothes, Va-Sheva says Shield leaves him here. Kailan distinguishes her choosing the containment that left him breathing from choosing him; she agrees. He thanks her for choosing breathing. Her hand almost touches the covered knot, and she leaves.

The second live-room interruption confirms that the listener knows Jean-Charles, Elise, and Ka-Sarah, then presses Kailan to finish the road and give his first sight of Sarah.

There is practical instruction, then vehicle and aircraft travel. From a mirror Kailan sees Va-Sheva cry, rest a hand on Jean-Charles's chest, and turn back to the desert. Jean-Charles keeps Kailan steady through the flight while also asking about the well route and suggesting Elise need not record every answer. Switzerland presents mountains without secrecy, exposed houses and churches, and a house above a lake. Kailan barely sleeps and does not speak for eleven days.

On the twelfth day, he meets the older Sarah Blackwood as Ka-Sarah: white hair, shaking thumb, gold cross, wit sharp enough to make him dislike her immediately. She states that Exception means survival by procedure, not freedom, and lists the prohibitions that define his custody, including no family contact, no unsupervised movement or writing, and no churches. She wants him first to survive the outside, then perhaps tell Horizon what Light saw before Shield buried him. She warns that distance flatters prisons and homelands equally. Jean-Charles is to teach him the world but not write him down; Elise keeps the record.

Kailan laughs at Jean-Charles's grim joke after the meeting. At food, Jean-Charles playfully uses Mountain cup words — "Measured" and "Witnessed" — and immediately apologises when he sees they still wound. Elise offers Kailan a translated optics manual under supervision. The diagram of light entering water catches him; the promise that they will begin there "tomorrow" is the first future offered since the hearing.

At night, Kailan inventories the held and imposed names, the covered white knot and the absent dark cord. With the curtains open, he reads the light-and-water page while a house lamp lays a trembling path across the lake to his window until dawn.

The final live-room interruption turns the completed testimony into a present emergency. The listener says Kailan is free of the signed obligation and would send him to bed, except a drum in the next room keeps drawing the same five every eleven minutes. Kailan is certain it is a Keeper rising, but that requires a Keeper's death and none of the five he left has died. The remembered last pressure is the demand: which half is the lie — the rising or the absence of a death?
