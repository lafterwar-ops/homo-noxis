## Executive ruling

Chapter 16 works. It is not an information bridge in search of a scene; it is Kailan's enacted civil death, transport, second naming, and first compromised tomorrow. Its strongest choice is that the world outside does not free him. It changes the architecture of custody, teaches him different names for the walls, and places an optics manual inside his reach. The chapter's soul is the movement from "First they erased me" to a path of light crossing open water, followed by a live signal that makes return unavoidable.

The production need is narrow. Do not rebuild, expand, or reorder the chapter. Make a bounded source-provenance pass on three assertions that presently exceed or blur Kailan's recoverable knowledge, then protect the existing architecture. The most consequential defect is the closing certainty that no Keeper has died since Kailan left: he cannot know events inside the sealed Mountain after his removal. His directly available facts — the Keepers were young, none was known to be failing when he left, and the signal is repeating every eleven minutes — are already sufficient to force Chapter 17. Calibrating his claim to those facts strengthens rather than weakens the emergency because the contradiction then rests on evidence instead of retrospective omniscience.

## Governing diagnosis: reading experience

The chapter delivers fear, discovery, conflict, and consequence through a continuous first-person testimony rather than an external camera. Kailan's desire keeps changing without becoming incoherent: first he wants a lawful answer for his family, then some agency on the route, then proof of what Va-Sheva chose, then a name he can inhabit, then an explanation of Sarah Blackwood, and finally a tomorrow. Each partial gain arrives as another form of control. That is the chapter's governing pressure.

The reading experience has five clean movements:

1. Formal erasure converts the Trial's outcome into bodily and household consequences. The ration, bed, prayer line, family place, burial ledger, wrist cords, and withheld rite make exile concrete.
2. The desert and dry-well route make discovery physical. The sky has no stopping place; the route attacks Kailan's trained body; Horizon appears not as an abstract organisation but as an outsider passage beneath a dead well.
3. Intake replaces Mountain identity with a controlled Horizon identity. The transfer packet, signature, surrendered dark cord, spectacles, and name sequence turn administrative exposition into action.
4. Va-Sheva's departure and the eleven-day spoken silence carry the emotional cost into Switzerland. Sarah's recognition scene then converts mystery into an adversarial custodial relationship.
5. The supervised optics offer gives Kailan the smallest credible future. The lamp crossing water pays the chapter title without pretending freedom has arrived. The final room return then breaks that fragile stillness with the repeated rising and forces the live emergency.

The middle is deliberately procedural, but it does not stall: every object alters Kailan's rights, identity, knowledge, or available action. The bread scene also prevents Horizon from becoming a dossier-only environment and differentiates Jean-Charles's carelessness from Elise's exactness.

Keep the contrast with the Mountain, but bind it to what the house sounded like to Kailan — absence of pipe murmur, stone pulse, or chain — rather than knowledge of concealed construction. 3. **Closing room exchange (current line 531):** "no Keeper of my five has died since" asserts an unseen post-exile fact. Preserve Kailan's certainty that the *shape* is a rising and preserve his last-known evidence that the offices were young and no death was impending. Let the repeated eleven-minute sequence make serial deaths impossible. Chapter 17 already performs that inference more securely: death does not keep an appointment; a machine does.

Sarah's summary of the flight and refusal of speech is recoverable through Elise and Jean-Charles's custody reporting. Kailan's interpretations of faces, rooms, and gestures remain visibly his interpretations, not grants of interior access. No other material provenance defect requires reopening.
