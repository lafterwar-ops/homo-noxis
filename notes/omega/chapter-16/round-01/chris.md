## Plate 2 — Lived system

The strongest world evidence is maintenance and allocation. Justice does not merely pronounce exile: it redistributes office, household, material, movement, and sequence, then closes food, sleep, labour, worship, kinship, and burial records one line at a time. Faith gives the family repeatable wording because a household must continue living under the lie. Balance anticipates dehydration and disability by packing what Kailan will need and what Shield must carry if he becomes useless. Shield's route requires night travel, day shelter, watches, languages, coded infrastructure, and wardens whose work remains mostly unglamorous. Horizon receives a body through signatures, washes, feeds, clothes, transports, teaches, records, supervises, and restricts it.

The people who make the transfer possible are present as workers rather than scenery: the Justice witness reads and signs; the Faith attendant performs a bounded rite; Balance supplies measures; wardens carry reserve weight and answer the well; Elise controls documents, food, tools, books, and instruction; Jean-Charles handles translation, transit, social error, and practical teaching. Their labour makes custody inhabitable.

The bodily costs are also concrete: split lip, turned ankles, bent backs in low seams, dust in facial creases, interrupted sleep, aircraft pressure, grip on a vehicle seat, a trembling thumb, a knife withheld at a plate, and a bed too soft to trust. These details keep institutional consequence inside bodies.

The thin point is the eleven-day spoken silence. It is canonically present and correctly sourced in Kailan's testimony, but most of its duration is compressed into one sentence: little sleep, food placed before him, short slate answers erased under Jean-Charles's eyes. The slate is an excellent carrier because it makes silence labour and makes erasure repeat in miniature. If the chapter ever receives a Chris revision, this is the one place that could bear more pressure without inventing a subplot: allow a very small recurrence of already-present routines—food, slate, window, switch, door, or supervised object—to show time being maintained around his refusal. The recurrence must change cost or choice; scenic Swiss detail alone would be ornamental.

## Plate 3 — Formal pressure

The chapter remains Kailan's formal first-person testimony. Its three `room` blocks are live interruptions and must remain exactly live: the first demands the ordered road promised by the Chapter 15 handoff; the second confirms that the listener knows Horizon's people while insisting the account continue to Ka-Sarah; the third terminates the retrospective account by bringing the sounding drum into the present. These are not interchangeable labels. Name, title, classification, and permissible address move through different custodians, and the sequence must not be regularised into a single clean renaming.

- Water moves from rite, to ration, to ordinary plumbing, to lake, to the optical behaviour of light.

## One material law for any later rewrite

**Let every opening be operated by someone.**

The chapter's transfer should remain legible through the handling of apertures and access: Justice closes paths; Shield opens a door and a well; Horizon opens packets, removes one cord, fastens another identity onto the body, controls doors, tools, speech, and books; Elise orders the curtains left open; Kailan finally opens the manual himself; the drum opens the completed testimony back into the live crisis. Under this law, the eleven-day silence may be strengthened only through repeated use of objects and routines already present, while explanatory sentences after successful physical proofs may be reduced. The law must not become an answer key: an open window exposes as well as releases, a hidden well rescues as well as confines, and an opened book remains supervised.

This is a material substitution rule, not a request for new plot, new causal machinery, scene blocking, omniscient access, or a changed outcome.
