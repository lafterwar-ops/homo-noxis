## Unaided reconstruction after the single cold read

The first thing remembered is not the crisis but the transfer of telling: in the same small Greater Seal room, Paul puts away the return reel, threads a fresh reel, and makes Kailan say that this is his own account. Kailan then enters Justice by a route different from his old Faith trial. The party is admitted through five small rooms, each presenting a different problem: unequal apparent and real weights; a square-and-diagonal approximation; the frightening but weak evidence of a rare wolf test; several clerks' remainders reconciled into one number; and a forecast fitted through five years. The arithmetic is less persistent than the fact that Justice makes admission itself a sequence of proofs while the Mountain is being struck.

The hearing takes place across a gulf, with the five offices separated. Kailan reports the camp, bore, armed ring, overhead eye, lost stations, and route rediscovered by an external enemy. Each Keeper responds in the grain of an office: Light demands proof; Shield says the pattern is already military fact; Balance counts the sealed city's finite survival; Faith hears a second voice under the Mountain; Justice asks how an eleven-thousand-year secret was found and who Sarah is. Sarah gives her history in her own voice rather than being explained by Kailan. Va-Sheva vouches for Jean-Charles's conduct but not Sarah's innocence, sends Jean-Charles toward the breach, and he does not return with the warden. Captured records then prove that the force wants the operators and mechanism alive and intact, not simply the Mountain destroyed.

Justice cannot lawfully combine the five offices' knowledge, so the five open the Threshold Exception. Paul's illicit ascended mark and his independence from every office make him the admissible holder. The Keepers enter their own rooms one at a time; Paul and his recorder cross each threshold under exact custody. Balance gives survival counts, Light the aim and physical geometry, Shield the external threat and evidentiary degrees, Justice the law and the irreversibility of a possession finding, and Faith the exact heard timing that cannot safely be paraphrased. Kailan did not hear these depositions at the time. He knows them now because the sealed reel that heard them is turning in the small room from which he tells.

Paul returns and attacks necessity by naming alternatives: disable the weapon but not the life-supporting engine; destroy calculations that can be remade from the sky; spend the firing harmlessly though the Earth supplies an immovable aim; remove operators whose successors already exist; evacuate people who cannot cross the watched desert; expose the secret and start a race; negotiate with a force organized not to bargain; wait while the breach advances; destroy everything and deny possession. Sarah calls Roger through the captured field instrument. His courteous refusal, his appropriation of the Mountain's own plural-hands law, and the Mountain answering the enemy's sounding make negotiation feel less like a road than an exhibit.

The last alternative remains genuinely open: total ruin offers one chance in five that the weapon never rises and four in five that the state above recovers it without the Mountain's divided law. Paul makes Justice preserve both propositions: choosing certainty may be the worst act humanity has committed, and he cannot prove the other four chances safe. He puts his eleven-year-old nephew John beside the abstraction of eight billion people. Justice nevertheless finds irreversible possession and therefore command, not permission.

An external charge kills Ka-Elun before the ruling is dry and leaves Faith's succession damaged: the elder Veil is alive but cannot hear or respond, and the younger has not received the final note and instruction. Ka-Elun dies telling Kailan he should have been Faith. Ka-Raedin, seeing the outside light through the collapse, receives a terrible second witness. Justice says the ruling stands, although the office needed to perform Faith's part has just been broken.

Shield raises the Greater Seal. The remembered sensation is a city becoming solid around its people: falling gates, ancient drill made actual, bread and ordinary bells displaced by the warning. Jean-Charles, whose absence has remained suspicious, returns at the last held gate with almost no time left, torn and empty-handed, never saying where he was or what he did. Va-Sheva counts him alive and names him Mountain. Sarah does not withdraw her suspicion and does not condemn him without proof. The last remembered movement is upward to the Pendulum: a narrowing line of gold is shut out, the Pendulum keeps moving in darkness, and Paul's small outside machine keeps taking down its sound and everybody's breath.

### Wants, residue, and honest gaps

- Kailan wants the Mountain to fear the danger before it hates Sarah, and wants the five offices to produce a clean fact that will relieve him of the impossible choice.
- Paul wants a whole account without taking authority: first he makes testimony belong to its teller, then makes every alternative answerable, and finally refuses to let the surviving uncertainty be laundered out of the record.
- Sarah wants the Mountain used against the force that dispossessed her, yet supplies evidence against the safety of Paul's refusal road and accepts neither innocence nor neutrality.
- The offices want to remain lawful under conditions designed to make their separateness fatal; Justice in particular wants a ruling that no single office has illicitly assembled.
- The strongest emotional rise is Paul's naming of John and his refusal to reconcile the two opposed claims. The sharpest fall is Ka-Elun's death immediately after the supposedly final ruling. The most durable aftermath is the Seal's competence followed by the silent Pendulum.
- What seemed likely next: the Seal would be raised, but the command could not be completed cleanly because Faith's living transmission had been severed. Jean-Charles's unexplained interval would matter later. The reel would make these voices evidence beyond the moment without escaping the Seal.
- What felt certain without proof: Jean-Charles did something consequential during his absence; the charge that killed Ka-Elun may have exploited the Faith road deliberately; the one-in-five estimate is an honest bound rather than a conveniently exact fact.
- What remained unresolved: whether denial should have been chosen; whether Jean-Charles betrayed, repaired, or complicated the breach; whether Faith's missing note can be recovered; whether the ruling's legal irreversibility will survive practical impossibility; whether the final darkness is protection, burial, or both.
- Details that did not survive unaided: the exact order and wording of some intermediate replies; several numerical answers in the five rooms; some individual office-deposition particulars. The chapter's moral and sensory residue was much stronger than its arithmetic residue.
- First remembered: Paul threading the fresh reel and handing narration back to Kailan. Last remembered: the Pendulum and the reel continuing together after the sky closes.

## Text against residue

### Alive and correctly retained

- The narration's legality is unusually memorable because it is physical: one reel is set aside, a fresh one is threaded, Kailan's name is written, and Kailan says that the words are his. This should remain the chapter's first gate.
- The distinction between the five office roads and the five rooms on Justice's road survives. The first paragraph supplies the old Faith labyrinth as contrast; the open arches at the antechamber keep the other four roads visibly separate.
- The room answers themselves mostly fade, while the kinds of judgment persist. That is the useful residue: the reader later recognizes why Balance belongs with a false beam, Light with an approximation, Shield with a false positive, Justice with reconciled records, and Faith with a forecast whose pauses matter.
- Each Keeper's voice survives as a different limit on knowledge. The chapter does not collapse the five into generic councillors even when the alternatives accelerate.
- Paul's admissibility survives as a paradox: he may receive the whole because he cannot operate any part. His wound-mark, the recorder, the inventory at each threshold, and the rule against copying make that paradox tangible.
- The explicit source sentence before the depositions arrives at exactly the needed moment: Kailan says he did not hear them then and knows them because the sealed machine carried them into the present room. The quotes that follow therefore remain first-person recoverable testimony, not omniscient access.
- The alternative ladder survives in remembered order, especially after Roger's call. The text earns the denial road by letting Paul fail repeatedly before finding the one road Light cannot close.
- The irreversible distinction between permission and command survives both the private deposition and the public ruling. The repetition is productive because the first occurrence teaches the danger and the second makes it actual.
- Ka-Elun's death and the damage to Faith survive not merely as loss but as a practical wound: the elder Veil cannot hear or follow; the younger lacks the final instruction and the note beneath the note.
- Jean-Charles's unexplained absence and last-second return remain bounded by what was seen. Sarah's non-condemnation also remains exact: she has accumulated indications, has not found proof, and therefore says nothing when Shield names him Mountain.
- The final Pendulum is the dominant terminal image. The reel beside it prevents the darkness from becoming mute closure.

### Vanished despite apparent local emphasis

- Most exact arithmetic answers disappeared after the cold read: `239/169`, `10/343`, `4969`, and `12.6` were not durable. This is not a correction target. Their work is to make admission concrete and to seed the later rooms, not to become facts the reader must carry.
- Much of the deposited data also compressed into Paul's five headings. Again, the text itself supplies the intended memory carrier — “number, sight, threat, law, heard return” — so amplifying individual figures would compete with the moral choice rather than clarify it.
- Sa-Tavan's repeated handling of slate, wet clay, and exact words was less vivid in unaided recall than Paul's machine. The clay recovers at the ruling and at Ka-Elun's death, so the asymmetry has artistic return: the modern reel carries voices while the ancient clay makes the command immovable.

### Falsely but plausibly inferred

- The cold read produced a belief that the charge killing Ka-Elun may have been aimed down Faith's road. The narration explicitly refuses that knowledge: the hearing cannot know whether the line was targeted or merely found. The suspicion is productive because the physical precision invites it and the teller stops at the evidentiary boundary.
- Jean-Charles's missing interval feels consequential. The text neither declares betrayal nor supplies an alibi. His torn, empty-handed return supports several stories without authorizing one.
- The one-in-five figure feels like an estimate the reader is invited to trust. Light carefully calls it a bound, not proof, and Sarah separately confirms only the recoverability of buried knowledge. The remaining epistemic instability is essential.

### Understood too early

- “The charge that killed Ka-Elun came” announces the outcome before stone falls and before the rescue attempt. This is the one significant reveal-order choice exposed by the native pass. It converts the ensuing action from rescue suspense into foreknown helplessness. Because her death still lands as the chapter's sharpest emotional break, the choice is viable; it should change only if the desired sensation is a brief live hope that she might be recovered.

### Explained after already being inferred

- The paragraph beginning “From the antechamber we saw only Justice's arch open” repeats procedural evidence after the reader has already followed the five depositions: second paper after Balance, recorder check after Light, case custody after Shield, long absence with Justice, exact playback after Faith. These are good character actions, but they form the chapter's densest patch of procedural reassurance. If later compression is desired, this is the first place to test, while retaining the explicit source clause, isolation, item counts, no-copy rule, and case custody.
- The five headings immediately after that paragraph are not redundant. They perform the compression the reader needs before the alternatives begin and should remain.

### Productively unresolved

- The destructive refusal is never disproved, and the ruling does not make it morally disappear.
- Sarah is neither absolved nor reduced to a hidden saint. Her candour about still wanting the whole is what makes her useful and dangerous at once.
- Roger's claim that a state is a plural hand is intellectually intelligible and practically fraudulent; the text trusts the reader to feel both without an appended rebuttal.
- Ka-Elun's final words can be grief, succession, accusation, belated recognition, or instruction. The chapter does not force one reading.
- Jean-Charles may have saved, betrayed, observed, delayed, or done several things during the missing interval. Sarah's silence preserves the proper evidentiary limit.
- The Seal is simultaneously defence, imprisonment, continuity, and preparation for catastrophe. The final Pendulum holds those meanings without selecting one.

### Confusing without artistic return

No major instance survived the echo read. The chapter is cognitively dense, but its difficult material repeatedly returns through a physical carrier: room, arch, mark, reel, clay, handset, falling gate, or Pendulum. The reader need not retain every number to retain the question.

## Smallest residue correction

No mandatory manuscript correction is justified by this pass. The intended people, actions, questions, and terminal images already dominate residue, while the important unknowns have visible evidentiary boundaries.

Two optional tests are available for a later rewrite, but they should not be bundled automatically:

1. If Ka-Elun's fall is meant to create rescue suspense, withhold the statement that the charge killed her until Kailan and Shield uncover her. If it is meant to create dread and ritual helplessness, keep the current announcement.
2. If pace through the depositions proves heavy in a separate first-reader test, compress only the post-deposition return recap. Do not compress the five actual voices, the room correspondences, Kailan's source statement, isolation, custody, or the five-heading memory carrier.

The echo read predicts that either intervention would alter a narrow local sensation without changing plot. No third reread is warranted: further proposals would be driven by familiarity rather than the cold-read evidence.
