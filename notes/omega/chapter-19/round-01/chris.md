## Artifact reading: what the chapter is made of

### Material constellation

The chapter’s principal objects are not ornaments; they pass authority and injury between people.

- **Reel and recorder.** Paul sets aside the return reel, threads a fresh reel, names its box for Kailan, checks its motion, carries it through every deposition, keeps it turning after stone catches his leg, receives later ward reports upon it, and ends by recording the Pendulum and breathing. The reel is witness, custody chain, physical burden, protection against paraphrase, and the small surviving piece of the outside. It remains plural because it holds assent, refusal, uncertainty, death, labour, and silence without reconciling them.
- **Stone, dust, and apertures.** An ordinary joint becomes a door; five cut problems become admissions and then deposition rooms; strikes shake dust loose; Faith listens through the floor; a true-cut line becomes collapse; the Greater Seal turns passage into solid wall; the last aperture loses its gold. Stone alternately keeps, teaches, answers, wounds, records a boundary, and refuses access. It should never be reduced to “the Mountain equals tradition” or any other single key.
- **Hands.** Va-Sheva’s heel opens the first joint; Kailan’s hands are bound; Ka-Elun’s palm hears the floor; Ka-Leth’s two hands confess the limit of Justice; five hands unbar the Threshold Exception; Kailan’s cut mark in Paul’s palm admits him; Paul promises to hold and not act; Justice places a hand on wet clay; Kailan’s bound hands touch Ka-Elun’s face; ordinary people beat hands against slabs; a gate warden counts remaining time on one hand; Va-Sheva counts Jean-Charles alive with a hand on his chest. Their custody matters: wax is broken, quantities are tested, objects are counted at thresholds, no copy is made, pages retain separated headings, wet clay receives the irrevocable ruling, and the outside voice enters on a seized line.
