## Phase 2 — Four artifact plates

### Material constellation

- **Paper / record:** The archive sheets distinguish lawful answer from counterfeit recurrence. Jean-Charles's image brings the remote camp into the room. Paul's replaced reels make the recording apparatus itself evidence of penetration. The route papers then refuse totality, and the fixed record yields to a travelling one. Paper moves from proof, to compromised object, to insufficient object; by the lawn, “There is no book to hold them but you” completes that transformation without explaining it away.
- **Drum / sweep / voice:** The eleven-minute recurrence begins as apparent supernatural alarm, becomes the pace of ordinary survey labour, and remains dangerous because the Mountain's answer is true even when the question is counterfeit. The distinction between blow and answer carries more pressure than a generic claim about deception.
- **Hand / mark:** The hand moves from perception to culpability to access to covenant. Jean-Charles's touch after the evidence against him is especially strong because it can still be care, control, habit, or all three. It must not be assigned one stable translation.
- **Wire / reel / car:** Paul's three concrete carriers accumulate without merging. The hidden wire proves an extra live channel; the scratched reel proves substitution; the black car proves physical exchange but not contents or owner. Their separate evidentiary limits are a strength.
- **Route / threshold:** The same distributed routes that Sarah built to keep power from one owner can be joined behind her back by the state. Elise's compartmented departure then uses that principle defensively. This recurrence is material, not merely thematic: crews, papers, vehicles, timings, and bodies enact it.
- **Stone / needle / blood:** The last portable stone can mark one hand once. Its scarcity prevents the initiation from becoming a convenient key-making device. Blood makes Paul's change of role irreversible while the tiny wound keeps the transformation physically modest and therefore morally uneasy.

No major object is decorative. The nearest risks are the one-use bread metaphor and the listened-to-house simile: both already arrive with their proposed meaning attached. The wet grass and tree are not garnish; they relocate the record, return the earlier aborted rite, and expose the body to departure noise.
