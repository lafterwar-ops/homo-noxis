## Governing diagnosis

Chapter 6 works. Its soul is not the Airy pattern by itself but the collision between Kailan's appetite to enter, his appetite to be seen entering, and a system designed to make discovery contingent on discipline he only partly possesses. The Festival is simultaneously civic life, religious account, optical apparatus, cover for trespass, and a moving clock. That is unusually efficient architecture. The chapter also converts the five lessons in Chapter 5 from instruction into action: Kailan does not explain what he learned; he spends each lesson to reach a chamber whose proof breaks the model those lessons had seemed to complete.

The strongest reading experience comes from three escalating humiliations. Va-Maera first exposes Kailan's attempt to use partial knowledge as leverage. The hidden experiment then humiliates the ray model with an observed result. Va-Raedin finally identifies the more dangerous fault beneath the intellectual one: Kailan wants the telling and congratulation as much as the finding. These are not three versions of one rebuke. Faith attacks entitlement across an institutional wall; the chamber attacks an explanatory model; the teacher attacks the door through which future catastrophe can enter Kailan. Together they give the science moral and emotional consequence.

The only material weakness is bounded causal-spatial legibility at the transition from the packed Hall to the hidden route. Kailan leaves the northern tier, passes a Faith attendant in a side passage, follows the descending beam, and then manipulates a disc and floor stone at the pillar's foot. The prose does not quite establish whether that foot and apparatus are screened within a service route, behind the pillar, or exposed to the five-thousand-person Hall. Because the chapter has just made Shield, Justice, Faith, and the crowd physically present, this moment can briefly read as authorial convenience rather than successful opportunism. One precise spatial anchor would close the gap.

A lesser pacing pressure occurs across the long search-and-scripture middle before the Festival begins. The Covenant text, Paul's adjacent Genesis comparison, and the ordinary Festival preparations are all load-bearing and should not be cut as categories: the first establishes the Mountain's single authorised account, the second creates the live comparison that Kailan later generalises, and the Festival details make the Mountain inhabited rather than constructed solely as a puzzle. The recoverable compression lies instead in repeated connective statements that say time passed, no clue appeared, or Kailan continued searching. A small payment pass there could sharpen arrival at the Festival without sacrificing form or world.

No omniscient narration was found. Prose outside `room` remains recoverable as Kailan's sustained testimony to Paul. Descriptions are available to his sight, hearing, memory, public instruction, or explicitly quoted documents and rites. His uncertain claims about Va-Raedin's earlier hunger are framed as questions, and the optical explanations arise from what Kailan observes or is told in the chamber. Any production intervention must preserve that source discipline and must not turn institutional inferences into external fact.

## Reading-experience trace

1. **Interpretive permission.** Kailan uses the five solids to force the Greek wedding into the Mountain's five-office grammar. Paul neither certifies nor dismisses the reading; he teaches that another account may coexist and supplies Eris's exclusion as motive. This answers the Chapter 1 gravure deferral while making plurality, exclusion, and keeperhood active concerns.
2. **The office refuses entitlement.** Ka-Syphiron defines advancement as finding rather than receiving. Va-Raedin defeats Kailan's attempts to follow him. These beats establish that the hidden path must be earned through the student's own reconstruction.
3. **Kailan crosses the wrong wall.** He approaches Faith and implicitly weaponises a glimpse of mechanism. Va-Maera correctly names what he is doing, strips him of clever ambiguity, and imposes a credible boundary. Kailan leaves shaken rather than secretly triumphant.
4. **Single account versus portable text.** The Mountain's five-day Genesis is delivered as a complete, publicly embodied account. Paul's book makes comparison physically possible without declaring either text original. Kailan immediately sees that the book has multiplied rather than abolished keepers. The room argument therefore complicates, rather than merely celebrates, open access.
5. **The Mountain lives before it becomes machinery.** Storage, loaves, Ventsingers, service-knot games, cloth counts, vents, tallow, matching rolls, and liturgy give the Festival labour, comedy, desire, record, and worship. The later apparatus is embedded in a society.
6. **The clue becomes a deadline.** Sunlight arrives at the prescribed words and discloses the first hidden reflection. Kailan lies to pass the attendant and spends the five lessons in order. Each successful operation changes available action; the moving sun prevents leisurely puzzle solving.
7. **Proof becomes reversal.** The token, carried since Chapter 2 and experimentally mishandled in Chapter 4, finally becomes the aperture for which its rings were made. What seemed an emblem of water around light becomes physical evidence that light behaves as a wave. The earlier image is paid in action rather than explained retroactively.
8. **Achievement becomes indictment.** Raedin confirms the result, limits its licence, repairs the disturbed apparatus, and identifies Kailan's desire to be seen. This prevents the chapter from ending as a clean scientific triumph.
9. **Social consequence and suspended status.** Sela reads the absence of a mark, attendants stop talking, and Kailan is made to wait. The summons forces Chapter 7. The closing interview room also carries Paul's Shadow plot forward through Kailan's lesson about waiting for a change in condition.
