## Phase 2 — Four artifact plates

### Plate 1: Material constellation

**Light** begins under deliberate control in the Keeper's lamp, becomes scattered evidence in dirty mirrors, becomes communal rite in the Festival apertures, becomes a red pool and a small burning sun during Kailan's descent, then ceases to behave as the line his office taught him. The wall rings persist after the red centre goes out, but only as black and white stone under Va-Raedin's lamp. Kailan reports every stage; no external narrator certifies its meaning.

**Water** moves from covered ritual vessel and Genesis spring to beer, stored food, optical tank, diluted reflecting surface, and old water beside the service stair. It is both subsistence and instrument. The strongest return is Kailan's own comparison of the light closing around obstruction to water closing around a stone; the comparison remains his, and the object continues to exceed it.

**Seals, counts, and witness marks** move from public provisioning into forbidden experiment and then institutional repair. Balance counts bread and loss; Light counts cloths and sheets; the hidden jar's witness line is broken by Kailan and redrawn by Va-Raedin. This is the chapter's most morally charged material sequence because the same culture that records custody also preserves a discovery by erasing evidence of its breach.

**Food** moves from strategic reserve to comic public relay to waste diverted into insect rooms, then to the end piece Sela quietly gives Kailan and the lunch trays entering Paul's room. Food keeps ceremony, secrecy, affection, and interview going without needing a stated symbolic key.

**Thresholds** recur as Eris's excluded hand, the Hall built with no place for another telling, the Keeper's locked-door lesson, Va-Maera's refusal, the hidden floor stone, the beam turned around the Mountain, the door clicked open by Kailan's finger, and the old-gallery door behind soot-black cloth. ### Plate 2: Lived system

This labour also bears consequence. Cleaning and opening the apertures makes the beam available. Recordkeeping establishes the witness line Kailan breaks. After the chamber, copying inventories replaces exceptional instruction; the sheets are counted at both hands. Sela's food service carries rumour and intimate knowledge to his table. These are not independent texture inserts: the institutions can produce revelation only because ordinary hands maintain them, and those same hands become the means of surveillance and circulation afterward.

The least integrated lived details are not failures, but they should not be expanded merely for colour. ### Plate 4: Moral topology

1. **Kailan's principle in the room:** no office owns the final reading; an excluded hand may place an object inside and let others reveal themselves. **Act justified by it:** he values competing accounts and interprets the five solids for himself. **Person placed opposite it:** Kailan later lies under the authority of “Light service,” breaks Balance's sealed witness line, and asks that the telling of the discovery be his. 2. **Paul's principle:** anyone may ask, write, contradict, and read; withholding hands are tempted to call themselves responsible. **Act justified by it:** he supplies media, fetches a Bible, speaks its English into Kailan's language, and promises translation. **Person placed opposite it:** Kailan cannot read the object without Paul's chosen voice and future labour. Kailan's “You have multiplied him” places Paul inside the custody problem rather than outside it.

3. **Va-Maera's principle:** little knowledge becomes sinful when used as leverage; protected arrangements are not proof that the worshipper is a fool. **Act justified by it:** she denies Kailan access and threatens consequence to defend Faith's boundary. **Person placed opposite it:** by Kailan's report, she condemns weaponized knowledge through a recital of secret names, curses, punishments, and institutional power used as a weapon against him. 4. **Va-Raedin's principle:** a door may protect either side; a future Veil must have his overconfidence broken before receiving greater instruments. **Act justified by it:** he lets the Chamber preserve a destabilizing proof but forbids Kailan to reproduce it. **Person placed opposite it:** Va-Raedin restores a wax seal and redraws a witness line after teaching Kailan that the office needs a truth beyond its ordinary instrument. 5. **The offices' public principle:** measurement, witness, stored provision, and ritual keep the Mountain steady. **Act justified by it:** loaves, cloths, vents, seed, jars, and losses are counted. **Person placed opposite it:** Kailan's discovery depends on breaking a counted boundary, while Va-Raedin's secrecy depends on making that break disappear. The public system is neither mere hypocrisy nor simple safety; the seal can carry both.

- **Cycle 1:** the custody law successfully joined the chapter's objects but risked turning every object into the same symbol. The correction is grammatical rather than symbolic: objects need only show different acts of transfer, refusal, damage, repair, and residue. Bread is not “knowledge”; the book is not the witness line; Sela's end piece is not the token.
- **Cycle 2:** the law was tested against ordinary life and moral reversal. Labour remains only where it changes survival, access, record, or relationship. The moral reversals arise from speakers' own principles and acts rather than punishment imposed by a narrator. The strongest physical proofs are allowed to stop.

Protected ambiguities:

- The ringed wall can be scientific proof, inherited craft, institutional humility, and a controlled initiation without becoming an answer to which reading is correct.
