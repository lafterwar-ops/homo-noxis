## Native artifact reading

The chapter is made first of handled things. Paper arrives already marked by another person: Edward's scored margin and crossed-out rates; Martinez's filing card; the folded instruction left on the pump; Sarah's drum-paper; the sealed instructions in three countries; the word *Horizon* on an outer fold; the photograph written on in two hands; files, dockets, logs, cores, catalogues, route sheets, the letter beside Paul's paper, the broken pencil, and finally the fresh drum trace placed under the lamp. These papers do not share a single moral value. Some threaten, some authorize, some conceal, some recruit, and the last refuses to prove more than its shape.

Hands are the other continuous material. Edward's stroke scores paper. Martinez breaks his own rule by taking Sarah's arm. Amastan lays a palm on unmarked rock, closes a hand on her sleeve, withholds bread, passes bread, carries the first message, and inspects seals. Ka-Xhian rests scarred authority in two hands on stone. Unknown hands own a camera and receive its record. Two Shield hands name the photograph. Five hands are meant to prevent one; Sarah builds the one hand that can interrupt five. Her right hand shakes, her left closes over it, and Paul's hand keeps the pencil point-down until the graphite breaks. The recurrence is already doing the ethical work before Sarah names herself as the hand that closes.

Stone is never merely landscape. It is unreadable surface, seam, table, archive threshold, and the hard pale background of the photograph. It moves from natural obstruction to operated door to institutional record. Bread, oil, wet stone, leather, flour, grit, graphite, water, lithium, glass, and broken vehicle metal keep the chapter from becoming a dossier made only of claims.

The inhabited system is audible behind the formal account: an animal breathing after engines cease; bread baked for thousands hidden behind stone; a child reciting numbers and another voice correcting the fifth; a woman required to carry food and then lie to her household; someone trimming the lamp twice; clerks keeping cases alive when governments wait; lawyers, mechanics, drivers, radio operators, readers, physicians, archivists, and field offices maintaining structures whose full uses they do not possess. Secrecy here consumes ordinary work. It also accidentally commits itself to ordinary lives.

The principal absences are equally material: the route never put into a machine, the archive Sarah does not enter, the mechanism neither seen nor named, the owner beyond the second camera, the reason Ka-Xhian judged the Exception worth its risk, and the cause of the two identical traces. These are apertures, not omissions to be filled by explanation.

### Plate II — Lived system

The Mountain is populated before it is explained. Breath, leather, cool air, lamp-oil, wet stone, bread, children, correction, laughter, a tray, and lamp trimming make five thousand lives more than an abstract number. The child corrected on the fifth belongs both to ordinary instruction and to the chapter's five-fold form, but the child must not be reduced to a cipher for the Keepers. The bread-bearer's required household lie is the cleanest small social consequence in the chapter: policy reaches a kitchen and changes what one person may say after work.

### Plate III — Formal pressure

The chapter contains ten rooms and must remain ten. Outside them, Sarah owns the testimony. Inside them, speech is attributable through turn, address, and established voice; the rooms operate as contested custody of a statement or object. Kailan takes the inherited name, the photograph, the gate, and the knock out of Sarah's sole keeping. Paul takes the camera evidence and his letter out of hers. Sarah concedes some claims and refuses others. No neutral narrative voice is needed or admissible.

Numbered recurrence performs escalation:

- “one” moves from one driver, one channel, and one word toward one Keeper removed, one hand, and one time;
- “three” binds hands, countries, oaths, companies, and promises without making them equivalent;
- “five” appears as permitted witnesses, separated offices, hands needed for unanimity, and the child corrected on the fifth;
- “second” changes meaning on every return: the second answer is shorter, the second camera makes the room unsafe, the second seal enters as wreckage, a second reader is ordered for independence, and the second knock invalidates the first available account.

The ellipses also change owner and pressure. Kailan pauses over Ka-Xhian and the drawer; Sarah pauses before the truer confession and Paul's recruitment answer; the household pauses as the first and second traces arrive. Silence is not atmospheric. It marks the point where the current holder of an account can no longer pass it on unchanged.

Long procedural paragraphs are appropriate when they accumulate the maintenance of concealment. They weaken only when they supply a category and then restate the category's meaning. The final room correctly shortens: paper, lamp, commands, correction, a second trace, and the handoff. Preserve its endpoint. It does not close interpretation; it transfers unfinished testimony into immediate work.
