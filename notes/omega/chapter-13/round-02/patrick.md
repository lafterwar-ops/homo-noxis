## Native performance finding

The chapter plays. Its exposition is not neutral information when spoken: Sarah uses documents, remembered surfaces, operational detail, and finally self-indictment to keep control of the terms on which Kailan and Paul may judge her. Kailan repeatedly refuses those terms. Paul waits until the confession has exposed the method and then applies that method to his own presence in the room. The interruption does not rescue Sarah from judgment; it forces her exposed machinery into motion.

The best pressure changes are carried by things bodies can do: Edward's pen scoring paper, Martinez taking an arm he would normally never touch, Amastan stopping Sarah with one hand on her sleeve, Ka-Xhian withholding bread by a nod, Kailan forcing the photograph to be spoken in front of its subject, Sarah closing her left hand over the right, Paul's pencil breaking, the messenger overruling “Not now,” and the live paper drawing a second time under the lamp. ## Continuous testimony: playable action units

### Edward's margin to Amastan's invitation

Sarah's immediate objective is to distinguish her method from her father's purchasing without claiming innocence. The scored margin, doubled rates, Martinez's filing card, the folded paper, and the absence of radio and instrument case do the work. The turn is not that Sarah finds Amastan; it is that the reply sets conditions and she obeys them. The line “I went” is an exit action in two words.

The sentence beginning “The first two of Martinez's seven years of refusals” catches in the mouth because the possessive makes the refusals briefly sound like Martinez's refusals rather than refusals received by him. This is a local clarity defect, not a missing fact. A performance revision should keep the whole action unit and simply let Martinez own the labour: “Martinez spent the first two of those seven years putting a face and a tent to the name.”

### Pump, crossing, and seam

Sarah's objective is to prove that entry remained in other hands. Martinez's touch, the engine noise, the boy taking and then surrendering the wheel, the animal, and Amastan's hand flat on the rock successively strip away Sarah's apparatus. The decisive blocking is one foot in and one foot out, with Amastan holding her sleeve until a voice inside permits release. This is the chapter's cleanest embodied statement of the bargain and should not be explained further.

The Martinez warning is emotionally right but overpacked into one inhalation. The full beat would speak more dangerously as: “At the pump Martinez took my arm. He never touched anyone. Under the engine noise, he told me that whatever kept the country ahead had kept it since before Rome. It wanted no money and no talking to. He could not follow, and would not, for anything I owned.” The split lets the anomalous touch register before information resumes. It preserves his objective, affection, and refusal.

### Stone table bargain

Ka-Xhian's objective is to neutralise Sarah without activating the instructions she has already placed abroad. Sarah's objective is to survive without surrendering the usefulness that makes survival possible. Their tactics are exact: he calls the instructions a knife; she insists the knife predates knowledge of the door; he converts her access to surveys and governments into a service; she demands warning without access to the mechanism.

The bread is the physical compact. Amastan first places Sarah's piece beyond reach and transfers it only after Ka-Xhian inclines his head. The flour line between their hands performs the arrangement before the three oaths verbalise it. The later lift of Ka-Xhian's hand and Amastan's opening of the door gives “The Exception protects itself” a threat carrier while leaving its mechanism unknown.

### Horizon's growth through the Foundation

Sarah tries to keep Horizon inside “liaison,” then “monitoring,” then “management of secrecy.” Each noun is a smaller moral word than the growing apparatus it covers. The Foundation paragraphs are playable if Sarah treats the genuine protections as a defence offered directly to Kailan and Paul, then lets “The screen around the Mountain acquired obligations of its own” overturn the defence into a fact she did not control.

One claim exceeds recoverable testimony: peoples “are alive today and would not otherwise be.” Sarah can know who remains alive and can give evidence of actions and outcomes; she cannot observe the counterfactual world. A bounded source-safe version should preserve her conviction while admitting its status: “Some of those peoples are alive today. I believe they would not be without that work; the files can prove what we did, not the world in which we failed to do it.” This strengthens Sarah's voice because she has just demanded that Kailan not make metaphor into evidence.

### Race, White Six, and the inward turn

The route pinned to canvas, three radio sets, repeated bearing, damaged steering, dismantled vehicle, sealed cores, and lithium assay keep the race account tactile. Sarah's objective is first to show Horizon doing the work Shield asked of it. The testimony then changes tactic: routes, mapping, identification, and contingency expose how external guardianship became an inward-reaching hand.

The survey paragraph is not a dead anecdote even though Kailan calls it “bricks.” In performance it is Sarah's last refuge in procedural mastery. She should accelerate through “two years and eleven hands,” take satisfaction in each manipulation, and allow Kailan's interruption to expose that satisfaction. Cutting the example would weaken his accusation; adding more examples would flatten it.

Two statements need a visible source only if the safety veto is applied strictly at sentence level: that White Six's pieces remain arranged in Shield's archive, and that the Mountain's children were told both seals were drills. Sarah had Amastan's channel and can recoverably know both, but the page does not cue the carrier there. A minimal future repair would attach “Amastan later reported” to that action unit. Do not specify which photograph Kailan held or where Sarah's authorised copy ultimately went; the current distinction between Shield's archival photograph, Sarah's authorised copy, and Kailan's later contact is useful custody ambiguity.

## Voice and microprosody reperformance

- **Sarah** speaks in long controlled clauses when procedure shields her, then becomes shortest at admission: “I went,” “No,” “Yes,” “None of it was chance.” Her repeated corrections—fact versus metaphor, shape versus owner, reason given versus truer reason—are character action. The long clauses should not all receive the same stately cadence: the survey requires acceleration, the oaths require measured enumeration, and the operational commands after the second knock require clipped breath without loss of syntax.
- **Kailan** speaks from remembered office and embodied analogy. His metaphors always give the listener something to answer: picked lock, hand on door, mirror in pillar, bricks, one hand, gate. The ellipses are not generic hesitation. In Rooms 3 and 5 they are the time needed for inherited knowledge to become personal injury; in Room 8 they are horror before accusation.
- **Paul** avoids metaphor and polices evidence. His inventory of recruitment facts is deliberately syntactically incomplete until it becomes a question. “Answer it” is his only necessary force. Giving him another speech after the confession would dilute the pencil action and his consequential silence during the alarm.
- **Ka-Xhian** speaks in compressed bargains and reversals: lie/useful lie, knife/door, danger/work, hunter/guard, trust/sleep. He never explains Shield for the reader after the listener has enough to answer. His damaged eyes, face tracking sound, hands on stone, close nails, and grit under the thumb keep authority bodily rather than mystical.
- **Martinez** has one breath too many in the current pump sentence, but his shame-faced indirection is distinct: he speaks affection to the pump and frames it as a breach of trade rules.
- **The messenger** is correctly allowed to violate deference once. “Not now.” / “Now.” is the fastest power reversal in the chapter. After that, “Madame” returns, but the paper keeps authority.

Room 7 requires two different breaths inside Sarah's long answer. Up to “That is the veto,” she is persuading. After Kailan's second prompt and the silence, “The truer one is that I wanted it” is confession. Room 8 should then be almost uninflected: Sarah's lack of defence is the pressure. The final room should preserve the clash between “Then it has begun” and “nothing has happened that we can yet name.” The first is Sarah's involuntary threshold response; the second is her recovered operational discipline. Their difference is playable, not a contradiction to smooth away.
