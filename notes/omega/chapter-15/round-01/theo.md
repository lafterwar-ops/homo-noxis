## Unaided reconstruction — sealed before reopening

The first memory to surface is the sentence that the ground kept a copy. The room begins in the irritation of another interrupted account. Sarah has stopped the telling; Mr Morgan is standing with the machine under his arm and seems unable to settle. He has delayed opening a folder until he could catch a lie and, having failed to catch one, places two old traces before Kailan. Sarah presses the question Kailan’s immediate recognition of the drums has created: how did he already know the figure meant a Keeper rising?

Kailan remembers two Keeper changes, one when he was fifteen and another four years later. The five left through five private arches along routes forbidden to a Veil. After long waiting, the Mountain answered through the deepest floor: five evenly separated strokes and a slower answer after another interval. He felt the force through his feet before hearing it, saw dust loosen from a pillar, heard an old woman bless the Mountain for settling, counted, and knowingly accepted the holy explanation for something made.

The folder contains paper traces from those two mornings. Morgan is careful not to claim perfect superposition: separate instruments and hiss prevent that. What survives is agreement among the ordered figure, the desert source, and the two dates. Kailan recognizes the Bowl of the Last Prayer as the hidden chamber. The cavity muffles the blow but leaks a thin seismic whisper. Morgan’s trade learned how to listen for such whispers; once he knew where and when to look, he kept them.

The emotional centre I retain is Kailan seeing the deaths he witnessed turned into marks on paper in a cold northern house: the wounded woman, the old teacher entering the stone box, and his own two mornings held in somebody else’s case. From there he understands the larger exposure. Matching outside signals to Horizon’s names reveals not only that the Mountain exists but its calendar of deaths, changes of office, and vulnerable open hours. Morgan confirms that this is exactly how he counted them: drums for dates, Horizon for names.

The present signal then changes the meaning of the archive. The next-room drums have drawn the readiness figure twice inside one hour. Kailan says one event might be emergency readiness, but two cannot be two lawful rites; the act cannot be spent twice in an hour, like a man cannot be born twice in one night. Somebody has struck the guarded thing, made the Mountain speak, or copied its word. The room cannot identify the hand or even decide whether the action came from inside the walls, outside in open ground, from one of the five, or from a counterfeiter. The answer is on the road. The retained endpoint is Kailan promising to resume from the well through which he was led when his name was taken.
