## Artifact-only first read

The chapter is made from handled evidence. Stone is shaved, engraved, crossed, polished, sealed, stained, repaired, and made to hide its own joins. Light is trimmed, blackened, returned, focused, extinguished, hooded, photographed, and stored as memory. These measures do not abolish uncertainty. They define what a speaker can responsibly say and expose each point at which that speaker goes beyond it.
