## Governing diagnosis

Chapter 10 works. Its governing achievement is that it makes epistemology into dangerous action. Kailan begins with a problem whose answer can be measured, repeated, back-tested, predicted, sealed, and independently observed. He then follows the same habits into evidence that cannot be made safe by method: a hidden route, a protected archive, a record joining Sarah, Amastan, and Ka-Xhian, a room built to swallow voices, Va-Sheva's ribbon in blood, and a present-day camera whose memory is physically carried away. The chapter's soul is not the information disclosed. It is the cost of deciding what counts as proof when proof itself can endanger the people named by it.

The opening room supplies the moral grammar. Eve crosses a boundary by her own choice; Kailan asks Paul to apply that question to him; the ensuing account repeatedly gives Kailan a chance to stop. Va-Maera then prevents a simple reason-versus-Faith story. She accepts predictive evidence while preserving the inherited false heading so that later readers can see both the record and the correction. Her method becomes the chapter's ethical counterweight: preserve uncertainty honestly rather than erasing it, but also do not mistake custody for innocence. Kailan obeys the evidentiary part of that lesson and violates its custodial part when he takes the torn strip.

The reading experience has discovery, adventure, fear, reversal, consequence, and character choice. The escalation is unusually clean: intellectual irritation becomes mastery; mastery earns access; access produces a small optical anomaly; controlled observation creates trespass; trespass yields an archive; the archive answers one question by opening several worse ones; the warning mechanism turns discovery into immediate danger; the ribbon changes institutional suspicion into intimate dread; escape converts knowledge into secrecy; the closing room proves that the old system of hidden observation remains active in a new form.

The chapter does not need structural invention. It has already completed the perpendicular-expansion work John would normally request: scientific demonstration, institutional argument, physical route, ordinary procedure, optical clue, timed obstacle, forbidden landscape, archive objects, documentary evidence, live opposition, personal stake, escape problem, moral non-choice, and a present-day echo all solve different aspects of the dramatic problem. Adding new people, objects, chases, explanations, or revelations would make the chapter less exact.

The remaining defects are bounded rather than architectural:

1. The three successive apertures—the Mountain-to-desert slab, the desert-to-archive mechanism, and the archive-to-Mountain service stone—are physically recoverable, but the repeated vocabulary of stone, slab, door, outer stone, hidden stone, and outer mechanism makes the route harder to hold than the action warrants.
2. The archive middle deliberately establishes breadth and historical depth, but the accumulation briefly flattens the pulse between first entry and the warning response. The objects and documents are load-bearing; the opportunity is compression of connective interpretation, not removal of discoveries.
3. Kailan's statement that he summoned the warden is a persuasive inference. The adjacent claim that the packet, clothes, and lamp had already been prepared before he touched the door is not directly witnessed at that time. Retrospective testimony does not by itself supply that temporal knowledge. The source should remain visibly inferential unless a later learned source is named.
4. The warden's lamp rests on Kailan's hiding place and moves on. The moment is thrilling, but a first reading can register the survival as authorial permission because the physical reason the panels defeat inspection is only implicit.
5. The exact pendulum relation and the chapter's April 2/Good Friday alignment require external technical and calendar validation in production. The chapter's internal reasoning is intelligible, but this firewall did not permit external verification or adjacent-canon audit.

## Fundamental dramatic question and chapter function

**Dramatic question:** When the knowledge Kailan desires can be obtained only by crossing a protected threshold, will he stop at the limit he chose in advance—and, once the evidence becomes human rather than abstract, what will he preserve, take, leave, or conceal?

**Chapter function:** Convert Kailan's scientific formation into his first self-authorised breach; disclose that the Mountain's isolation is managed, documented, and entangled with Blackwood; create personal uncertainty around Va-Sheva; place a stolen record in Kailan's custody; establish that Shield knows a breach occurred without knowing its author; and join the historical hidden-eye system to the present second camera and physical dead drop.

**Current furniture that serves the function and is therefore load-bearing:** the Genesis exchange; the Pendulum calculation; Va-Maera's back-test and sealed prediction; the twice-weekly route; reflected light; the mourning window; two oil marks; the weapon deliberately left behind; the exterior night and moving light; the polished plate and Shield mark; the archive's layered correction practice; the altered lens; the Blackwood files; Sarah and Amastan's photograph; Reggane glass; the blank map; the Threshold Exception fragment; the divided machine; the warning tongue and warden; the sound-dead room; the blood-held ribbon; the strip taken and ribbon left; the still-star escape; Kailan's silence; Sarah's promised arrival; Martinez's card exchange and Kailan's photograph.

## Causal map

1. Ka-Raedin assigns the Pendulum because Kailan's training must be tested against a problem he did not choose.
2. Kailan combines repeated drift, the fixed star, the engraved circle, and the unit-stick shadow; the agreement of the measurements lets him infer that the Mountain turns beneath the Pendulum.
3. Ka-Raedin verifies the arithmetic and makes Kailan a courier between Light and Faith because few people can understand the proof and Faith owns the long observational record.
4. Va-Maera back-tests his rule against old observations, includes a deliberately inconvenient repaired-bearing case, then requires a sealed prediction. The successful prediction earns continued exchange without forcing her to erase inherited language.
5. The recurring courier route exposes Kailan to a point of returned light in a cold vent. His training lets him distinguish reflection from self-generated light and test the geometry over twelve nights.
6. A child's mourning pulls Faith and Shield away from the relevant paths. Kailan turns that human loss into an access window, sets two fuel limits, and enters a service passage whose repairs prove repeated use by different bodies.
7. Outside, scale, wind, and darkness defeat Mountain habits. A moving light expands the unknown. Kailan relocates the reflected-light geometry, discovers Shield's mark, reaches his first fuel limit, and nevertheless opens the second mechanism.
8. The archive's objects and tags establish long custody, disagreement preserved in layers, and controlled suppression. The altered lens both embodies unequal capability and lets Kailan read the higher labels.
9. The Blackwood files establish representation without direct presence; the Mountain file, photograph, Reggane evidence, blank map, interior route, and torn exception copy join concealment, outside destructive power, Sarah, Amastan, and Ka-Xhian. Kailan takes the loose strip because it is portable proof whose absence may be less immediately visible.
10. The divided machine and warm lamp prove recent or continuing operational use. The warning tongue has transmitted the opening; a prepared Shield warden enters, nearly detects Kailan, then goes outside.
11. Kailan chooses the now-empty inward route rather than terrain where the warden has advantage. The side chamber's smell leads him to a voice-killing room, restraint seat, old blood, and Va-Sheva's ribbon. The ribbon proves presence only; it does not prove role or victimhood. Kailan leaves it because taking it would disclose entry and because he cannot identify a safe recipient.
12. The second fuel mark forces withdrawal. Kailan exits through the archive, avoids the warden's hooded search, and uses the still star plus his outward count to recover the first entrance.
13. He restores what traces he can and withholds the discovery from both Ka-Raedin and Va-Sheva because each potential disclosure could destroy the evidence or expose him before he has a second witness. The stolen strip remains against his body.
14. In the present room, Paul announces Sarah's imminent testimony. Kailan then supplies a parallel current chain: Martinez removes a camera card, leaves it beneath a newspaper, and an unknown disciplined man collects it. Kailan preserves a photograph rather than interfering, and he and Paul postpone confrontation until Sarah can test the hypothesis.

Nothing in that chain requires a new scene. The repairs needed are local proofs that make Steps 7, 10, and 12 easier to spatially simulate and Step 10 more exact about what Kailan knows versus infers.

## Room, teller, and source map

- The opening `room` is direct exchange between Kailan and Paul. Paul supplied the translated Genesis pages; Kailan compares their contents with his inherited account; each interpretation is presented as argument, not narrator-certified truth.
- After the first `\end{room}`, Kailan remains the established speaker giving sustained testimony to Paul. The account is first-person memory. Its calculations, actions, sensory impressions, guesses, and later self-judgments are recoverable from Kailan's own perception and reasoning.
- Ka-Raedin and Va-Maera's words are reported as remembered speech within that testimony. Documents, tags, photographs, maps, seals, and the torn strip are explicitly identified preserved sources.
- The text is disciplined about uncertainty at its most important points: Kailan cannot date the footprints; does not invent a category for the moving light; cannot decide what the female figure's forms mean; does not know the Threshold Exception's terms; cannot decide what the ribbon proves; cannot know whether the warden saw the tracks.
- One sentence cluster presses beyond the source boundary: the claim that the warden's packet, exterior clothes, and lamp were already prepared *before* Kailan touched the door. Kailan can observe the sealed packet, clothing, and residual lamp warmth, and he can infer preparation or a planned exterior task. Without a named later disclosure, he cannot know the exact preparation time. Production should mark the inference as inference; it should not add an external camera.
- “I had summoned him” and the warning-tongue explanation are acceptable as Kailan's conclusion only if their grammatical framing continues to register as his causal reading of the paired tongues and the response. They must not harden into neutral fact without additional source.
- The closing `room` returns to direct exchange. Kailan directly witnessed Martinez, the dead drop, and the unknown collector. His statements about the collector's training are character inference from gait, route, posture, and economy, and the Roger conclusion is explicitly conditional on Sarah's response.

The room exit therefore preserves established testimony. It never licenses an external authorial camera.
