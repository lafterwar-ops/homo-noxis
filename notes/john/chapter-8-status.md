## Governing diagnosis

The chapter already owns a powerful dramatic object: a tiny spectral line prepares the eye for a Hall whose total blaze is built around a ritual killing. Its soul is the collision of beauty, succession and cost. The present draft weakens that object in three ways. First, Kailan's sustained account repeatedly reports the Hall's memory, every worshipper's past, hidden lamp controls, collective emotion and ritual mechanics he cannot directly know. Second, Paul's long scientific explanation delays the ceremony and overstates what individual flame salts would show through this apparatus. Third, the death becomes a distant tableau: Kailan names an unseen liver wound and the crowd's gladness, but his own witnessed physical response scarcely enters the frame. Chapter 9 owns the explicit grief and recovery, so Chapter 8 must make the murder visible without interpreting it afterward.
