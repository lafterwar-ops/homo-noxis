## Governing diagnosis

Chapter 3 already has a soul, event and consequence. Kailan enters hungry for direct proof yet still wants the open world to answer him personally. The Oasis first defeats the obedient Genesis image through scale, work and living disorder; the Book of Blood then gives Paul and Kailan two opposed accounts of the same inheritance; Va-Sheva embodies both remembered intimacy and Shield's grown authority; and Kailan's private flash triggers a public containment whose sealed evidence crosses directly into Chapter 4 and whose official `mirage` account later helps condemn him at the Trial. The architecture map's old ruling that Chapter 3 has no irreversible event is false: the turned marker is held, the pale strand is sealed, the route remains under investigation, and Kailan becomes an identified witness.

The demonstrated defects are execution defects. Several narrated sentences claim other minds or institutional motives: the grieving men's reason for access, Va-Sheva's intended kindness, a whole office inheriting shame, the Balance workers' suppressed complaint, and Shield's reason for walking rather than running. The recovery of Mara is narrated without first establishing that Kailan can see the south-west markers. Interpretive narration repeatedly certifies what the scene has enacted: the Oasis as a perfect/not-perfect garden, all things ending into one another, Shield's inherited shame, the return of the practical world, the edge-of-world authority gloss, and the theological proof at re-entry. The long opening room occasionally advertises the moving-image thesis after Kailan has already made the stronger point and contains a malformed unmarked Paul turn.

The chapter should not be enlarged. Its current components already solve discovery, ordinary life, theology, desire, danger, action, evidence and forward consequence. The correct pass sources every assertion, lets concrete loops and commands carry their own meaning, tightens the live frame, and makes Kailan's sightline explicit during Mara's recovery.
