# Chapter 23 — Pre-John Artistic Lock

Locked by the author before any John pass on Chapter 23.

## What must stay

- **Style:** the Saint-John Perse mode already established by the chapter: elevated, incantatory, image-led and formally distinct from Kailan's prose account. Revision may clarify or strengthen it; revision must not normalise it into ordinary narrative prose or Kailan's voice.
- **Form:** quatrains. The four-line stanza is the chapter's governing unit and must remain intact throughout.
- **Verse shape:** each verse line should remain approximately **12–16 syllables**. Revisions must be made as verse, with line length and cadence checked rather than prose wrapped at arbitrary points.
- **Continuous poem:** do not insert a prose sentence, prose paragraph, documentary cue or explanatory block into the middle of the poem.
- **No room by default:** the artistically preferred design is one uninterrupted Founder poem with no opened `room` environment and no reading-room interlude.

## Option retained, not adopted

- Opening a room remains an available structural option if a later, demonstrated problem cannot be solved inside the poem.
- It is not the current plan. It may be adopted only by explicit author ruling or after presenting a concrete artistic necessity and the exact cost to the uninterrupted form.
- If ever adopted, a room must occur at a deliberate major boundary; it must not fragment a quatrain or place prose inside the flowing poem casually.

## Revision rule

Every proposed Chapter 23 change must preserve all three simultaneous disciplines:

1. Saint-John Perse register;
2. four-line stanzas;
3. approximately 12–16 syllables per line.

Meaning, continuity and mechanics must be repaired from within those constraints. No prose patch may be used to explain what the poem should carry.

## Protected textual anchors

- Preserve the exact phrase **`was the amplifier`** in one verse line.
- Preserve the exact phrase **`deliberate insertion was possible`** in a nearby verse line.
- Keep the two lines close enough to operate as one buried Fauci-indictment Easter egg; the preferred placement is within the same quatrain with one intervening line.
- Do not name, explain, gloss or otherwise advertise the contemporary reference. The phrases must also remain locally meaningful within Ravar's account and the Beast's physical logic.
