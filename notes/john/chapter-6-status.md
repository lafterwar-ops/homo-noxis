## Governing diagnosis

Chapter 6 already owns one of the manuscript's strongest adventures: Kailan tries three routes toward the Veil's Chamber, is humiliated by Faith, discovers the Festival beam under a five-thousand-person rite, then has the central object of his candidacy overturn Light's account of light. The chapter's dramatic question is sound, its physical discoveries pay four chapters of setup, and the ending forces Chapter 7's recognition and succession chain. It needs refinement rather than replacement.

The principal defect is that the narration repeatedly explains the meaning of images the scene has already made legible. It certifies collective feeling (`five hundred hands eager`, every matched couple glad), assigns unspoken motives or inner states to Va-Raedin and others, and converts visible action into thematic pronouncement. Those moves violate the permanent source law even when the underlying observation is excellent. The repair is to preserve each physical image, replace collective interiority with audible/visible conduct, and move necessary optical or moral interpretation into attributed dialogue.

The central apparatus also contains a scientific discontinuity. The first hidden disc already throws clean diffraction rings before the necessary point-source filtering, the focus station claims essentially the whole sunlight survives a severe narrowing, and the final carved pattern is vastly larger than the governing Airy geometry permits. Rebuild the route without changing its five-shadow order: reflection reveals the beam; shadow reveals the removable stop and floor lid; focus images a small part of the sun through a fine aperture and a second lens recollimates the surviving red cone; turning and threshold return steer it; only Kailan's crafted pinhole token produces the hand-sized Airy rings in the Veil's Chamber. The physics then becomes the ordeal instead of an explanatory appendix.

The secondary defects are smaller but consequential: Paul promises a translation `when next we meet`, while Chapter 10 establishes that it arrives three interviews later; one Paul turn uses `Let's`; the Book comparison and Festival-world texture occasionally state institutional intentions as fact; and the central revelation spends too many words proving that light is a wave in unquoted narration. Preserve the Eris/five-office reading, Va-Maera's condemnation-prayer seed, the full Mountain Genesis, the printed-book collision, the sex-locked-office/matching plant, ordinary Festival life, diffraction, token custody, Va-Raedin's warning, the day of waiting, and the Chapter 7 summons.
