## Governing diagnosis

Chapter 4 contains the correct hinge but divides its force unevenly. The present-day material has the chapter's real life: Kailan tests Paul's rule for trust, Paul admits the scale of his desire, Kailan chooses open sky, and a lightly sworn loyalty becomes binding before either man knows it. The account beneath it has a complete causal function: Shield's held evidence crosses from Chapter 3; Kailan shapes the `mirage` record; the beam exposes contraband light; Sela experiences the same withholding Kailan resents; and the token gives him a first refraction error. The chapter therefore needs no new plot.

The defects are execution and hierarchy. At 41.5% of the words, the room does not yet achieve the governing figure/ground turn; a long middle presents the Mountain as a guided curriculum and delays the live bargain. The account repeatedly claims other minds or collective states: Oren's resignation, Sela's unchosen movements and concealed judgment, the whole Mountain's labour, workers' devotion, Sa-Tavan's aspiration, and Sela's emotional withdrawal. Several passages give the physical image and then certify its meaning. The contraband-light proof is load-bearing but its route is difficult to reconstruct because Kailan first meets the diminished outgoing beam and only later reaches the whole incoming beam without naming those two sides cleanly. The existing version also omits the seasonal source-mirror work and Light's creed that make the theft measurable.

The correct rebuild does not enlarge the chapter. It compresses the working-day account into a fast, selective chain; sources all conduct through Kailan's sight, hearing, memory or later explicit Light knowledge; places brief room interruptions where Paul tries to recover the data Kailan is racing past; makes the `mirage` statement visibly Kailan's first deliberate record-shaping; and clarifies the contraband proof as `whole beam enters / half-beam leaves / no other light enters`. The room then resumes dominance through Paul's desire, the Mountain-door bargain, open-sky gift, mirror test, Sarah-defence reframe, recorded loyalty covenant and two-day delay.
