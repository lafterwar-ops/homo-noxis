\typesetchapter{Chapter 17}{Mock}

\begin{room}

(Sarah): --- At lunch we called it surface sounding on three stations. I want that checked against the full array. Urs, is the interval still holding?

(Urs): --- Eleven minutes, near enough. There is the next one.

(Kylian): --- I recognise something I felt at a rising. I cannot account for the shorter wait.

(Sarah): --- Or for its continuing through the night. The old sequences stopped after five. Bring me the drill cabinet, back to the first paper I folded, and the great seals with it. All of it, please.

\centerline{}

(Paul): --- The old sequences followed a Keeper's death or an emergency call. We have neither. The timing is wrong, the tail is wrong, and yet he recognises it. I do not know what to call that.

(Sarah): --- Forgery.

(Kylian): --- I felt it.

(Sarah): --- Yes. Someone has found a way to make your Mountain answer. These old papers have an event attached to each: a death here; at Reggane, an emergency call. Years between them. Tonight we have strokes without an event. Look at the first motion, before the tail. The old blow comes from below. This one reaches us from around the Mountain.

(Paul): --- Show me how paper knows the difference between under and around.

(Sarah): --- At lunch three common stations gave us the provisional surface solution. Here is the check against the full array. These three traces are the same lawful event caught at three stations. The first motion reaches the southern ear, then the western, then the northern. Work the arrival times backward and they meet beneath one fixed quarter, at depth. Take the second lawful event, years later: different instruments, the same meeting below. Now take tonight. The nearest ear receives the hard motion from the surface and the others receive it in ground order. Eleven minutes later the source has walked east. Eleven after that, east again. Each stroke resembles one from a rising, but the point that asks for it is moving over the skin.

(Paul): --- And the tail?

(Sarah): --- The five old strokes are followed by a slow component: the coupled body below returning phase to Faith. A surface plate cannot counterfeit that. Here the shallow arrivals keep coming from several directions.

(Paul): --- Could weather, a bad clock, or the wrong ground model make one deep source appear to walk?

(Sarah): --- One station could lie. Three could share a bad clock. Atum is silent, but its last buffer ends where the eastward walk reaches its sector. We have nine stations over two arrays reporting that same walk. Urs and a second reader worked separately on the second sheet. The second had never seen my archive. Both returned a surface source moving east.

(Urs): --- Again. One sweep and its echo. Eleven minutes from the last sweep.

(Sarah): --- Let it run.

(Kylian): --- Outside, on the desert floor.

(Sarah): --- Someone has put machines on the ground to shake it on purpose and buried rings of listeners around them. They settle a plate, drive one measured sweep into the rock, read the echo, lift, move, and settle again. Eleven minutes from one station to the next: the crew's pace, not the Mountain's. That is why it does not vary. You throw a known noise at a body you cannot see into, and the echo draws its insides. It is how I have mapped ore-bodies without laying an eye on them. Each return adds a line to the picture.

(Paul): --- Sounding.

(Kylian): --- The Bowl of the Last Prayer gives a true note back whole from its Mouth and answers a false note with silence. I thought the Bowl small. Someone has set an Ear against the whole Mountain and is singing every note it knows. One sweep crossed the note the buried chamber was made to answer.

(Sarah): --- That is what you felt. The sweep comes from above; the chamber gives the echo.

(Paul): --- Then they have not learned the note.

(Sarah): --- They passed through it. They know one note found something made beneath the rock. They are working out where it lies.

(Paul): --- How long?

(Sarah): --- Until the chart is good enough to use. This is no fuse. Their surface machines cannot loose the thing; that takes the five stations and the buried engine. But they can finish the map. Change the note, the force, the place of the plate, and the return sharpens: galleries, stairs, water, the deep answer that should not exist.

(Kylian): --- They can ask again as often as they wish.

(Sarah): --- Yes.

\centerline{}

(Jean-Charles): --- It is worse than the drums.

(Sarah): --- Jean-Charles, that sentence requires a picture, and you appear to have brought one.

(Jean-Charles): --- Radar from above the weather, taken in darkness less than an hour ago. The source sent it before the house knew to ask. I have marked the folds here. The Mountain is under this quarter.

(Paul): --- Describe what the paper shows. I cannot put a picture on a reel.

(Jean-Charles): --- A camp in straight lines across empty ground. Four heavy vehicles in the central lane. A drilling rig. A lattice of marked points running out from the vehicles. Dug positions on the ridges, facing inward.

(Paul): --- How much of that can radar actually tell you?

(Jean-Charles): --- Large metal objects, disturbed ground, regular edges, and change against an earlier pass. The trucks and rig are high-confidence classifications. Those small shapes are revetments and vehicles. Their placement and orientation give us a perimeter. They do not give us the faces inside it.

(Sarah): --- Four vibrator trucks. Steel plates under them, cables and geophones running out to make the ears. The trucks alone draw nothing; the ears make the picture. That rig is not part of ordinary reconnaissance. Nor are those positions on the second ridge.

(Kylian): --- Soldiers.

(Paul): --- Madame? The little marked points. You have stopped on them.

(Sarah): --- I drew that grid.

\centerline{}

(Paul): --- Say precisely what you mean by yours.

(Sarah): --- The spacing. The stagger at the western edge. That hook beside every seventh shot-point is how I told one field crew from another when a wind moved our flags. I taught it to four living men.

(Paul): --- When?

(Sarah): --- Twenty-three years ago, against a buried salt body. The working papers went into Horizon's technical archive. The public report gives the results. None of these marks.

(Paul): --- Could another surveyor reach the same spacing from the depth and ground conditions?

(Sarah): --- Near it. He has no reason to put in my useless hook. Whoever copied this had one of my pupils or the private sheets.

(Paul): --- It names Horizon.

(Sarah): --- A road out of Horizon. A pupil, a stolen archive, a bought office, a man I trusted. The mark will not choose among them for us.

(Paul): --- And the soldiers?

(Sarah): --- State resources, or a very expensive attempt to look like them.

(Paul): --- Madame, may I speak to you here, by the window?

(Sarah): --- Kylian, come with us. Keep your voices down.

\centerline{}

(Paul): --- When I came into this house, you told me to make a friend of your right hand and keep close to him. I did. I was not prepared to spend that friendship on evidence I could not yet read.

(Sarah): --- Put each fact down separately.

(Paul): --- The camera behind the instruction glass is not the only lead out. A wire finer than a hair is spliced from the fixed recorder into the wall. It was laid after your system and avoids the junction Elise showed me. I traced it to a locked service chase. I stopped before I broke a seal the other end would notice.

(Sarah): --- When?

(Paul): --- The morning after we exposed the camera. Before that, I had not known what to look for. I checked against Elise's plans. The wire remained live.

(Sarah): --- Why did you not bring me that at once?

(Paul): --- I could not tell whether it was another of your systems. I marked it and watched what it touched. That led me to the reels. Twice, the reels I locked after we used the instruction room were not the reels I unlocked. Boxes, seals, recorded length: all agreed. The tape did not. I made a scratch inside the flange where a hand closing the box would not see it. The scratch went out and came back on another reel.

(Sarah): --- Dates.

(Paul): --- The first and third times we used that room after the confrontation. Both substitutions were after the camera was exposed.

(Sarah): --- Copies.

(Paul): --- Probable. Substitution is what I can prove without destroying the remaining chain.

(Sarah): --- Go on.

(Paul): --- Twice before dawn a car with black glass and no country came to the low gate. The first visit was the morning after the Trial reel changed. Your right hand carried a flat case down and returned without it. Less than a minute. Yesterday the car came again. This time no case. The rear window lowered; he spoke to someone inside for thirty-seven seconds. I could not hear the words.

(Sarah): --- Name the right hand.

(Paul): --- Jean-Charles.

\centerline{}

(Sarah): --- He is ten paces behind us. Keep your voice where it is.

(Paul): --- I have. The wire could be yours under another name. The reels could have been changed for a technical reason no one gave me. The case could hold money, medicine, reports, or nothing. I could ask him about every item and receive three innocent answers. But I know about the hidden camera, Montel's collection, and now your grid. I cannot keep these facts separate merely because I like the man.

(Kylian): --- At the safe house, while Elise telephoned from the next room, he asked about the marks on the door I had left unopened. He wanted the soundless room's position beside it, and to know whether the Mountain had stayed under seal until I was out. I told him what little I knew. He said there was no need to trouble Elise: a man should keep some of his own weather.

(Sarah): --- Before or after Elise received the Justice packet?

(Kylian): --- After. Horizon had already taken the dark cord and my name.

(Sarah): --- Why did you not tell her?

(Kylian): --- He had helped me wash and dress. I could scarcely open a door without him. When he asked about the Mountain, I could answer him at last. I wanted to keep talking.

(Sarah): --- A man may be kind while he is collecting. Keep both possibilities open.

(Paul): --- You still intend to put the route in his hands.

(Sarah): --- I intend to put the field movement in the most competent hands I possess while keeping the full destination out of every hand that can be separated from the party. Suspicion is not proof, and an unproved traitor who is also my best field operator is a danger I can aim. Urs will open the transport, Elise will build the route in compartments, and Jean-Charles will receive each leg when he needs it.

(Kylian): --- My first night here I listened to this house as I would to the Mountain. It hummed in its walls with a steadiness I distrusted. I could not find where the listening ended.

(Paul): --- The camera gave us Montel as collector and Roger as carrier. The wire, reels, car, and grid do not yet give us one owner.

(Sarah): --- They require an archive, border access, protected aircraft, field soldiers. Montel gives us a French hand. I cannot give you the office that commands the rest.

(Paul): --- You have an office in mind.

(Sarah): --- I do not give an office without evidence. I will give you the most probable shape. There is a part of the French state that has wanted this desert for as long as my family worked it. My father fed it concessions. My husband fed it science. The elder Martinez fed it doors, and Roger inherited the feeding. For sixty-six years I gave it crumbs because crumbs kept its mouth away from the loaf. Now it has my grid, some part of Horizon, a protected camp, and men on the inward ridges. It has decided it no longer needs the woman who portions the bread.

(Kylian): --- Does it know what the answer can become?

(Sarah): --- I would pay to know that.

(Paul): --- For sixty-six years you watched, and we find this inside a week.

(Sarah): --- I lacked two ears. One had to belong to a man raised inside the thing, honest enough to feel a made blow beneath a prayer and count it. That is you, Kailan. The other had to belong to an outsider with no stake, stubborn enough to lay your morning against mine and distrust my house while eating at my table. That is you, Mr Morgan. I arranged my life so no two people would hold enough to see it whole. You did it inside a week.

(Paul): --- You ought to be furious.

(Sarah): --- I am relieved. We are, the three of us, on one side of this for as long as that remains true. My Scottish father's version of the Auld Alliance was uranium for France. My English half is fighting France now. Try not to get us killed with the novelty.

(Urs): --- Another stroke.

(Sarah): --- Then stop counting and move.

\centerline{}

(Paul): --- We are about to cross a desert on the orders of the one person who has told us that she wants the thing under it for herself. You have understood that.

(Kylian): --- I have.

(Paul): --- And?

(Kylian): --- She is the only soul who can put us there. She wants the soldiers off my Mountain as fiercely as we do, for reasons that are bad. My council's reasons were spotless, and they erased me with them. I will take the bad reasons that point toward my people and watch her hand on the door.

(Paul): --- I have two readings. It is the wisest thing you have said all week. Or it is precisely how she takes everyone.

(Kylian): --- Both. That is what she is.

(Sarah): --- Urs. Wake the parts of Horizon that are still mine, that never touched Martinez and do not know his masters exist. Open every route into that quarter the camp does not know it controls. Close the routes it believes it owns after we pass. You ran those roads against smugglers and competitors. Run them now against soldiers using my maps. Jean-Charles, you take the field when Elise gives you the tactical packet.

(Jean-Charles): --- And the boy. You are sending him.

(Sarah): --- Ask him.

(Kylian): --- I am going. I spent my account explaining why I could never go home. None of it weighs against that paper. The five sit behind their walls keeping their one secret from one another. They do not know an army has begun to draw their rooms.

(Paul): --- They made you a finder.

(Kylian): --- They invented a mission so my exile would have a lawful shape: a Veil sent out to determine whether the Enemy Within was real. I took it for a lie made to be rid of me. It was a lawful lie. I am the one man who can reach that Threshold carrying an errand the five themselves decreed. I have findings to report.

(Jean-Charles): --- They will kill you, man. There is no door in that Mountain that opens inward for you. They opened one to push you out. If they open it again, it will be to put you in the ground.

(Kylian): --- I know what they do. A people who will kill a man for bringing warning have forgotten how to be warned. Someone must walk past that fear. I was born to it and am already standing outside.

(Paul): --- That is the lawful reason. It is not the reason under it.

\centerline{}

(Kylian): --- Va-Sheva is behind that door, Paul. She chose the one containment that left me breathing and walked back into the Mountain that erased me. I cannot lie beside this lake while strangers draw the chamber beneath her feet. I do not know what I am to her. I know what she chose to keep alive. I will carry warning to that choice if they kill me for it at the seam.

(Paul): --- Thank you. I did not ask because the record was owed it.

(Kylian): --- I know.

(Sarah): --- We need leave within the hour. The household can put three through the outer chain. The inner seam is another matter.

(Kylian): --- I will not go in under one marked hand. The Threshold does not open to knowledge, right, or an office once held. It opens to a mark set into the hand. My erased hand still carries one. Sarah carries one. That is the danger: I will not enter my people's last chamber holding only my stolen right and hers, with no third hand to tell what was done there or stop us if either reaches for the whole.

(Paul): --- You want mine.

(Kylian): --- I need yours marked. You will not be a spare key. You will be the hand that belongs to neither of us.

(Paul): --- Can you simply make such a mark?

(Kylian): --- Once. The rite spends a stone that cannot be replaced, and I carry the last that ever left the Mountain: enough for one hand, set one time. That scarcity is why a Veil's mark was holy. No one can mint keys by the fistful. I can give you the mark. I cannot give it twice, and I would not if I could.

(Paul): --- My work is the record. That is the one thing in this house I have been sure of. If I go, I cease to be a man keeping a record and become a man inside the thing the record concerns.

(Kylian): --- I am asking you to come.

(Sarah): --- A record delivered to no one is paper, Mr Morgan. Bring it with you.

(Paul): --- If they reach the Mountain before this reaches anyone able to act, the reels become an account of how the world ended. I will not stay behind glass to protect the purity of my notes while the thing in them is killed. Box the reels.

(Sarah): --- Start the travelling machine before you stop this one. I will not have the record break at the moment it acquires legs.

(Paul): --- The travelling machine is running. You may stop the fixed one.

(Sarah): --- Good. From this word onward, it does not leave your hand.

\centerline{}

(Elise): --- Your names, Mr Morgan. You will have three before the desert. Learn them on the way. The first boards the helicopter here. The second is entered on the private aircraft. The third belongs to the country where the vehicle chain begins. Do not answer to the wrong one.

(Sarah): --- Give us the chain.

(Elise): --- Blackwood helicopter from the lawn to the family field; private aircraft to a prepared strip; vehicles south; animals from the folds. Amastan's line meets us there, or what remains of it. No single movement is remarkable enough to hold in a clerk's memory. Together they put us inside the outer ring.

(Kylian): --- Who holds the whole route?

(Elise): --- I hold the order and the identities. Urs opened aircraft, fuel and vehicles without receiving the final ground. Each air crew has its own leg. The vehicle lead receives its road after the aircraft lands. Amastan's line knows the last ground and none of the air. Jean-Charles gets the tactical packet when we leave the final vehicle. Sarah knows where the pieces meet. No written page carries all of them.

(Paul): --- And the recorder?

(Elise): --- In your hand, manifested as Madame's medical instrument until the vehicle chain. Then on your body. The fixed instruction reels go into the fire case with one copy of the route papers. If this house is entered after we leave, the case seals and the archive room burns separately. An intruder may take the house or follow us. He should not be able to do both from the same paper.

(Paul): --- You prepared this before tonight.

(Elise): --- I prepared evacuation from a compromised house. Tonight supplied the direction. Contingency is only prophecy with filing instructions.

(Sarah): --- How long?

(Elise): --- The helicopter leaves soon. The rest depends on air permission, fuel, road, and whether Sarah's routes still belong to Sarah.

(Paul): --- A cheerful qualification.

(Elise): --- It is an accurate one.

(Jean-Charles): --- Come, man. We do not make ceremony of leaving in this house.

(Kylian): --- You put your hand on my shoulder like that at the dead well.

(Jean-Charles): --- Occupational habit. You were walking the wrong way.

(Kylian): --- Paul. Bring the machine. There is one thing before the aircraft. Under the tree.

\centerline{}

(Paul): --- Here?

(Kylian): --- Here. The last time I brought you under this tree I lost my nerve. I put the needle away and gave you a word instead.

(Paul): --- Shadow.

(Kylian): --- It asked one thing, and you did it without my spelling it out: you watched this house for the enemy within it, on no authority but a captive's. You found the wire and the reels. You came to me with what you saw. Now it costs you the rest.

(Paul): --- We do not have much time. The helicopter will leave soon.

(Kylian): --- Then hear the charges. There is no book to hold them but you.

\begin{scripture}
By the dark the first morning left unlit: \\
I call on you to guard the Mountain from the enemy within. \\
By the proud who gathered the whole into one hand, and drowned for it: \\
I call on you never to seek the whole. \\
By God, who held His breath while the first Light was struck: \\
I call on you to carry this to your grave, and give it up to no pain past it. \\
And by the Beast beneath the Mountain, keeper of the first wrath and the next: \\
for Shadow must guard also from Shadow: \\
if ever I am the hand that reaches for the whole, \\
be the hand that ends me.
\end{scripture}

(Kylian): --- And now the covenant. Answer me true. One day they will take you and open you. What will they find?

(Paul): --- \ldots{}

(Kylian): --- Stop smiling. This is no joke.

(Paul): --- Of course. Very serious indeed.

(Kylian): --- One day they will take you and open you. What will they find?

(Paul): --- My face. And nothing behind it.

(Kylian): --- Give me the hand. Hold still; it stings, and I have waited a long while for a use for my needle and stone.

\centerline{}

(Kylian): --- Your blood, Paul, on the stone. Then it is sworn.

\begin{scripture}
blood enters service; \\
the face enters the dark; \\
the name enters silence.
\end{scripture}

(Kylian): --- Rise now as Va-Paul, Veil of Shadow: a name you will hear once, keep to your last hour, and never say aloud; for in Shadow we speak neither prefix nor suffix.

\centerline{}

(Kylian): --- It is barely a wound. It will heal to a mark no untrained eye can read: the mark of the ascended, pricked under a Swiss tree. It is done. How do you feel?

(Paul): --- \ldots{} This is the moment I am meant to say a deep and meaningful thing. \ldots{} Unlit?

\end{room}
