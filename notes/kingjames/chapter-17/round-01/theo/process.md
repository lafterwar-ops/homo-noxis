## Phase 2 — Unaided reconstruction (immutable from this point)

What happened, in remembered order: Sarah and the others compare the repeated knocks with old lawful events. The new strokes are eleven minutes apart and do not stop at five. She initially calls them forgery. Paul asks how the paper can distinguish a source underneath from one around the Mountain. The new source walks east; several instruments and independent readings make an error unlikely. It becomes a surface survey, with trucks moving and sounding, and a true chamber answering one note within a sweep. The surveyors have not learned the note itself but are getting closer to a map. Their machines cannot directly release the buried danger.

Jean-Charles brings a recent radar image. It reveals a military camp, trucks, a drilling rig, and an inward-facing perimeter. Sarah recognises her private survey grid, including a useless hook repeated at intervals. Her method escaped through a pupil or archive, but the evidence does not yet convict one route. Paul then discloses what he found after the hidden camera: a wire, switched recording reels marked with a scratch, and Jean-Charles at a car before dawn. I remember a discrepancy in this account: a claim of two deliveries seemed followed by one delivery and one conversation. Kylian remembers Jean-Charles asking about the unopened door and soundless room when Kylian was newly dependent on him. The kindness is allowed to remain kindness even if it was also collection.

Sarah suspects a French state interest but refuses to name an office. She has spent decades feeding that interest a little, and now it has bypassed her. The outsiders have exposed what her compartmentalisation hid even from her. She is relieved, and there is a sharp Auld Alliance joke. They arrange to return to the Mountain despite suspecting their field operator. Sarah and Elise will compartment the route; Jean-Charles will receive late instructions.

Kylian says the council's mission of exile gives him a lawful errand back. Jean-Charles warns that the Mountain will kill him. Paul asks for the reason beneath legality; it is Va-Sheva. She preserved Kylian's life, and he cannot remain safe while people map the chamber below her. Kylian wants Paul's hand marked as an independent third hand, neither his nor Sarah's. Only one stone/one new mark remains. Paul must choose between the purity of recording and entering events. He chooses to go. The portable recorder starts before the fixed one stops.

Elise gives the stages: helicopter, private aircraft, vehicles, animals. Different identities, compartmented information, a protected case for reels, and a house/archive destruction contingency. Jean-Charles touches Kylian and Kylian remembers the dead well; I do not know precisely what that recollection implies. Kylian takes Paul outside under a tree to complete an earlier aborted initiation. The first oath charges him to guard the Mountain, never seek the whole, keep silence under pain, and kill Kylian if Kylian reaches for the whole. Paul jokes during the grave question, then promises that torturers will find his face and nothing behind it. Blood goes onto the stone, a shorter liturgy follows, and he becomes a Veil of Shadow. The last line is his awkward joke, 'Unlit?'

Who wanted what: Sarah wants control of her threatened Mountain and her networks, and the survey stopped. Kylian wants to warn his people and reach Va-Sheva even at risk of death. Paul wants an honest account but chooses responsibility that destroys his claim to be outside it. Jean-Charles wants or at least is instructed to take them through the field; his allegiance is not known. Elise wants a feasible escape/return chain. Urs confirms the clock and opens routes.

Residue: the useless hook on the grid; the scratched flange travelling away and back; a chamber answering something its builders did not mean to ask it; Va-Sheva under that map; one irrecoverable mark; 'my face and nothing behind it'; the tree and blood; Paul as the hand that can end Kylian. The technical distinctions are understood but the exact array figures and examples have mostly vanished. The first remembered thing is eleven minutes and the mountain being sounded; the last is 'Unlit?'

Emotional high: Kylian's admission about Va-Sheva, followed by making Paul a hand capable of stopping him. Emotional low: the long qualifying evidence exchange made me less sure how much danger I should feel; I remember several insistences on what cannot yet be proved more distinctly than the immediate threat. The route instructions briefly displaced the decision they were serving.

Next expectation: the trio travels through compromised routes; Jean-Charles may betray or unexpectedly preserve them; Paul's new mark will matter at the Threshold and his new obligation may turn against Kylian. A return to Va-Sheva should be dangerous, not simply a reunion.

Unresolved: who really owns the camp and the collection network; whether Jean-Charles is a traitor; whether the rite is physically dangerous beyond a prick; what exactly it means for a recorded name never to be spoken aloud; whether the survey will find an actionable entrance before the party arrives; how the dormant thing can actually be released. I felt certain Jean-Charles was guilty without the chapter proving it. I felt certain Sarah's desire was dangerous, but her present cooperation was real. Those should not be flattened into a verdict or a guarantee.

Omissions and uncertainty: I cannot reconstruct the precise lawful-event locations, the exact number of array stations, or all the household chain-of-custody details. I remember the stone as the last available outside the Mountain, but do not know whether it is used up in the rite or leaves a continuing object. I remember the recorded ritual being spoken by Kylian even though its two set blocks have no internal speaker labels. These are memory facts, not corrections.
