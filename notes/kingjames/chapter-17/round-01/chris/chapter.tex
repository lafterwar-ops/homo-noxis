\typesetchapter{Chapter 17}{Mock}

\begin{room}

(Sarah): --- Urs, bring the drill cabinet. The whole run, back to the first folded paper. Bring the great seals too. At lunch we had three stations. I want the full array beside them.

(Kylian): --- The shape is one I felt at a rising. The wait is wrong.

(Urs): --- Eleven minutes again. It held through the night.

(Paul): --- Whereas the old sequences stopped after five. Those followed a Keeper's death or an emergency. I can recognise the resemblance. I cannot account for that tail.

(Sarah): --- Keep your finger on it. Here is Reggane. Here is a succession, years away from it. Small marks, a few sheets in a lifetime of paper. Now put tonight's sheet beside them.

(Paul): --- You called it sounding at lunch.

(Sarah): --- Provisionally. Someone strikes, something in the Mountain answers, and someone outside writes the answer down. We have to separate those three things.

(Kylian): --- The stroke I knew was under us.

(Sarah): --- Yes. Look at the three station traces of this old event. South receives it first, west next, north last. Work backward from those arrivals: the same fixed quarter, below ground. Another year, another set of instruments, and the arrivals meet there again. Tonight the nearest ear receives a hard motion from the surface. The other ears receive it in ground order. Eleven minutes later the source is east of that point. Eleven minutes after that, farther east.

(Paul): --- And the tail I am keeping my finger on?

(Sarah): --- The old five strokes leave a slow component. The coupled body below returns phase to Faith. A plate on the surface cannot give us that. Tonight we have shallow arrivals from several directions. The resemblance survives on a single strip. Lay the strips together and it comes apart.

(Paul): --- An instrument can drift. Clocks can agree and all be wrong. What does a bad ground model do to your walking source?

(Sarah): --- Urs, your second reading.

(Urs): --- Nine stations over two arrays. The other reader worked separately and had never seen Madame's archive. We both returned a surface source moving east. Atum's last buffer ends where the movement reaches its sector. We have nothing from it after that.

(Paul): --- Nothing tells us why Atum stopped.

(Sarah): --- Correct. Nor does one faulty station make the rest draw the same eastward movement at eleven-minute intervals. Keep Atum's last buffer in the comparison. Its silence begins after these readings. We still have the other eight.

(Urs): --- Another sweep. The echo follows it. Eleven minutes.

(Sarah): --- Leave the drum running.

(Kylian): --- They are on the desert floor.

(Sarah): --- With a plate against the ground. Settle it, drive a measured sweep through it, read the return, lift, move, settle again. That eleven minutes is enough for a crew's work. They are not waiting for your Keepers.

(Paul): --- A survey.

(Sarah): --- It is how I mapped ore-bodies I could never see. A known noise goes in. You take back the echo. Change the note, the force, the position of the plate. Put each return beside the last and you can begin to draw what is buried.

(Kylian): --- The Bowl of the Last Prayer returns a true note whole from its Mouth. A false note gets silence. They have put an Ear against the Mountain and passed through the notes until something answered. The chamber gave the note I know. Their machine gave the sweep.

(Paul): --- They have not learned to call it deliberately?

(Sarah): --- They passed through its note. They know there is something made beneath that rock. Every pass places it more closely.

(Paul): --- Put the danger on the record in one sentence.

(Sarah): --- They are using the chamber's answer to make a map of it.

(Paul): --- How long before they can loose it?

(Sarah): --- Their surface trucks cannot do that. The five stations and the buried engine do it. Do not give us a fuse we can watch burning. The surveyor keeps working until he has enough on his chart: galleries, stairs, water, a deep chamber that ought not to be there. Then he can use the chart.

(Kylian): --- So the thing that came through the floor was still ours.

(Sarah): --- The answer was. The paper cannot tell us who asked for it.

(Paul): --- Then we must not label the whole sheet false. Someone could come after us, find the same answer and decide you had concealed it.

(Sarah): --- Keep the measurements. Keep the question separate. I have spent too many years with those folds opening under my fingers to throw out an honest trace because I dislike the machine that made it. If you want a heading, write that the call is being imitated. The chamber is real.

\centerline{}

(Jean-Charles): --- Here is the ground above it. Radar, less than an hour old. Taken above the weather, in darkness. Our source sent it before we asked. I marked the folds. Your Mountain lies under this quarter.

(Paul): --- Before you name anyone, tell me what that instrument can see. No faces through canvas, I assume.

(Jean-Charles): --- Large metal objects. Disturbed ground. Regular edges. Change since the earlier pass. Four heavy vehicles and a drilling rig: high confidence. Those shapes on the ridges are revetments and vehicles. Placement and orientation give me a perimeter. I cannot tell you who is sitting inside it.

(Paul): --- Describe the arrangement for the machine, please.

(Jean-Charles): --- Straight camp lines. Four heavy vehicles along the central lane. The rig beside them. A lattice of points running outward. The ridge positions are dug in and face inward.

(Sarah): --- Vibrator trucks. Those four lower steel plates under their own weight. The lines running away are cables and geophones. Ears. A truck without the ears is a very expensive way to shake dust off a stone. They have laid the ears out for a picture. And brought a rig to use it.

(Kylian): --- Soldiers on the second ridge.

(Jean-Charles): --- That is my reading.

(Sarah): --- Wait. The western edge. Let me have it flat.

(Paul): --- What is there?

(Sarah): --- Mine.

\centerline{}

(Paul): --- The land, the survey, or the mark under your finger?

(Sarah): --- The spacing. The stagger on that edge. And that hook beside every seventh shot-point. Wind moved the flags; I needed to distinguish one crew's work from another's. Four living men learned to put that hook there from me.

(Paul): --- When?

(Sarah): --- Twenty-three years ago. A buried salt body. The public report has the results. The sheets with these marks went into Horizon's technical archive.

(Paul): --- Could another surveyor arrive at that spacing?

(Sarah): --- With the same depth and ground, near enough. He would have no use for my hook.

(Paul): --- One of your four men, then.

(Sarah): --- Or his pupil. Or someone with the private sheets. I can tell you whose marks those are. I cannot give you the hand holding the pencil.

(Paul): --- You could check the archive.

(Sarah): --- And what would an intact drawer tell me? The men who needed those sheets had to be allowed to unfold them. They had to carry them into the field. You cannot keep a survey working by locking the paper away from the surveyors. A sheet put back in its folder may already have supplied a hundred copies.

(Paul): --- Horizon let them out.

(Sarah): --- A road out of Horizon. A pupil, a stolen archive, an office bought, a man trusted. Leave the alternatives on your record.

(Paul): --- And the soldiers?

(Sarah): --- Whoever is using my work has state resources. Or enough to dress a camp in them. That rig is already over the ground my marks divide.

\centerline{}

(Paul): --- Madame, a private word. When you told me to make a friend of your right hand, I did. There are things I have been unwilling to lay beside one another. I cannot keep them apart now.

(Sarah): --- Separately first. Keep your voice down.

(Paul): --- The instruction room's fixed recorder has a second lead. A wire finer than a hair, spliced into the wall after your system was installed. It avoids the junction on Elise's plans. I followed it to the locked service chase and stopped before I left a visibly broken seal.

(Sarah): --- When did you find it?

(Paul): --- The morning after we exposed the camera behind the glass. I checked the plans, traced it, and left it live.

(Sarah): --- Without telling me.

(Paul): --- I had a wire entering part of your house I was not allowed to enter. You might have owned it. I marked it and watched what it touched.

(Sarah): --- What did it touch?

(Paul): --- The reels. Twice, the reels I unlocked were not those I had locked away after the instruction room was used. Same boxes, same seals, same recorded length. I scratched the inside of a flange, out of sight when the box was closed. That scratch left. A different reel came back in its box.

(Sarah): --- Dates.

(Paul): --- The first and third uses of the room after the confrontation. Both after we exposed the camera. The second channel was still open.

(Sarah): --- They were copying.

(Paul): --- Probably. I can prove substitution. I have left the rest intact.

(Sarah): --- Next.

(Paul): --- Two visits to the low gate before dawn. Black glass, no country on the car. Your right hand took a flat case down on the first visit and came back without it. Less than a minute. The morning after the instruction reel changed. Yesterday he went down empty-handed. The rear window lowered. He spoke into it for thirty-seven seconds. I could not hear a word.

(Sarah): --- Name him.

(Paul): --- Jean-Charles.

\centerline{}

(Sarah): --- Ten paces behind us. No louder.

(Paul): --- The wire may be yours. The reels may have been replaced for a reason no one thought to give me. The case could have held money, medicines, reports. But I have a hidden camera, Montel receiving its collection, and now your private grid. I cannot put the case back among harmless things because I like the man who carried it.

(Kylian): --- He asked me three things. At the safe house, while Elise was telephoning in the next room. The marks on the door I had left shut. Where the soundless room lay beside it. Whether the seal held until I was out. I told him.

(Sarah): --- Had Elise received the Justice packet?

(Kylian): --- Yes. She already had the packet and dark cord. My name was gone. Jean-Charles said not to trouble her; a man should keep some of his own weather.

(Sarah): --- And you kept it.

(Kylian): --- He had bound the cut in my hand. Later he showed me how to empty the basin and pulled my collar straight. I had been given clothes I could scarcely fasten and a name I had to copy until I could make it alone. When he asked about the Mountain, I had something I could do for him. I wanted him to ask again.

(Sarah): --- He did help you. We do not have to erase that to examine the questions.

(Kylian): --- I still had the book on my knees. He waited when I stopped. He did not make me give him any of it.

(Paul): --- That is worth keeping. So are the questions he chose while Elise was elsewhere. Will you give him the route?

(Sarah): --- A leg at a time. Urs will open the transport. Elise will divide the route. Jean-Charles will receive the ground he needs when he needs it. I have no better field operator, and I have no proved traitor. I can withhold a page from either.

(Paul): --- And if he separates from us with his page?

(Sarah): --- It will not take him to the final ground.

(Kylian): --- My first night I lay listening to this house. The walls hummed without a break. I distrusted it.

(Paul): --- That belongs on the record as what you heard. We cannot make it identify a wire. The camera gave us Roger as carrier and Montel as collector. These other things may share that road. We do not yet have one owner for them.

(Sarah): --- We have private archive access, borders passed, protected aircraft, soldiers in the field. Montel supplies a French hand. He has not supplied the name of the office above it.

(Paul): --- Your judgment?

(Sarah): --- A part of the French state that has wanted this ground as long as my family worked it. My father gave concessions. My husband gave science. The elder Martinez opened doors, and Roger took over at those doors. I gave them crumbs for sixty-six years. Now someone has taken my grid out of the cupboard and laid it under a drilling rig.

(Kylian): --- They will keep asking the Mountain until they can draw what answers.

(Paul): --- Sixty-six years of watching. What did you lack?

(Sarah): --- You two. Kailan could put a prayer beside the mark on my paper. You could put my paper beside a wire in my wall. Neither of you asked the other's office for leave. I spent my life making sure no two people held enough to do that.

(Paul): --- Are we to apologise?

(Sarah): --- Do not waste my relief. We have work. My Scottish father's Auld Alliance meant uranium for France. My English half appears to be fighting France tonight.

(Paul): --- Your relief is conditional. The minute either of us asks for something you mean to keep, we are back outside your door.

(Sarah): --- You are standing here with a charge against the man I have trusted to take you through a desert. I have heard it. I am changing what he is given. You can put that on the same record as my appetite for the Mountain. I shall not ask you to rub either out.

(Urs): --- Another stroke, Madame.

(Sarah): --- Have someone keep the sheets. Wake the part of Horizon that never touched Martinez. Open the roads into that quarter and the transport we still own. Close behind us where we can. You ran those roads against smugglers, Urs. Use them against men carrying my maps.

(Urs): --- And Jean-Charles?

(Sarah): --- Field command. Elise will give him his tactical packet.

\centerline{}

(Paul): --- Kylian. Before we agree to anything else: she has told us she wants what is under that Mountain. We are about to travel on her orders.

(Kylian): --- I heard her.

(Paul): --- I should like more than that.

(Kylian): --- She can get us there. She wants those soldiers off the Mountain. My Keepers had better reasons when they took my name away. I will watch what she does with her hand when we reach the door.

(Paul): --- She has taken men a long way on that arrangement.

(Kylian): --- Then come and watch it with me.

(Jean-Charles): --- Madame. Are you sending the boy?

(Sarah): --- Ask him.

(Kylian): --- I am going. The five are inside with their separate portions. There is an army putting their rooms on one sheet of paper.

(Paul): --- They sent you to find an enemy.

(Kylian): --- They sent a Veil to determine whether the Enemy Within was real. That was the errand they gave my exile. I have findings. They must hear what their own finder brings to the Threshold.

(Paul): --- You called that errand a lie.

(Kylian): --- I thought they needed words to cover what they were doing to me. They used lawful words. I can take those back even if they refuse my name. I will give the report they sent me to make.

(Jean-Charles): --- They must? Man, they opened a door to throw you out. The next time they open it for you, they will have a place in the ground ready. You know them.

(Kylian): --- I do. I can still give warning through a door they will not let me cross.

(Paul): --- And Va-Sheva?

\centerline{}

(Kylian): --- She is behind it. She found a way to send me out breathing and went back. I have a bed beside a lake. They are drawing the chamber underneath her feet. I cannot keep the bed.

(Paul): --- Even after what you have just told us about her?

(Kylian): --- She is not waiting for me, Paul. I saw whom she chose. That does not move her out from under the drill.

(Paul): --- You need not justify her to me.

(Kylian): --- I was not trying to.

(Sarah): --- Within the hour. The household can put three of us through the outer chain. The inner seam is yours.

(Kylian): --- It cannot be mine alone. The Threshold reads a mark set into a hand. The office they erased does not matter to it. My hand still carries the mark. Yours carries one too.

(Sarah): --- I have not forgotten.

(Kylian): --- I will not go into my people's last chamber with just those two hands. Yours wants what is there. Mine has no lawful name left. There must be someone who can tell what either of us does, and stop us.

(Paul): --- Mine.

(Kylian): --- If you will give it.

(Paul): --- A third key?

(Kylian): --- A hand belonging to neither of us. It will open the seam. That is not all I shall ask of it.

(Paul): --- Say the rest before I give it.

(Kylian): --- To guard against me as well. If I reach for the whole, you must be able to refuse the man who put the mark there. You must be able to do more than refuse him. Hear the charge before you put your blood to it.

(Paul): --- How many can you mark?

(Kylian): --- One. The rite spends the stone. This is the last that ever left the Mountain; enough for one hand, one time. I cannot go back to it for another. That scarcity was part of what made a Veil's mark holy.

(Paul): --- And you brought it to the lawn once before.

(Kylian): --- Yes.

(Paul): --- My work was to keep your account. I have asked you to distinguish what you witnessed from what you want to believe. Now you want the man holding the machine inside the thing it records.

(Sarah): --- Then take the machine inside. A reel left here will not warn anyone at your Threshold.

(Paul): --- Nor will a very careful account of their destruction. If they get there before this reaches anyone able to act, I shall have preserved how it was lost. I will not sit behind glass to keep my part in it clean. Box the fixed reels. I will carry the travelling machine. Keep the marks on the boxes, please, and do not put the substituted lesson copies among my interview reels.

(Sarah): --- Start it before stopping this one. I want the overlap.

(Paul): --- It is running. Both are taking this.

(Sarah): --- From here it stays with you.

(Paul): --- Elise, will you take the fixed reels when I stop them? I want the boxes kept together.

(Elise): --- In the fire case. These are your travel papers. Keep your own sets in this order. The first name takes the helicopter from here. The second goes on the private aircraft's manifest. The third starts at the vehicle chain. Learn them on the way; answer to the name on the page you are carrying.

(Paul): --- I am stopping the fixed machine now. The travelling machine continues.

\centerline{}

(Sarah): --- The chain, Elise.

(Elise): --- Helicopter from the lawn to the family field. Private aircraft to a prepared strip. Vehicles south, animals from the folds. Amastan's line meets us there, or what remains of it. None of the separate bookings should give a clerk much to remember.

(Kylian): --- Who has them all?

(Elise): --- I have their order and your identities. Urs opened the aircraft, fuel and vehicles without the final ground. Each air crew knows its own leg. The vehicle lead gets his road when the aircraft lands. Amastan's line has the last ground and none of the air. Jean-Charles gets his tactical packet when we leave the final vehicle. Sarah knows where the pieces meet. There is no page with the whole journey on it.

(Paul): --- You have given me no papers for the recorder.

(Elise): --- Madame's medical instrument until the vehicles. The manifest says so. After that, on your body. Keep it with you if a man offers to carry it.

(Paul): --- And the reels I have just surrendered?

(Elise): --- The fire case takes them with one copy of the route papers. It comes with us, sealed. If anyone enters the house after we leave, the archive room burns separately. The instruction boxes stay together, as you asked. Your interview reels keep their own seals. Nothing is being put back into the instruction archive.

(Paul): --- How much of this did you prepare before tonight?

(Elise): --- The way out of a compromised house. I did not have tonight's destination. Please learn the first name before you reach the pilot.

(Sarah): --- Time?

(Elise): --- The helicopter is nearly ready. After that we require air permission, fuel, roads, and the routes still to belong to you.

(Paul): --- I had hoped at least the roads were certain.

(Elise): --- So had I.

(Jean-Charles): --- Come, man. We do not make ceremony of leaving in this house.

(Kylian): --- That hand again. You put it on my shoulder at the dead well.

(Jean-Charles): --- You were walking the wrong way.

(Kylian): --- Paul, bring the machine. Before the aircraft. Under the tree.

\centerline{}

(Paul): --- Here?

(Kylian): --- Yes. The grass was wet last time too. I brought the needle and stone and put them away again. I gave you a word.

(Paul): --- Shadow.

(Kylian): --- You watched the house for me. I had no standing to ask you. You found the wire and kept watching after you found it.

(Paul): --- I have not found an owner for it.

(Kylian): --- I am not asking you to name one. I am asking for your hand. There are charges before the mark.

(Paul): --- We have very little time.

(Kylian): --- Hear them. There is no book to hold them but you.

\begin{scripture}
By the dark the first morning left unlit: \\
I call on you to guard the Mountain from the enemy within. \\
By the proud who gathered the whole into one hand, and drowned for it: \\
I call on you never to seek the whole. \\
By God, who held His breath while the first Light was struck: \\
I call on you to carry this to your grave, and give it up to no pain past it. \\
And by the Beast beneath the Mountain, keeper of the first wrath and the next: \\
for Shadow must guard also from Shadow: \\
if ever I am the hand that reaches for the whole, \\
be the hand that ends me.
\end{scripture}

(Kylian): --- And now the covenant. Answer me true. One day they will take you and open you. What will they find?

(Paul): --- \ldots{}

(Kylian): --- Stop smiling. This is no joke.

(Paul): --- Of course. Very serious indeed.

(Kylian): --- One day they will take you and open you. What will they find?

(Paul): --- My face. And nothing behind it.

(Kylian): --- Give me the hand. Hold it still. It stings.

(Paul): --- I can hold the machine in the other.

(Kylian): --- Do that. Your blood on the stone, Paul. Then it is sworn.

\begin{scripture}
blood enters service; \\
the face enters the dark; \\
the name enters silence.
\end{scripture}

(Kylian): --- Rise now as Va-Paul, Veil of Shadow: a name you will hear once, keep to your last hour, and never say aloud; for in Shadow we speak neither prefix nor suffix.

\centerline{}

(Paul): --- May I close it now?

(Kylian): --- Yes. It is barely a wound. It will heal to a mark no untrained eye can read. The mark of the ascended, under a Swiss tree. How do you feel?

(Paul): --- \ldots{} This is the moment I am meant to say a deep and meaningful thing. \ldots{} Unlit?

\end{room}
