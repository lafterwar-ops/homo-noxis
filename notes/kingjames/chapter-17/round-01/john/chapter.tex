\typesetchapter{Chapter 17}{Mock}

\begin{room}

(Sarah): --- Urs. At lunch you gave me three stations and a surface source. Give me the other six.

(Urs): --- They agree. Nine stations across two arrays. Atum's last packet survived in the receiving buffer. It puts the source moving east into Atum's sector before the transmission stops.

(Kylian): --- And the three people?

(Urs): --- The buffer tells me nothing about them.

(Sarah): --- The second reading?

(Urs): --- Done separately, without your old papers. Surface source, moving east. The same result as mine.

(Paul): --- Eleven minutes still?

(Urs): --- Near enough. It has held through the night. There is the next one.

(Kylian): --- I know part of that shape. At a rising we felt it through the floor. But we waited longer, and after five it stopped.

(Sarah): --- Bring the drill cabinet here, Urs. The great seals too. Paul, keep this sheet apart from them until you have seen the difference.

(Paul): --- I can see a smaller stroke and a different tail. I cannot yet see a different cause.

(Sarah): --- Then start with place. This old event reached the southern station first, the western next, the northern last. Their clocks let us work back along the paths through the ground. The paths meet beneath one fixed quarter, at depth. Years later, another sequence. Different instruments; the same meeting below.

(Paul): --- Allowing for what lies between the source and each station.

(Sarah): --- Yes. A wrong ground model sends you to the wrong ridge. We have the corrected model here. Put tonight's sheet beside the old one. The nearest station takes the first hard motion almost from ground level. The others receive it in that order. On the next stroke the point has moved east. Eleven minutes later, east again.

(Paul): --- A bad clock could move it.

(Urs): --- One could. Three could share an error. I tried the corrections in their logs. I cannot bring these arrivals back to one deep point, and neither could the second reader. Both arrays give the same walk.

(Sarah): --- Now the tail. The old five are followed by a broad, slow return. Your account put Faith beside that answer, Kailan. Here it is missing. We get shallow arrivals from several directions instead.

(Paul): --- I want to separate two claims. We have a source on the surface and something in its return that Kylian recognises. Does his recognition help place the source?

(Urs): --- No. I had the position before he said it.

(Sarah): --- Nor did I ask the second reader whether this looked like the old sequence. He had no old sequence to please me with. The resemblance is a question for the three of us. The surface position has been checked without it.

(Paul): --- Good. Then the measurements are not all borrowing their certainty from one another.

(Kylian): --- Those shallow arrivals. From around the Mountain.

(Sarah): --- From around it. Someone is putting a known sweep into the surface and listening to the rock return it. Lift the plate, move, lower it, sweep again. Eleven minutes is the crew's pace.

(Paul): --- So the sounding hypothesis holds.

(Sarah): --- It holds. These are made strokes. They did not come from a Keeper taking office.

(Kylian): --- But the part I recognise?

(Sarah): --- Belongs to what answered. When I survey an ore-body, I change the note, force and position. Each return tells me more about something I cannot see. Whoever is working there has crossed a note your Mountain answers unusually well.

(Kylian): --- The Bowl. The Ear receives the note; the Mouth answers. The right note comes back whole. I have been thinking of the small bowl in the Hall while someone sings every note into the stone around it.

(Paul): --- They have found the right one?

(Sarah): --- They passed through it. They can pass through it again, from another position, and see where the answer comes from.

(Kylian): --- Can the song loose the Beast?

(Sarah): --- No. A surface plate cannot do the work of your buried engine and its five offices. This is a survey, not a fuse. They are finding where to put their hands.

(Paul): --- How much have they found?

(Sarah): --- I cannot read their chart from mine. Galleries, stairs, water, a chamber that answers: every pass gives them more. I can tell you what they are doing. I cannot tell you which return made them decide they had enough.

(Urs): --- Another sweep. Eleven minutes from the last.

(Sarah): --- Keep the drum running.

(Kylian): --- Then the shape on this paper is ours, and the question is theirs.

(Jean-Charles): --- Here is something to put beside it.

(Sarah): --- How recent?

(Jean-Charles): --- Less than an hour. Radar from above the weather, taken in darkness. The source sent it before we asked. I marked the folds. The Mountain is under this quarter.

(Paul): --- Before we use it: what does radar actually establish? I cannot identify a soldier from a grey dot.

(Jean-Charles): --- Nor can I. Metal, worked ground, regular edges, changes since an earlier pass. Four heavy vehicles and a drilling rig, high confidence. Those shapes along the ridge are vehicles and revetments. I call them a perimeter because they face inward.

(Paul): --- And the lines across the centre?

(Sarah): --- A survey grid. Four vibrator trucks in the lane, each lowering a steel plate to put the sweep through. Cables and geophones along the grid to receive it. Without the receiving line, those trucks make a noise and no picture. The drilling rig is another matter.

(Kylian): --- And the men on the ridge.

(Jean-Charles): --- Yes.

(Sarah): --- Leave that corner flat, please.

(Paul): --- What have you found?

(Sarah): --- Mine.

\centerline{}

(Paul): --- Which part?

(Sarah): --- The spacing. The stagger on the western side. There, after every seventh shot-point, the line hooks back. That was how I distinguished our crews when wind moved the flags. I taught four men to lay it out that way.

(Paul): --- Could the ground require the same spacing?

(Sarah): --- Similar spacing, certainly. It does not require my stagger and a useless private hook. Twenty-three years ago I used that combination to map a buried salt body. The report was published without the field marks. The working sheets went into Horizon's technical archive.

(Paul): --- So someone has taken the sheets.

(Sarah): --- Or learnt from one of the four. Or bought somebody who had access. The method has escaped. We have not yet found the hand that carried it.

(Kylian): --- Could my lessons have given them this?

(Sarah): --- Your lessons could tell them what you recognised and what surprised you. They could not teach a field crew a survey I made before you came here.

(Paul): --- Then the camera and the grid need not have travelled together.

(Sarah): --- Correct. I am not joining them because they have arrived on the same evening. Someone collected the instruction; someone laid this survey. We have evidence of each. If one man owned both, he could compare the witness with the ground. That is the danger I am acting on, not a connection I can yet prove.

(Jean-Charles): --- Shall I have the ground packet brought through?

(Sarah): --- Elise is preparing it. Wait for her.

\centerline{}

(Paul): --- Sarah. Come to the window a moment. Kylian too. There is something I should have brought you sooner.

(Sarah): --- How much sooner?

(Paul): --- I did not have the grid. I had three observations, each with an innocent explanation. You told me to make a friend of your right hand. I did, and then I found myself counting the occasions on which he left the house.

(Sarah): --- Begin with what you saw.

(Paul): --- A splice from the fixed recorder in the instruction room. A wire finer than a hair, laid after your system and avoiding the junction on Elise's plans. I followed it into a locked service chase. I stopped there, before opening it would break a visible seal. The wire remained live.

(Sarah): --- When did you find it?

(Paul): --- The morning after we exposed the hidden camera. I had not known what to look for before that.

(Sarah): --- You knew enough to follow it. Why did you not tell me?

(Paul): --- Because you might have owned it. I wanted to know whether it touched the record. I marked the instruction reels I locked away after the room was used. Twice, what I unlocked was not what I had locked.

(Sarah): --- The seals?

(Paul): --- Agreed. Boxes, seals and recorded length, all agreed. I had scratched the inside of a flange, out of sight of a hand closing the box. A reel went out with the scratch and came back without it.

(Sarah): --- Copied.

(Paul): --- Probably. Substituted is what I can prove. The first and third uses of the instruction room after the confrontation. Not the interview reels. I have found no break in their custody.

(Sarah): --- And your third observation?

(Paul): --- A car at the low gate twice before dawn. Black glass, no country I could identify. The first morning was after I found the changed instruction reel. Jean-Charles carried a flat case down to it and returned empty-handed. Yesterday he went down without a case. The rear window lowered. He spoke for thirty-seven seconds. I heard none of it.

(Sarah): --- Keep your voice low. He is ten paces behind you.

(Paul): --- I know. The case could have held medicine. The wire could be yours. The reels could have been exchanged for a reason somebody neglected to give me. I was prepared to keep looking. Now a protected camp is using your private survey marks.

(Kylian): --- At the safe house he asked me about the door.

(Sarah): --- Which door?

(Kylian): --- The one with three Shield seals that I had left unopened. He wanted the marks, and where the soundless room stood beside it, and whether they kept the Mountain sealed until I was out. Elise was telephoning in the next room. I told him all three.

(Sarah): --- Before or after she received Justice's packet?

(Kylian): --- After. She had the packet and the dark cord. She had already given me my new name.

(Paul): --- Did he ask you to keep it from her?

(Kylian): --- He said there was no need to trouble her. She wrote everything down; a man should keep some of his own weather.

(Sarah): --- Why did you agree?

(Kylian): --- He had just kept me alive. There was glass in my robe, and he bound my hand. Later he helped me with the shirt. I could not manage its little discs. When he asked about the Mountain, there was something I could do for him. I answered.

(Sarah): --- That is useful. Keep the order exactly as you have given it.

(Kylian): --- You heard what happened on the road. He did not have to take us through it.

(Sarah): --- I heard. His kindness can be real. So can his questions.

(Paul): --- And you are still giving him the field movement.

(Sarah): --- Who would you put in his place?

(Paul): --- I have no name.

(Sarah): --- Neither do I. Urs can open transport. Elise can build a route. Jean-Charles can take people through ground held against them. Suspicion does not give me another operator. It tells me how much of the route he may hold.

(Paul): --- Which is?

(Sarah): --- The next leg, when he needs it. Elise holds the compartments. No page with the whole journey goes into his pocket. And no accusation is made until it can survive an answer.

(Kylian): --- My first night here I listened to the walls. The house hummed steadily. I thought I could hear it holding its breath.

(Paul): --- You told me the walls might have ears. That sent me behind the glass. It does not tell us whose ear is on this wire.

(Sarah): --- Montel collected the camera's memory through Roger. That gives us a French connection. Put the rest beside it: archive access, aircraft kept clear, borders opened, soldiers on a ridge, a survey financed without my desk hearing of it. I can give you the probable shape. I cannot yet give you the office.

(Paul): --- A part of the French state.

(Sarah): --- The part that has wanted that desert since my father sold it concessions. My husband supplied science. The elder Martinez supplied passage. Roger inherited the doors. For sixty-six years I let enough go through them to keep the rest in my hands. Now somebody has gone past me.

(Kylian): --- They are making the Mountain answer. They do not know what its answer may become.

(Paul): --- You watched for sixty-six years. How did we find this in a week?

(Sarah): --- You compared what I paid people to keep separate. Kailan gave you the prayer; you brought it to my instrument. Then you distrusted my house while eating at my table. I had made arrangements to prevent precisely that meeting.

(Paul): --- You ought to be furious.

(Sarah): --- I am relieved. Do not mistake it for reform. For the moment the three of us need the same road. My Scottish father's Auld Alliance was uranium for France. My English half will have to fight France with whatever he left us.

(Urs): --- Sarah. Another stroke.

(Sarah): --- Keep the readings coming. Open the transport now.

\centerline{}

(Paul): --- Kylian. She has told us she wants the thing beneath your Mountain in her own keeping. You heard her.

(Kylian): --- I heard her say she was the one hand.

(Paul): --- And you are going with her.

(Kylian): --- Can you put me there?

(Paul): --- No.

(Kylian): --- Neither can I. She wants the soldiers gone. I want them gone. My council meant nothing but good when it erased me. Sarah has bad reasons for taking the road I need. I will go with her and watch what she reaches for when we arrive.

(Paul): --- That may be sound judgment. It may also be how she recruits everybody.

(Kylian): --- Yes. Stay beside me if you can.

(Sarah): --- Urs, use the parts of Horizon that never touched Martinez. Wake them yourself. I want routes into that quarter the camp does not know it controls, and the old routes closed behind us. You ran them against smugglers. Run them against men using my maps. Jean-Charles, the field is yours when Elise gives you the tactical packet.

(Jean-Charles): --- You're sending the boy back?

(Kylian): --- She is taking me back.

(Jean-Charles): --- To what? They gave you one door out. If they open it again, it will be to finish what they began.

(Kylian): --- They sent me to find the enemy within. The five decreed it; Va-Sheva is the ear I report to. At that Threshold I can name the errand before they decide what to do with the man carrying it.

(Paul): --- The mission you said was a lawful way of getting rid of you.

(Kylian): --- It was. They still gave it to me. Now I have findings.

(Jean-Charles): --- Findings do not stop a blade.

(Kylian): --- No. Keep me alive until I reach their blade, and I will take the answer there.

(Sarah): --- I need to know whether you can do this, Kailan. We cannot carry you up to the seam and then turn back.

(Kylian): --- I can.

(Paul): --- A little while ago you could scarcely speak of her.

\centerline{}

(Kylian): --- She is still there.

(Paul): --- Yes.

(Kylian): --- Nothing I learnt about her this evening moved her outside the Mountain. She went back. I keep seeing her with him at the well. I cannot make that stop. But I can see where those machines stand, too. I cannot stay beside this lake and let them reach her without warning.

(Paul): --- You need not say more.

(Kylian): --- I want her alive, Paul. Let that be enough to get me to the door.

(Sarah): --- Then we leave within the hour. I can put us through the outer chain. At the inner seam I need you.

(Kylian): --- You need more than me. The Threshold opens to a mark in a hand. My office was taken; the mark remains. You carry one too. I will not take only those two hands into my people's last chamber.

(Sarah): --- You think I shall reach for it.

(Kylian): --- You told me you would. And I have spent my life reaching past the door set for me. There must be a third hand that belongs to neither of us.

(Paul): --- Mine.

(Kylian): --- Yours. To witness what we do. To stop either of us. I can mark it, if you consent.

(Paul): --- The needle and stone?

(Kylian): --- Yes. The rite spends the stone. What I carry is the last that ever left the Mountain: one hand, one setting. After you there is no other. That is why a Veil's mark was holy. It cannot be multiplied like words on your paper.

(Paul): --- I asked you to take me there. I offered you a very eloquent account of what an archaeologist would pay for the privilege. I did not then expect you to name the price.

(Kylian): --- You can refuse.

(Paul): --- That needs to remain true after you mark me. I am not swearing that whatever you call the Mountain's interest will become mine. I may disagree at the very moment you most need me to agree.

(Kylian): --- Then disagree where I can hear you. If I only wanted your hand to open a door, I would have said so.

(Paul): --- A marked witness may be more difficult to send away.

(Kylian): --- I know what happens when the person who wants the question closed also holds the person asking it. That is why I want you with us. Not behind us, waiting for the account we choose to bring back.

(Paul): --- I know. My proper place might be here, making certain the testimony survives whatever happens to its witnesses.

(Sarah): --- Here, in the house with the wire?

(Paul): --- Somewhere outside. I could take the reels out and remain outside with them.

(Kylian): --- You could.

(Paul): --- And then I would possess the account up to the moment we learnt enough to act. Whatever followed would belong to the people who returned to tell me. If none returned, it would end here.

(Sarah): --- Bring your machine, Mr Morgan.

(Paul): --- Yes. I am coming. I will not call it disinterested work after this.

(Kylian): --- I never asked you to.

(Paul): --- Box the reels. Not that one yet. I need the travelling machine running before this stops.

(Sarah): --- You have it?

(Paul): --- Running. I am stopping the fixed recorder now.

\centerline{}

(Elise): --- Keep it in your hand, Paul. These names are yours. First for the helicopter, second for the aircraft, third for the country where the vehicle chain begins. Kylian, the same for you. Learn them on the way. If someone asks a question you do not understand, wait for me.

(Kylian): --- As before.

(Elise): --- Yes. We begin on the lawn. Helicopter to the family field, private aircraft to a prepared strip, vehicles south, animals from the folds. Amastan's line meets us there, if it is still open.

(Sarah): --- Who has received what?

(Elise): --- Urs has opened fuel, aircraft and vehicles. He does not have the final ground. Each air crew knows its own leg. The vehicle lead receives the road after we land. Amastan's line holds the last ground and none of the air. Jean-Charles receives the tactical packet at the last vehicle. I hold the order and identities. You know where they meet.

(Paul): --- No complete route on paper?

(Elise): --- None. These pages will not take anyone from this house to the Mountain.

(Kylian): --- Does that keep us safe if one of them is taken?

(Elise): --- It keeps him from giving away what he was never told. He can still give away his own leg. We must have crossed it before anybody can use him to stop us there.

(Paul): --- And if one hand fails to meet the next?

(Elise): --- We do not improvise a replacement identity in front of a border officer. Stay beside me. At the folds it will be Amastan's people you obey, however familiar the ground looks to you, Kylian. You came out once. They have to go on living there.

(Paul): --- Where do the fixed reels go?

(Elise): --- Into the fire case with one copy of the route papers. The instruction reels stay separate from your sealed interviews and notebooks. The travelling machine is Madame's medical instrument until the vehicle chain. After that it stays on your body.

(Sarah): --- And the house?

(Elise): --- If it is entered after we leave, the case seals and the archive room burns separately. Taking the house should not also give them the papers travelling with us.

(Paul): --- You had this prepared.

(Elise): --- Evacuation, yes. Tonight gave me the destination. Do not confuse those things; I have had much longer to prepare one than the other.

(Sarah): --- How long?

(Elise): --- The helicopter is almost ready. After that, air permission, fuel, the road. Then we learn how much of your route is still yours.

(Paul): --- I shall take that as the accurate estimate.

(Elise): --- Please do. I have no reassuring one.

(Jean-Charles): --- Come, man. This way. We do not make a ceremony of leaving.

(Kylian): --- You need not turn me by the shoulder. I know which way to go. You did that at the dead well.

(Jean-Charles): --- You were going towards the camels. Same habit. Come.

(Kylian): --- One thing first. Paul, bring the machine. Under the tree.

\centerline{}

(Paul): --- Do we have time for this?

(Kylian): --- We have time for what I am asking you to accept. Last time we stood here I put the needle away. I gave you the word and kept back the wound.

(Paul): --- Shadow.

(Kylian): --- You watched the house on a captive's word. You found the eye and did not pull the cord. You kept your face while we waited. I have no completed office to hand you, Paul. Only the charges, and the mark.

(Paul): --- Then give me the charges.

(Kylian): --- Hear them. There is no book to hold them but you.

\begin{scripture}
By the dark the first morning left unlit: \\
I call on you to guard the Mountain from the enemy within. \\
By the proud who gathered the whole into one hand, and drowned for it: \\
I call on you never to seek the whole. \\
By God, who held His breath while the first Light was struck: \\
I call on you to carry this to your grave, and give it up to no pain past it. \\
And by the Beast beneath the Mountain, keeper of the first wrath and the next: \\
for Shadow must guard also from Shadow: \\
if ever I am the hand that reaches for the whole, \\
be the hand that ends me.
\end{scripture}

(Kylian): --- And now the covenant. Answer me true. One day they will take you and open you. What will they find?

(Paul): --- \ldots{}

(Kylian): --- Stop smiling. This is no joke.

(Paul): --- Of course. Very serious indeed.

(Kylian): --- One day they will take you and open you. What will they find?

(Paul): --- My face. And nothing behind it.

(Kylian): --- Give me your hand. Hold still. I have waited a long time for a use for this needle.

(Paul): --- You need not enjoy it quite so much.

(Kylian): --- I am trying to keep it straight.

\centerline{}

(Kylian): --- Your blood on the stone. Then it is sworn.

\begin{scripture}
blood enters service; \\
the face enters the dark; \\
the name enters silence.
\end{scripture}

(Kylian): --- Rise now as Va-Paul, Veil of Shadow: a name you will hear once, keep to your last hour, and never say aloud; for in Shadow we speak neither prefix nor suffix.

\centerline{}

(Paul): --- Is that all?

(Kylian): --- It will heal to a mark no untrained eye can read. The stone has done its work. Give me a moment to wrap your hand.

(Paul): --- I need that hand for the machine.

(Kylian): --- Then keep it. I will work round you. There. How do you feel?

(Paul): --- \ldots{} This is the moment I am meant to say a deep and meaningful thing. \ldots{} Unlit?

\centerline{}

\end{room}
