\typesetchapter{Chapter 17}{Mock}

\begin{room}

(Sarah): --- At lunch we called it surface sounding on three stations. We still have to establish that against the full array and determine what is making the strokes.

(Kylian): --- I recognise something I felt at a rising. I cannot account for the shorter wait.

(Sarah): --- Eleven minutes between strokes, and it has continued through the night. The old sequences stopped after five. Watch the drum. There is another.

\centerline{\textit{[\,Urs at the adjoining drum.\,]}}

(Urs): --- The interval is holding: eleven minutes, near enough, between one and the next. It does not vary.

(Sarah): --- Bring me the drill cabinet, all of it back to the first paper I folded, and the great seals with it. Now, please.

(Paul): --- I do not understand what I am looking at, and it is my trade to say so aloud. The old sequences followed a Keeper's death or an emergency call. These strokes resemble them, but the timing and the tail are wrong. I do not know what to call that.

(Sarah): --- Forgery. Someone has found a way to make this Mountain answer, is striking at it, and is writing down what comes back.

\centerline{\textit{[\,Cabinet wheels. Paper unfolded beneath the lamp.\,]}}

(Sarah): --- These are the true replies. Every one small, rare, years from the next. This sequence followed a death; this one, at Reggane, an emergency call. Each has an event attached to it. And this new sheet arrives without one and wants to be their brother. It is not. Look at the tail. A true knock strikes; the Mountain answers once, deep, then rolls over and sleeps. This one strikes and the answer does not come from under. It comes from around. They made it around the Mountain.

(Paul): --- Show me how paper knows the difference between under and around.

(Sarah): --- At lunch three common stations gave us the provisional surface solution. Here is the check against the full array. These three traces are the same lawful event caught at three stations. The first motion reaches the southern ear, then the western, then the northern. Work the arrival times backward and they meet beneath one fixed quarter, at depth. Take the second lawful event, years later: different instruments, the same meeting below. Now take tonight. The nearest ear receives the hard motion from the surface and the others receive it in ground order. Eleven minutes later the source has walked east. Eleven after that, east again. Each stroke resembles one from a rising, but the point that asks for it is moving over the skin.

(Paul): --- And the tail.

(Sarah): --- In the old sequences, the five strokes are followed by a slow component no surface plate can counterfeit: the coupled body below returning phase to Faith. Here we keep getting shallow arrivals from several directions. It is an echo chamber pretending to be a throat.

(Paul): --- Could weather, an instrument fault, or the wrong ground model make one deep source appear to walk?

(Sarah): --- One station could lie. Three stations could share a bad clock. Atum is silent, but its last buffer ends where the eastward walk reaches its sector. Nine stations over two arrays do not invent the same eastward walk at the same eleven-minute measure. Urs and a second reader worked separately when the second sheet came. The second had never seen my archive. Both returned a surface source moving east.

\centerline{\textit{[\,Paper feeds from the adjoining drum.\,]}}

(Urs): --- Again. One sweep and its echo. Eleven minutes from the last sweep.

(Sarah): --- Let it run. We know the hand now.

(Kylian): --- Around it. You mean the source is outside, on the desert floor.

(Sarah): --- I mean that someone has put machines on the ground to shake it on purpose and buried rings of listeners around them. They settle a plate, drive one measured sweep into the rock, read the echo, lift, move, and settle again. Eleven minutes from one station to the next: the crew's pace, not the Mountain's. That is why it does not vary.

(Paul): --- Sounding.

(Sarah): --- Yes. You throw a known noise at a body you cannot see into, and the echo draws its insides. It is how I have mapped ore-bodies without laying an eye on them. Change the note, the force, the position of the plate; each return adds a line to the picture.

(Kylian): --- The Bowl of the Last Prayer gives a true note back whole from its Mouth and answers a false note with silence. I thought the Bowl small. Someone has set an Ear against the whole Mountain and is singing every note it knows. One sweep crossed the note the buried chamber was made to answer. The echo comes from the chamber; the sweep comes from above.

(Paul): --- Then they have not learned the note.

(Sarah): --- No. They passed through it. They know that one note found something made beneath the rock, and every pass tells them more nearly where it lies.

(Paul): --- Say the danger in one sentence, so that I have it clean on the record, whatever the record is now for.

(Sarah): --- They are using a stolen echo to map the one chamber that must never be found, and every pass makes the map more exact.

(Paul): --- How long?

(Sarah): --- Wrong question. This is no fuse. Their surface machines cannot loose the thing; that takes the five stations and the buried engine. Their sweep can finish the map. Every change of note, force, place, and plate sharpens it: galleries, stairs, water, and the deep answer that should not exist. The clock belongs to the surveyor. It runs until his chart is good enough to use.

(Kylian): --- Then what I felt come up through the floor was the answer, and never the blow. The shape was never theirs to make.

(Sarah): --- Exactly. The paper is honest about what the rock gave back. It says nothing at all about why anyone asked, and that is the part I would pay to have.

\centerline{\textit{[\,A door opens. Jean-Charles enters; paper is laid on the table.\,]}}

(Jean-Charles): --- It is worse than the drums.

(Sarah): --- Jean-Charles, that sentence requires a picture, and you appear to have brought one.

(Jean-Charles): --- Radar from above the weather, taken in darkness less than an hour ago. The source sent it before the house knew to ask. I have marked the folds here. The Mountain is under this quarter.

(Paul): --- Radar does not photograph men through canvas in darkness. What exactly am I allowed to trust in it?

(Jean-Charles): --- The large metal objects, disturbed ground, regular edges, and change against an earlier pass. The four trucks and rig are high-confidence classifications. The small shapes on the ridge are revetments and vehicles, not faces. I call them a perimeter because of placement and orientation. I do not claim the paper can read the mind of the man inside one.

(Paul): --- For the record, describe what the paper shows.

(Jean-Charles): --- A camp in straight lines across empty ground. Four heavy vehicles in the central lane. A drilling rig. A lattice of marked points running out from the vehicles. A perimeter on the ridges, dug in and facing inward.

(Sarah): --- Those four are vibrator trucks. They lower steel plates and put the sweep through them. The thin lines are cables and geophones, the ears. The trucks alone draw nothing; the ears make the picture. The rig is not part of ordinary reconnaissance. Nor are those shapes on the second ridge.

(Kylian): --- Soldiers.

(Paul): --- The little marked points. You have stopped on them.

(Sarah): --- I drew that grid.

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

(Paul): --- Say precisely what you mean by yours.

(Sarah): --- The spacing is mine. The stagger at the western edge is mine. That hooked mark beside every seventh shot-point is how I told one field crew from another when a wind moved our flags. I taught it to four living men. One of those men taught the hand that drew this, or drew it himself.

(Paul): --- When did you last use it?

(Sarah): --- Twenty-three years ago, against a buried salt body. The working papers went into Horizon's technical archive. The public report contains the results and none of these marks. Whoever copied this had a pupil or the private sheets.

(Paul): --- Could another surveyor reach the same spacing from the depth and ground conditions?

(Sarah): --- Near it, yes. The same spacing, stagger, and useless private hook, no. Do not make me claim more. It proves that my method escaped me. It does not yet name the hand that carried it.

(Paul): --- It names Horizon.

(Sarah): --- It names a road out of Horizon. That road may have passed through a pupil, a stolen archive, a bought office, or a man I trusted. I will not collapse four possibilities into one because one of them hurts more.

(Paul): --- The perimeter does more. Surveyors do not hide a water line behind soldiers.

(Sarah): --- Correct. The hand using my grid has state resources or wants us to believe it does.

\centerline{\textit{[\,At the window. Voices lowered. Footsteps and doors behind them.\,]}}

(Paul): --- When I came into this house, you told me to make a friend of your right hand and keep close to him. I did. I was not prepared to spend that friendship on evidence I could not yet read. I can read it now.

(Sarah): --- Put each fact down separately.

(Paul): --- First: the camera behind the instruction glass is not the only lead out. A wire finer than a hair is spliced from that room's fixed recorder into the wall. It was laid after your own system and avoids the junction Elise showed me. I traced it to a locked service chase and stopped before the broken seal became visible.

(Sarah): --- When.

(Paul): --- I found the splice the morning after we exposed the hidden camera. Before that, I had not known what to look for. I checked it against Elise's plans, then traced it as far as I could without telling the other end it had been seen. It remained live.

(Sarah): --- Why did you not bring me the wire at once?

(Paul): --- Because a wire entering a locked chase proved only that this house had another system you might have forgotten to name. I marked it and watched what it touched. That led me to the reels. Twice since the camera was exposed, the reels I locked after we used the instruction room were not the reels I unlocked. The boxes, seals, and recorded length agreed. The tape did not. I made a scratch inside the flange where no hand closing the box would see it. The scratch went out and came back on another reel.

(Sarah): --- Dates.

(Paul): --- The first and third times that room was used after the confrontation. Both substitutions happened after we exposed the camera. Someone lost one channel and began drawing harder on another.

(Sarah): --- Copies.

(Paul): --- Copies are probable. Substitution is what I can prove without destroying the remaining chain. Third: twice before dawn, a car with black glass and no country came to the low gate. Your right hand carried a flat case down to it and returned without the case. The car stayed less than a minute.

(Sarah): --- Dates again.

(Paul): --- The morning after the Trial reel changed, and yesterday. On the second occasion he carried no case down. The rear window lowered; he spoke to someone inside for thirty-seven seconds. I could not hear the words.

(Sarah): --- Name the right hand.

(Paul): --- Jean-Charles.

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

(Sarah): --- He is ten paces behind us. Keep your voice where it is.

(Paul): --- I have. Each fact alone is little. The wire could be yours under another name. The reels could have been changed for a technical reason no one gave me. The cases could hold money, medicine, reports, or nothing. Laid beside a hidden camera, Montel's collection, and your stolen grid, they will not lie down.

(Kylian): --- One fact from the road out remains. At the safe house, while Elise telephoned from the next room, Jean-Charles asked about the marks on the door I had left unopened, the soundless room's position beside it, and whether the Mountain had stayed under seal until I was out. I told him what little I knew. He said there was no need to trouble Elise with it, because a man should keep some of his own weather.

(Sarah): --- Did he ask before or after Elise received the Justice packet?

(Kylian): --- After. Horizon had already taken the dark cord and my name.

(Sarah): --- Why did you not tell her?

(Kylian): --- He had helped me wash and dress. I could scarcely open a door without him. When he asked about the Mountain, I could answer him at last. I wanted to keep talking.

(Sarah): --- A man may be kind while he is collecting. Keep both possibilities open.

(Paul): --- You still intend to put the route in his hands.

(Sarah): --- I intend to put the field movement in the most competent hands I possess while keeping the full destination out of every hand that can be separated from the party. Suspicion is not proof, and an unproved traitor who is also my best field operator is a danger I can aim. Urs will open the transport, Elise will build the route in compartments, and Jean-Charles will receive each leg when he needs it.

(Kylian): --- There is my small fact. My first night here I listened to this house as I would to the Mountain. It hummed in its walls with a steadiness I distrusted. A house being listened to hums the way a man holds his breath.

(Paul): --- The camera gave us Montel as collector and Roger as carrier. The wire, reels, car, and grid give us a second shape inside the same house. They do not yet give us one owner.

(Sarah): --- Together they require an archive, border access, protected aircraft, field soldiers, and authority to erase a country from a vehicle. Montel gives it a French hand. He does not tell us which part of France commands the rest.

(Paul): --- Which part.

(Sarah): --- I do not give an office without evidence. I will give you the most probable shape. There is a part of the French state that has wanted this desert for as long as my family worked it. My father fed it concessions. My husband fed it science. The elder Martinez fed it doors, and Roger inherited the feeding. For sixty-six years I gave it crumbs because crumbs kept its mouth away from the loaf. Now it has my grid, some part of Horizon, a protected camp, and men on the inward ridges. It has decided it no longer needs the woman who portions the bread.

(Kylian): --- And it is knocking until the Mountain answers, without knowing what the answer can become.

(Paul): --- Madame, for sixty-six years you watched and never caught this.

(Sarah): --- I lacked two ears. One had to belong to a man raised inside the thing, honest enough to feel a made blow beneath a prayer and count it. That is you, Kailan. The other had to belong to an outsider with no stake, stubborn enough to lay your morning against mine and distrust my house while eating at my table. That is you, Mr Morgan. I arranged my life so no two people would hold enough to see it whole. You did it inside a week.

(Paul): --- You ought to be furious.

(Sarah): --- I am relieved. We are, the three of us, on one side of this for as long as that remains true. My Scottish father's version of the Auld Alliance was uranium for France. My English half is fighting France now. Try not to get us killed with the novelty.

\centerline{\textit{[\,Paper advances in the adjoining room. A telephone bell begins.\,]}}

(Urs): --- Another stroke.

(Sarah): --- Then stop counting and move.

\centerline{\textit{[\,Telephone bells. Doors opening throughout the house.\,]}}

(Paul): --- We are about to cross a desert on the orders of the one person who has told us that she wants the thing under it for herself. You have understood that.

(Kylian): --- I have understood it.

(Paul): --- And?

(Kylian): --- She is the only soul who can put us there, and she wants the soldiers off my Mountain as fiercely as we do, for reasons that are bad. My council's reasons were spotless, and they erased me with them. I will take the bad reasons that point toward my people and watch her hand on the door.

(Paul): --- I have two readings. It is the wisest thing you have said all week. Or it is precisely how she takes everyone.

(Kylian): --- Both. That is what she is.

(Sarah): --- Urs. Wake the parts of Horizon that are still mine, that never touched Martinez and do not know his masters exist. Open every route into that quarter the camp does not know it controls, and close the routes it believes it owns after we pass. You ran those roads against smugglers and competitors. Run them now against soldiers using my maps. Jean-Charles, you take the field when Elise gives you the tactical packet.

(Jean-Charles): --- And the boy. You are sending him.

(Sarah): --- I am not sending him. Look at him. He was going when the second knock drew.

(Kylian): --- I spent my account explaining why I could never go home. None of it weighs against that paper. The five sit behind their walls keeping their one secret from one another. They do not know an army has begun to draw their rooms.

(Paul): --- They made you a finder.

(Kylian): --- They invented a mission so my exile would have a lawful shape: a Veil sent out to determine whether the Enemy Within was real. I took it for a lie made to be rid of me. It was a lawful lie. I am the one man who can reach that Threshold carrying an errand the five themselves decreed. I have findings to report.

(Jean-Charles): --- They will kill you, man. There is no door in that Mountain that opens inward for you. They opened one to push you out. If they open it again, it will be to put you in the ground.

(Kylian): --- I know what they do. A people who will kill a man for bringing warning have forgotten how to be warned. Someone must walk past that fear. I was born to it and am already standing outside.

(Paul): --- That is the lawful reason. It is not the reason under it.

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

(Kylian): --- Va-Sheva is behind that door, Paul. She chose the one containment that left me breathing and walked back into the Mountain that erased me. I cannot lie beside this lake while strangers draw the chamber beneath her feet. I do not know what I am to her. I know what she chose to keep alive. I will carry warning to that choice if they kill me for it at the seam.

(Paul): --- Thank you. I did not ask because the record was owed it.

(Kylian): --- I know.

(Sarah): --- We need leave within the hour. The household can put three through the outer chain. The inner seam is another matter.

(Kylian): --- I will not go in under one marked hand. The Threshold does not open to knowledge, right, or an office once held. It opens to a mark set into the hand. My erased hand still carries one. Sarah carries one. That is the danger: I will not enter my people's last chamber holding only my stolen right and hers, with no third hand to tell what was done there or stop us if either reaches for the whole.

(Paul): --- You want mine.

(Kylian): --- I need yours marked, Paul. You will not be a spare key. You will be the hand that belongs to neither of us.

(Paul): --- Can you simply make such a mark?

(Kylian): --- Once. The rite spends a stone that cannot be replaced, and I carry the last that ever left the Mountain: enough for one hand, set one time. That scarcity is why a Veil's mark was holy. No one can mint keys by the fistful. I can give you the mark. I cannot give it twice, and I would not if I could.

(Paul): --- My work is the record. That is the one thing in this house I have been sure of. If I go, I cease to be a man keeping a record and become a man inside the thing the record concerns.

(Sarah): --- A record delivered to no one is paper, Mr Morgan. Bring it with you.

(Paul): --- If they reach the Mountain before this reaches anyone able to act, these reels become an account of how the world ended. I will not stay behind glass to protect the purity of my notes while the thing in them is killed. Box the reels.

(Sarah): --- Start the travelling machine before you stop this one. I will not have the record break at the moment it acquires legs.

(Paul): --- The travelling machine is running.

(Sarah): --- Good. From this word onward, it does not leave your hand.

\centerline{\textit{[\,The fixed machine stops. Its reels are drawn and boxed; the travelling machine continues.\,]}}

\centerline{\textit{[\,Va-Elise enters. Passports and route papers placed on the table.\,]}}

(Elise): --- You will be three different men before the desert. Learn them on the way. The first name boards the helicopter here. The second is entered on the private aircraft. The third belongs to the country in which the vehicle chain begins. Do not answer to the wrong one.

(Sarah): --- Give us the chain.

(Elise): --- Blackwood helicopter from the lawn to the family field; private aircraft to a prepared strip; vehicles south; animals from the folds. Amastan's line meets us there, or what remains of it. No single movement is remarkable enough to hold in a clerk's memory. Together they put us inside the outer ring.

(Kylian): --- Who holds the whole route?

(Elise): --- I hold the order and the identities. Urs opened the aircraft, fuel and vehicles without receiving the final ground. Each air crew holds only its own leg. The vehicle lead receives its road after the aircraft lands. Amastan's line knows the last ground and none of the air. Jean-Charles receives the tactical packet when we leave the final vehicle. Sarah knows where the pieces meet. No written page carries all of them.

(Paul): --- And the recorder?

(Elise): --- It remains in Mr Morgan's hand, manifested as Madame's medical instrument until the vehicle chain. After that it travels on his body. The fixed instruction reels go into the fire case with one copy of the route papers. If this house is entered after we leave, the case seals and the archive room burns separately. An intruder may take the house or follow us. He should not be able to do both from the same paper.

(Paul): --- You prepared this before tonight.

(Elise): --- I prepared evacuation from a compromised house. Tonight supplied the direction. Contingency is only prophecy with filing instructions.

(Sarah): --- How long?

(Elise): --- The helicopter leaves soon. The rest depends on air permission, fuel, road, and whether Sarah's routes still belong to Sarah.

(Paul): --- A cheerful qualification.

(Elise): --- It is an accurate one.

(Jean-Charles): --- Come, man. We do not make ceremony of leaving in this house.

\centerline{\textit{[\,Jean-Charles touches Kailan's shoulder. Doors; footsteps; rotor noise outside.\,]}}

(Kylian): --- You used that hand at the dead well.

(Jean-Charles): --- Occupational habit. You were walking the wrong way.

(Kylian): --- Paul. Bring the machine. There is one thing before the aircraft.

\centerline{\textit{[\,The lawn beneath the wide tree. Travelling recorder.\,]}}

(Kylian): --- The wet grass, a tree, and the two of us. The last time I brought you here I lost my nerve, put the needle away, and gave you a word instead.

(Paul): --- Shadow.

(Kylian): --- It asked one thing, and you did it without my spelling it out: you watched this house for the enemy within it, on no authority but a captive's, and found his shape. Now it costs you the rest.

(Paul): --- We do not have much time. The helicopter will leave soon.

(Kylian): --- Then hear the charges. There is no book to hold them but you.

\begin{scripture}
By the dark the first morning left unlit: \\
I call on you to guard the Mountain from the enemy within. \\
By the proud who gathered the whole into one hand, and drowned for it: \\
I call on you never to seek the whole. \\
By God, who held His breath while the first Light was struck: \\
I call on you to carry this to your grave, and give it up to no pain past it. \\
And by the Beast beneath the Mountain, keeper of the first wrath and the next: \\
for Shadow must guard also from Shadow: \\
if ever I am the hand that reaches for the whole, \\
be the hand that ends me.
\end{scripture}

(Kylian): --- And now the covenant. Answer me true. One day they will take you and open you. What will they find?

(Paul): --- \ldots{}

(Kylian): --- Stop smiling. This is no joke.

(Paul): --- Of course. Very serious indeed.

(Kylian): --- One day they will take you and open you. What will they find?

(Paul): --- My face. And nothing behind it.

(Kylian): --- Give me the hand. Hold still; it stings, and I have waited a long while for a use for my needle and stone.

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

(Kylian): --- Your blood, Paul, on the stone. Then it is sworn.

\begin{scripture}
blood enters service; \\
the face enters the dark; \\
the name enters silence.
\end{scripture}

(Kylian): --- Rise now as Va-Paul, Veil of Shadow: a name you will hear once, keep to your last hour, and never say aloud; for in Shadow we speak neither prefix nor suffix.

\centerline{[\,---\,]}

(Kylian): --- It is barely a wound. It will heal to a mark no untrained eye can read: the mark of the ascended, pricked under a Swiss tree. It is done. How do you feel?

(Paul): --- \ldots{} This is the moment I am meant to say a deep and meaningful thing. \ldots{} Unlit?

\centerline{\textit{[\,Rotor noise rises. Tape ends.\,]}}

\end{room}
