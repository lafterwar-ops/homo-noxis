\typesetchapter{Chapter 17}{Mock}

\begin{room}

(Sarah): --- At lunch we had three stations and a surface solution. I want the full array before we act on it. Urs, the drill cabinet. Back to the first paper I folded. Bring the great seals too.

(Kylian): --- I recognise what came through the floor at a rising. The wait between the blows was longer.

(Sarah): --- Eleven minutes here. All night, without stopping at five. Urs?

(Urs): --- Another. Eleven minutes from the last, near enough. The interval is holding.

(Paul): --- We have a resemblance and two differences. I can put those on the record. I cannot yet put a name to them.

(Sarah): --- Forgery.

(Paul): --- That gives us a forger before we have established a machine.

(Sarah): --- Then hold this sheet flat and make me establish it. These are the old replies. Small, rare, years between them. This sequence followed a Keeper's death. Reggane followed an emergency call. We can attach an event to each. Tonight we have this.

(Paul): --- The tail is different.

(Sarah): --- Yes. But begin with the arrivals. Same old event, three stations. South receives it first, west next, north last. Work those times backward and the paths meet under one fixed quarter, at depth. Here is another lawful event, years later. Different instruments. Where do they meet?

(Paul): --- At the same depth, according to these figures. I cannot check your arithmetic in my head.

(Sarah): --- You need not. Urs has checked it. Now follow tonight's first motion. The nearest ear takes the hard motion from the surface; the others receive it in ground order. Eleven minutes later the source is east of that position. Eleven after that, east again.

(Kylian): --- Outside. On the desert floor.

(Sarah): --- Moving over it. That is what the full array gives us.

(Paul): --- Could a wrong ground model make a deep source appear to walk? Or weather? A clock error?

(Sarah): --- On one station, certainly. Three could share a bad clock. Nine stations over two arrays do not invent this same eastward walk, at the same eleven-minute measure.

(Paul): --- Atum has stopped transmitting. You cannot count it as a present witness.

(Sarah): --- I count its last buffer. That ends where the walk reaches its sector. I do not tell you why it stopped. Urs worked the second sheet independently; another reader worked it without ever seeing my archive. Both gave a surface source moving east. Ask him.

(Urs): --- That is what I returned. I had no deep solution that fitted those arrivals.

(Paul): --- And now the tail.

(Sarah): --- Put the old sheet against this one. There, after the fifth stroke. A slow component: the coupled body below returning phase to Faith. A true knock strikes, the Mountain answers deep, then sleeps. No surface plate gives you that tail. Tonight the late arrivals keep coming shallow, from several directions. Someone has made the Mountain answer from around it.

(Paul): --- So matching the first shape would not be enough to make you accept it.

(Sarah): --- It would get my attention. It got yours. Keep the tail attached when you describe it.

(Urs): --- Again. One sweep and its echo. Eleven minutes.

(Sarah): --- Leave it running.

(Kylian): --- What does the man outside do? Tell me what he puts his hands on.

(Sarah): --- He settles a steel plate on the ground. His machine drives a measured sweep through it. Buried listeners take the return. He lifts the plate, moves, settles it again. Eleven minutes is his crew's pace. Change the note, the force, the place, and the next return gives him another line of the body underneath. I have drawn ore-bodies that way without seeing a piece of them.

(Paul): --- Seismic sounding. Urs's hypothesis at lunch.

(Sarah): --- Now supported by the array.

(Kylian): --- The Bowl of the Last Prayer gives the true note back whole from its Mouth. A false note gets silence. I thought the Bowl small. Someone outside has put an Ear against the Mountain and is singing every note he knows. He crossed the one the buried chamber answers.

(Paul): --- He did not already know that note?

(Sarah): --- He passed through it. Now he knows something made is answering under the rock. Each pass tells him more nearly where.

(Paul): --- What is the immediate danger? One sentence, please. We may need someone to hear it without hearing the rest of us.

(Sarah): --- They are using a stolen echo to map the one chamber that must never be found, and every pass makes the map more exact.

(Paul): --- How long have we?

(Sarah): --- I cannot give you a fuse. Their plates cannot loose the thing. That takes the five stations and the buried engine. They can finish their chart. Galleries, stairs, water, that deep answer which should not exist. When the chart is good enough, they can use it.

(Kylian): --- Then what I felt through the floor was the answer. The blow was outside.

(Sarah): --- Yes.

(Kylian): --- And the men who made it heard that same answer.

(Sarah): --- Through instruments. They are keeping their paper as carefully as I keep mine.

\centerline{}

(Jean-Charles): --- Madame. You need this beside the drums.

(Sarah): --- Leave it open. What is the source?

(Jean-Charles): --- Radar from above the weather. Taken in darkness, less than an hour ago. Sent before the house knew to ask. Here are the folds; the Mountain is under this quarter.

(Paul): --- What may I trust it to show? Not men through canvas, I take it.

(Jean-Charles): --- Metal. Disturbed ground. Regular edges. Change against an earlier pass. Four trucks and a rig are high-confidence classifications. On the ridge, revetments and vehicles. I call it a perimeter because of their placement and orientation. I cannot give you the faces.

(Paul): --- Describe it for the machine, please.

(Jean-Charles): --- A camp in straight lines on empty ground. Four heavy vehicles in the central lane, a drilling rig, a lattice of points running out. The perimeter is dug in on the ridges and faces inward.

(Sarah): --- Four vibrator trucks. Steel plates underneath. Those thin lines are cables and geophones: the ears. Without the ears the trucks draw nothing. A rig already. And those shapes on the second ridge.

(Kylian): --- Soldiers.

(Paul): --- Madame? The grid. What have you found?

(Sarah): --- I drew it.

\centerline{}

(Paul): --- This survey?

(Sarah): --- The spacing. The stagger at the western edge. That hook beside every seventh shot-point. Wind used to move our flags; the hook told me which field crew had worked a line. Four living men learned it from me. One drew this, or taught whoever did.

(Paul): --- When did you last use it?

(Sarah): --- Twenty-three years ago. A buried salt body. The working sheets went into Horizon's technical archive. Our public report gave the results, without these marks.

(Paul): --- Could the depth and conditions give another surveyor the same spacing?

(Sarah): --- Near enough to it. With the stagger and a useless private hook? No. My method has got out. That is all I can prove from this paper.

(Paul): --- Through Horizon.

(Sarah): --- Through a pupil, a stolen archive, a bought office. Or a man I trusted. Leave all four open. I shall not choose one because I dislike it most.

(Paul): --- The perimeter narrows the purpose. Surveyors do not need soldiers facing inward to find a water line.

(Sarah): --- They have state resources. Or enough to imitate them.

(Paul): --- May we speak by the window?

(Sarah): --- Leave the sheet. Kailan, you too.

(Paul): --- You told me to make a friend of your right hand and keep close to him. I did. What I am about to say may finish that friendship. I wanted more than a suspicion before I spent it.

(Sarah): --- Facts. Separately.

(Paul): --- A wire is spliced from the instruction room's fixed recorder into the wall. Finer than a hair. It avoids the junction Elise showed me and was laid after your system. I traced it to a locked service chase. I stopped before I would have had to break the seal visibly.

(Sarah): --- When?

(Paul): --- The morning after we exposed the camera. Until then I had not known where to look. I checked Elise's plans, then followed the splice. It remained live.

(Sarah): --- And you kept it from me.

(Paul): --- I had found another system in a house where you had already omitted to mention one. It could have been yours. I marked it and watched what it touched. That brought me to the reels.

(Sarah): --- Go on.

(Paul): --- Twice I locked up the instruction reels and unlocked different ones. Same boxes, seals, recorded length. I had scratched the inside of a flange, where no one closing the box would look. The mark let me establish the substitution.

(Sarah): --- You are certain you did not exchange them yourself?

(Paul): --- I checked them before I locked them away. I was looking for an exchange. That was the purpose of putting a mark where the person handling the box would have no reason to look.

(Sarah): --- Dates, Mr Morgan.

(Paul): --- The first and third uses of that room after the confrontation. Both after we exposed the camera. Losing that channel did not end the collection.

(Sarah): --- They copied the tapes.

(Paul): --- Probably. Substitution is what I can prove. I have left the chain intact. Third: a car with black glass and no country came to the low gate twice before dawn. On the first visit your right hand carried down a flat case. He returned without it. Less than a minute.

(Sarah): --- And the second?

(Paul): --- No case. A rear window lowered. He spoke to whoever was inside for thirty-seven seconds. I could not hear a word.

(Sarah): --- When were these visits?

(Paul): --- The morning after the Trial reel changed. And yesterday.

(Sarah): --- Name him.

(Paul): --- Jean-Charles.

\centerline{}

(Sarah): --- He is ten paces behind us. No louder than that.

(Paul): --- Each fact admits another explanation. Your wire. A technical reason for new reels. Money or medicine in the case. But with Montel's camera, Roger carrying its collection, and your grid in that camp, I cannot keep treating each as a private oddity.

(Kylian): --- He asked me about the door.

(Sarah): --- Which door?

(Kylian): --- The one I left unopened in the Mountain. At the safe house, while Elise telephoned next door. He wanted its marks, the soundless room's position beside it, and whether the seal held until I was outside. I told him. He said not to trouble Elise. A man should keep some of his own weather.

(Sarah): --- Before or after she received the Justice packet?

(Kylian): --- After. She already had the dark cord and my name.

(Sarah): --- Why obey him about Elise?

(Kylian): --- He had helped me wash and dress. I could scarcely get a door open without his help. Then he asked something I could give him. I wanted him to keep asking.

(Sarah): --- His care may have been genuine. So may his collection. Do not make yourself choose.

(Paul): --- Will you still give him our route?

(Sarah): --- I shall give him the field work. He is the best operator I have.

(Paul): --- That is not an answer about the route.

(Sarah): --- He gets each leg when he needs it. Urs opens the transport; Elise divides the route. No hand that can be detached from the party gets the whole destination. I have a suspected traitor and an immediate need for his competence. I shall restrict the first and use the second.

(Paul): --- You could ask him about the car.

(Sarah): --- And receive an explanation I cannot check before we leave. You stopped at the service chase to keep the other end from knowing. I shall keep the same advantage. Give me a fact requiring his removal and I will remove him. You have given me facts requiring care.

(Kylian): --- My first night here I listened to the walls. The hum was too steady. I distrusted it, but I could not have shown you a wire.

(Paul): --- We still cannot show one owner. Montel collected the camera; Roger carried it. The wire, reels, car and grid establish more access. They need not all belong to the same office.

(Sarah): --- Agreed. But put the requirements together: archive, border access, protected aircraft, soldiers in the field. A vehicle allowed to have no country. Montel gives us a French hand.

(Paul): --- Which French hand?

(Sarah): --- I shall not invent an office for your record. A part of the French state has wanted this desert since my family worked it. My father supplied concessions. My husband supplied science. The elder Martinez supplied doors; Roger inherited them. For sixty-six years I gave it enough to keep it asking me. Now it has my grid, a piece of Horizon, and soldiers over the Mountain. I must act on the probability that it has finished asking.

(Kylian): --- It is asking the Mountain now. It does not know what an answer can become.

(Paul): --- Sixty-six years. How did you miss it?

(Sarah): --- I had no man from inside who could count a made blow under a prayer. And no guest stubborn enough to distrust my house while eating at my table. I kept people apart so nobody would hold enough to see the whole. You two put your accounts together inside a week.

(Paul): --- Are you angry with us?

(Sarah): --- Relieved. Do not expect that to last. We three are on the same side while we can keep it so. My Scottish father's Auld Alliance was uranium for France. My English half is fighting France now. Try not to get us killed with the novelty.

(Urs): --- Madame. Another stroke.

(Sarah): --- Then leave the drum and move. Wake the parts of Horizon that are still mine. Nobody who touched Martinez. Open the routes that camp does not know, and close the ones it thinks it owns after we pass. You ran those roads against smugglers. Run them against soldiers using my maps.

\centerline{}

(Paul): --- Kailan. Before we follow those orders. She has told us she wants the thing under the desert for herself.

(Kylian): --- I heard her.

(Paul): --- She has not withdrawn that claim.

(Kylian): --- Ask her to withdraw it and she will say a few words for you. Will that change whose aircraft we need? She wants those soldiers off my Mountain. So do I. I can go with her and watch her hand when we reach the door.

(Paul): --- You are proposing to use her.

(Kylian): --- With reasons as bad as hers, if you like. My council had clean reasons when they erased me. I am going.

(Paul): --- That may be the wisest thing you have said all week. It may also be precisely how she takes everyone.

(Kylian): --- Then come and watch me too.

(Sarah): --- Jean-Charles. You take the field when Elise gives you the tactical packet.

(Jean-Charles): --- And the boy? You are sending him?

(Sarah): --- Ask him.

(Kylian): --- I am going back.

(Jean-Charles): --- To the men you have spent the week telling us will kill you.

(Kylian): --- I spent my account explaining why I could never go home. I meant it. None of those reasons tells the five about this paper. Five Keepers behind five walls, keeping their part from one another, while an army draws the rooms under their feet. I can stand at the Threshold and tell them.

(Paul): --- They sent you to find the Enemy Within.

(Kylian): --- Yes. That was the errand they invented to give my exile a lawful shape. A Veil sent out to find whether the enemy existed. I have findings. Let them tell me at the door that their own command was a lie.

(Jean-Charles): --- They will not argue it, man. They opened a door to put you out. If they open it again, they can put you in the ground. You know them. You know what they do.

(Kylian): --- I do. You cannot frighten me with a thing I have to carry there already.

(Paul): --- The lawful errand gets you to the door. What makes you want to go through it?

\centerline{}

(Kylian): --- Va-Sheva is behind it. She chose the one containment that left me alive, then went back among the people who erased me. I saw whom she wanted. It was not me. Must I wait beside this lake because she did not want me?

(Paul): --- No.

(Kylian): --- Strangers are drawing the chamber beneath her feet. I can warn her. I can do that much without asking anything back.

(Paul): --- Thank you. I did not ask for the record.

(Kylian): --- I know.

(Sarah): --- We leave within the hour. I can put three through the outer chain. What happens at the inner seam is another question.

(Kylian): --- Paul comes through with us.

(Paul): --- You have not asked him yet.

(Kylian): --- I am asking. The Threshold opens to a mark set into a hand. Knowledge does not open it. Nor an office I once held. My erased hand still carries the mark; Sarah has hers. I will not take those two hands into my people's last chamber with nobody to stop either of us reaching for the whole.

(Paul): --- Mine is unmarked.

(Kylian): --- I can mark it.

(Paul): --- As readily as that?

(Kylian): --- Once. The rite spends the stone, and I have the last one that ever left the Mountain. Enough for one hand, one time. That is why the mark was holy. A Veil could not make keys for everyone who asked.

(Paul): --- And you want to spend it on me.

(Kylian): --- I need a hand belonging to neither Sarah nor me. Someone who can tell what we did, and stop us. You will hear what that asks before you give your hand.

(Paul): --- You have spent weeks asking me to believe you. Now you want me because I may refuse.

(Kylian): --- You have refused often enough. You make me answer the question I wish you had not asked. I cannot take that from you when we reach the door and still call you my witness.

(Paul): --- I came to keep a record. If I enter this, I become one of the people whose conduct needs recording. I cannot interview myself into independence afterwards.

(Sarah): --- Can you carry the machine?

(Paul): --- That is not the difficulty.

(Sarah): --- I know. Answer his request, then.

(Paul): --- If those men finish their map before this account reaches anyone able to act, what shall I have preserved? How it happened. A splendidly disinterested account of how it happened. I will go. But the reels must be boxed before anyone leaves this room.

(Sarah): --- Start the travelling machine first. No break between them.

(Paul): --- It is running.

(Sarah): --- Then stop the fixed one and box its reels. The travelling machine stays in your hand from this word onward.

(Paul): --- Including words you would prefer to withdraw.

(Sarah): --- Especially those. Elise, the papers.

\centerline{}

(Elise): --- Take the passports in order. You will be three different men before the desert. The first name boards the helicopter; the second is entered on the private aircraft. The third belongs to the country where the vehicle chain begins. Learn them on the way. Do not answer to the wrong one.

(Sarah): --- Give us the chain.

(Elise): --- Helicopter from the lawn to the family field. Private aircraft to a prepared strip. Vehicles south, animals from the folds. Amastan's line meets us there, or what remains of it. Separately, none of those movements should stay in a clerk's memory. Together they get us inside the outer ring.

(Kylian): --- Who holds the whole route?

(Elise): --- I hold the order and identities. Urs has opened aircraft, fuel and vehicles without the final ground. Each air crew receives its own leg. The vehicle lead gets the road after the aircraft lands. Amastan's line knows the last ground and none of the air.

(Paul): --- Jean-Charles?

(Elise): --- His tactical packet is for when we leave the final vehicle. Sarah knows where the pieces meet. No written page carries the whole.

(Paul): --- And this machine?

(Elise): --- In your hand. Madame's medical instrument on the manifest until the vehicle chain. After that, on your body. Give me the fixed reels when the box is shut. They go in the fire case with one copy of the route papers. If the house is entered, the case seals. The archive room burns separately.

(Paul): --- You mean to burn the archive?

(Elise): --- If the house is entered after we leave. I will protect the case. An intruder may take the house or try to follow us; I shall not leave him one sheet that lets him do both.

(Paul): --- You had this ready before tonight.

(Elise): --- Evacuation from a compromised house. Tonight gave me a direction.

(Sarah): --- How long?

(Elise): --- The helicopter leaves soon. After that, air permission, fuel, road. Whether Sarah's routes still belong to her.

(Paul): --- A cheerful qualification.

(Elise): --- An accurate one. Keep the passports in order.

(Jean-Charles): --- Come, man. We do not make ceremony of leaving in this house.

(Kylian): --- Let go of my shoulder. You turned me at the dead well.

(Jean-Charles): --- You were walking the wrong way. Come.

(Kylian): --- In a moment. Paul, bring the machine. Under the tree. There is something before the aircraft.

\centerline{}

(Kylian): --- Here. The last time I brought you here, I put the needle away. I lost my nerve and gave you a word.

(Paul): --- Shadow.

(Kylian): --- You watched the house for the enemy within it. I had no right to order that. You did it, and brought me what you found. Now hear what else the word asks.

(Paul): --- The helicopter will not wait for a long ceremony.

(Kylian): --- It must wait for your answer. Kneel. These are the charges. There is no book to hold them but you.

\begin{scripture}
By the dark the first morning left unlit: \\
I call on you to guard the Mountain from the enemy within. \\
By the proud who gathered the whole into one hand, and drowned for it: \\
I call on you never to seek the whole. \\
By God, who held His breath while the first Light was struck: \\
I call on you to carry this to your grave, and give it up to no pain past it. \\
And by the Beast beneath the Mountain, keeper of the first wrath and the next: \\
for Shadow must guard also from Shadow: \\
if ever I am the hand that reaches for the whole, \\
be the hand that ends me.
\end{scripture}

(Kylian): --- And now the covenant. Answer me true. One day they will take you and open you. What will they find?

(Paul): --- \ldots{}

(Kylian): --- Stop smiling. This is no joke.

(Paul): --- Of course. Very serious indeed.

(Kylian): --- One day they will take you and open you. What will they find?

(Paul): --- My face. And nothing behind it.

(Kylian): --- Give me your free hand. Hold still; it stings. I have waited a long while for a use for my needle and stone.

\centerline{}

(Kylian): --- Your blood, Paul, on the stone. Then it is sworn.

\begin{scripture}
blood enters service; \\
the face enters the dark; \\
the name enters silence.
\end{scripture}

(Kylian): --- Rise now as Va-Paul, Veil of Shadow: a name you will hear once, keep to your last hour, and never say aloud; for in Shadow we speak neither prefix nor suffix.

\centerline{}

(Kylian): --- It is barely a wound. It will heal to a mark no untrained eye can read. The mark of the ascended, under a Swiss tree. It is done. How do you feel?

(Paul): --- \ldots{} This is the moment I am meant to say a deep and meaningful thing. \ldots{} Unlit?

\centerline{}

\end{room}
