\typesetchapter{Chapter 17}{Mock}

\begin{room}

(Sarah): --- At lunch you gave me three stations and a surface source. Urs, give me the other six.

(Urs): --- They agree. Nine stations across two arrays. Atum's last packet survived in the receiving buffer. The source was moving east into its sector when transmission stopped.

(Kylian): --- And the three people?

(Urs): --- The buffer tells me nothing about them.

(Sarah): --- Bring the drill cabinet. Back to the first paper I folded, and the great seals too.

(Kylian): --- I recognise something I felt at a rising. The wait is wrong.

(Urs): --- Eleven minutes again. It held through the night.

(Paul): --- Whereas the old sequences stopped after five. A rising or an emergency call. I can recognise the resemblance. I cannot account for that tail.

(Sarah): --- Keep your finger on it. Here is Reggane. Here is a succession, years away from it. Small marks, a few sheets in a lifetime of paper. Now put tonight's sheet beside them.

(Paul): --- You called it sounding at lunch.

(Sarah): --- Provisionally. Someone strikes, something in the Mountain answers, and someone outside writes the answer down. We have to separate those three things.

(Kylian): --- The stroke I knew was under us.

(Sarah): --- Yes. These three traces are the same old event. South receives it first, west next, north last. Work backward from those arrivals and they meet beneath one fixed quarter, at depth. Another year, different instruments, and they meet there again. Now tonight's first motion. The nearest station takes it almost from ground level; the others receive it in that order. Eleven minutes later the point has moved east. Eleven after that, east again.

(Paul): --- Allowing for the ground between the source and each station.

(Sarah): --- Yes. A wrong model sends you to the wrong ridge. We have the corrected model here.

(Paul): --- Or a clock error moves the point.

(Urs): --- One could. Three could share an error. I tried the corrections in their logs. I cannot bring these arrivals back to one deep point. The second reader worked separately, without Madame's old papers. He returned the same surface source moving east.

(Sarah): --- Now the tail. The old five leave a broad, slow return. Your account put Faith beside that answer, Kailan. Here it is missing. We get shallow arrivals from several directions instead.

(Paul): --- Does Kylian's recognition help you place the source?

(Urs): --- No. I had the position before he said it.

(Paul): --- Good. Then the measurements are not borrowing their certainty from the witness.

(Kylian): --- Outside, on the desert floor. What does the man put his hands on?

(Sarah): --- A steel plate against the ground. His machine drives a measured sweep through it. Buried listeners take the return. He lifts the plate, moves, settles it again. Eleven minutes is his crew's pace. Change the note, the force, the place, and the return gives him another line of the body underneath. I have drawn ore-bodies that way without seeing a piece of them.

(Paul): --- Seismic sounding. Urs's hypothesis at lunch.

(Sarah): --- Now supported by the array.

(Kylian): --- The Bowl. A note enters through the Ear, and the Mouth answers. The right note comes back whole. I have been thinking of the small bowl in the Hall while someone sings every note into the stone around it.

(Sarah): --- One sweep crossed the note your buried chamber answers.

(Paul): --- They have learned to call it?

(Sarah): --- They passed through its note. They can pass through it again from another position and see where the answer comes from.

(Paul): --- Put the danger on the record in one sentence.

(Sarah): --- They are using the chamber's answer to make a map of it.

(Kylian): --- Can the song loose the Beast?

(Sarah): --- No. A surface plate cannot do the work of your buried engine and its five offices. This is a survey, not a fuse.

(Paul): --- How much have they found?

(Sarah): --- I cannot read their chart from mine. Galleries, stairs, water, a chamber that answers: every pass gives them more. I can tell you what they are doing. I cannot tell you which return will give them enough.

(Kylian): --- Then the thing I felt through the floor was still ours.

(Sarah): --- The answer was. The paper cannot tell us who asked for it.

(Paul): --- Then we must not label the whole sheet false. Someone could find the same answer and decide you had concealed it.

(Sarah): --- Keep the measurements. I have spent too many years with those folds opening under my fingers to throw out an honest trace because I dislike the machine that made it.

(Urs): --- Another sweep. Eleven minutes from the last.

(Sarah): --- Keep the drum running.

\centerline{}

(Jean-Charles): --- Here is something to put beside it.

(Sarah): --- How recent?

(Jean-Charles): --- Less than an hour. Radar from above the weather, taken in darkness. The source sent it before we asked. I marked the folds. The Mountain is under this quarter.

(Paul): --- Before you name anyone, what can that instrument see?

(Jean-Charles): --- Large metal objects. Disturbed ground. Regular edges. Changes since an earlier pass. Four heavy vehicles and a drilling rig: high confidence. Those shapes on the ridges are revetments and vehicles. Placement and orientation give me a perimeter. I cannot tell you who is sitting inside it.

(Paul): --- Describe the arrangement for the machine, please.

(Jean-Charles): --- Straight camp lines. Four heavy vehicles along the central lane. The rig beside them. A lattice of points running outward. The ridge positions are dug in and face inward.

(Sarah): --- Vibrator trucks. Those four lower steel plates under their own weight. The lines running away are cables and geophones. Ears. A truck without the ears is a very expensive way to shake dust off a stone. They have laid the ears out for a picture. And brought a rig to use it.

(Kylian): --- Soldiers on the second ridge.

(Jean-Charles): --- That is my reading.

(Sarah): --- Wait. The western edge. Let me have it flat.

(Paul): --- What is there?

(Sarah): --- Mine.

\centerline{}

(Paul): --- Which part?

(Sarah): --- The spacing. The stagger on that edge. After every seventh shot-point the line hooks back. Wind moved our flags; I needed to distinguish one crew's work from another's. I taught four men to lay it out that way.

(Paul): --- When?

(Sarah): --- Twenty-three years ago. A buried salt body. The public report has the results. The sheets with these marks went into Horizon's technical archive.

(Paul): --- Could the depth and ground give another surveyor the same spacing?

(Sarah): --- Near enough. They would not give him my stagger and a useless private hook.

(Paul): --- One of your four men, then.

(Sarah): --- Or his pupil. Or someone with the private sheets. I can tell you whose marks those are. I cannot give you the hand holding the pencil.

(Paul): --- You could check the archive.

(Sarah): --- And what would an intact drawer tell me? Those men had to unfold the sheets, carry them into the field. You cannot keep a survey working by locking the paper away from the surveyors. A sheet put back in its folder may already have supplied a hundred copies.

(Paul): --- Could Kylian's lessons have given them this?

(Sarah): --- His lessons could tell them what he recognised and what surprised him. They could not teach a field crew a survey I made before he came here.

(Paul): --- Then the camera and grid need not have travelled together.

(Sarah): --- Correct. If one man owned both, he could compare the witness with the ground. That is the danger I am acting on, not a connection I can yet prove.

(Paul): --- May we speak by the window?

(Sarah): --- Leave the sheet. Kailan, you too.

\centerline{}

(Paul): --- You told me to make a friend of your right hand and keep close to him. I did. What I am about to say may finish that friendship. I wanted more than a suspicion before I spent it.

(Sarah): --- Facts. Separately.

(Paul): --- A wire is spliced from the instruction room's fixed recorder into the wall. Finer than a hair. It avoids the junction on Elise's plans and was laid after your system. I traced it to a locked service chase. I stopped before I would have had to break a visible seal.

(Sarah): --- When?

(Paul): --- After I first went behind the glass. Before we told you about the camera. I had begun looking for another way out of that room.

(Sarah): --- And you kept this from me too.

(Paul): --- It could have been yours. I left it live and watched the room's records. Twice I locked away the instruction reels and unlocked different ones.

(Sarah): --- The seals?

(Paul): --- Agreed. Boxes, seals and recorded length, all agreed. I had scratched the inside of a flange, out of sight of a hand closing the box. I locked away a reel with that scratch. The one I unlocked had none.

(Sarah): --- You are certain you did not exchange them yourself?

(Paul): --- I checked before locking them away. I was looking for an exchange. That was the purpose of a mark the person closing the box would have no reason to look for.

(Sarah): --- Dates, Mr Morgan.

(Paul): --- The first and third uses after I began marking the instruction reels. Both before we brought you the camera. Not the interview reels. I have found no break in their custody.

(Sarah): --- Copied.

(Paul): --- Probably. Substituted is what I can prove. Third: twice before dawn a car with black glass and no country I could identify came to the low gate. Your right hand carried down a flat case and came back without it. Less than a minute.

(Sarah): --- Both times?

(Paul): --- No. That was the morning after the first changed instruction reel. Yesterday he went down without a case. A rear window lowered; he spoke to whoever was inside for thirty-seven seconds. I heard none of it.

(Sarah): --- Name him.

(Paul): --- Jean-Charles.

\centerline{}

(Sarah): --- He is ten paces behind us. No louder than that.

(Paul): --- The wire may be yours. The reels may have been replaced for a reason no one gave me. The case could have held money, medicines, reports. But with Montel receiving the camera's collection and your private grid in that camp, I cannot put it back among harmless things because I like the man who carried it.

(Kylian): --- And the three things he asked me at the safe house. The marks on the door I had left shut on my first visit. Where the soundless room lay beside it. Whether the seal held until I was out.

(Sarah): --- Before or after Elise received the Justice packet?

(Kylian): --- After. She already had the packet and dark cord. She had given me my new name. He asked while she was telephoning next door. He said not to trouble her; a man should keep some of his own weather.

(Paul): --- You agreed.

(Kylian): --- He had bound the cut in my hand. Later he showed me how to empty the basin and pulled my collar straight. I had clothes I could scarcely fasten and a name I had to copy until I could make it alone. When he asked about the Mountain, there was something I could do for him. I wanted him to ask again.

(Sarah): --- He did help you. We do not have to erase that to examine the questions.

(Kylian): --- I still had the book on my knees. He waited when I stopped. He did not make me give him any of it.

(Paul): --- That is worth keeping. So are the questions he chose while Elise was elsewhere. Will you give him the route?

(Sarah): --- A leg at a time. Urs opens the transport. Elise divides the route. Jean-Charles receives the ground he needs when he needs it. I have no better field operator, and I have no proved traitor. I can withhold a page from either.

(Paul): --- You could ask him about the car.

(Sarah): --- And receive an explanation I cannot check before we leave. You stopped at the service chase to keep the other end from knowing. I shall keep the same advantage. Give me a fact requiring his removal and I will remove him. You have given me facts requiring care.

(Kylian): --- My first night here I listened to the walls. The house hummed steadily. I thought I could hear it holding its breath.

(Paul): --- You sent me behind the glass. That does not tell us whose ear is on this wire. The camera gives us Roger as carrier and Montel as collector. We do not yet have one owner for the rest.

(Sarah): --- Put the requirements together: archive access, borders passed, protected aircraft, soldiers in the field. Montel supplies a French hand. He has not supplied the name of the office above it.

(Paul): --- Your judgment?

(Sarah): --- A part of the French state that has wanted this ground as long as my family worked it. My father gave concessions. My husband gave science. The elder Martinez opened doors, and Roger took over at those doors. I gave them crumbs for sixty-six years. Now someone has taken my grid out of the cupboard and laid it under a drilling rig.

(Kylian): --- Do they know what an answer can become?

(Sarah): --- I would pay to know that.

(Paul): --- Sixty-six years of watching. What did you lack?

(Sarah): --- You two. Kailan could put a prayer beside the mark on my paper. You could put my paper beside a wire in my wall. Neither of you asked the other's office for leave. I spent my life making sure no two people held enough to do that.

(Paul): --- Are we to apologise?

(Sarah): --- Do not waste my relief. We have work. My Scottish father's Auld Alliance meant uranium for France. My English half appears to be fighting France tonight.

(Paul): --- Your relief is conditional. The minute either of us asks for something you mean to keep, we are back outside your door.

(Sarah): --- You have brought a charge against the man I trust to take us through a desert. I have heard it. I am changing what he is given. Put that on the same record as my appetite for the Mountain. I shall not ask you to rub either out.

(Urs): --- Another stroke, Madame.

(Sarah): --- Have someone keep the sheets. Wake the parts of Horizon that never touched Martinez. Open the routes into that quarter the camp does not know it controls. Close the old roads behind us where we can. You ran them against smugglers, Urs. Use them against men carrying my maps.

\centerline{}

(Paul): --- Kylian. Before we follow those orders. She has told us she wants the thing beneath your Mountain in her own keeping.

(Kylian): --- I heard her say she was the one hand.

(Paul): --- She has not withdrawn that claim.

(Kylian): --- Ask her to withdraw it and she will say a few words for you. Will that change whose aircraft we need? She wants the soldiers gone. I want them gone. My council meant nothing but good when it erased me. Sarah has bad reasons for taking the road I need. I will go with her and watch what she reaches for when we arrive.

(Paul): --- That may be sound judgment. It may also be how she recruits everybody.

(Kylian): --- Then come and watch me too.

(Sarah): --- Jean-Charles, the field is yours when Elise gives you the tactical packet.

(Jean-Charles): --- You're sending the boy back?

(Sarah): --- Ask him.

(Kylian): --- I am going back.

(Jean-Charles): --- To what? They gave you one door out. If they open it again, it will be to finish what they began.

(Kylian): --- I asked them to send me to find the enemy within. The five decreed it; Va-Sheva is the ear I report to. At that Threshold I can name the errand before they decide what to do with the man carrying it.

(Paul): --- The distance was meant to keep you from bringing the danger back.

(Kylian): --- It is already there. Five Keepers behind five walls, keeping their part from one another, while an army draws the rooms under their feet. I have findings.

(Jean-Charles): --- Findings do not stop a blade.

(Kylian): --- No. Keep me alive until I reach their blade, and I will take the answer there.

(Sarah): --- I need to know whether you can do this, Kailan. We cannot carry you up to the seam and then turn back.

(Kylian): --- I can.

(Paul): --- A little while ago you could scarcely speak of her.

\centerline{}

(Kylian): --- She is still there.

(Paul): --- Yes.

(Kylian): --- Nothing I learnt this evening moved her outside the Mountain. I keep seeing her with him at the well. I cannot make that stop. But I can see where those machines stand, too. I cannot stay beside this lake and let them reach her without warning.

(Paul): --- You need not say more.

(Kylian): --- I want her alive, Paul. Let that be enough to get me to the door.

(Sarah): --- Then we leave within the hour. I can put us through the outer chain. At the inner seam I need you.

(Kylian): --- You need more than me. The Threshold opens to a mark in a hand. My office was taken; the mark remains. You carry one too. I will not take only those two hands into my people's last chamber.

(Sarah): --- You think I shall reach for it.

(Kylian): --- You told me you would. And I have spent my life reaching past the door set for me. There must be a third hand that belongs to neither of us.

(Paul): --- Mine.

(Kylian): --- Yours. To witness what we do. To stop either of us. I can mark it, if you consent.

(Paul): --- The needle and stone?

(Kylian): --- Yes. The rite spends the stone. What I carry is the last that ever left the Mountain: one hand, one setting. After you there is no other. That is why a Veil's mark was holy. It cannot be multiplied like words on your paper.

(Paul): --- Then before you spend it, let us agree what you are buying. I am not swearing that whatever you call the Mountain's interest will become mine. I may disagree at the very moment you most need me to agree.

(Kylian): --- Then disagree where I can hear you. If I only wanted your hand to open a door, I would have said so.

(Paul): --- A marked witness may be more difficult to send away.

(Kylian): --- I know what happens when the person who wants the question closed also holds the person asking it. That is why I want you with us. Not behind us, waiting for the account we choose to bring back. You can refuse.

(Paul): --- My work is the record. If I enter this, I become one of the people whose conduct needs recording. I cannot interview myself into independence afterwards.

(Sarah): --- Can you carry the machine?

(Paul): --- That is not the difficulty. My proper place might be outside, making certain the testimony survives whatever happens to its witnesses.

(Sarah): --- Here, in the house with the wire?

(Paul): --- Somewhere else. I could take the reels out and remain outside with them.

(Kylian): --- You could.

(Paul): --- And possess the account up to the moment we learnt enough to act. Whatever followed would belong to the people who returned to tell me. If none returned, it would end here.

(Sarah): --- Bring your machine, Mr Morgan.

(Paul): --- Yes. I am coming. I will not call it disinterested work after this.

(Kylian): --- I never asked you to.

(Paul): --- Box the reels. Not this one yet. I need the travelling machine running before this stops.

(Sarah): --- You have it?

(Paul): --- Running. Elise, will you take this interview reel when I stop it? Keep it with the sealed interviews and my notebooks, please. Not among the substituted lesson copies.

(Elise): --- I will. The instruction reels and those copies go together in the fire case. They keep their own boxes.

(Paul): --- I am stopping the fixed machine now. The travelling machine continues.

\centerline{}

(Elise): --- These are your travel papers. Keep your own sets in this order. The first name takes the helicopter from here. The second goes on the private aircraft's manifest. The third starts at the vehicle chain. Learn them on the way; answer to the name on the page you are carrying.

(Kylian): --- And if they ask me something I do not understand?

(Elise): --- Wait for me.

(Kylian): --- As before.

(Elise): --- Yes.

(Sarah): --- The chain, Elise.

(Elise): --- Helicopter from the lawn to the family field. Private aircraft to a prepared strip. Vehicles south, animals from the folds. Amastan's line meets us there, or what remains of it. None of the separate bookings should give a clerk much to remember.

(Kylian): --- Who has them all?

(Elise): --- I have their order and your identities. Urs opened the aircraft, fuel and vehicles without the final ground. Each air crew knows its own leg. The vehicle lead gets his road when the aircraft lands. Amastan's line has the last ground and none of the air. Jean-Charles gets his tactical packet when we leave the final vehicle. Sarah knows where the pieces meet. There is no page with the whole journey on it.

(Paul): --- And if one hand fails to meet the next?

(Elise): --- We do not improvise a replacement identity in front of a border officer. Stay beside me. At the folds it will be Amastan's people you obey, however familiar the ground looks to you, Kylian. You came out once. They have to go on living there.

(Paul): --- You have given me no papers for the recorder.

(Elise): --- Madame's medical instrument until the vehicles. The manifest says so. After that, on your body. Keep it with you if a man offers to carry it.

(Paul): --- And the fire case?

(Elise): --- It comes with us, sealed, with one copy of the route papers. If anyone enters the house after we leave, the archive room burns separately. Your interview reels keep their own seals. Nothing is being put back into the instruction archive.

(Paul): --- How much of this did you prepare before tonight?

(Elise): --- The way out of a compromised house. I did not have tonight's destination. Please learn the first name before you reach the pilot.

(Sarah): --- Time?

(Elise): --- The helicopter is nearly ready. After that we require air permission, fuel, roads, and the routes still to belong to you.

(Paul): --- I had hoped at least the roads were certain.

(Elise): --- So had I.

(Jean-Charles): --- Come, man. We do not make ceremony of leaving in this house.

(Kylian): --- You need not turn me by the shoulder. I know which way to go. You did that at the dead well.

(Jean-Charles): --- You were going towards the camels. Same habit. Come.

(Kylian): --- One thing first. Paul, bring the machine. Under the tree.

\centerline{}

(Paul): --- Do we have time for this?

(Kylian): --- We have time for what I am asking you to accept. Last time we stood here I put the needle away. I gave you the word and kept back the wound.

(Paul): --- Shadow.

(Kylian): --- You watched the house on a captive's word. You found the eye and did not pull the cord. I have no completed office to hand you, Paul. Only the charges, and the mark.

(Paul): --- I have not found an owner for the wire.

(Kylian): --- I am not asking you to name one. Kneel. Hear the charges. There is no book to hold them but you.

\begin{scripture}
By the dark the first morning left unlit: \\
I call on you to guard the Mountain from the enemy within. \\
By the proud who gathered the whole into one hand, and drowned for it: \\
I call on you never to seek the whole. \\
By God, who held His breath while the first Light was struck: \\
I call on you to carry this to your grave, and give it up to no pain past it. \\
And by the Beast beneath the Mountain, keeper of the first wrath and the next: \\
for Shadow must guard also from Shadow: \\
if ever I am the hand that reaches for the whole, \\
be the hand that ends me.
\end{scripture}

(Kylian): --- And now the covenant. Answer me true. One day they will take you and open you. What will they find?

(Paul): --- \ldots{}

(Kylian): --- Stop smiling. This is no joke.

(Paul): --- Of course. Very serious indeed.

(Kylian): --- One day they will take you and open you. What will they find?

(Paul): --- My face. And nothing behind it.

(Kylian): --- Give me your free hand. Hold still. I have waited a long time for a use for this needle.

(Paul): --- You need not enjoy it quite so much.

(Kylian): --- I am trying to keep it straight.

\centerline{}

(Kylian): --- Your blood on the stone. Then it is sworn.

\begin{scripture}
blood enters service; \\
the face enters the dark; \\
the name enters silence.
\end{scripture}

(Kylian): --- Rise now as Va-Paul, Veil of Shadow: a name you will hear once, keep to your last hour, and never say aloud; for in Shadow we speak neither prefix nor suffix.

\centerline{}

\needspace{10\baselineskip}
(Paul): --- Is that all?

(Kylian): --- It will heal to a mark no untrained eye can read. The stone has done its work. Give me a moment to wrap your hand.

(Paul): --- I need both hands for the machine.

(Kylian): --- Then keep it. I will work round you. There. How do you feel?

(Paul): --- \ldots{} This is the moment I am meant to say a deep and meaningful thing. \ldots{} Unlit?

\end{room}
